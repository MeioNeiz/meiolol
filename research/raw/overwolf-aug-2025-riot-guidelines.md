## **LoL Season 3 Policy Changes**

With Season 3 of League of Legends right around the corner, Riot has introduced new guidelines that will affect the functionality of many third-party apps. Please make sure to review and act on these changes before August 27, 2025.  
  
Features that must be removed:

-   Notifications that alert power spikes (e.g., "X champion has hit level 6")
-   Notifications that suggest game decisions (e.g., "Gank top lane now!")
-   Tracking enemy ability or summoner spell cooldowns
-   Displaying minor rune data

These changes aim to preserve competitive integrity and gameplay balance. We’ve updated our [Riot Compliance documentation](https://dev.overwolf.com/ow-electron/guides/game-compliance/riot-games/?ref=blog.overwolf.com#general-feature-compliance) to reflect these new policies and provide guidance.  
  
Please notify your Developer Relations Manager once your app has been updated. If you’re unclear about any requirements, don’t hesitate to reach out.
