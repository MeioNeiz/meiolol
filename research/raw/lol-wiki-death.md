**Death** occurs when a [champion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion "Champion") takes sufficient [damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage "Damage") to be reduced to zero [health](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Health "Health"). Dying removes the champion from active play and causes them to enter the **death screen**, where they will have to wait some time before they _respawn_ at their allied [fountain](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Fountain "Fountain"). If slain by an enemy champion,  [experience](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)") and [gold](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gold "Gold") [bounties](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#Bounty "Kill") are dispensed and granted to the [killer](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill "Kill"), their [assistors](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Assist "Assist"), and nearby enemies.

Certain effects are able to defer death or prevent it from triggering its penalties altogether, allowing the player to continue some or all of their actions for a varying amount of time.

## Death screen

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Death screen") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Death screen")\]

While in the death screen, the player's view becomes grey, the [Death Recap](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death_Recap "Death Recap") summarizes the player's combat, and other champion or situation-specific events may occur. A special "Ally Spectate" menu also appears—within the menu, players can  select the portrait of allies who are alive to jump the camera to them and follow their view. The menu can also be moved by clicking and dragging.

While dead:

-   Players **cannot** perform the vast majority of map-impacting actions. They are prohibited from movement, [attacking](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Basic_attack "Basic attack"), and casting [champion abilities](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_ability "Champion ability"), [item active abilities](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Active_ability_items "Active ability items") and [summoner spells](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner_spell "Summoner spell").
-   Players **can** purchase [items](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Item "Item") from the [shop](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Shop "Shop") as if they were inside their shopping platform, look around the map, cast [pings](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ping "Ping"), and use the scoreboard and chat. There are 5 items that **can** be used while dead, these are:  [Elixir of Iron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Elixir_of_Iron),  [Elixir of Sorcery](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Elixir_of_Sorcery),  [Elixir of Wrath](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Elixir_of_Wrath),  [Redemption](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Redemption), and  [Your Cut](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Your_Cut) (of these, 4 are consumable).

Some passive effects from [champion abilities](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_ability "Champion ability") or [items](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Item "Item") may be disabled while in the death screen. The death screen does not affect currently incurred cooldowns and they will continue being incurred as normal.

## Death timer

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Death timer") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Death timer")\]

Upon death, every player must wait an amount of time before they respawn, called the **death timer**. A countdown above the "Ally Spectate" portraits indicates this timer. Decimals within the countdown are not displayed and always round up.

When the death timer reaches zero, the player's champion respawns at the center of their allied fountain.

### Summoner's Rift

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Summoner's Rift") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Summoner's Rift")\]

In  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift), death timers increase both by champion **level** and by **game time**. The sum of these two increases is the total death timer.

By level, death timers consider a set time duration, called **Base Respawn Wait time (BRW)** here, in their calculations:

**BRW** = 10 / 10 / 12 / 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 25 / 28 / 32.5 / 35 / 37.5 / 40 / 42.5 / 45 / 47.5 / 50 / 52.5 seconds

  
By game time, death timer calculations involve a multiplier that modifies the **BRW**, called the **Time Increase Factor** (**TIFx**) here.

After 15 minutes, the **TIFx** increases「 2 times per minute 」, and it is also multiplied by:

-   **0.425%** between 15 and 30 minutes
-   **0.3%** between 30 and 45 minutes
-   **1.45%** after 45 minutes, until 55 minutes

## Loss of buffs or effects

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Loss of buffs or effects") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Loss of buffs or effects")\]

Death does not mean that every ongoing effect is interrupted or removed when the source dies. For example, [damage over time](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage_over_time "Damage over time") and already launched [projectiles](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Projectile "Projectile") are not affected by death.

By default, death interrupts [basic attack windups](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Basic_attack#General "Basic attack"), [cast times](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Cast_time "Cast time"),  [channels](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Channel), and all forms of movement (e.g.  [dashes](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dash),  [displacements](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Airborne)). Entering  [resurrection](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Ways_to_prevent_death_when_reaching_zero_health) will not interrupt an ability's cast time, unless it is special-cased to be interrupted by the resurrection source. Any other effect that is removed or interrupted by death is specifically implemented to do so.

If the slain champion possessed a [status effect](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Buff "Buff") that doesn't _persist through death_, it is removed. For example, the  [Crest of Cinders](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Cinders) and  [Crest of Insight](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Insight) buffs are removed upon dying. This may also apply to debuffs:  [Exhaust](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Exhaust) does not persist through death, meaning spells that are able to continue dealing damage after the enemy's death may inadvertently deal more damage to the team than desired, due to the debuff's removal.

Many other status effects may be reapplied again upon respawning, depending on their particular conditions; this is common among [item effects](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Item_effects "Item effects"). Yet others may simply be permanent and unaffected by the death state (e.g. the  [Bloody Petals](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Bloody_Petals) neutral buff).

-   Common buffs that are lost upon death:
    -    [Health Potion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Health_Potion)
    -    [Refillable Potion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Refillable_Potion)
    -    [Hand of Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hand_of_Baron)
    -    [Aspect of the Dragon](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aspect_of_the_Dragon)
-   Buffs that are transferred to the killer:
    -    [Crest of Insight](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Insight) (conditionally)
    -    [Crest of Cinders](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Cinders) (conditionally)
-   Champion ability stacks lost upon death:
    -    [Draven's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Draven)  [League of Draven](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Draven#League_of_Draven)
-   Item stacks lost upon death:
    -    [Dark Seal](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dark_Seal) (loses 5 stacks)
    -    [Hubris](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hubris) (loses all stacks)
    -    [Mejai's Soulstealer](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mejai%27s_Soulstealer) (loses 10 stacks)

## Ways to prevent death when reaching zero health

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=6&veaction=edit "Edit section: Ways to prevent death when reaching zero health") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section's source code: Ways to prevent death when reaching zero health")\]

 [**Edit**](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Tip_data/Resurrection?action=edit) A unit that is protected by **Resurrection** will enter a revival state and  [dispel](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Debuff) most  [debuffs](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Debuff) upon taking  fatal damage. After the state completes, the unit is revived. **Resurrection** effects do not count for kills or assists.

**Resurrection** sources have a hierarchy, and will trigger in the following order:

1.   [Zilean's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zilean)  [Chronoshift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zilean#Chronoshift)
2.   [Zaahen's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zaahen)  [Cultivation of War](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zaahen#Cultivation_of_War)
3.   [Guardian Angel](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Guardian_Angel)
4.   [Anivia's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Anivia)  [Rebirth](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Anivia#Rebirth) and  [Zac's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zac)  [Cell Division](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zac#Cell_Division)

**Resurrection** will always take precedence over  [zombie state](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Zombie_states) effects. Entering **resurrection** does not cancel cast times.  [Renata Glasc's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Renata_Glasc)  [Bailout](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Renata_Glasc#Bailout) always takes priority over **resurrection**.

-    [Renata Glasc's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Renata_Glasc)  [Bailout](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Renata_Glasc#Bailout) prevents the target from dying upon reaching zero health.

## Ways to prevent dying by not losing health

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=7&veaction=edit "Edit section: Ways to prevent dying by not losing health") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section's source code: Ways to prevent dying by not losing health")\]

A unit that has gained a  **minimum health threshold** cannot have their health reduced below it from damage dealt by enemies.

The following effects grant a minimum health threshold:

-    [Kindred's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kindred)  [Lamb's Respite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kindred#Lamb's_Respite)
-    [Pantheon's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Pantheon)  [Grand Starfall](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Pantheon#Grand_Starfall)
-    [Tryndamere's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Tryndamere)  [Undying Rage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Tryndamere#Undying_Rage)

 [Executes](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#Execute) against units within this state will set the target's health to **1**. All other sources of  [raw damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage) ignore the threshold.

Other effects that trigger from damage dealt will still take place (e.g.  [life steal](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Life_steal "Life steal")) even if the unit under the effect of a minimum damage threshold ignores the damage itself.

## Zombie states

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=9&veaction=edit "Edit section: Zombie states") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=9 "Edit section's source code: Zombie states")\]

 [**Edit**](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Tip_data/Zombie_state?action=edit) A buff that allows a player to keep controlling their  [champion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion) after  Death always flags the champion to be in a **zombie state**. It prevents enemies from gaining full kill credit or triggering most on-kill effects from the champion a second time, and some abilities (e.g.  [You and Me!](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Yuumi#You_and_Me!)) interact differently with champions in this state.  [Renata Glasc's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Renata_Glasc)  [Bailout](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Renata_Glasc#Bailout) and  [Resurrection](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Ways_to_prevent_death_when_reaching_zero_health) will always take precedence over **zombie state** effects.

The following effects trigger a champion to enter a **zombie state**:

-    [Karthus'](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Karthus)  [Death Defied](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Karthus#Death_Defied)
-    [Kog'Maw's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kog%27Maw)  [Icathian Surprise](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kog%27Maw#Icathian_Surprise)
-    [Sion's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion)  [Glory in Death](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion#Glory_in_Death)

### Notes

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=10&veaction=edit "Edit section: Notes") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=10 "Edit section's source code: Notes")\]

-   Zombie states trigger upon taking  lethal damage.
    -    [Raw damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage) effects will not prevent zombie states from triggering.
-   All  [resurrection](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Ways_to_prevent_death_when_reaching_zero_health) effects will take precedence over effects that reanimate as a zombie.
-   A champion that enters a zombie state will start their death timer upon triggering the effect.
-   Most buffs and debuffs will be lost upon transitioning into a zombie state.
    -   Those that persist through death, such as  [Brand's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Brand)  [Blaze](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Brand#Blaze), will remain.
    -   Undisplayed marker buffs, such as those imposed by  [Veigar's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Veigar)  [Event Horizon](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Veigar#Event_Horizon) and  [Viktor's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Viktor)  [Gravity Field](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Viktor#Gravity_Field) to prevent being affected multiple times by the same effect, will be lost.(bug)
    -   This does not apply when transitioning from one zombie state to another zombie state.(bug)
-   Units within a zombie state cannot:
    -   Use  [Recall](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Recall) or  [Refillable Potion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Refillable_Potion).
    -   Activate  [resurrection](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Ways_to_prevent_death_when_reaching_zero_health) effects.
    -   Take  [Dark Passage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Thresh#Dark_Passage), [Catapult](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Catapult "Catapult"), or  [gates](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Warp_gate).
    -   Initate the  [Rift Herald Rodeo](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rift_Herald#Abilities_2 "Rift Herald").
    -   Pick up the  [Eye of the Herald](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Eye_of_the_Herald).
    -   Be targeted by the following:
        -    [Bailout](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Renata_Glasc#Bailout)
        -    [Chronoshift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zilean#Chronoshift)
        -    [Umbral Trespass](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kayn#Umbral_Trespass).
    -   Be affected by the following:
        -    [Curse of the Black Mist](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Senna#Curse_of_the_Black_Mist)
        -    [Fear Beyond Death's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Urgot#Fear_Beyond_Death_2) chain missile
        -    [Test of Spirit's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Illaoi#Test_of_Spirit) initial missile

## Order of events upon death

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?section=12&veaction=edit "Edit section: Order of events upon death") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death?action=edit&section=12 "Edit section's source code: Order of events upon death")\]

Some champions have abilities that trigger after their death or the death of another targeted champion, allowing for limited control by one of the involved parties. These will only occur after the previously mentioned sequence and when more than one of them apply they will occur in the following order.

1.   [Mordekaiser's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mordekaiser)  [Realm of Death](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mordekaiser#Realm_of_Death) (keeping the stolen stats)
2.   [Viego's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Viego)  [Sovereign's Domination](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Viego#Sovereign's_Domination) (Mist Wraith appear)
3.   [Zombie state](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Zombie_states) effects -  [Karthus'](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Karthus)  [Death Defied](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Karthus#Death_Defied) /  [Sion's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion)  [Glory in Death](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sion#Glory_in_Death) /  [Kog'Maw's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kog%27Maw)  [Icathian Surprise](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kog%27Maw#Icathian_Surprise)
4.   [Lissandra's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Lissandra)  [Iceborn Subjugation](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Lissandra#Iceborn_Subjugation)
