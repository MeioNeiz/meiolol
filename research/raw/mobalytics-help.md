## I can't login / sign in / sign up

<https://support.mobalytics.gg/hc/en-us/articles/360046861311-I-can-t-login-sign-in-sign-up> (updated 2026-10-14)

If you are using the Desktop app, make sure Google Chrome is set as your default browser and that any AdBlock software is disabled as it does not allow the correct connections to be made for you to log in!

If you are on the webpage, try to clear cache/cookies and see if that works, or try an Incognito browser and see if that will let you log in!

To clear cache/cookies in Chrome

- On your computer, open Chrome.

- At the top right, click More .

- Click More tools  Clear browsing data.

- At the top, choose a time range. To delete everything, select All time.

- Next to "Cookies and other site data" and "Cached images and files," check the boxes.

- Click Clear data.

If the steps above did not help, please submit a ticket on our website or on our Discord.

## The Live Companion is not detecting my games

<https://support.mobalytics.gg/hc/en-us/articles/360060919652-The-Live-Companion-is-not-detecting-my-games> (updated 2026-10-15)

If you are using our Overwolf application, please try the following:

- Go to Overwolf's `Overlay & Hotkeys` settings

- Enable Mobalytics for League of Legends

- Restart Overwolf

If you are using our standalone application (not Overwolf), please try the following:

- Attempt to restart the app.

- To fully close the app you must open your tray (bottom right corner of your taskbar), right-click the Mobalytics symbol and select "Quit". Then relaunch the app normally.

- Please ensure you have the correct location of your game folder.

- Click the settings cog wheel in the top right and select "Game settings".

- From there select the tab labeled “Desktop”

- Confirm the game folder listed here is the correct path to your League installation.

If you have done all these steps and Live Companion is still not detecting your games, please submit a ticket on our website or on our Discord.


## My Profile is showing the wrong account. 

<https://support.mobalytics.gg/hc/en-us/articles/4415454785933-My-Profile-is-showing-the-wrong-account> (updated 2024-08-22)

Our app automatically detects your account when you log in to League client. If it does not, please do the following:

- Go to our app settings

- Select "Game account" on the left

- Insert your Summoner name and region

- Press "Save changes"

You should now see the correct profile. If you do not, please submit a help ticket on our website or on our Discord.
