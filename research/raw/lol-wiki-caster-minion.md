**Caster minions** are minions that are frail and have  [ranged](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ranged) attack range. Three caster minions spawn in each minion wave.

## Notes

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Notes") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Notes")\]

-   Minions deal 60% damage against [structures](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Structure "Structure") and  [champions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion).
-   Minions _upgrade_ every 90 seconds. Caster minions may increase their health and attack damage, up to a cap.
-   Caster minions have the cosmetic item  Phreakish Gusto, which describes the following combat rules:
    -   **Unique – [Phreakish Gusto](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Phreakish_Gusto "Named item effect"):**  [Turret](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret) basic attacks are modified to deal 70% of this minion's **maximum** health as pre-mitigation damage.
    -   **Unique – [Minion Damager](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Minion_Damager "Named item effect"):** Against lane minions, this minion deals **bonus** physical damage on-hit equal to 3.5% of the target's **current** health.

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Map-Specific Differences") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Map-Specific Differences")\]

## Trivia

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\]

-   The ' Phreakish Gusto' effect that caster minions have is named after [**Phreak**](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/David_%27Phreak%27_Turley "David 'Phreak' Turley").
    -   The connection to the caster minion could also reference Phreak's role as a shoutcaster.
-   In [Teamfight Tactics](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Teamfight_Tactics "Teamfight Tactics") Sets 1, 2 and 4, their  Red Team variants featured as [PvE Round monsters](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Monster "TFT:Monster") in Rounds 1–3 and 1–4.
-   The caster minion had different names during development:
    -   In Beta 1, they were called _Barracks Wizard_.
        -   During this time there were also ranged minions called _Archer Minion_.
    -   In Beta 3, were called _Wizard Minion_.
    -   Summoner's Rift Update caster minions are referred to as _ranged minion_ in the game files.

## Patch History

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Patch History") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Patch History")\]

[V26.09](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.09 "V26.09")

-   On-hit minion health ratio reduced to 3.5% of target's **current** health from 4%.

[V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01 "V26.01")

-   Base experience reduced to 31 from 32.

[V25.S1.1](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V25.S1.1 "V25.S1.1")

-   Health changed to 284 – 600 (based on upgrades) from 296 – 485 (based on upgrades).
-   Attack damage changed to 21 – 125 (based on upgrades) from 24.5 – 120 (based on upgrades).
-   **New Effect:** Now deals **bonus** damage on-hit equal to 4% of target's **current** health against lane minions.

[V8.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.23 "V8.23")

-   Health changed to 296 – 485 (based on upgrades) from 296 – 425 (based on minutes).
    -   Time threshold to trigger health cap increased to 37 minutes from 27 minutes.
    -   Maximum health increased to 485 from 425 (previous maximum at 27 minutes). _Value at 27 minutes remains 425._
-   Attack damage changed to 24.5 – 120 (based on upgrades) from 22.5 (+1.5 / 90s).
    -   Attack damage now has a maximum threshold instead of scaling infinitely.
    -   Maximum attack damage implemented at 120.5 (at 37 minutes). _Previous value at 37 minutes was 60.5._
-    [Hand of Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hand_of_Baron)
    -   Incoming champion damage reduction increased to 70% from 50%.

[V8.7](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.7 "V8.7")

-   Summoner's Rift:
    -   Base gold reward reduced to  14 from  17.
    -   Gold reward growth reduced to  0 from  0.125.

[V8.2](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.2 "V8.2")

-   **New Effect:** Now have the cosmetic item  [Phreakish Gusto](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Phreakish_Gusto).

[V6.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V6.22 "V6.22")

-   Health growth over time decreased by ~60%.
-   Ranged minions now gain ~100% attack damage as the game progresses
-   Now receives a fixed ~70% max health damage per bullet from Turret bullets.

[V5.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.22 "V5.22")

-   Initial gold increased to 17 from 13.8.
-   Gold growth reduced to 0.125 per minute from 0.17.

[V5.8](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.8 "V5.8")

-   Visual and Gameplay Update on Howling Abyss map (to match Summoner's Rift).
    -   Minions no longer gain magic resistance over time.
    -   Minion health gain over time increased to compensate the loss of resistances.

[V4.21](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.21 "V4.21")

-   Ranged minion gold reward 14 ⇒ 15.

[V4.20](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.20 "V4.20")

-   Minions no longer gain armor or magic resistance over time.
-   All minion health gain over time has been increased to be roughly as durable as when they had resistances.

[V4.19](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.19 "V4.19")

-   Visual Update on Summoner's Rift map.

[V3.14](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.14 "V3.14")

-   Lane Minions are no longer worth more experience based on game time.

[V1.0.0.152](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.152 "V1.0.0.152")

-   Base gold value reduced to 15 from 16.

[V1.0.0.112](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.112 "V1.0.0.112")

-   Caster minion armor now increases by 1.25 per 3 minutes, up from 0.5.

[V1.0.0.87](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.87 "V1.0.0.87")

-   Gold from minion kills increased by 3 for all minions.

[V1.0.0.61](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.61 "V1.0.0.61")

-   Gain 0.5 additional armor every 3 minutes.
-   Gain 2 additional magic resist every 3 minutes.
-   Gold bounty increased to 16 from 14 on Summoner's Rift and to 19 from 17 on Twisted Treeline.

[May 15, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_15,_2009_Patch "May 15, 2009 Patch")

-   Basic minion gold yield growth reduced by 50%.

[May 1, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_1,_2009_Patch "May 1, 2009 Patch")

-   New caster minions for both blue and red teams.

[Alpha Week 6](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_6 "Alpha Week 6")

-   Archer minion health increased to 280 from 260.

[Alpha Week 4](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_4 "Alpha Week 4")

-   Caster minion gold bounty reduced to 18 from 22.

[Alpha Week 2](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_2 "Alpha Week 2")

-   Reduced the selection radius of archers (caster minions)
