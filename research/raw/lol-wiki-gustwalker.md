Passive

**Unique – [Jungle Companions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Jungle_Companions "Named item effect"):** Summon a _Gustwalker Hatchling_ companion to assist you in combat against monsters.

**Unique – [Gustwalker's Gait](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Gustwalker's_Gait "Named item effect"):** Feed your companion enough treats to evolve it and upgrade your  [Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Smite). **Role Binding:** Must purchase a Jungle item in order to unlock purchasing any non-[consumable](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Consumable_item "Consumable item") item in the shop. Cannot be resold to the shop.

Gustwalker's Gait

[Edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Template:Buff_data_Gustwalker%27s_Gait?action=edit)

Description

While in a  [brush](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Brush), gain 30% **bonus** movement speed, decaying over 1.5 seconds after leaving the brush. Additionally, killing a large monster grants you 45% **bonus** movement speed decaying over 1.5 seconds.

If the large monster is killed while you are within a brush, the **bonus** movement speed from being inside the brush is increased to 45% instead. This increased movement speed lasts until you exit the brush.

Stats

Duration

Permanent

Source

 [Gustwalker Primal Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gustwalker_Primal_Smite "Gustwalker Primal Smite")

Affect

-   Gain 10% increased damage against any non-epic  [monster](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster). This damage modifier does not apply to [true damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/True_damage "True damage") dealt, including through  [Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Smite).
-   Take 50% damage from non-epic  [monsters](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster).
-   While in the jungle or river, you gain **bonus** mana regeneration equal to 8.62% – 19.08% (based on level) **missing** mana.
-   Killing a large monster:
    -   Awards 80 **bonus** experience at all times, plus an additional 150 **bonus** experience from the first one slain.
    -   While you are more than 1.1 levels behind the game's average champion level, awards an additional 50 **bonus** experience for every integer level of disadvantage. This is rounded to the nearest integer.
    -    [Heals](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Healing) you between 0 and 90 – 250 (based on average champion level) after a 0.3\-second delay. This value is increased by 1.25% per 1% **missing** health.
    -    [Restores](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mana_regeneration "Mana regeneration") 19 – 87 (based on average champion level) mana after a 0.3\-second delay. This value is increased by 1.25% per 1% **missing** mana.
    -   Restores 15 energy after a 0.3\-second delay.
-   A **Jungle companion** (or **pet**) is summoned to follow you and help you fight  [monsters](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster).
-   Pets will attack **all** monsters currently attacking you within a radius of  650 radius from you. They attack at a rate of once per second, dealing 20 – 150 (based on level) (+ 10% **bonus** AD) (+ 16% AP) (+ 25% **bonus** armor) (+ 25% **bonus** magic resistance) (+ 4% **bonus** health) true damage to each monster. While the pet is attacking, you heal for 6 – 36 (based on level) per second.
    -   This damage counts as [area of effect](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage "Damage") regardless of how many targets are hit and does not benefit from [omnivamp](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Omnivamp "Omnivamp").
    -   After you stop attacking the current camp, the pet will attack two more times before stopping as well.
    -   If your pet is not near you, damaging a camp will cause it to leap from its current location and attack as normal.
    -   Pets cannot deal lethal damage to epic monsters.
-   Pets can undergo two **evolutions** by being fed **treats**.
    -   Feed your pet 1 treat each time you kill a large monster.
    -   You store 1  _bonus_ treat every 60 seconds starting from time 1:30 (with the first treat granted at 2:30), increased to 90 after your pet reaches its final evolution form.
        -    _Bonus_ treats are tracked on your buff bar.
        -   If you have a  _bonus_ treat stored, killing a large monster consumes 1 stack to grant you  20 when you feed your pet.
        -   After the pet reaches its final evolution form, it will now consume 2 stacks.
-   Your item tracks the remaining treats required to feed your pet until it reaches its final evolution. When your pet reaches its final evolution, the item is consumed.
    -   The item's effects and item group limitations persist through the item's consumption from the inventory.
-   At 15 treats, your pet reaches its first evolution, upgrading  [Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Smite) to  [Unleashed Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Unleashed_Smite).
-   At 35 treats, your pet reaches its second and final evolution (adulthood), upgrading  [Unleashed Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Unleashed_Smite) to  [Primal Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Primal_Smite), and consuming the pet's egg from your inventory to grant you a permanent buff:
    -    **[Gustwalker's Gait](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gustwalker%27s_Gait)**: While in a  [brush](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Brush), gain 30% **bonus** movement speed, decaying over 1.5 seconds after leaving the brush. Additionally, killing a large monster grants you 45% **bonus** movement speed decaying over 1.5 seconds.
        -   If the large monster is killed while you are within a brush, the **bonus** movement speed from being inside the brush is increased to 45% instead. This increased movement speed lasts until you exit the brush.
            -   If the user enters a  [brush](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Brush) while the **bonus** movement speed from killing a large monster is decaying, the decay will stop and the current bonus movement speed value is maintained while they remain within the brush. If the bonus movement speed decayed to less than 30%, however, then the value is increased to that amount upon entering the brush.
    -    **[Mosstomper's Courage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mosstomper%27s_Courage)**: Gain a 200 – 360 (based on level)  [shield](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Shield) that lasts until broken and refreshes after killing a monster camp or 10 seconds without combat.
    -    **[Scorchclaw's Slash](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Scorchclaw%27s_Slash)**: Gain 3 _Ember_ stacks every 0.5 seconds, up to 100. Killing a large monster grants **maximum** stacks. When fully stacked, your next damaging [basic attack](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Basic_attack "Basic attack") or instance of [ability damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ability_damage "Ability damage") against an enemy champion consumes all stacks to burn them and all enemies within  250 units of them, dealing **bonus** true damage equal to「 5% of the target's **maximum** health over 4 seconds 」and  [slowing](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Slow) them by 30% decaying over 2 seconds.
        -   The damage dealt by this effect is classified as  [proc damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage) and is tagged as  [periodic](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage) without applying  [spell effects](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ability_effects).
-   The user receives 70% – 0% (based on minutes) reduced experience from all lane minions, unless they are 1.5 levels behind the average game level.
-    **Monster Hunter:** If your total gold earned from minion kills is greater than 40% of your gold from monster kills, killing a lane minion will grant you  13 less gold and 50% reduced experience. This penalty is indefinitely removed at 14 minutes.
-   Killing a _[Draconic](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dragon_pit#Elemental_Rift "Dragon pit")_  [Blue Sentinel](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blue_Sentinel "Blue Sentinel") or  [Red Brambleback](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Red_Brambleback "Red Brambleback") leaves behind a _wisp_ at the location of their camp for 60 seconds, which can be picked up by walking within its marked radius and grants the  [Crest of Insight](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Insight) or  [Crest of Cinders](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Cinders) buff, respectively. If an ally has slain the camp, the wisp can only be picked up by you; if you are its killer, the wisp can be picked up by one other ally.

-   **Unique Passive - Jungle Companions:** Summon a _Gustwalker Hatchling_ companion to assist you in combat against monsters.
-   **Unique Passive - Gustwalker's Gait:** Feed the companion  treats to evolve it and upgrade your  [Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Smite). Upon the companion reaching its final evolution, this item is consumed, granting you the  _Gustwalker's Gait_ buff:
    -   While in a brush, gain  45% **bonus** movement speed, decaying over 2 seconds after leaving the brush. Killing a large monster increases this effect to 60% for 2 seconds.
-   Limited to 1 **Jungle / Support** item.
