## League of Legends Patch Notes Breakdown

Welcome to our patch notes breakdown for **League of Legends Season 26.21 (Split 3 for LoL)**!

Let’s break down all the new changes in LoL coming this week.

We recommend following along with the [official patch notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/tags/patch-notes/) since we won’t be listing all the specific stat changes, but rather focusing on the impact of the changes and the champions updated place in the current meta.

If you’re wondering how these changes will affect the meta, check out our LoL champion tier list!

### **Buffs, Nerfs, and Changes**

  

### Champion Changes

**Azir (Buff)** 

-   [Azir’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/azir/build/mid) mana growth is increased.
-   This will give him more time before he runs out of mana. While the buff is nice, it won't make much of a difference, since he has more issues to worry about than mana.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 46.1% win rate.

  

**Caitlyn (Nerf)** 

-   [Caitlyn’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/caitlyn/build/adc) Headshot Passive damage scaling is reduced. 
-   Her Q base damage is increased, which will help with pushing and poking.
-   I think overall this is going to make Caitlyn a lower-priority pick and worse moving forward.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 49.9% win rate.

  

**Darius (Buff)** 

-   [Darius’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/darius/build/top) Q cooldown is reduced.
-   This will make Darius an even stronger laner and will probably make him a pick or ban in Low ELO, as he is a very dominant lane opponent.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 48.4% win rate.

  

**Jinx (Nerf)** 

-   [Jinx’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/jinx/build/adc) AD growth is reduced. This will reduce her scaling throughout the game.
-   Her base HP is also reduced, meaning she will be slightly weaker and more vulnerable early on, especially vs all-in or mage Supports.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 52.2% win rate.

  

**Lee Sin (Nerf)** 

-   His W cooldown is increased from 7 to 8 seconds. This change will impact his clear, reduce his 1v1 skirmishing power and make him a weaker Jungler.
-   Furthermore, [Lee Sin’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/leesin/build/jungle) Ult kick damage is reduced too.
-   Both changes will negatively impact Lee Sin in the Jungle role moving forward, and he will be a lower-priority pick. 
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 47.8% win rate.

  

**Locke (Adjustment)** 

-   [Locke’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/locke/build/mid) Q refund is changed based on unused charges.
-   His mana growth is reduced, which will affect him throughout the game.
-   Furthermore, his W mana cost is increased, and his health store is changed.
-   However, his E damage and scaling are both improved.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 48.3% win rate.

  

**Miss Fortune (Buff)** 

-   Her E damage is increased, which is going to help her in the ADC and Support roles. Her Ultimate damage modifier is also increased, which will help her in those post-level 6 all-ins and in team fights.
-   These buffs are nice, but I doubt they'll make [MF](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/missfortune/build/adc) a pick-or-ban champion moving forward.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 50.5% win rate.

  

**Nidalee (Nerf)** 

-   [Nidalee’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/nidalee/build/jungle) Cougar W damage is reduced.
-   Her Human E attack speed bonus is also reduced.
-   This will make Nidalee a worse Jungler moving forward. It will slow down her clear and also reduce her 1v1/2v2 potential. 
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 46.7% win rate.

  

**Nunu (Buff)** 

-   His Ultimate max damage is increased. 
-   It will deal more damage throughout the whole game. However, I think it’s quite difficult for [Nunu](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/nunu/build/jungle) to really see the full benefit of this buff.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 48.3% win rate.

  

**Orianna (Buff)** 

-   Both [Orianna’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/orianna/build/mid) Q and W AP ratios are increased. This will help Orianna clear waves, push and poke the enemy down in lane. This is a good buff for Orianna, and I think she'll be a higher-priority pick moving forward.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 45.7% win rate.

  

**Qiyana (Nerf)** 

-   [Qiyana’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/qiyana/build/jungle) Q damage is reduced.
-   This lowers her burst damage and will make it harder for Qiyana to one-shot enemy squishies. 
-   This will impact her in both the Mid and Jungle roles.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 45.4% win rate.

  

**Rammus (Nerf)** 

-   His W bonus armor is slightly reduced, meaning he will be weaker vs AD champions. Similarly, his bonus magic resist is also nerfed, meaning his W will make [Rammus](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/rammus/build/jungle) less durable throughout fights.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 53.8% win rate.

  

**Ryze (Buff)** 

-   His Q damage is adjusted, and the extra movement speed is increased as well.
-   [Ryze’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/ryze/build/mid) W AP ratio is increased, which will help him in the mid and late game.
-   Overall, these changes are good for Ryze, but I doubt they’ll increase his pick or win rate much. 
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 46.1% win rate.

  

**Sion (Buff)** 

-   [Sion’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/sion/build/top) health growth per level is increased. This change will help him in every matchup, especially the tougher ones.
-   His Ultimate damage is also increased.
-   These changes will help Sion in the Top Lane. 
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 50.6% win rate.

  

**Taliyah (Buff)** 

-   [Taliyah’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/taliyah/build/mid) E damage is increased. This will make Taliyah’s trades more effective and help her clear her early jungle faster.
-   This buff will help her in both the Mid and Jungle roles. 
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 47% win rate.

  

**Tristana (Nerf)**

-   Her Q attack speed is reduced. This will make [Tristana](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/tristana/build/adc) weaker in those early trades and all-ins. She will be worse off with this change.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 50.7% win rate.

  

**Varus (Adjustment)** 

-   [Varus’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/varus/build/adc) Q damage and charge scaling are increased.
-   His W damage, cooldown refund and missing-health scaling are adjusted. 
-   I think these changes will affect Varus in both the ADC and Mid roles. I also believe Varus will rely more on poking with his Q now rather than auto-attacking/AS builds.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 47.4% win rate.

  

**Vel’Koz (Buff)** 

-   [Vel’Koz’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/velkoz/build/support) Q damage is increased. 
-   This change will help Vel’Koz in the Mid, Bot and Support roles.
-   While this is a nice buff, I don't think it will make much of a difference in pick, win, or play rate. 
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 51.1% win rate.

  

**Viktor (Nerf)** 

-   [Viktor’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/viktor/build/mid) Q shield is reduced, which is going to reduce his survivability while trading in all roles.
-   His base movement speed is also reduced. This will make it harder for him to reposition and escape sticky situations and all-ins versus engage Supports or assassins in the Mid Lane.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 51.6% win rate.

  

**Wukong (Nerf)** 

-   [Wukong’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/monkeyking/build/jungle) HP per 5 is increased; however, his Passive armor per stack is reduced. This is going to make him weaker versus AD champions, but slightly stronger versus AP laners.
-   This will impact Wukong in both the Top and Jungle roles.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 53.1% win rate.

  

**Xin Zhao (Buff)** 

-   [Xin Zhao’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/xinzhao/build/jungle) Q bonus AD ratio is increased.
-   His E attack speed scaling is also increased. 
-   This buff will help Xin Zhao in his early clear and in 1v1 skirmishes with the enemy Jungler.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 49.4% win rate.

  

**Yone (Nerf)** 

-   [Yone’s](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/yone/build/mid) Q bonus AD ratio is reduced. This will negatively impact him as he scales throughout the game. 
-   It is going to impact him in both the Top and Mid roles.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 51.7% win rate.

  

**Zyra (Buff)** 

-   Her W seed cooldown is changed. 
-   This will let [Zyra](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/zyra/build/support) generate seeds faster, which will help her in both the Jungle and Support roles.
-   On patch 26.20, they had a 51.5% win rate.

  

#### **Item Changes**

**Bastion Breaker** 

-   Bastion Breaker’s lethality is reduced, and its Shaped Charge damage is adjusted. 

  

**Dawncore**

-   Dawncore’s AP per 100% base mana regeneration is increased.

  

**Gluttonous Greaves**

-   Gluttonous Greaves’ cost is increased.

  

**Hubris**

-   Hubris’s base bonus AD is increased for melee champions. The bonus AD per stack is adjusted for both melee and ranged champions.
