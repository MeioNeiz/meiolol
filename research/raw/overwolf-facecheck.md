## About the App

### Stop going in blind. Win more games with Facecheck.

Facecheck is your all-in-one companion for League of Legends and Teamfight Tactics, giving you the edge you need to climb the ranks. We provide you with all the data and tools to make smarter decisions, master your favorite champions, and dominate the competition.

**_Get rewarded for playing! Enter our weekly giveaways for a chance to win RP prizes, just for being a Facecheck user._**

### Before the Match: Prepare for Victory in Champion Select

Enter every game with a winning strategy. Facecheck gives you the complete pre-game advantage.

-   **Dominate the Draft:** Receive intelligent pick and ban recommendations based on your team composition, enemy counters, and the current meta.
-   **Scout Your Lobby:** Identify champion and team patterns based on customized and smart Tags
-   **One-Click Setups:** Import the most effective, high-level builds and runes directly into your client, saving you time and giving you the optimal setup.
-   **ARAM:** Playing ARAM? We got you with the most picked and highest win rate champions right now in the meta.

### During the Match: Execute with Confidence

Focus on your gameplay with powerful, real-time tools that help you make better decisions.

-   **Essential In-Game Widgets:** Track everything that matters with clean, simple overlays for enemy summoner spells, jungle camp respawns, and key player stats.
-   **Anticipate Your Opponents:** Get a sneak peek into enemy habits with Summoner's Insights, allowing you to predict their moves and make smarter, game-winning counters.
-   **Instantly Understand Any Champion:** Facing an unfamiliar foe? Champion Tags reveal their core strengths and weaknesses, so you immediately know how to play against them.
-   **Perfect Your Jungle Clear:** Autofilled or learning a new jungler? We provide optimal jungle pathing routes to ensure you have an efficient and impactful early game.
-   **Conquer the Arena:** Get dedicated support for Arena mode with the best Augment and item recommendations, plus in-depth scouting of all your opponents.
-   **Unlock Your Second Screen Advantage:** Use a second monitor for a rich, expanded view of in-game stats, including real-time damage dealt, damage taken, and other critical data.
-   **Automatic Highlight Capture:** Play without distraction while our system automatically records your best moments, from epic outplays to game-winning teamfights.

### After the Match: Analyze and Improve

Understand your performance to climb faster.

-   **In-Depth Game Analysis:** Get a detailed Postgame Breakdown of your performance. See who dominated, who underperformed, and compare key stats to understand exactly what decided the match.
-   **Track Your Long-Term Progress:** Monitor your stats across all champions to identify your strengths, pinpoint areas for improvement, and see how you stack up against the competition.

### Teamfight Tactics Support

Facecheck also includes dedicated TFT tools for compositions, champions, items, Augments, and quick in-game access.

### Prepare. Play. Improve.

Download Facecheck and get more out of every game.
