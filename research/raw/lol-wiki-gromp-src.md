The '''Gromp''' is a neutral {{tip|monster}} camp on {{tip|Summoner's Rift}}. '''Gromp's''' [[basic attack]]s uniquely deal {{sti|ad|{{as|100% AD}}}} {{as|magic damage}}.

Killing this camp awards {{g|gold}} and {{sti|{{as|experience}}}}.

== Notes ==
* {{monster patience|Gromp|{{#var:ms}}}}
* '''Gromp''' deals {{as|5% of the target's '''current''' health}} {{as|'''bonus''' magic damage}} with its attacks.
* '''Gromp's''' attacks are parried by {{tip|dodge}}, {{tip|block}}, and {{tip|blind}}.

== Trivia ==
* '''Gromp''' is a replacement for the {{ui|Wight}} on [[Summoner's Rift]] since patch [[V4.20]].
* '''Gromp's''' full name is '''Lord Grompulus Kevin Ribbiton of Croaksworth'''.
* Its animations were recycled for the {{ui|Gromp|file=DozermawSquare.png|Dozermaw|link=Odyssey: Extraction#Map elements}} and {{ui|Gromp|file=HogtongueSquare.png|Hogtongue|link=Odyssey: Extraction#Map elements}} monsters from {{tip|Odyssey: Extraction}}, a [[featured game mode|rotating game mode]], and {{TFTc|Nomsy|set=7.5}} from {{TFTt|Trainer|set=7}}'s pet trait and [[Dragonlands: Uncharted Realms (Teamfight Tactics)|mid-set expansion]]'s {{tip|TFT Dragonlands|Dragonlands}} [[Champion (Teamfight Tactics)|champ unit]] of the {{tip|Teamfight Tactics}} game.
