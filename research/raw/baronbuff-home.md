Baron Buff is a League of Legends helper, overlay, companion app, AI coach and LoL tracker that helps you make better decisions in games. When your queue pops, auto-accept can accept the match for you. It guides you through champion select with live draft analysis, runes, summoner spells and builds recommendations then gives matchup-specific tips once the game loads. You learn by playing and not by studying lectures. Track your match history and progress.

Baron Buff is ad-free and uses a lightweight native Windows connector and overlay, without Overwolf or an embedded Electron browser. It’s designed to stay responsive and minimize background resource use, helping protect your game’s FPS.

**Blitz:** Baron Buff combines an ad-free experience with guidance for your actual match. You get a game plan based on both team compositions, including your team’s win condition and what to prioritize in the early, mid and late game.

**Mobalytics:** Baron Buff makes the post-game review easier to act on. It connects feedback to evidence from your match, explains why your decisions mattered and identifies one specific improvement to focus on next game.

**iTero:** Baron Buff keeps the coaching concise: one main win condition and up to three tips per game phase. You can adjust the level of detail and choose what appears in the overlay, making the advice easier to follow while playing.

**Porofessor:** Baron Buff turns information about the teams into practical direction. It explains how your champion and role contribute to winning the match, with priorities for each phase of the game.

**U.GG:** Baron Buff extends build recommendations into a complete coaching process. Alongside runes, spells and items, you get a plan for playing the matchup, guidance during the game and a review of what to improve afterward.
