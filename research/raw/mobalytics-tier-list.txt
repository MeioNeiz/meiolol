Top Tier List

Yone keeps our Top Lane spot, offering plenty of carry potential once his items come together. His W shield helps him come out ahead in trades, while using his Q knock-up to create openings to engage. You don’t need to start every teamfight yourself either; following up on your team’s crowd control makes it much easier to land your ultimate and get onto the enemy carries.

Jungle Tier List

Cho’Gath is our new Jungle Pick of the Patch, bringing plenty of crowd control and a frontline that gets harder to kill as he builds Feast stacks. Keep your farm up and look to follow up on your teammates’ crowd control with Q, then use W to stop enemies casting as your team closes in. Around Dragon and Baron, having Feast alongside Smite gives you a strong way to secure the objective.

Mid Tier List

Viktor takes our Mid Lane spot thanks to his strong scaling and ability to make life difficult for anyone walking into his spells. Look to catch your opponent with E as they step up to farm, then use its upgrade to clear waves quickly. Around objectives, his W can control narrow entrances while his ultimate punishes enemies who stay grouped together. Give yourself room to keep dealing damage safely.

Bot Tier List

Jinx takes our ADC spot thanks to her strong scaling and ability to take over teamfights from a single takedown. Focus on farming well, use rockets to punish enemies standing together, and keep your traps ready for anyone trying to reach you. You don’t need to force your way onto the enemy carries; attack whoever you can safely reach and let your passive give you the speed to chase once the fight turns in your favour.

Support Tier List

Lulu takes our Support spot because she makes it so difficult to finish off the teammate she’s protecting. Her E shield helps your ADC trade, while her W can boost an ally’s attack speed or polymorph an enemy who gets too close. Keep an eye on whoever poses the biggest threat to your carry, and use your ultimate’s bonus health and knock-up to turn their engage against them.
