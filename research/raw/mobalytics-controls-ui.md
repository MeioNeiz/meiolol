## Ahri General information

### Ahri tips and tricks

Constantly look to poke and chip away at the enemies health bar before looking for the all-in.

In team fights, look to peel at first. When an enemy steps too far forward or is caught out of position- look to all-in them.

If you’re not able to kill the enemy in lane, push the wave and roam with your Ultimate. Ahri is really good at roaming, so make sure you’re proactive about it.

## The Best Ahri Builds, Runes and Items for Mid in Emerald +

### Most Popular

If you want to use the most popular Ahri Mid build, you should take Flash and Ignite as your Summoner Spells. For runes, you should pick Electrocute as your keystone, followed by Taste of Blood, Grisly Mementos and Ultimate Hunter in the primary tree, as well as Manaflow Band and Transcendence in the secondary tree. For rune shards, you want Attack Speed in offense, Adaptive Force in flex and Health Scaling in defense, though it depends on the matchup. Finally, for your items, you should build Malignance as your first item, followed by Sorcerer's Shoes and Shadowflame. For your last 3 items, you should build Zhonya's Hourglass, Rabadon's Deathcap and finish with Void Staff. For the skill order, you want to stick with Q - W - E. Right now, this build has 51.6% winrate over 12 529 matches played.

### Alternative

If you're looking for alternatives, you can use Flash and Ignite as your Summoner Spells, with runes being Electrocute as your keystone, Taste of Blood, Grisly Mementos and Ultimate Hunter in the primary tree, and Manaflow Band with Transcendence in the secondary tree, as well as Attack Speed, Adaptive Force, and Health Scaling as your rune shards. The items that this build incorporates are Luden's Echo as your first item, then Sorcerer's Shoes as a second item and Shadowflame as a third item. You want to finish the build with Zhonya's Hourglass, Rabadon's Deathcap and Void Staff. As far as ability max order goes, you want to keep it Q - W - E. This build is currently sitting at a 50.9% win rate across 2 104 matches played.

### Off-Meta

If you want to try an Off-Meta Ahri Mid build in the current patch, try Flash and Ignite as your Summoner Spells, Deathfire Touch, Manaflow Band, Transcendence and Scorch as your primary rune tree, and Taste of Blood with Ultimate Hunter as your secondary tree. Use Attack Speed, Adaptive Force and Health Scaling as your shards. For items in this build, you want to complete Blackfire Torch as your first item, then finish Ionian Boots of Lucidity as your second item, and Lich Bane as third item. The last 3 options are Zhonya's Hourglass, Rabadon's Deathcap and Void Staff. The ability order for this build is Q - W - E. As of the current patch, this build has a 56.0% win rate over 1 158 matches played.

### Situational Ahri Runes and Items

Depending on the context of your game, your matchup, and enemy champions, you might want to consider building some situational items or take specific runes. Carefully examine your team, enemy team, game state , and your matchup to plan your Ahri build accordingly! For example, when it comes to rune shards, you might want to take either Armor or Magic Resist, depending on which champion you are about to lane against.

-   [vs. Naafiri](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/ahri/counters/mid/vs-naafiri) - 54.7% win rate over 190 games.

-   [vs. Katarina](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/ahri/counters/mid/vs-katarina) - 48.9% win rate over 1 530 games.
