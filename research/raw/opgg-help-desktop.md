## OP.GG Ad-free payment and usage guide

<https://help.op.gg/hc/en-us/articles/31091527473561-OP-GG-Ad-free-payment-and-usage-guide> (updated 2025-12-31)

What is OP.GG Ad-free?
OP.GG Ad-free is a subscription service that allows ad-free stats searches and access to additional features. ✨
- Learn more about OP.GG Ad-free
Payment methods
OP.GG Ad-free currently supports credit/debit card payment only and payment can only be completed through the PC web browser. 

- Go to [Account Management] - [Subscription] page.
- Click [Learn more about OP.GG Ad-free] to go to the subscription information page.
- Click [Subscribe] to open the payment screen.
Registering and using a payment method

- Enter the card information for payment, agree to the required terms, and click [Next].
- Enter personal information and complete mobile phone verification.
- After agreeing to the paid membership terms, click [Pay] to complete the OP.GG Ad-free subscription.

Changing payment method
The payment method cannot be changed while the subscription is active.
To change the payment method, cancel the current membership and resubscribe.

💡

The subscription management page will be updated in the future to allow payment method registration, change, and deletion.

Payment receipt
Once payment is completed, a receipt will be sent to the email address linked to the OP.GG account.

Payment failure
If payment fails on the scheduled date due to insufficient balance, exceeding the card limit, etc., the membership will be automatically cancelled. To resume membership, payment method registration and payment must be completed from the beginning.

Cancelling membership

- Go to [Account Management] - [Subscription] page.

- Click [Cancel Membership].

- After checking the expiration date, click [Confirm Cancelation] to complete membership cancellation.

✅

After cancelling the membership, the OP.GG Ad-free subscription remains active until the expiration date. The subscription will end after the expiration date passes.

Refund policy
Refunds for OP.GG Ad-free subscription payments are processed according to the paid membership terms. To request a refund, please contact us through 1:1 inquiry.

Frequently asked questions
Q. I want to change my payment date.
The payment date cannot be changed during an active subscription. If a different payment date is required, please cancel the membership before the next payment date and then resubscribe on the desired date after the expiration date.

Q. How long does a refund take?
Refund processing is completed within 3 business days after your request is received. The length of time required to cancel the card payment may vary depending on the card issuer's policy.

Q. I can’t use Ad-free features after payment is complete.
If the OP.GG Ad-free subscription features are not activated after payment, check the following:

- After logging in to your OP.GG account, go to [Account Management] - [Subscription] tab and check if the membership subscription is active.

- Some Ad-free features require a main account to be set. Please check that the desired Riot ID is set as your main account.

✅ Real-time Data Reflection
OP.GG's real-time recommended champions data is updated every hour, allowing for quick identification of champions with high win rates based on the latest meta. 
✅ Clicking a champion takes you to the Pro Builds page
Clicking on the portrait of a recommended champion will take you to the detailed Pro Builds page for that champion, where you can find practical information such as runes, items, and skill builds.

## About the rival feature

<https://help.op.gg/hc/en-us/articles/48465978187673-About-the-rival-feature> (updated 2025-12-31)

✅ Rank distribution overview

## What features does OP.GG AI Voice offer?

<https://help.op.gg/hc/en-us/articles/58426534694041-What-features-does-OP-GG-AI-Voice-offer> (updated 2026-10-20)

💡

Subscribing to OP.GG AI Voice includes an ad-free experience on OP.GG.

OP.GG AI Voice is a personalized AI gaming partner that uses the voices of favorite creators.
It not only makes gaming more enjoyable but also provides real-time information necessary for gameplay.

1. Easy voice selection
Users can freely choose and apply owned voices from the settings menu before starting a game.
Partner with the voice that matches the mood or play style of the day.

2. Free real-time conversation
During the game, when questions are asked by text or voice, the AI responds in real time.
For example, if unsure how to handle a lane phase, ask and receive specific advice tailored to the situation.

Player: "I'm playing Brand in bottom lane. How should I play it out?"
AI Voice: "Focus on CS first, coordinate skill timings with Annie for poke. Use your long range to harass but avoid wasting mana, and always track the enemy jungler’s position."

3. Real-time play guidance
The AI voice provides guidance aligned with the game's key flow and recommends items based on the situation.
Beyond simple replies, it reads the match context and offers real-time information.

Real-time alerts — Highlights easily missed timings such as objective spawns, enemy respawns, and core item completions.
"Enemy Annie will respawn soon, be ready to stun."
"Dragon will spawn in about 30 seconds. Secure vision and prepare."
"Ezreal just completed Trinity Force. His damage output in fights will increase significantly."

Teamfight analysis — Evaluates teamfight and macro situations to advise on joining fights, positioning, and vision control.
Player: "Our team is gathered on the right side of the map. Should we stay together or split?"
AI Voice: "Herald will spawn in about 30 seconds, so prepare for the fight. For now, staying grouped with your team and securing vision is best."

Build coaching — Suggests item builds and timing adjustments based on game state and enemy composition.
Player: "Which item should I change now?"
AI Voice: "Upgrade the lost essay to complete the Dark Flame Torch and replace the Tracker’s Wrist Guard with Zhonya’s Hourglass. If Darius keeps diving, this will help balance damage and survivability."

4. Ongoing content updates
The guidance system will become more refined over time, and voices of various popular influencers will be added sequentially. Meet an AI partner that grows smarter and more diverse over time.

Step 2. Launch the OP.GG desktop app and activate AI voice
AI voice is available in the OP.GG desktop app. Install and log in to the app, then select and activate the purchased AI voice in the settings menu.

When the game starts, the AI game partner with the selected voice will connect. There are two ways to connect, so choose the preferred method.

Method 1. Automatic connection in voice mode

- Launch Agent by OP.GG

- Switch to voice mode

- The AI Voice will automatically connect when the game starts.

Method 2. Connect via the 'Chat' button

- Start the game.

- Click the [Chat] button at the bottom right of the app.

- The AI Voice will connect automatically.

After connection, enjoy real-time conversation and gameplay support together.

- Ask questions during the game via text or voice.
