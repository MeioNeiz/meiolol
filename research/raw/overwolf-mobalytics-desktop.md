## About the App

### Conquer the meta with your all-in-one Mobalytics companion

Always stay one step ahead with your favorite games including League of Legends, Teamfight Tactics, Diablo 4, Destiny 2 and VALORANT.

**League of Legends**

-   Get the best item + rune builds based on champ data from around the world and import them automatically.
-   Sharpen your gameplan with Pre Game scouting for every player, including playstyle badges and stats.
-   Stay ahead of the competition with an Overlay that provides powerspike notifications, live gold leads, and jungle timers. -Use our new Summoner Spell Timer and Gold to Next Item Tracker to take your in-game experience to the next level.
-   Discover how to improve after your match with a Post Game report that includes Smart Highlights of your key plays.
-   Track your progress throughout the season with your Profile that includes LP gain graphs and matchup data.

**Teamfight Tactics**

-   Activate the in-game Overlay to see every meta comp and guide while playing your match.
-   Use our Lobby Scouter to see who you're up against and what comps they're likely to play.
-   Find out which team comp you should target based on the champions and items you have available.
-   Create your own team comps guides and access them in-game .
-   Remember every item recipe combination with a handy cheat sheet you can easily reference.

**Diablo 4**

-   Explore meta builds for every class by creators like Kripparian, AceOfSpades, and more.
-   Create, save, and share builds from your own personal creator Profile.
-   Easily access any build from in-game so you can focus on playing without distractions.
-   Never miss a timing again with Event Timers for Helltides, World Bosses, and Legions.
