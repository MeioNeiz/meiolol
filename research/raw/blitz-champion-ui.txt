Darius Top Build Guide

Darius Top is A Tier with a 50.7% win rate, 3.9% pick rate, and 7.4% ban rate across 26,974 Emerald+ matches in the World region on Patch 26.21.

The recommended core item build is Plated Steelcaps → Youmuu's Ghostblade → Dead Man's Plate. Conqueror is the recommended primary rune keystone. Vayne is one of the more difficult matchups.

Review Darius counters, synergies, and abilities for more matchup and champion details.
