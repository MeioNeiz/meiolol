Darius Top Build Guide

Darius Top is A Tier with a 50.8% win rate, 3.7% pick rate, and 7.2% ban rate across 12,410 Emerald+ matches in the World region on Patch 26.21.

The recommended core item build is Plated Steelcaps → Youmuu's Ghostblade → Dead Man's Plate. Conqueror is the recommended primary rune keystone. Cho'Gath is one of the more difficult matchups.

Review Darius counters, synergies, and abilities for more matchup and champion details.
