**What Champicker does**
Champicker helps players improve by coaching them while they play, not through static guides. During champion select, it analyzes picks/bans in real time and recommends champions based on counters, synergies, and strategic composition needs. After draft, it provides matchup- and comp-aware guidance (e.g., win conditions, side-specific strategy, jungle pathing, split push decisions, teamfight approach, and how to play the selected champion within the team composition).
