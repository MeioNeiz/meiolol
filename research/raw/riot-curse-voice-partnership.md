# Riot Games announces official partnership with Curse

2 Jul 2015

[Hamish Lindsay](https://stevivor.com/author/hammerguy/)

Riot Games and Curse have today [announced](http://www.curseinc.com/news/64-riot-has-made-an-investment-in-curse) a mutual investment to bring Curse Voice to _League of Legends_.

Curse Voice is a chat communication tool designed specifically for ease of use in online games. It supports a wide variety of games but has run in to trouble for some of its feature with Riot in the past.

It seems Riot and Curse have finally reached an agreement however, and we should expect to see total integration in the near future.
