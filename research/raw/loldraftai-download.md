# Download LoLDraftAI

Download the latest version of LoLDraftAI for Windows to analyze your League of Legends games with live game tracking. Get draft analysis, rune pages, and item builds during champion select, and apply them to your client in one click.

## Before You Install

Since LoLDraftAI is new to the Windows ecosystem, Windows SmartScreen might display a prompt during installation. This is normal because:

-   We're a new application that hasn't built up a reputation with Microsoft yet
-   The app only talks to your local League client - to read your live draft, and to apply recommended runes, item sets, and summoner spells when you ask it to
-   Thousands of players are already using LoLDraftAI successfully

To proceed with installation, click "More info" → "Run anyway" when prompted.
