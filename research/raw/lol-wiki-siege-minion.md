**Siege minions**, also referred to as **cannon minions**, are durable minions that have  [ranged](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ranged) attack range and are specifically equipped to fight, or _[siege](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Terminology#Siege "Terminology")_, enemy  [turrets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret). In a regular wave, one siege minion spawns between the  [melee](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion "Melee minion") and  [caster](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion "Caster minion") minions in line.

## Notes

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Notes") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Notes")\]

-   Minions deal 60% damage against [structures](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Structure "Structure") and  [champions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion).
    -   Siege minions uniquely deal **84%** damage against  [turrets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret) specifically instead.
-   Minions _upgrade_ every 90 seconds. Siege minions may increase their health, attack damage, as well as [gold bounty](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#Bounty "Kill").
-   Siege minions have the cosmetic item  Anti-Tower Socks, which describes the following combat rules:
    -   **Unique – [Reinforced Armor](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Reinforced_Armor "Named item effect"):**  [Turret](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret) basic attacks are modified to deal 14% / 11% / 8% (based on turret type) of this minion's **maximum** health as pre-mitigation damage.
    -   **Unique – [Minion Slayer](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Minion_Slayer "Named item effect"):** Against lane minions, this minion deals **bonus** physical damage on-hit equal to 5% of the target's **current** health.
-   On [Classic](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Classic "Classic")  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift):
    -   Siege minions spawned in the middle [lane](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Lane "Lane") gain 30% **bonus** attack speed while attacking  [turrets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret) specifically.
        -   This bonus is granted upon hitting a turret and removed upon hitting a non-turret unit.

## Map-Specific Differences

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Map-Specific Differences") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Map-Specific Differences")\]

-   In [ARAM](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/ARAM "ARAM"), siege minions:
    -   Spawn every two waves, starting with the third wave.
    -   Have a base health of 828.
    -   Have a health growth of 23 / 32 (based on wave).
    -   Have a gold bounty equal to  45 (+ 0.35 / 90s).

## Trivia

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\]

-   Since siege minion spawning accelerates as the game goes on, [pushing](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Terminology#Push "Terminology") toward the enemy base generally becomes easier as well.
-   While buffed by  [Hand of Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hand_of_Baron), siege minions possess a significantly higher attack range than turrets.
-   Siege minions have gone by many names during development:
    -   In Beta 1, they were called _[Siege rider minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_rider_minion "Siege rider minion")_.
    -   Summoner's Rift Update siege minions are referred to as _cannon minion_ in the game files, as they are equipped with a cannon-like weapon. This name stuck among the community.
-   Since the earliest builds of [Alpha](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha "Alpha"), the red side (Chaos) siege minion had 20 units less  [attack range](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Range "Range") than its blue side (Order) counterpart, which could result in wave imbalances. This was finally amended in patch [V10.16](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V10.16 "V10.16").
-   In Beta, the Blue Siege Minion was a mobile ballista.
    -   An unused Blue Siege Minion model at the time also became the next Blue Siege Minion for their first visual update.

## Patch History

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Patch History") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Patch History")\]

[V26.09](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.09 "V26.09")

-   On-hit minion health ratio reduced to 5% of target's **current** health from 6%.

[V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01 "V26.01")

-   At wave 15+, attack damage increases by 4 instead of 1.5 on each upgrade.
-   Base experience reduced to 75 from 100.
-   Gold reduced to ( 50 + 1 every upgrade) from 60 – 90 (based on upgrades).
-   **Bonus** damage to turrets reduced to 40% from 50%.

[V25.S1.1](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V25.S1.1 "V25.S1.1")

-   Health changed to 835 – 5850 (based on upgrades) from 912 – 10850 (based on upgrades).
-   Attack damage changed to 37.5 – 126 (based on upgrades) from 41.5 – 10040 (based on upgrades).
-   First spawn time increased to 4th wave (02:35) from 3rd wave (02:05).
    -   _Respawn interval unchanged._
-   **New Effect:** Now deals **bonus** damage on-hit equal to 6% of target's **current** health against lane minions.

[V12.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V12.22 "V12.22")

-   **Removed:** Gold from mid lane siege minions are no longer reduced by  10 for the first 14 minutes of the game.

[V12.14](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V12.14 "V12.14")

-   **Undocumented/Bug Fix:**  [Howling Abyss](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Howling_Abyss) red side siege minion attack range increased to 300 from 280.

[V10.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V10.23#November_16th_Hotfix "V10.23") - November 16th Hotfix

-   **Bug Fix:** Fixed a bug where they were doing more damage then intended against all targets instead of just turrets.

[V10.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V10.23 "V10.23")

-   **New Effect:** Gold from mid lane siege minions are reduced by  10 for the first 14 minutes of the game.
-   **New Effect:** Mid lane siege minions gain 30% **bonus** attack speed while attacking turrets.

[V10.16](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V10.16 "V10.16")

-   **Bug Fix:** Red side siege minion attack range increased to 300 from 280.

[V8.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.23 "V8.23")

-   Health changed to 912 – 10850 (based on upgrades) from 900 (+ (50 / 60 (based on wave)) / 90s).
-    [Anti-Tower Socks](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Anti-Tower_Socks)
    -   Incoming turret damage modifier changed to 14% / 11% / 8% (based on turret type) **maximum** health from 14% **maximum** health from all turrets.

[V8.8](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.8 "V8.8")

-   Summoner's Rift:
    -   Capped gold increased to  90 from  87.

[V8.7](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.7 "V8.7")

-   Summoner's Rift:
    -   Base gold reward increased to  60 from  45.
    -   Gold reward growth increased to  3 from  0.35.
    -   **New Effect:** Gold reward is now capped at  87.
    -   Base health increased to 900 from 828.
    -   Health growth changed to 50 from 23.
    -   Health growth at wave 16 increased to 60 from 32.

[V8.2](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.2 "V8.2")

-   **New Effect:** Now have the cosmetic item  [Anti-Tower Socks](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Anti-Tower_Socks).

[V6.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V6.22 "V6.22")

-   Now receives a fixed ~14% max health damage per bullet from Turret bullets.

[V5.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.22 "V5.22")

-   Initial gold increased to 45 from 36.8.
-   Gold growth reduced to 0.35 per minute from 0.425.

[V5.8](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.8 "V5.8")

-   Visual and Gameplay Update on Howling Abyss map (to match Summoner's Rift).
    -   Minions no longer gain magic resistance over time.
    -   Minion health gain over time increased to compensate the loss of resistances.
    -   Siege minions no longer have base armor.

[V4.20](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.20 "V4.20")

-   Minions no longer gain armor or magic resistance over time.
-   Siege minions no longer have base armor
-   All minion health gain over time has been increased to be roughly as durable as when they had resistances

[V4.19](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.19 "V4.19")

-   Model in Summoner's Rift changed.

[V3.14](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.14 "V3.14")

-   Lane Minions are no longer worth more experience based on game time.

[V3.5](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.5 "V3.5")

-   Siege Minions now take 70% damage from Turrets, increased from 50% damage.
-   Siege Minions now deal 50% **bonus** damage to turrets, down from 100%.

[V1.0.0.152](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.152 "V1.0.0.152")

-   Base gold value increased to 40 from 27.
-   Scaling gold value based on time increased to 1 from 0.5.

[March 9th Hotfix](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.112 "V1.0.0.112")

-   Siege minions now reduce turret damage by 50%, down from 65%.

[V1.0.0.112](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.112 "V1.0.0.112")

-   Armor and magic resist now increase by 3 per 3 minutes, up from 2.
-   Siege minions now reduce turret damage by 65%, up from 50%.
-   Fixed a tooltip bug "This unit receive..." with siege minions.

[V1.0.0.100](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.100 "V1.0.0.100")

-   Fixed a bug that caused purple Siege minions to deal and receive normal damage from towers.

[V1.0.0.87](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.87 "V1.0.0.87")

-   Gold from minion kills increased by 3 for all minions.

[V1.0.0.61](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.61 "V1.0.0.61")

-   Gain 2 additional armor every 3 minutes.
-   Gain 2 additional magic resist every 3 minutes.
-   Gold bounty increased to 27 from 25 on Summoner's Rift and to 19 from 17 on Twisted Treeline.

[V0.9.22.15](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V0.9.22.15 "V0.9.22.15")

-   Siege minions now have a new portrait.
-   Siege minion health boost per 3 minutes increased to 27 from 25.

[June 19, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/June_19,_2009_Patch "June 19, 2009 Patch")

-   Health increased to 700 from 600.

[June 12, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/June_12,_2009_Patch "June 12, 2009 Patch")

-   Red and blue siege minions now have Turret Shield (receives 50% reduced damage from turrets).
-   Red and blue siege minions now deal 100% **bonus** damage to turrets.

[May 23, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_23,_2009_Patch "May 23, 2009 Patch")

-   Brightened the new siege minions.
-   Corrected the collision sizes of the new siege minions.

[May 15, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_15,_2009_Patch "May 15, 2009 Patch")

-   Added new rider minion model.

[May 15, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_15,_2009_Patch "May 15, 2009 Patch")

-   Removed aura from rider minions.

[Alpha Week 2](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_2 "Alpha Week 2")

-   Reduced the selection radius of riders (siege minions)
