## About the App

### Capture.

Outplayed is the ultimate video capturing app for gamers. While playing, the app automatically captures your best moments and biggest plays, as well as manually recording on demand.

### Review.

Once the match is over, you can flip through your captured clips in the match timeline. See the best (and not so best) moments, review your APM speed, and relive the game.

### Share.

Time to let the world know. Grab the clips you love most, give it a quick trim if you like, or move to the video editor to combine them into an epic collage. Once you're done, easily share it with friends and foes alike across social networks.

Talk with us on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/umcE2wqHJz).  
Learn more about [How to use Outplayed](https://support.overwolf.com/en/support/solutions/articles/9000208995-how-to-record-on-outplayed).

**Outplayed supports over 5000 games.**
