## About the App

WaveClear is a **real-time mental performance training and VOD debrief copilot** for League of Legends - free, no registration, no hardware.

Mental training is now simple and accessible - set your goal, play live skill challenges, then review and mental debrief in the VOD with Coach Aimes.

-   VOD & Mental Debrief - review gameplay with mental and game-skill insights synced to key moments.
-   Focus Tracker - train live challenges while tracking focus, consistency, and performance.
-   Coach Aimes - experimental AI mental performance coach for reflection, debrief, and training.
-   Personalized Skill Training - train one skill at a time and track your improvement over time.
-   Draft Companion - set your goal and prepare before the match.

Supports Draft, Ranked Solo/Duo, Ranked Flex, and ARAM.

\*\*So stop tracking stats - Start training mental performance like a pro, today. \*\*

_WaveClear is an early-stage product, actively built and improved with players, coaches, and communities. Your play and feedback help shape what comes next._

## About the Creator

### WaveClear LTD

WaveClear Ltd is built by gamers and behavioral researchers combining gaming, wellness, and performance science.  
  
We come from the world of gaming apps, stats, and player tools - and learned that more data is not enough. Most players already know what to do. The hard part is staying focused, composed, and consistent when the game gets messy.  
Our systems are designed to be safe, hardware-free, and publisher-rule friendly: no cheating, no automation, no gameplay control.  
  
Our vision is simple: make mindset and performance training accessible to everyday gamers, reserved until today to pro only.
