# #1068 [BUG] Swiftplay queue matches are no longer returned by the RGAPI
state: closed created: 2025-04-29T18:42:58Z closed: 2025-05-12T20:22:06Z
author: 1337MARCEL

**Bug & Problem Description**  
For the past week or so, it seems like games played on Swiftplay queue are no longer returned by the RGAPI. `/lol/match/v5/matches/by-puuid/{puuid}/ids` nor `/lol/match/v5/matches/{matchId}` work as expected. The only way I can see them is by viewing my own profile in the League client.

e.g. `EUW1_7383662079` is a Swiftplay match that was played yesterday.
