THE BEST DARIUS BUILDS, RUNES AND ITEMS FOR TOP IN EMERALD +
Most Popular

If you want to use the most popular Darius Top build, you should take Flash and Ignite as your Summoner Spells. For runes, you should pick Stormraider's Surge as your keystone, followed by Nimbus Cloak, Celerity and Gathering Storm in the primary tree, as well as Legend: Alacrity and Triumph in the secondary tree. For rune shards, you want Attack Speed in offense, Adaptive Force in flex and Health Scaling in defense, though it depends on the matchup. Finally, for your items, you should build Youmuu's Ghostblade as your first item, followed by Plated Steelcaps and Dead Man's Plate. For your last 3 items, you should build Death's Dance, Sterak's Gage and finish with Randuin's Omen. For the skill order, you want to stick with Q - E - W. Right now, this build has 54.4% winrate over 24 193 matches played.

Optional

Your second option for Darius Top build would be to take Flash and Ghost as your Summoner Spells. The runes that this build takes advantage of are Conqueror as your keystone, Triumph, Legend: Alacrity and Last Stand in the primary tree, and Axiom Arcanist with Celerity in the secondary tree. The shards used in this build are Attack Speed in offense, Adaptive Force in flex and Health Scaling in defense. The items for the build are Youmuu's Ghostblade, which you should build first, followed by Plated Steelcaps as your second item and Dead Man's Plate as your third item. The rest of the items in order are Death's Dance, Sterak's Gage and Randuin's Omen. The skill order for this build is Q - E - W. In the current patch, this build has a 51.6% win rate over 12 503 matches.

Alternative

If you're looking for alternatives, you can use Flash and Ghost as your Summoner Spells, with runes being Conqueror as your keystone, Triumph, Legend: Alacrity and Last Stand in the primary tree, and Axiom Arcanist with Celerity in the secondary tree, as well as Attack Speed, Adaptive Force, and Health Scaling as your rune shards. The items that this build incorporates are Stridebreaker as your first item, then Plated Steelcaps as a second item and Dead Man's Plate as a third item. You want to finish the build with Death's Dance, Force of Nature and Sterak's Gage. As far as ability max order goes, you want to keep it Q - E - W. This build is currently sitting at a 51.3% win rate across 10 235 matches played.

Off-Meta

If you want to try an Off-Meta Darius Top build in the current patch, try Flash and Ghost as your Summoner Spells, Conqueror, Triumph, Legend: Alacrity and Last Stand as your primary rune tree, and Axiom Arcanist with Celerity as your secondary tree. Use Attack Speed, Adaptive Force and Health Scaling as your shards. For items in this build, you want to complete Trinity Force as your first item, then finish Plated Steelcaps as your second item, and Sterak's Gage as third item. The last 3 options are Death's Dance, Force of Nature and Randuin's Omen. The ability order for this build is Q - E - W. As of the current patch, this build has a 53.2% win rate over 3 421 matches played.
