## About the App

## Your in-game co-pilot for League of Legends

Team Advisor handles the busywork so you can focus on winning — live tier lists, smart draft help, and one-click quality-of-life across every mode you play.

### Know the meta, every mode

-   **Tier lists with live stats** for Summoners Rift, ARAM, Arena and TFT — win rates, pick & ban rates, placements and best duos, always up to date.
-   **TFT comps** ready to copy, with items, levels and positioning.

### Win champ select before it starts

-   **Draft suggestions** built for your exact lobby, weighing both your team and the enemy.
-   **AI predictions** that read the draft and flag your strongest picks.
-   **Auto-hover** your ban and your favourite pick the moment select opens.
-   **Runes, items, spells and skill order**, sorted for your champion.

### Less hassle, more games

-   **One-click accept or decline** in queue.
-   **Remote Control** — run champ select from your phone by scanning a QR code.
-   …plus a bunch of small touches that make every lobby smoother.

Free to use — just download, launch League, and climb.
