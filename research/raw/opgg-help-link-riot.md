# How to link a Riot account

Bushy

1 year ago Updated

1.  Log in to your OP.GG account and go to the account management page.
    -   [Account management page](https://member.op.gg/)
2.  In the \[Connected Apps\] tab, click the Riot Games connect button.
3.  You will be redirected to the Riot Games login page. Sign in with the account you want to link, and the link will be completed.

💡

**If a Riot account is already linked to an OP.GG account and it is linked again to a new OP.GG account, the link with the previous OP.GG account will be automatically disconnected.**
