# champion tier calculation explained

Bushy

9 months ago Updated

**Q. How are champion tiers calculated?**

Champion tiers are calculated using data extracted from ranked games of Platinum tier and above, through our proprietary algorithm. The tier calculation includes various metrics such as pick and ban rates, win rates, gold, experience, crowd control, KDA, damage taken, and damage dealt, among others.

Champion tiers can be filtered by server, tier, and patch version to view the desired statistical data.

  
 

**Q. What does OP Tier mean?**

OP Tier is awarded to champions with the greatest power (Over Power). It refers to champions that have recorded the most superior data in the comprehensive champion tier calculation.
