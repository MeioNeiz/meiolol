## FAQs

-   ### What is the strategy for Razer AVA?
    
    Razer AVA has evolved from Project AVA, the 2025 esports AI coach concept, into a fully integrated AI companion. Designed to support every facet of modern life, Razer AVA lives both as a desktop hologram and an on-screen AI companion, bridging the gap between virtual assistance and digital companionship. With a 24/7 AI presence, AVA enhances productivity, personal organization, and gaming performance while adapting to your unique interactions.
    
-   ### What technology powers Razer AVA’s voice and personality?
    
    Razer AVA currently utilizes state-of-the-art LLMs to demonstrate its sophisticated reasoning and dynamic personality. AVA is built with an open, future-ready architecture intended to support compatibility with other leading AI platforms—including a dedicated Razer AI—to give users intelligence flexibility upon commercialization.
    
-   ### What are the connectivity and system requirements?
    
    Razer AVA is designed specifically for Windows users and requires a direct connection to a Windows PC via a USB Type C cable. This wired connection ensures the high-bandwidth data transfer required for "PC Vision Mode" to analyze screen content with minimal latency between the local hardware and the PC operating system.
    
-   ### What can Razer AVA do?
    
    Razer AVA is an AI companion designed to support you across life, work, and play. From planning your day and talking through everyday decisions to brainstorming ideas, translating conversations, and helping you explore games and other interests, AVA is there whenever you need a second opinion, a helping hand, or simply someone to talk to.
    
-   ### When will Razer AVA be available?
    
    Razer AVA is expected to launch under Early Access in early 2027. In the meantime, users can sign up for the Razer AVA Beta for early access. Beta participation is limited, and only selected applicants will be invited to join. For more information, [click here](https://www.razer.com/razer-ava/beta-program).
    
-   ### What happens to my $20 deposit?
    
    If you’ve reserved Razer AVA with a $20 deposit, you’re all set. Your reservation remains secured, and we’ll share updates as we get closer to launch. The deposit will be credited toward your final purchase and remains fully refundable.
    
-   ### Is my data secure with Razer AVA?
    
    Absolutely. All interactions with Razer AVA are protected by your Razer ID, and sensitive information is stored securely. Beta data is wiped at the end of the program.
    
-   ### What platforms and devices support Razer AVA?
    
    Razer AVA will first be available on PC and the desktop hologram. Additional platforms and cross-device experiences will be available in the future.
