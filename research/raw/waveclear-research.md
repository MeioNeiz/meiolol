# Game Skills Improvement Results

In which skills we perform worse?

Team Play is Difficult

WaveClear detected Assists (“Playmaker”) as the most common worst skill to improve, while surviving was the least common.

Assisting required team play capabilities and communications, which are harder to pull off when playing solo/duo.

How fast can one improve?

1-3 Matches to Improve

Gamers show they can die half the times few matches only. Without new knowledge, role, or a champ, this proves FOCUS was the key to improve, which we all already possess.
