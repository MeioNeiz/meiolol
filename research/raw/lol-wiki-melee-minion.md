**Melee minions** are moderately durable minions that have  [melee](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee) attack range. Three melee minions spawn in each minion wave.

## Notes

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Notes") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Notes")\]

-   Minions deal 60% damage against [structures](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Structure "Structure") and  [champions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion).
-   Minions _upgrade_ every 90 seconds. Melee minions may increase their health, attack damage, and armor, up to a cap.
-   Melee minions have the cosmetic item  Gusto, which describes the following combat rules:
    -   **Unique – [Gusto](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Gusto "Named item effect"):**  [Turret](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret) basic attacks are modified to deal 45% of this minion's **maximum** health as pre-mitigation damage.
    -   **Unique – [Minion Wounder](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Named_item_effect#Minion_Wounder "Named item effect"):** Against lane minions, this minion deals **bonus** physical damage on-hit equal to 2% of the target's **current** health.

## Trivia

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\]

-   In [League of Legends: Turret Defence](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_of_Legends:_Turret_Defence "League of Legends: Turret Defence"), their old  Purple Team variants featured as wave enemies.

## Patch History

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Patch History") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Patch History")\]

[V26.09](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.09 "V26.09")

-   Health changed to 465 – 1550 (based on upgrades) from 465 – 1500 (based on upgrades).

[V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01 "V26.01")

-   Base experience reduced to 62 from 65.
-   Gold reduced to  20 from  21.

[V25.S1.1](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V25.S1.1 "V25.S1.1")

-   Health changed to 465 – 1500 (based on upgrades) from 477 – 1300 (based on upgrades).
-   Attack damage changed to 11 – 80 (based on upgrades) from 12 – 80 (based on upgrades).
-   Armor changed to 0 – 20 (based on upgrades) from 0 – 16 (based on upgrades).
-   **New Effect:** Now deals **bonus** damage on-hit equal to 2% of target's **current** health against lane minions.

[V9.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V9.23 "V9.23")

-   Base XP increased to 65 from 64.

[V8.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.23 "V8.23")

-   Health changed to 477 – 1300 (based on upgrades) from 473 – 1200 (based on minutes).
    -   Maximum health (at 37:00) increased to 1300 from 1200.
-   Armor increased to 0 – 16 (based on upgrades) from 0 at all times.
    -   Maximum armor (at 37:00) increased to 16 from 0.
-   Attack damage increased to 12 – 80 (based on upgrades) from 12 at all times.
    -   Maximum attack damage (at 37:00) increased to 80 from 12.
-    [Hand of Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hand_of_Baron)
    -   Incoming champion damage reduction reduced to 70% from 75%.
    -   **Removed:** 30% reduced damage from turrets.

[V8.7](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.7 "V8.7")

-   Summoner's Rift:
    -   Base gold reward increased to  21 from  20.
    -   Gold reward growth reduced to  0 from  0.125.

[V8.5](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.5 "V8.5")

-   Turret damage per shot increased to 45% of **maximum** health from 43%.

[V8.2](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.2 "V8.2")

-   **New Effect:** Now have the cosmetic item  [Gusto](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gusto).

[V6.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V6.22 "V6.22")

-   Health growth over time increased by ~60%.
-   Melee minions no longer gain attack damage as the game progresses.
-   Now receives a fixed ~45% max health damage per bullet from Turret bullets.

[V5.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.22 "V5.22")

-   Initial gold increased to 20 from 18.5.
-   Gold growth reduced to 0.125 per minute from 0.17.

[V5.8](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.8 "V5.8")

-   Visual and Gameplay Update on Howling Abyss map (to match Summoner's Rift)
    -   Minions no longer gain magic resistance over time.
    -   Minion health gain over time increased to compensate the loss of resistances.

[V4.21](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.21 "V4.21")

-   Melee minion gold reward 19 ⇒ 20.

[V4.20](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.20 "V4.20")

-   Minions no longer gain armor or magic resistance over time.
-   All minion health gain over time has been increased to be roughly as durable as when they had resistances.

[V4.19](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.19 "V4.19")

-   Visual Update on Summoner's Rift map.

[V3.14](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.14 "V3.14")

-   Lane minions are no longer worth more experience based on game time.

[V1.0.0.152](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.152 "V1.0.0.152")

-   Base gold value reduced to 20 from 22.

[V1.0.0.112](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.112 "V1.0.0.112")

-   Melee minion magic resist now increases by 1.25 per 3 minutes, up from 0.5.

[V1.0.0.87](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.87 "V1.0.0.87")

-   Gold from minion kills increased by 3 for all minions.

[V1.0.0.61](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.61 "V1.0.0.61")

-   Gain 2 additional armor every 3 minutes.
-   Gain 0.5 additional magic resist every 3 minutes.
-   Gold bounty increased to 22 from 20 on Summoner's Rift and to 26 from 24 on Twisted Treeline.

[V1.0.0.52](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.52 "V1.0.0.52")

-   Twisted Treeline:
    -   Melee minion gold per kill increased to 24 from 22.

[May 15, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_15,_2009_Patch "May 15, 2009 Patch")

-   Basic minion gold yield growth reduced by 50%.

[May 1, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_1,_2009_Patch "May 1, 2009 Patch")

-   New melee minions for both blue and red teams.

[Alpha Week 6](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_6 "Alpha Week 6")

-   Melee minion health increased to 445 from 425.

[Alpha Week 4](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_4 "Alpha Week 4")

-   Melee minion gold bounty increased to 26 from 22.
