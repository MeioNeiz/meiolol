## Product Description

DraftEdge is an analytics app built for League of Legends teams:

- **Game Analytics** — import and parse .ROFL replay files to extract
  player and team performance metrics
- **Draft Strategy Tool** — plan pick/ban scenarios and draft patterns
- **Scrim Hub** — organize practice sessions, track custom coaching
  objectives, and rate team performance across scrims

Target audience: coaches and analysts in the amateur competitive ecosystem
(French leagues, German Prime League) looking for a centralized platform to
track performance, prepare drafts, and improve through data-driven insights
— which we believe directly benefits the Riot Games ecosystem.

A test account is available in the application notes on the Developer Portal
for the review team.
