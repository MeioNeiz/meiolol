## How to get the 2025 LoL recap

First of all, you will need to [go to the YearIn.LoL site through a browser](https://yearin.lol/). You’ll then be redirected to a page that shows you every stat you want to know for your 2025. You will need to write your in-game name and tag, so the site can access your in-game statistics. If you have multiple accounts, click the appropriate button under the Summoner Name field to get an accurate representation of your year of League of Legends.

After these, all you need to do is have fun looking at how many days you have spent in a gray screen!

1.  Search for YearInLoL
2.  Write your name and tag
3.  Have fun discovering your stats!

## All the statistics

Here is a complete list of all the statistics you can expect in your LoL 2025 recap.

-   Games played (Win rate)
-   KDA
-   Double/triples/quadra/pentakills
-   Minutes played
-   Grayscreen time
-   Champions played
-   Damage breakdown
-   Buttons pressed
-   Gold earned
-   Ping counter
-   Farming data
-   Objectives
-   Winrate per side
-   ARAM data (and other game modes)
-   Vision score
-   Game modes played
-   Friends you played the most games with
