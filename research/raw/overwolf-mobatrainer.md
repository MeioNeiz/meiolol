## About the App

Crafted by top League of Legends pro players and coaches. It’s free, effective and makes expert training accessible to all.

### Master Strategic Concepts and Decision Making

Complete daily interactive puzzles to practice strategic concepts and enhance quality and speed of your decision making in League of Legends.

### Master Mechanical Skills

Warm up and improve your mechanics with our fun mechanical training games.

### Personalised Learning

Puzzles are recommended for your role and rank, allowing you to learn at your own pace—whether you're a new player or a seasoned Challenger.

### Stay Motivated

Keep your day streak going by solving puzzles to maximise your learning and get rewards.
