## TFT Standalone Client

> Hooray! TFT is all grown up! They’re officially moving to their own standalone client starting on November 4th, 2026. To accommodate their new home, you can expect this patch to be larger than usual. We are making sure that it’s as easy as clicking a button to swap between League of Legends and Teamfight Tactics whenever you want.   
>   
> Despite this upcoming separation, all existing Teamfight Tactics cosmetics will remain usable in League. The first batch of TFT-only Little Legends will arrive on December 1st, with December 15th marking the date of full separation for TFT cosmetics including emotes and icons.   
>   
> In a future patch, we'll be adding the option to fully opt out of TFT downloads in League patches. To make this possible, 26.22 will be a larger download than usual. For more information, [read more here](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/new-client-set-19-tease-and-compensation-rewards/) and we’ll see (some of) you on the Convergence!

## ARAM: Mayhem

> Hello Superstars! This patch, we’re lowering the power of some new augments on the scene, buffing some survivability augments, and fixing a bunch of bugs. We’ve been monitoring the explosiveness of combat in the new update and want to alleviate some of the more frustrating situations.   
>   
> Fighters have become exceptionally good at closing out games and poke dominates early in the most recent update while tanks have been struggling. We’re aiming to bring down the power of some of the augments that have been making tanks feel so fragile like Take ‘Em Down Down Down and Weapon of Destiny. We’re also bringing up the power of survivability augments like Soul Eater and Stay Resolute. We're looking at overall burst and game pacing right now to make sure things don’t feel too snowbally.   
>   
> The stage crews have also finished setting up on Butcher’s Bridge and Koeshin’s Crossing so both of those maps will be back in the rotation with Headliner performances.   
>   
> With Worlds in full swing, the last page of the Journey to Stardom is now accessible. Complete all the missions and make your way to the Worlds stage to earn all the rewards! Two new augments are also joining the fray including one based on a classic World’s Anthem that’ll definitely make your enemies remember you.   
>   
> As always, thank you for playing and for all your feedback!

## Classic

> Our penultimate content patch arrives with three new old champions to play. Up first, winner of Council Vote #2, Volibear! Run down your enemies, fear nearby minions, and take a bite out of crime. Swain also makes his re-entrance, full of bird-fueled mayhem. And finally, Elise, while not particularly different, has a lot of single-target damage with which to lay waste to her opposition.   
>   
> We’ve also got a bigger balance patch than usual, taking aim at some of the game’s largest outliers on both the top and bottom end. Yes, Janna, your time has come.

* * *

#### Champions

  **NEW**   **- Volibear**

> This version of Volibear was active from 2011 to 2018, before his E got the knock-back and anti-dash synergy. He’s a tanky melee fighter who excels in sustained combat before finally dealing a significant burst of damage to low-health enemies.

-   **Passive - Chosen of the Storm**: When reduced to low health, Volibear heals for a portion of his maximum health over time. This effect has a long cooldown.
-   **Q - Rolling Thunder**: Volibear gains bonus movement speed, increased while running toward enemy champions. His next attack deals bonus damage and flips his target over his head.
-   **W - Frenzy**: Volibear passively gains stacking bonus attack speed on-attack. While at max stacks, Frenzy can be activated to deal high single-target damage, increased based on their missing health.
-   **E - Majestic Roar**: Volibear damages and slows nearby enemies. Minions and monsters are additionally feared.
-   **R - Thunder Claws**: Volibear harnesses the power of the storm for several seconds, causing all basic attacks to inflict chain lightning.

* * *

  **NEW**   **- Swain**

> This version of Swain was active from 2010-2016, where he received a minor rework. If you never experienced the glory of the Laser Bird, now is your time!

-   **Passive - Carrion Renewal**: Swain recoups some mana every time he kills an enemy.
-   **Q - Decrepify**: Swain sics his raven on a target enemy, slowing and dealing damage over time while they remain in range.
-   **W - Nevermove**: After a brief delay, Swain damages and roots all enemies in a small area.
-   **E - Torment**: Swain afflicts an enemy with Torment, dealing damage over time and causing them to take increased damage from him. Unlike most other damage amps, this also impacts true damage, like Ignite.
-   **R - Ravenous Flock**: Swain transforms into a giant raven, consuming increasing amounts of mana while transformed. In this state, he releases lesser ravens to damage enemies and return to heal Swain based on the damage they dealt.

* * *

  **NEW**   **- Elise**

> Elise hasn’t changed too much over the years, but this older version put a lot more power into her Q spells. She is seriously bursty and an incredibly potent top laner with all her health-based damage. Make sure to stack some magic pen!

-   **Passive - Spider Swarm**: While in human form, abilities that hit enemies store a Spiderling, up to a cap. In spider form, Elise releases these Spiderlings to attack nearby enemies.
-   **Q - Neurotoxin / Venomous Bite**: In human form, Elise spits neurotoxin that deals bonus damage based on her target’s current health. In spider form, Elise and her Spiderlings lunge at an enemy and deal bonus damage based on their target’s missing health.
-   **W - Volatile Spiderling / Skittering Frenzy**: In human form, Elise releases a volatile spiderling that explodes on the first enemy it hits, dealing damage in an area. In spider form, Elise and her Spiderlings gain bonus attack speed and heal Elise on-hit.
-   **E - Cocoon / Rappel**: In human form, Elise fires a cocoon that stuns the first enemy it hits. In spider form, Elise and her Spiderlings rappel into the air, becoming untargetable. She can then re-cast Rappel to descend upon her choice of target.
-   **R - Human Form / Spider Form**: Elise transforms back and forth between human form and spider form. In human form she’s ranged. In spider form, she is melee and deals bonus damage on-hit.

> Mordekaiser had a few bugs when he came out last patch, and while we fixed many of them in a micropatch, some of them took a while. Here’s a full recounting of bugfixes from our micropatches and the ones coming out today.

#### R - Children of the Grave

In the interest of experimentation, we’re playing up her ability to deal a lot of damage if she can land everything and optimize for it. And hey, how about Mid Nami?

#### Q - Aqua Prison
