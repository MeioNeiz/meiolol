## Darius General information

### Darius tips and tricks

Make the most of your champions kit by auto-attacking before you activate your W as your W is an auto-attack reset.

Darius biggest weakness is that he can get kited easily. If you have to fight in a team fight, look to attack from the side to reduce the chances of being poked down or kited.

Try to go for longer trades over short ones as Darius benefits more from extended trades thanks to his Hemorrhage Passive.

## The Best Darius Builds, Runes and Items for Top in Emerald +

### Most Popular

If you want to use the most popular Darius Top build, you should take Flash and Ignite as your Summoner Spells. For runes, you should pick Stormraider's Surge as your keystone, followed by Nimbus Cloak, Celerity and Gathering Storm in the primary tree, as well as Legend: Alacrity and Triumph in the secondary tree. For rune shards, you want Attack Speed in offense, Adaptive Force in flex and Health Scaling in defense, though it depends on the matchup. Finally, for your items, you should build Youmuu's Ghostblade as your first item, followed by Plated Steelcaps and Dead Man's Plate. For your last 3 items, you should build Death's Dance, Sterak's Gage and finish with Randuin's Omen. For the skill order, you want to stick with Q - E - W. Right now, this build has 54.8% winrate over 5 760 matches played.

### Alternative

If you're looking for alternatives, you can use Flash and Ghost as your Summoner Spells, with runes being Conqueror as your keystone, Triumph, Legend: Alacrity and Last Stand in the primary tree, and Axiom Arcanist with Celerity in the secondary tree, as well as Attack Speed, Adaptive Force, and Health Scaling as your rune shards. The items that this build incorporates are Stridebreaker as your first item, then Plated Steelcaps as a second item and Sterak's Gage as a third item. You want to finish the build with Dead Man's Plate, Force of Nature and Death's Dance. As far as ability max order goes, you want to keep it Q - E - W. This build is currently sitting at a 54.3% win rate across 2 313 matches played.

### Off-Meta

If you want to try an Off-Meta Darius Top build in the current patch, try Flash and Ghost as your Summoner Spells, Conqueror, Triumph, Legend: Alacrity and Last Stand as your primary rune tree, and Axiom Arcanist with Celerity as your secondary tree. Use Attack Speed, Adaptive Force and Health Scaling as your shards. For items in this build, you want to complete Trinity Force as your first item, then finish Plated Steelcaps as your second item, and Sterak's Gage as third item. The last 3 options are Death's Dance, Force of Nature and Dead Man's Plate. The ability order for this build is Q - E - W. As of the current patch, this build has a 56.0% win rate over 752 matches played.
