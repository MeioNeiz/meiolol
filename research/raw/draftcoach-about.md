# About Us

## 1\. Who We Are

We’ve been playing League of Legends for more than a decade—ever since we were teenagers. This game has been a huge part of our lives. Through it, we connected with people across countries and continents, built friendships, shared late-night laughs, survived tilt, and celebrated hard-earned victories.

League was always there for us—whether we wanted to blow off steam after a rough day, queue up with friends for some chaotic ARAM fun, or take ranked seriously and grind the ladder alone.

Beyond playing, we are passionate fans of the esports scene. From MSI to Worlds and regional championships, we've spent countless hours rooting for our favorite teams and marveling at the strategic depth of professional play.

## 2\. Why We Created Draft Coach

Despite our love for the game, one part was always unnecessarily stressful: **Drafting.**

With more than 170 champions and countless possible team compositions, we often felt lost. We looked at the pro stage and saw that every professional team has a coach helping them navigate drafts, analyze matchups, and choose champions that give the best chance of winning.

We asked ourselves: why shouldn't everyday players like us have that same advantage? We wanted to solve the confusion of:

-   What should I pick here?
-   What works well with my team’s comp?
-   What counters the enemy’s draft?
-   What beats my lane opponent?

## 3\. What It Does

Draft Coach is your personal in-game assistant during champion select. As soon as the draft starts, the app analyzes everything happening in real time: your allies’ picks, the enemy team’s champions, bans, matchup win rates, and team composition synergies.

"It feels like having your own esports coach sitting next to you. It cuts through the noise and recommends the optimal champion for YOU—based on statistics, performance data, and meta-driven insights."

## 4\. What Makes It Unique

While other tools bombard you with endless charts and raw data, Draft Coach focuses on one thing above all else: **Helping you pick the right champion.**

We filter out the distractions to give you exactly what you need: the best possible pick based on your role, your specific lobby, the draft state, and global win-rate matchups. No guessing, no stress—just the strongest possible pick for the current situation.

## 5\. Our Mission

We built Draft Coach because we love this game—and we want to make the experience better for every player. Our goals are simple: help you save time, reduce stress, and guide you toward smarter, data-driven decisions.

We’ve been in your shoes. We know how overwhelming drafts can be, and we know how much better the game feels when you go into lane with confidence. Draft Coach is our way of improving the League experience for players who want to make better choices, win more games, and enjoy the process.

## 6\. Join Us

We’re excited to share this project with the community that shaped us. Download the app, try it out, and let Draft Coach help you draft smarter—so you can focus on what really matters:

**Playing the game you love.**

The Draft Coach Team
