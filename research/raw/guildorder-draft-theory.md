# League of Legends Draft & Champion Select Theory

Champion select wins or loses games before the first minion spawns - counter-pick chains, ban priorities, team composition theory, and how pro teams actually structure their draft.

-   calendar\_todayApril 24, 2026
-   history\_edu[Mia Choudhury](https://guildorder.com/u/mia-choudhury)
-   5 min read

[Team Play & Coordination](https://guildorder.com/guides/coordination)

League of Legends rounds are decided 6 minutes before the Nexus falls - they're decided in champion select. Clash tournaments, scrims, and ranked solo queue all use the same draft mechanic, and the teams that understand draft theory systematically win more games than teams with better mechanical players who didn't. This guide is about how to draft deliberately, not reactively.

lightbulb

Who this is for

Clash team captains, ranked 5-stacks, aspiring amateur competitive players. Applies to draft mode (Clash, ranked flex, scrims). Blind pick / ARAM are different contexts. Assumes [role and lane theory](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/role-and-lane-theory) is clear.

## [How draft works](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#how-draft-works)

Standard draft proceeds in phases:

### [Ban phase 1](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#ban-phase-1)

-   3 bans per team, alternating (blue first, red first, blue second, red second, blue third, red third)
-   6 champions removed from the pool

### [Pick phase 1](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#pick-phase-1)

-   Blue picks 1st
-   Red picks 2-3 (two picks)
-   Blue picks 4-5 (two picks)
-   Red picks 6th

### [Ban phase 2](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#ban-phase-2)

-   2 bans per team, alternating. Note the swap: **Red bans first** in phase 2 (Red ban 4 → Blue ban 4 → Red ban 5 → Blue ban 5), the inverse of phase 1.

### [Pick phase 2](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#pick-phase-2)

-   Red picks 7th
-   Blue picks 8-9 (two picks)
-   Red picks 10th

At the end: 5 champions per team, 10 bans, full draft visible.

## [The positions you draft around](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#the-positions-you-draft-around)

Blue side and red side have different dynamics:

### [Blue side advantages](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#blue-side-advantages)

-   First pick: grab a priority champion before the opponent can
-   Smaller ban-phase ordering advantage
-   Traditionally slight win-rate advantage in pro play

### [Red side advantages](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#red-side-advantages)

-   **Last pick**: see all enemy picks before committing to a counter
-   Counter-pick priority
-   The "adapt to the opponent" side

Most amateur and mid-tier competitive players overvalue first pick. At pro level, red side's last-pick counter often wins.

## [Ban priorities](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#ban-priorities)

A team's bans shouldn't just hide enemy picks - they should:

-   **Remove counter-picks your carries hate**: if your top is playing Riven, ban Fiora
-   **Remove wide-impact champions**: ones that affect multiple roles (Karma, Senna, Bel'Veth)
-   **Remove meta outliers**: champions with disproportionate win rates in the current patch
-   **Remove pocket picks**: if the enemy has a specific OTP signal, ban their pocket

### [Ban order matters](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#ban-order-matters)

-   **First ban**: usually a high-impact / widely-played champion (K'Sante, Zeri, etc.)
-   **Second ban**: counter-meta pick or enemy strong signal
-   **Third ban**: pocket-pick denial or specific champion matchups

## [Pick priorities](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#pick-priorities)

### [The "blind pick"](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#the-blind-pick)

Blue side's first pick is blind - you can't see the enemy's response yet. What to first-pick:

-   **Flex picks** that can play multiple roles (Karma, Galio, Swain, Senna, Ryze)
-   **Uncontested champions** with no direct counter
-   **Your team's synergy anchor**: if your whole comp revolves around Azir, you first-pick Azir

### [Counter-picks](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#counter-picks)

The last pick (red side 10th pick) is for the counter. Common counter patterns:

-   **Anti-hook laner**: picks Yasuo or Viego against a hook support to punish missed hooks
-   **Anti-engage**: picks Vladimir to punish engage champions
-   **Anti-assassin**: picks Kassadin or Cassiopeia to punish roaming assassins
-   **Matchup-specific**: if enemy top locked Fiora, counter with Jax or Renekton

### [Flex picks](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#flex-picks)

Flex picks are champions that can play multiple roles. Using them in draft:

-   **Galio**: mid or support
-   **Gragas**: jungle, top, or support
-   **Pantheon**: top, mid, or support
-   **Sett**: top, support, or jungle
-   **Senna**: ADC or support

By flex-picking, you force the enemy to commit their counter before you reveal the role. Example: pick Pantheon at slot 4; enemy locks K'Sante top to counter-top, then you reveal Pantheon as support.

## [Team composition archetypes](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#team-composition-archetypes)

A team composition is the strategy the team executes. Choose one before drafting, then pick champions that fit.

### [Engage comp](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#engage-comp)

-   **Strategy**: team fights centered on engage champion initiating
-   **Needs**: strong engage (Leona, Amumu, Malphite), front-line, AoE follow-up
-   **Counters**: teams that poke you down before the engage lands

### [Poke comp](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#poke-comp)

-   **Strategy**: deal sustained long-range damage before the fight starts
-   **Needs**: multiple ranged/poke champions (Jayce, Xerath, Zoe), mobility to disengage
-   **Counters**: engage comps that close the gap; assassin comps

### [Scaling comp](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#scaling-comp)

-   **Strategy**: survive early game, win mid-late
-   **Needs**: scaling carries (Kassadin, Kayle, Vayne), team that can peel
-   **Counters**: early-game snowball comps that win before your team scales

### [Protect the carry](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#protect-the-carry)

-   **Strategy**: one hyper-carry wins all the fights if the team keeps them alive
-   **Needs**: tanks, peel, enchanter support (Janna, Lulu, Yuumi)
-   **Carries**: Vayne, Kog'Maw, Twitch, hyper-scaling ADCs
-   **Counters**: assassin comp that bypasses peel; split-push pressure

### [Split push](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#split-push)

-   **Strategy**: one top laner creates cross-map pressure while 4 defend
-   **Needs**: strong 1v1 top (Fiora, Camille, Tryndamere), 4v4 winning team
-   **Counters**: teleport + dive comps that punish the split

## [Draft flow (simplified example)](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#draft-flow-simplified-example)

### [Blue side draft (typical path)](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#blue-side-draft-typical-path)

1.  **Ban**: Meta carry enemy wants (Aatrox, K'Sante)
2.  **First pick**: Flex engage (Karma / Maokai / Senna)
3.  **Bans phase 2**: Pocket picks + counter to your first pick
4.  **Pick 2-3**: Jungler + one more flex
5.  **Bans phase 3**: Mid + support counters
6.  **Pick 4-5**: Your carries (mid, adc)
7.  **Last pick**: Support, chosen based on enemy top/mid reveal

### [Red side draft](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#red-side-draft)

1.  **Ban**: High-priority enemy carry (Zeri, Viego)
2.  **Pick 2-3**: Strong flex picks
3.  **Ban phase 2**: Protect your comp from counters
4.  **Pick 4-5**: Roles still flexible
5.  **Last pick**: Counter to enemy's final reveal

## [Common draft mistakes](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#common-draft-mistakes)

-   **No plan**: picking champions ad-hoc results in no team strategy
-   **Forced team comp**: picking assassins with enchanter support makes no sense
-   **Overvaluing one lane**: enabling one lane at the cost of all others
-   **No flex picks**: giving away your role assignments at pick 2-3
-   **Ignoring patch meta**: picking champions the patch just nerfed
-   **Over-focus on bans**: 10 bans won't save you if your comp doesn't work

## [Common draft patterns in pro play](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#common-draft-patterns-in-pro-play)

### [KR-style draft](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#kr-style-draft)

-   Heavy scaling, protect-the-carry mid
-   Multiple engage options
-   Tank + enchanter support combos

### [EU-style draft](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#eu-style-draft)

-   Flexibility, drafting with counter-picks in mind
-   Split-push potential preserved
-   Jungler + mid synergy prioritized

### [NA-style draft (post-2023)](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#na-style-draft-post-2023)

-   Mechanical outplay-focused
-   ADC-heavy damage
-   Less creative flex picks than EU

## [What to do this week](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory#what-to-do-this-week)

If you're a Clash captain:

1.  ### Write your team's default team comp archetype
    
    One of: Engage, Poke, Scaling, Protect the Carry, Split Push. Pin in Discord.
    
2.  ### Build a 15-champion pool per role
    
    Enough to survive bans and flex into multiple matchups. Know each champion's matchup table.
    
3.  ### Learn 3 flex picks the team runs
    
    Karma, Galio, and one other. Your team knows exactly when to flex.
    
4.  ### Practice draft simulation
    
    Online tools like drafts.lol let you simulate. Run 3-5 drafts before Clash.
    
5.  ### Review losing drafts
    
    Any loss that happened in draft, review. Would a different pick have worked?
    

Draft in League is the single most under-practiced skill for amateur teams. Clash captains who spend 20 minutes on draft prep before a Clash weekend win 60% of their matchups on draft alone. It's not the mechanical gap; it's the strategic gap. Close it. The wins follow.
