## Motifs

[

### Advanced pawn_393,556_

One of your pawns is deep into the opponent position, maybe threatening to promote.](https://lichess.org/training/advancedPawn)[

### Attacking f2 or f7_46,933_

An attack focusing on the f2 or f7 pawn, such as in the fried liver opening.](https://lichess.org/training/attackingF2F7)[

### Capture the defender_43,660_

Removing a piece that is critical to defence of another piece, allowing the now undefended piece to be captured on a following move.](https://lichess.org/training/capturingDefender)[

### Discovered attack_332,877_

Moving a piece (such as a knight), that previously blocked an attack by a long range piece (such as a rook), out of the way of that piece.](https://lichess.org/training/discoveredAttack)[

### Double check_33,679_

Checking with two pieces at once, as a result of a discovered attack where both the moving piece and the unveiled piece attack the opponent's king.](https://lichess.org/training/doubleCheck)[

### Exposed king_192,000_

A tactic involving a king with few defenders around it, often leading to checkmate.](https://lichess.org/training/exposedKing)[

### Fork_845,637_

A move where a piece attacks two or more opposing pieces simultaneously.](https://lichess.org/training/fork)[

### Hanging piece_255,344_

A tactic involving an opponent piece being undefended or insufficiently defended and free to capture.](https://lichess.org/training/hangingPiece)[

### Kingside attack_556,312_

An attack of the opponent's king, after they castled on the king side.](https://lichess.org/training/kingsideAttack)[

### Pin_390,498_

A tactic involving pins, where a piece is unable to move without revealing an attack on a higher value piece.](https://lichess.org/training/pin)[

### Queenside attack_96,208_

An attack of the opponent's king, after they castled on the queen side.](https://lichess.org/training/queensideAttack)[

### Sacrifice_476,139_

A tactic involving giving up material in the short-term, to gain an advantage again after a forced sequence of moves.](https://lichess.org/training/sacrifice)[

### Skewer_144,775_

A motif involving a high value piece being attacked, moving out the way, and allowing a lower value piece behind it to be captured or attacked, the inverse of a pin.](https://lichess.org/training/skewer)[

### Trapped piece_74,442_

A piece is unable to escape capture as it has limited moves.](https://lichess.org/training/trappedPiece)

## Advanced

[

### Attraction_230,353_

An exchange or sacrifice encouraging or forcing an opponent piece to a square that allows a follow-up tactic.](https://lichess.org/training/attraction)[

### Clearance_83,470_

A move, often with tempo, that clears a square, file or diagonal for a follow-up tactical idea.](https://lichess.org/training/clearance)[

### Collinear move_7,840_

Two opposing pieces face each other, and one slides along the line of attack without capturing the enemy piece.](https://lichess.org/training/collinearMove)[

### Discovered check_108,803_

Move a piece to reveal a check from a hidden attacking piece, which often leads to a decisive advantage.](https://lichess.org/training/discoveredCheck)[

### Defensive move_385,342_

A precise move or sequence of moves that is needed to avoid losing material or another advantage.](https://lichess.org/training/defensiveMove)[

### Deflection_278,399_

A move that distracts an opposing piece from another duty that it performs, such as guarding a key square. Sometimes also called "overloading".](https://lichess.org/training/deflection)[

### Interference_23,736_

Moving a piece between two opponent pieces to leave one or both opponent pieces undefended, such as a knight on a defended square between two rooks.](https://lichess.org/training/interference)[

### Intermezzo_79,781_

Instead of playing the expected move, first interpose another move posing an immediate threat that the opponent must answer. Also known as "Zwischenzug" or "In between".](https://lichess.org/training/intermezzo)[

### Quiet move_264,376_

A move that does not check, capture, or create an immediate threat to capture. Instead, it prepares a hidden and unavoidable threat for a later move.](https://lichess.org/training/quietMove)[

### X-Ray attack_22,733_

A piece attacks or defends a square, through an enemy piece.](https://lichess.org/training/xRayAttack)[

### Zugzwang_65,465_

The opponent is limited in the moves they can make, and all moves worsen their position.](https://lichess.org/training/zugzwang)

## Mate themes

[

### Anastasia's mate_7,675_

A knight and rook or queen team up to trap the opposing king between the side of the board and a friendly piece.](https://lichess.org/training/anastasiaMate)[

### Arabian mate_7,700_

A knight and a rook team up to trap the opposing king on a corner of the board.](https://lichess.org/training/arabianMate)[

### Back rank mate_212,379_

Checkmate the king on the home rank, when it is trapped there by its own pieces.](https://lichess.org/training/backRankMate)[

### Balestra mate_1,368_

A bishop delivers the checkmate, while a queen blocks the remaining escape squares](https://lichess.org/training/balestraMate)[

### Blind Swine mate_6,361_

Two rooks team up to mate the king in an area of 2 by 2 squares.](https://lichess.org/training/blindSwineMate)[

### Boden's mate_3,833_

Two attacking bishops on criss-crossing diagonals deliver mate to a king obstructed by friendly pieces.](https://lichess.org/training/bodenMate)[

### Corner mate_10,781_

Confine the king to the corner using a rook or queen, and use a knight to deliver the checkmate.](https://lichess.org/training/cornerMate)[

### Double bishop mate_3,800_

Two attacking bishops on adjacent diagonals deliver mate to a king obstructed by friendly pieces.](https://lichess.org/training/doubleBishopMate)[

### Dovetail mate_4,156_

A queen delivers mate to an adjacent king, whose only two escape squares are obstructed by friendly pieces.](https://lichess.org/training/dovetailMate)[

### Epaulette mate_22,646_

Two adjacent escape squares for a checked king are occupied by other pieces.](https://lichess.org/training/epauletteMate)[

### Hook mate_11,007_

Checkmate using a rook, knight, and pawn, where an opposing pawn blocks the king's escape.](https://lichess.org/training/hookMate)[

### Kill box mate_5,519_

A rook is next to the enemy king and supported by a queen that also blocks the king's escape squares. The rook and the queen catch the enemy king in a 3 by 3 "kill box".](https://lichess.org/training/killBoxMate)[

### Pillsbury's mate_67,644_

A rook delivers checkmate to the king, while a bishop helps confine it.](https://lichess.org/training/pillsburysMate)[

### Morphy's mate_7,132_

A bishop delivers checkmate to the king, while a rook helps confine it.](https://lichess.org/training/morphysMate)[

### Opera mate_63,940_

Check the king with a rook and use a bishop to defend the rook.](https://lichess.org/training/operaMate)[

### Swallow's tail mate_8,398_

A checkmate pattern that visually resembles the appearance of a swallow’s tail, similar to a V shape.](https://lichess.org/training/swallowstailMate)[

### Triangle mate_7,854_

The queen and rook, one square away from the enemy king, are on the same rank or file, separated by one square, forming a triangle.](https://lichess.org/training/triangleMate)[

### Vuković mate_2,487_

A rook and knight team up to mate the king. The rook delivers mate while supported by a third piece, and the knight is used to block the king's escape squares.](https://lichess.org/training/vukovicMate)[

### Smothered mate_25,005_

A checkmate delivered by a knight in which the mated king is unable to move because it is surrounded (or smothered) by its own pieces.](https://lichess.org/training/smotheredMate)
