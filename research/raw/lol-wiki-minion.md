# Minion

(Redirected from [Minion (League of Legends)](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion_\(League_of_Legends\)?redirect=no "Minion (League of Legends)"))

Minions of League of Legends  
(by Riot Contracted Artist [Marie Magny](https://www.artstation.com/skyzocat))

 **Minions** are [units](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Unit "Unit") that comprise the main offensive force sent by the [Nexus](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Nexus "Nexus"). They spawn periodically from each team's Nexus in groups and advance along a [lane](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Lane "Lane") towards the enemy Nexus, automatically engaging any enemy unit they encounter within the lane. They are controlled by artificial intelligence and only use [basic attacks](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Basic_attack "Basic attack").

A group of spawned minions is called a **wave**. The game's pace fundamentally depends on minion waves.

## Gameplay - Summoner's Rift

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Gameplay - Summoner's Rift") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Gameplay - Summoner's Rift")\]

### Minion waves

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Minion waves") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Minion waves")\]

Minions on [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift "Summoner's Rift")

Minions start spawning from each [Nexus](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Nexus "Nexus"). Minions within a wave spawn 0.792 seconds apart from each other.

-   Starting from **00:30**, _waves_ spawn every 30 seconds.
-   At **14:00**, _waves_ spawn every 25 seconds (with sequence of 13:30 / 14:00 / 14:25).
-   At **30:00**, _waves_ spawn every 20 seconds (with sequence of 29:25 / 29:50 / 30:10).

Each minion wave comprises several types of minions, which spawn in this order:

1.   [Super minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Super_minion "Super minion")
    -   If a team's inhibitor is destroyed, one super minion spawns for the enemy team on the same lane as the destroyed inhibitor.
    -   If all of a team's inhibitors are destroyed, two super minions spawn on each lane for the enemy team instead.
2.   [Melee minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Melee_minion "Melee minion")
    -   Three of them spawn in each wave.
    -   After **14:00**, when a siege minion spawns on these waves, one fewer melee minion spawns.
3.   [Siege minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_minion "Siege minion")
    -   They do not spawn on waves in which  [super minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Super_minion "Super minion") are present.
    -   They spawn between the melee and caster minions.
    -   The first one spawns with the third wave (at 01:30).
    -   Then, for the first 14 minutes, one spawns every three waves.
    -   After the 14 minute mark, one spawns every two waves (with sequence of 12:00 / 13:30 / 14:25 / 15:15).
    -   After the 25 minute mark, one spawns every wave (with sequence of 24:25 / 25:15 / 25:40).
4.   [Caster minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Caster_minion "Caster minion")
    -   Three of them spawn in each wave.
    -   After **30:00**, one fewer caster minion spawns per wave.

### Behavior

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=4&veaction=edit "Edit section: Behavior") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section's source code: Behavior")\]

While minions have not acquired a target, they will strictly path linearly along a lane until they find a valid enemy unit. Upon finding one, they will move toward and attack it within their range. Minions respect unit collision as well as [player-generated terrain](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Terrain#Player-Generated "Terrain").

The **first wave**'s minions have modified behavior. Top and bottom lanes' minions are ghosted for 28 seconds, while middle lane minions are ghosted for 18 seconds. They will ignore enemy champions until a champion walks much closer to them or until meeting with the enemy wave. When the waves meet, minions will spread out their attacks on the three enemy melee minions regardless of distance. This special behavior exists so that, in every game, neither side gains a pushing advantage without champion interference since the first wave's melee minions are guaranteed to die at roughly the same time.

Minion bearing **aggro**

Nearby enemy minions will become aggressive toward, or "**aggro**", an enemy champion within their own  [variable acquisition range](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Basic_attack#Acquisition) and under certain conditions. When activated by enemy champion actions, this aggression is formally referred to as a **Call for Help signal** in-game and throughout the League of Legends Wiki, and it is indicated by an icon of three concentrated lines above a minion's health bar (this icon indicates all non-playable units' aggro, including  [monsters](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster),  [turrets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret),  [pets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_summoned_units), etc.).

On the Wiki, abilities' interaction with  /  minion Call for Help is noted under their respective "Details" section within the formatted table of properties.

Call for Help signals trigger when:

-   A champion stands in the minion's path, without any other targets within the minions' attack range and outside of a [turret](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret "Turret")'s range.
-   A champion deals damage to an enemy champion with a basic attack, the majority of [unit-targeted](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Unit-targeted "Unit-targeted") abilities, and a minority of abilities which are marked as being similar to those two. Minions will prioritize the closest champion in the case there are multiple sources.
    -   Bar the first wave (which is noted above as an exception), Call for Help signals are also ignored while minions are already attacking an enemy turret.

#### Priority

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Priority") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Priority")\]

When minions find several enemy units, they choose their target using a priority system that follows this order:

1.  Enemy champions attacking an allied champion.
2.  Enemy minions attacking an allied champion.
3.  Enemy minions attacking an allied minion.
4.  Enemy turrets attacking an allied minion.
5.  The closest enemy minion.
6.  The closest enemy champion.

Once a minion has chosen a target, it only switches to a new target if that new target has a higher priority. The minion cannot acquire a new target that has the same priority as their current target (and thus continues attacking its current target).

#### Acquisition range

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=6&veaction=edit "Edit section: Acquisition range") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section's source code: Acquisition range")\]

In general, valid targets must be within 500 units of the minion in order to be acquired.

Allied champions being attacked by an enemy champion may be within 1000 range of the minion to trigger a Call for Help signal instead.

#### Timing

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=7&veaction=edit "Edit section: Timing") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section's source code: Timing")\]

Minions reevaluate their target in between each attack windup every few seconds. For instance, if an enemy champion is attacking an allied champion and stops doing so, minions will not stop targeting that champion for a short time afterwards.

#### Vision

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=8&veaction=edit "Edit section: Vision") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=8 "Edit section's source code: Vision")\]

When the minion loses sight of its target, the minion switches to a new target or keeps advancing if there are no targets left within sight.

#### Death grace

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=9&veaction=edit "Edit section: Death grace") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=9 "Edit section's source code: Death grace")\]

When a minion is below 0.35% of its **maximum** health and receives lethal damage from another minion, its health is set to 1. If it has not received damage from another source within the next 0.066 seconds, it will then proceed to die at the end of the delay.

This effect does not take place if the damage to the minion would exceed 190.

### Stats mechanics

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=10&veaction=edit "Edit section: Stats mechanics") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=10 "Edit section's source code: Stats mechanics")\]

-   Starting at 0:30 and every 90 seconds thereafter (regardless of which minion spawn), minions gain an "upgrade", gaining health, attack damage, and possibly certain [buffs](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion#Buffs) depending on the game state.
-   Against champions and structures, minions deal 60% damage.
-   Against other minions, minions deal **bonus** physical damage equal to a percentage of their target's **current** health, varying based on the attacking minion.
-   Minions have  350 / 375 / 400 / 425 / 450 (based on minutes) **base** movement speed ([before caps](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Movement_speed "Movement speed")).
-   New spawning minions level up based on their team's average champion level, rounded down to integer (e.g. champion levels 15, 13, 18, 15, 10 = minion level 14). Minion levels interact with the minion pushing buff explained below.
-   Upon dying, a minion's [experience](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)") bounty is split equally among the killer and all nearby enemy champions within 1500 units of its death location.

### Sidelane Speed

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=13&veaction=edit "Edit section: Sidelane Speed") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=13 "Edit section's source code: Sidelane Speed")\]

Exclusive to  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift)

After the first wave and until 14:00 game time, all top and bottom lane (= side lane) minions gain a  movement speed buff for a set duration upon their spawn.

Each spawned minion in the second minion wave of the game gains 111 **bonus** movement speed as an _initial speed_. Each subsequent wave reduces its spawned minions' _initial speed_ by 4.5.

The bonus is removed in four _stages_ as the minion travels:

-   The first, second, and third stages last 7 seconds; the fourth stage lasts 4 seconds. (Total: 25 seconds)
-   Upon a minion's time in each stage completing, reduce its movement speed by 15.
-   After the stages' collective time has elapsed, this buff is removed.

### Minion Pushing

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=14&veaction=edit "Edit section: Minion Pushing") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=14 "Edit section's source code: Minion Pushing")\]

On  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift)

The team with the higher average champion level gains the minion pushing buff. The buff increases the minion damage against enemy minions and reduces the damage they take from them.

**Bonus damage** = (5% + (5% × _Turret advantage of the lane_)) × _Team level advantage_

**Damage reduction** = 1 + (_Turret advantage of the lane_ × _Team level advantage_)

-   Team level advantage is the difference between the (decimal) average of the (integer) levels of the players in one team and the average of the levels of the other. This difference can be a decimal value, and is capped at 3.
-   Turret advantage is the difference between the number of turrets destroyed in one team on a specific lane and the number of turrets destroyed on the other team on that same lane. Note that Nexus turrets are not lane turrets. This difference cannot be below 0.

Since there are three turrets on each lane, the maximum possible benefits on Summoner's Rift are:

**Bonus damage** = (5% + (5% × 3)) × 3 = 60%

**Damage reduction** = 1 + (3 × 3) = 10

This buff starts to apply at 3:30. Already-spawned minions will also receive the buff and these values will update for all minions when the level and turret advantages change.

On  [Howling Abyss](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Howling_Abyss)

Minions in the team with higher average champion level receive a buff that reduces damage taken from enemy minions.

**Damage reduction** = _Level advantage_ × _Turret advantage_

-   Team level advantage is the difference between the (decimal) average of the (integer) levels of the players in one team and the average of the levels of the other. This difference can be a decimal value, and is **not** capped.
-   Turret advantage is the difference between the number of inner and nexus turrets destroyed for each team.

This buff starts to apply at 3:30. Already-spawned minions will also receive the buff and these values will update for all minions when the level and turret advantages change.

## Creep Score

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=20&veaction=edit "Edit section: Creep Score") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=20 "Edit section's source code: Creep Score")\]

**Creep Score**, or **CS**, is the number of minions, monsters, and champion pets a champion has slain by dealing the killing blow to these units. Dealing this hit is known as **last hitting** and is considered one of the most important skills in League of Legends.

Every minion is worth 1 CS.

In the early- and mid-game, CS along with [KDA](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill_to_Death_Ratio "Kill to Death Ratio") can be used to measure a player's performance against their lane opponent.

### Wave Gold Value

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=21&veaction=edit "Edit section: Wave Gold Value") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=21 "Edit section's source code: Wave Gold Value")\]

-   As of [V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01 "V26.01"), a **minion wave** has an _average_ gold value that varies from  115 and up to  148, depending on game time.

## Last Hit Indicator

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=22&veaction=edit "Edit section: Last Hit Indicator") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=22 "Edit section's source code: Last Hit Indicator")\]

In non-ranked Summoner's Rift queues ([Co-op vs. AI](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Co-op_vs._AI "Co-op vs. AI"), [Custom Lobbies](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Custom_game "Custom game"), [Tutorial](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Tutorial "Tutorial"), [Swiftplay](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swiftplay "Swiftplay"), [Draft Pick](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Draft_Pick "Draft Pick") and [Featured game modes](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Featured_game_mode "Featured game mode")), players have the option to enable an indicator that shows when their next basic attack will guarantee a kill on a minion. The indicator's damage calculation only factors in permanent sources of damage, such as  [Varus'](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Varus)  [Blighted Quiver's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Varus#Blighted_Quiver) on-hit effect or increased attack damage from a  [Long Sword](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Long_Sword), and does not include temporary sources of damage, such as the  [Sheen's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sheen) Spellblade effect. The indicator's damage calculation factors in the minion's [armor](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Armor "Armor") and [magic resistance](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Magic_resistance "Magic resistance"), as well as the champion's [lethality](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Armor_penetration#Lethality "Armor penetration"), but does not factor in [percentage armor penetration](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Armor_penetration#Percentage_armor_penetration "Armor penetration") or [magic penetration](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Magic_penetration "Magic penetration").

On  [Zeri](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zeri), Last Hit Indicators instead show her  [Living Battery's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zeri#Living_Battery)  [execute](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#Execute) threshold.

This option is enabled by default for newly created accounts, but is disabled by default for accounts created prior to [V26.05](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.05 "V26.05"). It can be found in Settings under **Interface** → **Health and Resource Bars** → "**Show Last Hit Assist**."

## Wave Strategy

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=23&veaction=edit "Edit section: Wave Strategy") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=23 "Edit section's source code: Wave Strategy")\]

-   Without player interference, a lane's wave is neither advancing or retreating in any significant amount.
-   A minion wave that catches up with its preceding wave starts pushing the lane towards enemy turret.
-   Big waves of minions can overwhelm a turret.
-   When a player interrupts an enemy minion wave, ensuring their minions deal more damage to the opponent's minions than they are receiving, the lane starts to **push**.
    -   In the simplest way, this is done by killing every minion in a wave without interference from your own minions. This changes where in the lane the minion waves clash.
    -   On more sophisticated turning the player makes sure their side is making just slightly more damage thus allowing the wave to build up as the wave gets reinforcements before than the enemy wave.
-   If a player becomes targeted by the minion wave, then the result will be that if the targeted player has a minion wave with them then this will cause the wave to push much faster since during the period of time the player was targeted, that player's minion wave was able to freely attack the opposing wave without taking damage in return.
    -   This is important since even if a wave is pushing away from a player, if that player does not want the wave to move further from their side of the map. That player can try to tempt an enemy player into attacking them to draw the attention of the minions, so that the wave starts moving back to the player who was attacked.

## Game Terminology

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=24&veaction=edit "Edit section: Game Terminology") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=24 "Edit section's source code: Game Terminology")\]

Some game terms related to minions:

-   **[Farming](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Farming "Farming")**, or **Last-hitting**: Dealing the killing blow on minions in order to earn their  gold bounty along with experience.
-   **Lane Momentum**: The strength with which the minions in a lane advance without champion aid.
-   **Pushing**: Advancing allied minions along the lane.
-   **Freezing**: Preventing the advancement of allied and enemy minions along the lane.
-   **"Winions"**: A wave of minions so big or so strong that it can destroy [turrets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret "Turret"), [nexuses](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Nexus "Nexus"), or champions without champion aid.
-   **Free Farming**: Farming without being confronted by an enemy champion.
-   **Proxy Farming**: Farming behind an enemy tower. The enemy champion has to remain in front of their turret to last-hit minions, since they would die to the turret due to freely advancing to it. However the "proxying" player is also in much more danger.
-   **Split Pushing**: Attacking several objectives at the same time when at least one of the objectives is a lane turret.

## Trivia

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?section=26&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion?action=edit&section=26 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\]

-   If the game plays out without champion interference, the minions in one of the teams will eventually win the game. The team that wins is random.
-   When minions acquire a target, it is possible for a champion to pick up aggro multiple times from a minion. Minions can constantly pick up aggro from you and can follow you through the jungle.

-   Previously, midlane minions had reduced gold bounties, which lasted until 14:00 (until the wave at 13:35). The gold penalty was ultimately removed in [V13.12](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V13.12 "V13.12").
    -   Between [V10.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V10.23 "V10.23") and [V12.21](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V12.21 "V12.21"), midlane siege minions were worth  10 less. Compared to other lanes, the total amount of gold from midlane minions was  80 less.
    -   Between [V12.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V12.22 "V12.22") and [V13.11](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V13.11 "V13.11"), all midlane minions were worth  1 less (i.e., siege minions were in total worth  1 less instead of  10 less than their regular bounty as before). Compared to other lanes, the total amount of gold from midlane minions was  164 less.
-   Blue Siege Minion in beta is a mobile ballista.
    -   An unused Blue Siege Minion model found in the files during beta also became the actual Blue Siege Minion in their next version.
-   Visually, Blue [Super Minion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Super_Minion "Super Minion")'s pilots use two control sticks similar to analog sticks on [Siege Minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Siege_Minion "Siege Minion") to control their mech while Red [Super Minion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Super_Minion "Super Minion")'s pilots control their mech with a red crystal sphere, suggesting that magic is used.

-   Minions should no longer be able to get killing streaks.

[Alpha Week 6](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_6 "Alpha Week 6")

-   Minions no longer give half gold and experience when you have lost an inhibitor.

[Alpha Week 5](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_5 "Alpha Week 5")

-   Minion health bars are now brighter.

[Alpha Week 4](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_4 "Alpha Week 4")

-   Minions now spawn after 1 minute instead of 1 minute and 40 seconds.
-   Minions now spawn from the Nexus instead of inhibitors.

[Alpha Week 2](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_2 "Alpha Week 2")

-   Minions can no longer be denied.
