## **What is OP.GG Premium?**

OP.GG Premium is a subscription plan that offers ad-free stats search and additional features. ✨

[Subscribe to OP.GG Premium](https://member.op.gg/membership)

## **Setting up your primary account**

Some of OP.GG Premium features require setting up a primary account. You can set up your primary account by logging into your OP.GG account and navigating to "My Page."

  

1.  After logging in, click on "My Page" located at the top right corner of the OP.GG homepage.
2.  You can select a primary account from the Riot accounts linked to your OP.GG account. If you haven't linked any Riot accounts yet, click on "Link Riot account" to link one.
3.  After linking your Riot account, click on "Set primary account."
4.  Choose the Riot account you want to set as your primary account from the list of linked Riot accounts.
5.  Click "Save" to complete the process.
    
    (**Note: Once the primary account is set, it cannot be changed for 24 hours, and it applies to all OP.GG services.**)
    

## **Premium features**

### **Ad removal**

When logged in with an account subscribed to OP.GG Premium, ads are removed from all OP.GG services, including mobile and desktop apps when logged in with the same account.

### **My Page**

Setting up a primary account lets you access your personalized dashboard on My Page. Here, you can explore a wider range of personalized match history and data.

💡The above image is for illustration purposes only. More data will be available on My Page in the future.

### **Premium favorites**

Premium favorites allows you to add more summoners to your favorites list and save them to your account for consistent management on both web and mobile apps. In the mobile app, you'll receive **game start notifications** for summoners added to your favorites list.

(For more information on mobile app favorites & notifications, click here.)

  

The number of favorites and account sync varies depending on the login and membership level:

Q. What happens to previously saved Favorites?

When you first log in to the web or mobile app, a pop-up will appear to save your current list of favorites. Clicking \[Save\] will sync them on the mobile app, while \[Cancel\] will delete the existing list, so please be aware.

### **Premium stat update**

The "Update" button for the match history of the Riot account set as your primary account will be upgraded to a Premium button. Now you can update your latest match history more frequently without time limits.

  

## **Frequently asked questions**

### **Q. I can't use OP.GG Premium features after completing payment.**

If premium features are not activated after subscribing to OP.GG Premium, please check the following:

1.  After logging in to your OP.GG account, go to "Subscription" to check if OP.GG Premium subscription is activated.
    
2.  Some premium features require setting up a primary account. Make sure your desired Riot account is set as the primary account.
