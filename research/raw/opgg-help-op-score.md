# Q. What is the OP Score?

The OP Score is an evaluation system that estimates in-game performance based on summoner stats. Scores range from 0 to 10, depending on performance in the match.

At the end of a match, the top-performing summoner on each team is awarded either the MVP or ACE badge.

-   **MVP**: The summoner on the winning team with the highest OP Score among all 10 players

-   **ACE**: The summoner on the losing team with the highest OP Score

# Q. What is the timeline OP Score?

The timeline OP Score is OP.GG’s scoring system that analyzes in-game performance across multiple factors. The timeline graph displays performance trends over time, and allows for side-by-side comparison by champion.  
  
The timeline OP Score is calculated every 5 minutes on Summoner’s Rift and every 3 minutes in ARAM, with the final value at the end of the match recorded as the official OP Score.  
You can also see a total of 14 keywords and analyses, such as 'Tenacity', 'Unstoppable', and 'Indomitable Will', based on each graph's shape.

# Q. OP Score results are inaccurate.

The OP Score is currently in beta and may occasionally produce inaccurate results depending on the characteristics of each game.

OP.GG is continually working to improve the accuracy of the OP Score. If an OP Score result appears inaccurate for a specific match, please report it through 1:1 inquiry.
