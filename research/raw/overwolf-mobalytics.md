## About the App

### Find our your strengths and weaknesses and carry your teammates with Mobalytics

Mobalytics is an analytics-driven improvement platform for League of Legends. The app uses the Gamer Performance Index (GPI) to help you analyze your strengths and weaknesses, and assigns you personalized advice according to your individual performance.  
Ultimately, our goal is to go above and beyond the simple statistical numbers that other tools give and provide you with highly relevant insights to level up your game.  
  

### The Mobalytics platform includes four POWERFUL tools:

GPI - Provides personalized advice based on your play style.  
  
PRE GAME - Gives you vital information on all summoners in your match before it starts.  
  
POST GAME - Helps you evaluate your match and helps you do better next time.  
  
PERSONAL DAHSBOARD - Allows you to better understand your long term development and progress.  
  
These core features work together to provide you with a feedback loop which comprehensively covers every stage of your improvement process. While you focus on playing, we gather all your data and crunch it through machine learning algorithms to give you a multifaceted assessment of your game.  
  

### So what are you waiting for? Improve your skills now with Mobalytics.

For more information about Mobalytics, feel free to check our official website [mobalytics.gg](https://mobalytics.gg/).  
  
Have questions or feedback? Let us know on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/maYKBJM).
