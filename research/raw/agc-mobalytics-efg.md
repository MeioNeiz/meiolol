 AGC Partners Advises Mobalytics on Its Acquisition by ESL FACEIT Group

 Nate Hennings
 Partner

 Transaction Details

 AGC Partners is pleased to announce that its client, Mobalytics, a leading gaming analytics platform, has been acquired by ESL FACEIT Group, a subsidiary of Savvy Games Group, backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund. With this acquisition, Mobalytics will enhance ESL FACEIT Group's gaming ecosystem, solidifying its leadership position in competitive gaming analytics and player development. With a team led by Nate Hennings, AGC Partners acted as sole financial advisor to Mobalytics.
