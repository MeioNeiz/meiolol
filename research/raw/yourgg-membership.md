 Product FAQs 

 When does my AI coaching allowance reset? 
 It resets monthly. Monthly and annual subscriptions receive the same monthly allowance for the same plan. Unused allowance doesn’t roll over. You can check your usage and next refill date in the app under AI Coaching → Usage.

 Is usage deducted if I leave before a response arrives? 
 AI tokens already consumed for analysis and response generation remain deducted, even if you leave or don’t read the response.

 What is PLAYREPORT? 
 PLAYREPORT is an AI-powered report that objectively analyzes your playstyle, identifying your strengths and weaknesses based on gameplay data, and suggests clear improvement directions.

 How do I generate a PLAYREPORT? 
 Click the person icon at the top-right of the YOUR.GG homepage to access My Page , then select your preferred champion, lane, and number of games to generate a report. On a summoner's profile page, the PLAYREPORT tab shows a sample report for any champion played at least 3 times in the last 30 games. [ View generation guide ] 

 What if I don’t have enough recent games? 
 To generate a report, you need at least 4 games with the same champion and lane in your last 100 games. Those games must include at least 2 wins and 2 losses, and games that end before 20 minutes don't count. For example, 4 games as top Garen is valid, but 3 games as top Garen + 1 game as mid Garen won't work. Also, if there aren’t enough other users in your tier who played that champion (e.g., top Yuumi), the report cannot be generated.

 What’s the difference between one-time and subscription Membership? 
 The one-time(PLAY) allows you to generate a single report — perfect for trying it out. With a Membership (PLUS / PRO / PRO+) , you can set your champion and generate unlimited reports to deeply analyze and improve your gameplay over time. The PRO+ offers no champion limit and includes AI coaching and additional premium benefits , making it highly recommended for users serious about climbing the ladder.

 What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription? 
 Reports generated during your subscription are only viewable while your Membership is active. Once canceled, you can no longer access them but your data is saved , and all past reports will become accessible again if you resubscribe.

 How can I cancel or request a refund? 
 To cancel your Membership, click the person icon on the YOUR.GG homepage, go to Memberships → Cancel membership . To request a refund, please join our official Discord and create a ticket in the #payment-inquiry channel. Our team will get back to you shortly.
