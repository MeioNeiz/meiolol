[Jump to navigation](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster#searchInput)

_This is about  [League of Legends](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_of_Legends) Monsters. For other uses of **Monster**, see [Monster (disambiguation)](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster_\(disambiguation\) "Monster (disambiguation)")_

Several monsters from [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift "Summoner's Rift")

 **Monsters** are neutral units in **League of Legends**. Unlike  [minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion), monsters do not fight for either team, and will only do so if provoked (except for the  [Rift Scuttler](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rift_Scuttler "Rift Scuttler"), which simply runs away when attacked). Monsters have tiers, depending on their general importance and durability; Epic is the highest tier, and Epic monsters grant immense rewards to the team that takes them down.

## Gameplay

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Gameplay") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Gameplay")\]

### Monster behavior & Patience

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Monster behavior & Patience") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Monster behavior & Patience")\]

Monsters reside in the [jungle](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Jungling "Jungling"), and will not move from their designated camp until attacked, at which point they become aggressive (are "_aggroed_"). When aggroed, the monster will acquire the nearest champion, regardless of  [vision](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sight), and then attempt to attack them back. Monsters' distance to their target considers walking travel distance (pathing distance), **not** straight line distance.

Monsters have a maximum radius they can move before they start to lose their **patience**. This distance is called **leash range** and is indicated by a flowing circle around the camp's location that appears for each player when they initiate combat with that monster (with the exception of  [Baron Nashor](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_Nashor "Baron Nashor"), who is stationary). The visual ring will fade away after a few seconds if the monster isn't attacked, attacking will refresh the VFX. **Leash range** varies per camp. If the monster is unable to find their acquired target (e.g. the champion enters  [stealth](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Stealth)) or moves outside of its **leash range**, its patience will start to deplete. Patience depletes faster the farther away the monster is from its maximum leash range and/or the less it is attacked while being there. Non-epic monsters' patience additionally drops when they change targets. They may change their target up to 6 times (without accounting for patience loss while outside leash range) before their patience fully depletes.

When a monster's patience reaches zero, it will enter a state of _soft reset_ for 6 seconds. While in this state, the monster will begin walking back toward its neutral position, healing for 6% of its **maximum** health every second and ignoring calls for aggression from its attackers until it has returned inside its **leash range**. Attacking a monster while it is within its **leash range** ends this _soft reset_ prematurely to restore some of its patience and prevent it from losing any further patience for 1.5 seconds.

When the _soft reset_ grace period ends, the monster will enter a state of _hard reset_ instead, again ignoring all attackers, but now healing at a much faster rate and running back to its neutral position more rapidly. Once the monsters have returned to the camp's neutral position, they will recover their patience after 2 seconds of their arrival and over 2 seconds.

-   Monsters can have different range types.

### Camp respawn

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=4&veaction=edit "Edit section: Camp respawn") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section's source code: Camp respawn")\]

After a camp has been completely cleared, it will remain empty for an amount of time specific to that camp (its "respawn time"), then all of the monsters will reappear. A camp's respawn timer can be viewed in the scoreboard. An accurate timer can only be given to the ally team if the ally team had vision of the camp when it was cleared; however, gaining vision of the camp's location at any point may still provide an accurate timer on the minimap when the camp is close to spawning (a greyed out timer means 60 seconds remaining, a filled one means 10 seconds remaining until respawn). This does not apply to epic monster camps, which will always provide an accurate timer and be announced to both teams when cleared.

-   When a non-epic monster camp is cleared outside of vision, it is not possible to know that an enemy has recently been at that location.

A monster camp will not start its respawn timer until **all** of the monsters in the camp have died. For example, if the  [Greater Murk Wolf](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Greater_Murk_Wolf "Greater Murk Wolf") has been slain, but at least one of its two  [Murk Wolf](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Murk_Wolf "Murk Wolf") companions has not, the camp's respawn timer will not start until the last Wolf has been slain. The game will automatically kill any remaining lesser monsters in a camp whose large monster has been killed, after 10 seconds of them returning to a neutral state. This allows them to respawn automatically as well, albeit with this important delay.

### Camp level

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Camp level") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Camp level")\]

Each camp has a level, which is calculated using the average of all champions' current  [level](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)") (rounded to the nearest integer) at the time of their spawning. Level may influence the monsters' stats—particularly their [health](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Health "Health") and damage—as well as their rewards. Some or all of these effects may be capped at a certain level, or even at different levels for each one for the same monster. These caps also vary by camp.

Epic monster camps may feature an absolute minimum level for determining their effects. Their stats or attacks may scale with game time, exclusively or additionally to camp level.

## Monster Rewards

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=6&veaction=edit "Edit section: Monster Rewards") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section's source code: Monster Rewards")\]

As a general rule, monsters dispense their full  gold and  [experience](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)") bounty to the killer. Epic monsters may dispense bounties to the killer as well as locally and/or globally to the members of the team that slay them instead. Some large monsters and all epic monsters can also offer individual or team-wide [buffs](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Buff "Buff").

Owning a jungle item ( [Gustwalker Hatchling](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gustwalker_Hatchling),  [Mosstomper Seedling](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mosstomper_Seedling), or  [Scorchclaw Pup](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Scorchclaw_Pup)) imposes a number of rules on the user, the most notable being that they gain **bonus** experience from killing large monsters, greatly increased for the first large monster kill of the game. In turn, the user is heavily penalized if they kill too many minions.

Notable buff rewards include:

-   The  [Crest of Insight](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Insight) and  [Crest of Cinders](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Cinders) buffs are gained respectively from the  [Blue Sentinel](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blue_Sentinel "Blue Sentinel") and the  [Red Brambleback](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Red_Brambleback "Red Brambleback"), and may be transferred to another champion. This occurs when a champion kills someone holding a crest, causing the crest to be transferred to the killer and resetting the buffs duration. Either buff disappears entirely if the champion holding the crest is executed by a minion, monster, or tower, or when the crest simply wear offs on its own after a few minutes.
    -   When the map transforms into the _[Elemental Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dragon_pit#Elemental_Rift "Dragon pit")_, the  [Blue Sentinel](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blue_Sentinel "Blue Sentinel") and the  [Red Brambleback](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Red_Brambleback "Red Brambleback") become _Draconic_, gaining this form if alive and respawning in it each time. Killing them in this form leaves behind a buff wisp at the camp's location for 60 seconds. The wisp can be picked up by an allied champion moving near it, granting them the buff. If the killer did not have  [Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Smite), the wisp can only be picked up by a champion that does so.
-   The  [Glimpse of the Void](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Glimpse_of_the_Void) buff is granted to the team that has taken down the  [Rift Herald](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rift_Herald "Rift Herald") in the [baron pit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_pit "Baron pit") after they pick up her dropped  [Eye of the Herald](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Eye_of_the_Herald). This buff allows the holder to summon an allied [Rift Herald Mercenary](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rift_Herald#Summoned_form "Rift Herald"), which will then march down the nearest lane and [push](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Push "Push") it until it dies.
-   The stacking  [Dragon Slayer](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dragon_Slayer) buffs acquired for slaying the  [Dragon](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dragon "Dragon") are applied to all team members, whether living or dead, and last indefinitely. The exception to this is the  [Aspect of the Dragon](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aspect_of_the_Dragon) from the  [Elder Dragon](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Elder_Dragon "Elder Dragon"), which is only granted to living teammates, lasts a few minutes, and is lost upon death.
-   The  [Touch of the Void](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Touch_of_the_Void) buff is granted to all teammates when a  [Voidgrub](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Voidgrub "Voidgrub") is slain. The buff stacks for each grub killed.
    -   At 3 stacks of this buff, champions also gain the  [Hunger of the Void](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hunger_of_the_Void) buff.
-   The  [Hand of Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hand_of_Baron) buff is only granted to living teammates when  [Baron Nashor](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_Nashor "Baron Nashor") is killed. The buff lasts for a few minutes and is lost upon death.

## Lore

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=12&veaction=edit "Edit section: Lore") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=12 "Edit section's source code: Lore")\]

### Summoner's Rift

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=13&veaction=edit "Edit section: Summoner's Rift") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=13 "Edit section's source code: Summoner's Rift")\]

Historically, the forest where the Rift resides was the site of several battles between the Protectorate (Stag crest) and Magelords (Owl crest) summoners, two sides who sought out to use the natural magical energy of the area for their personal use.

The large amount of concentrated magic has mutated the local wildlife. From  [giant frogs](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gromp "Gromp"),  [Giant Birds](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crimson_Raptor "Crimson Raptor") to  [Two-headed Wolves](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Greater_Murk_Wolf "Greater Murk Wolf") it has also attracted the attention of powerful  [Dragons](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dragon "Dragon") who currently reside in one of the battle torn ruins.  [Sentinels](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blue_Sentinel "Blue Sentinel"),  [Bramblebacks](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Red_Brambleback "Red Brambleback") and  [Krugs](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ancient_Krug "Ancient Krug") are creatures made from local flora and earth infused to construct a magical being. The Rift's unstable magic has inadvertently cause the reemergence of  [Baron Nashor](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_Nashor "Baron Nashor"), a large Serpent/Worm largely believed to be dead for centuries. It has his corruption affecting the land around him.

### Twisted Treeline

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?section=14&veaction=edit "Edit section: Twisted Treeline") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster?action=edit&section=14 "Edit section's source code: Twisted Treeline")\]

After the fall of the Shadow Isles, the islands inhabitants were changed by [the Black Mist](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/The_Black_Mist "The Black Mist"). Numerous individuals and creatures now serve the [Harrowing](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Harrowing "Harrowing")'s will.

 [Vilemaw](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Vilemaw "Vilemaw") is an ancient spider of unknown origin currently residing in the cursed forest. The _"Cult of the Spider"_ has most of its followers located in [Noxus](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Noxus "Noxus") and [Demacia](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Demacia "Demacia").  [Elise](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Elise) serves as its High Priestess, traveling to and from between the two nations and the [Shadow Isles](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Shadow_Isles "Shadow Isles"), bringing her followers to live with the Spider God, Vilemaw, for eternity, or so they believe.

[V12.22](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V12.22 "V12.22")

-   While a monster is aggroed, their leash range will be briefly highlighted and persist around the camp until they exit combat and fully reset. This is the distance outside of which their patience starts to diminish.
-   **New Effect:** When a monster loses all of its patience, it will _soft reset_ for 6 seconds instead of fully resetting its patience. While in the process of _soft resetting_, the monster will path towards the center of its camp, heal for 6% of its **maximum** health every second, and ignore attackers outside of its leash range.
    -   Attacking a monster while it is within its leash range ends this _soft reset_ prematurely to restore some of its patience and prevents it from being lost for 1.5 seconds.
    -   If the _soft reset_ ends from after 6 seconds, the monster will _hard reset_ and in the process will ignore all attackers, rapidly heal, and path to its original position.
        -   Monsters move slow while _soft resetting_ and much more faster while _hard resetting_.
-   **New Effect:** Monsters now regenerate patience that they have lost after 2 seconds of being back within their leash range.
-   **New Effect:** Small monsters now match the patience of their respective large monster as long as it is alive and within 700 units.
-   **New Effect - Marked For Death:** When the large monster of each non-epic camp dies, all other smaller monsters in their camp will be _marked for death_ for 10 seconds. After 10 seconds of being out of combat with a champion, the marked monsters will die.
-   **New Effect:** Killing large monsters rewards an additional  15 and 15 when owning a [jungle item](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Jungling#Items "Jungling").

[V5.19](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.19 "V5.19")

-   Jungle monsters now have a _Patience_ characteristic. Monsters no longer reset their aggression based on distance or positioning - Instead, once the monster's Patience is reduced to zero, the monster will reset and return to their original position. During this reset, they rapidly regenerate health and do not respond to further aggression.
    -   Monsters lose Patience under the following conditions:
        -   When a monster is in an aggressive state but can't find any targets to attack (ex. its target died or became untargetable)
        -   When a monster's target is too far away from the monster's initial spawn location or the monster is VERY far away from its initial spawn location
        -   When the monster is forced to switch targets
    -   Large monsters display their Patience in a meter above their heads.
    -   Once a monster has recovered to 90% health, they regain all of their _Patience_.
    -   Abilities that pull or displace monsters no longer completely reset the monster's aggro if they already had a previous target.
