## About the App

**Sick of losing before the game even begins?**

ProComps is built to give League players and teams an edge right from champ select.

We'll help you turn every Draft into a game-winning advantage. Here's how:

### Real-Time Draft Assistant

-   Live insights into team gamestyles, scaling, and strategic alerts.
-   Champion suggestions based on counters, synergies, gamestyles, scaling and more.
-   Pre-game guide for planning tactics tailored to each match.
-   Integrates with League Client, Prodraft, & Draftlol

### Instantly Build Your Champion Pool & Share With Friends

-   Generate your personalized champion pool in seconds
-   Gain full control to customize, update, and refine as you like.
-   Invite friends and share champion pools

### Bring Teammates & Draft Together

-   Create teams, invite friends, and integrate champion pools seamlessly across devices.
-   Outsmart opponents with tailored drafting insights
-   Every pick and counter sets the team up for the perfect draft

### Custom Tier Lists for Strategists

-   Create custom champion tier lists for each lane
-   Personalize insights during live drafts.
-   Perfect for coaches and analysts and Meta-Entushiasts.

### Comp Builder - Craft Your Winning Comps Before The Draft

-   Manually create and refine team compositions
-   Tailor each comp with ProComps' insights and tips
-   Save your best compositions for future drafts.

### Comp Creator - Instantly Generate Optimal Team Comps

-   Input your champion pool and preferences
-   Create the perfect team comps in seconds

    I've been using this app for a year and have been very happy with it. The drafting advice this app offers is simply better than any app (I know of) out there. The bans and picks recommendation is just at a pro level I have not seen anywhere else. If you do not agree with the tier-list (for example, you have high skills with a certain champion), there is the option to create your own tier-list. This app also provides information on what the state of the game should look like. What to do at level 1, what lanes should be winning/balanced/losing, what objectives should be taken/looked at/given. There is also the recommended strategy for both teams, and more information on how to play with/against this strategy. This apps gets updated according to data from pro-play and matches of the highest caliber found online. Due to their current way of gathering data and applying it into the app, it sometimes takes up to a few days into a patch before it actually gets updated to the new patch. Once the info does come through it's solid. Overall this app has increased the quality of my bans & picks by an insane amount, and due this also tremendously increased my fun & knowledge within/about the game. I recommend this app to everyone I play with, even if it's just to see the strategy for our games. If you play with a team of 5 and every single player has the app downloaded, I'm certain the quality of your games will increase by a lot. The app still has a lot of room to grow, but for what it is currently advertising itself, is in my opinion, the best out here.
