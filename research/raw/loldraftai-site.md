## What you get.

— Features

01 — DRAFT ANALYSIS

### Whole-draft AI, not pair stats.

A neural network reads all 10 picks in one pass, capturing synergies, scaling, damage profile, and lane matchups together, the way the game is actually played.

Trained on millions of ranked games · re-trained every patch

02 — CHAMPION SUGGESTIONS

### The right pick for _this_ draft.

Star your favorites by lane once. The model ranks every champion against your team and the enemy team, so you can see what fits this specific game.

Counterpick-aware mode · Flex-safe mode

03 — RUNE AI

### Runes that read the room.

The same model brain, applied to runes. Considers your team, the enemy team, and every matchup, so the page suits this game rather than a generic build.

Full rune page · summoner spells · keystone alternatives

04 — ITEM BUILDS

### A build for _this_ game.

Item builds conditioned on the whole picture: your champion, both teams, your runes and summoner spells, elo, and patch. You get the final build and the full purchase order, and on desktop you can apply it to your client as an item set in one click.

Final build · purchase order by minute · apply to client

## Questions.

— FAQ

Why use LoLDraftAI for draft analysis?

Most draft tools rely on simple champion-vs-champion win rate statistics and fixed formulas to estimate draft strength. LoLDraftAI is fundamentally different: it uses a custom-trained neural network that learns directly from millions of game outcomes. This means it captures complex interactions like team composition synergies, multi-lane dynamics, and scaling patterns that formula-based tools miss. The model is updated with every new patch.
