### Features

#### Patch recap & champion tiers

See every champion buffed, nerfed or adjusted this patch, then check lane-by-lane tiers to find who's strong right now.

#### Match history

Check your record in every mode, from Ranked Solo and Flex to ARAM: Mayhem, along with recent KDA and your most-played champions, all on one screen.

#### Champion analysis

Compare win, pick and ban rates and matchups by region, tier and patch, then pull up each champion's skill order, runes and recommended item builds.

### Overlays

### AI Agent Control

Talk with the AGENT using voice and easily control your mic and speaker from the overlay. You can also use the Push-to-Talk key to chat with the AGENT only when you want.

### Jungle timers

Track jungle monster and Scuttle Crab respawn timers on the minimap. (Herald/Baron excluded)

### Jungle Path

You can view the most commonly used jungle path for your selected champion on the minimap. Clear the jungle more easily and efficiently!

### Inhibitor timers

You can check the respawn time of allied and enemy inhibitors after they are destroyed.

### ARAM Health Timers

Keep track of health relic respawn timers in Howling Abyss and never miss a clutch heal.

### Augment Tier

You can quickly see the tier of augments that fit your current champion.

### Real-time stats

You can compare your current skill level using key metrics based on the lane you selected and the average tier.

### Skill recommendations

See the optimized skill order for champions based on OP.GG stats.

### Item builds

Check out the most popular item builds for champions based on OP.GG stats.

### Gold & item comparison

Compare the total items and gold difference between teams.

### Loading screen

Check recent match details, overall AD/AP ratio for all champions and more.

### Healing item tracker

Check the enemy team's healing items and your team's healing reduction items.

### Ward count
