# Team drafting

(Redirected from [Draft pick](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Draft_pick?redirect=no "Draft pick"))

[Jump to navigation](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#searchInput)

_

"Draft" redirects here. For the Normal game queue on Summoner's Rift, see [Draft Pick](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Draft_Pick "Draft Pick").

_

**Team drafting** or simply **drafting** refers to the process by which players are assigned individual roles and [champions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion "Champion") to form a team before the game begins. In [League of Legends](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_of_Legends "League of Legends"), **drafting** takes place over two stages, with the first being in the pre-game lobby, and the second being in the _champion selection_ screen (_champion select_ or _champ select_ for short). In the pre-game lobby, drafting may be facilitated by each player selecting their  preferred position(s) before they can enter the [matchmaking queue](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Queuing_and_Matchmaking "Queuing and Matchmaking").

During champion selection, players pick a champion to play and/or ban any number of champions from a _selection pool_ that usually (but not necessarily) consists of all playable characters that exist in  [League of Legends](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_of_Legends). The combination of picks and bans in choice, ordering and display determines the various drafting types.

This article contains information on role selection, the champion select screen and the various drafting types employed across regular and professional [League of Legends](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_of_Legends "League of Legends") games.

## Lobby

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Lobby") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Lobby")\]

Drafting begins in the **lobby**, where any number of players, at least 1, form a _party_. A party of more than 1 player is also called a _pre-made party_.

### Role selection

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Role selection") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Role selection")\]

In organized/professional play as well as many [matchmade queues](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Queuing_and_Matchmaking "Queuing and Matchmaking"), it is required to select a role that facilitates drafting and gameplay. A team whose players are assigned these roles is called a "**drafted** team".

In matchmade queues where there is no role selection available, roles are either implied or fully decided by the players (e.g. through chat) instead of by matchmaking assigning their roles.

Players choose their primary and secondary roles by clicking on the role buttons at the bottom of the screen: the first dial corresponds to the primary role, and the second dial corresponds to the secondary role. Certain queues may have restrictions for roles in parties larger than 1. In a party size equal to the maximum for that queue, players can only select one role, which is guaranteed to be assigned to them.

Different maps and game modes present different contexts for roles, for example:

-   In the [Normal](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Normal_game "Normal game") and [Ranked Solo/Duo and Flex](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ranked_game "Ranked game") queues on  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift), the selectable roles are ** [Top](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Top_champion)**, ** [Mid](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Middle_champion)**, ** [Bot](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Bottom_champion)** (or _Bot/carry_), ** [Support](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Support_champion)** (or _Bot/support_), ** [Jungle](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Jungle_champion)**, and  [Fill](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Lanes) (with 1 toplaner, 1 midlaner, 1 jungler, 1 botlane carry and 1 support foreseen per match by [matchmaking](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Matchmaking "Matchmaking")).
    -   Despite the implication, the two botlane roles are not class-restricted: "**Bot**" refers to the person who wants to actively earn the major share of the available map resources (bounties from  [minion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion) and  [champion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion) kills) and be the primary damage dealer; whereas "**Support**" refers to the person who will [passively generate](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gold_income "Gold income") their own significant gold while being given a minor share of the available map resources and influencing the game in other ways.
-   In [ARAM](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/ARAM "ARAM"), there is no role selection.
    -   This means that team drafting solely depends on the champions' composition.
-   In  [Nexus Blitz](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Nexus_Blitz), the available roles are **Lane**, **Jungle**, and **Fill** (with 3 laners and 2 junglers foreseen per match by [matchmaking](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Matchmaking "Matchmaking")).
    -   On the mode's current map, this grouping evidently leaves players to decide which lane they will occupy on their own, as only two exist in total: meaning one lane must be occupied by two laners.

"Fill" and "filling" refer to being assigned a role that other players on the team have not selected yet—in other words, taking the highest availability role.

## Champion Selection

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Champion Selection") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Champion Selection")\]

When all players successfully pass the ['Ready' check](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Queuing_and_Matchmaking#Ready_check "Queuing and Matchmaking"), they enter the **champion select** screen. _In [Swiftplay](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swiftplay "Swiftplay") specifically, champion select is determined before players enter the queue, so it is skipped entirely._

### General

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=4&veaction=edit "Edit section: General") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section's source code: General")\]

Upon entering champion select, players are notified of their assigned role and team/side of the map they will play on. The side is randomly decided. Every ally may view other allies' assigned roles.

Opponents' player names and assigned roles are hidden until the game begins.

-   In the [Ranked Solo/Duo](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ranked_game "Ranked game") queue exclusively, allies' player names are additionally hidden (dubbed _Champion Selection anonymity_). Allies' player names are substituted with aliases, from positions 1 through 5 top-to-bottom and in this order, with the names: "_Gromp_, _Murk Wolf_, _Raptor_, _Krug_, _Scuttle Crab_". Duos' names are still visible to each other.

The order that players are initially assigned to can be random (independent of the lobby) or determined in the lobby before champion select, differing by queue. For example:

-   In the [Normal](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Normal_game "Normal game") and [Ranked](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ranked_game "Ranked game") queues on  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift), the order is random.
-   In  [ARAM](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/ARAM), the order is random.
-   In [Clash](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Clash "Clash"), the team captain is always first.
-   In  [Arena](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Arena), the order is 1-to-1 for players that queue together as a team of 2 in the lobby, and random for players that queue alone in a team.
-   In  [Custom](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Custom_game) and tournament games, the order is strictly one-to-one with the order shown in the lobby.

An ally's [summoner spells](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner_spell "Summoner spell") become visible next to their portrait when it is their turn to pick and for the remainder of champion select.

### Picks and Bans

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Picks and Bans") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Picks and Bans")\]

"**Pick**" refers to champions that players choose to play for the match. Gameplay in  [League of Legends](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_of_Legends) revolves around the champion that a player selects.

"**Ban**" refers to champions that players choose to remove from play for the match.

Different queues may have a different paradigm for picks:

-   Whether there is choice freedom or champions are pre-selected
-   Whether there are multiple pick phases
-   Whether all picks take place simultaneously or in turns
-   The moment at which enemy team picks are revealed, which may range from after locking a selection, to after the game has already started
-   The size of the available champion selection pool

As well as a different paradigm for bans:

-   Whether banning is available
-   Whether there are multiple ban phases
-   Whether all bans take place simultaneously or in turns
-   The maximum number of bans that may be issued
-   Which team member issues the bans

Typically, the maximum number of bans allowed is one for each player in the game. This is true for the premier queues and most newer [Featured game modes](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Featured_game_mode "Featured game mode"), but it is not a strict rule for drafting types that include bans.

Picks taking place in turns instead of simultaneously, means that the order of players in champion select from top-to-bottom matters for the drafting process (since one player could pick a champion before another would).

### Declaring Intent - Locking

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=6&veaction=edit "Edit section: Declaring Intent - Locking") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section's source code: Declaring Intent - Locking")\]

When players are allowed to select picks and/or bans freely, they usually follow a process of _declaring intent_ on a selection and then _locking_ it. At any point before and during the player's turn to pick or ban a champion, a drawer with the respective champion pool is available. The ban phase includes all champions that can be played in the game mode. The pick phase includes a subset of those that the player owns, and in some queues, the [free champion rotation](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Free_champion_rotation "Free champion rotation").

Selecting a champion _declares intent_ for picking or banning and announces it to the ally team. Pressing the button at the bottom of the drawer _locks in_ that selection and confirms it. Pick intent selections are displayed in the main (circular) portrait of the player. Ban intent selections are displayed in a separate square portrait next to it.

Banning a champion is entirely optional, but recommended. Attempting to lock in a ban for an ally's pick intent selection opens a prompt, warning the player of this fact and deterring them:

-   `[Player name] has declared intent to play the champion you are about to ban. Do you want to ban anyway?`

_When all players in their turn have locked in their pick or ban, the next turn or phase will ensue automatically._

### Interruption handling

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=7&veaction=edit "Edit section: Interruption handling") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section's source code: Interruption handling")\]

If champion selection is disrupted in any way, these rules will apply:

-   Failing to select a ban will result in the player automatically banning none.
-   Failing to lock in a ban will result in the player automatically banning that selection.
-   Failing to lock in a pick will disband champion selection and incur a [dodge penalty](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Queue_dodging#Penalties "Queue dodging").
-   Exiting the client at any point before the game is confirmed to begin will disband champion selection immediately and also incur a dodge penalty.
-   Player disconnections do not disrupt champion selection by themselves, but through the ways mentioned above.

### Swapping

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=8&veaction=edit "Edit section: Swapping") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=8 "Edit section's source code: Swapping")\]

There are three types of **swaps** (or trades) available in champion select to further facilitate drafting: Pick Order, Champion, and Role swaps.

-   Pick Order swaps are allowed in only certain matchmade modes. They are strictly unavailable in tournament modes, which includes [Clash](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Clash "Clash").
-   Role swaps only apply to certain matchmade queues that require role selection.
-   Champion swaps are allowed in all queues and modes by default.

All swap options are accessed via the "Swap menu" button next to each player's portrait. Selecting one of the options initiates a swap request with that player. Players cannot initiate any swap with someone who has an active swap request with another teammate. If a player declines a swap request, the initiating player cannot initiate another swap request of the same type with them. The Swap menu is unavailable and any active swaps are automatically canceled during the last 5 seconds of champion select, at the end of the finalization phase.

1.  Pick Order swaps may take place before any of the two swapping players have locked in their pick. They last up to 10 seconds. If the pick order swap is accepted, the two players' pick orders are swapped.
    -   All in-progress pick order swaps will be automatically cancelled when there are 5 seconds left within the current turn of pick phase, and will be temporarily disabled until the next turn ensues.
    -   The swap does not alter the assigned name substitutes offered by [Ranked Solo/Duo queue's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ranked_game#Queues "Ranked game") player name anonymity; the names of the swapping players follow their swapping.
2.  Role swaps may take place at any point. They last up to 15 seconds.
    -   In addition to drafting purposes, role swaps exist because of some rules _binding_ players to a certain drafted role. For example, only the player assigned  [Jungle](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Jungle_champion) is allowed to select  [Smite](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Smite) and cannot remove it from their loadout, whereas no other player is allowed to select the summoner spell.
3.  Champion swaps may take place during the Finalization Phase only, after all players have locked in their pick. Both players must own the champion in order to initiate the request. They last up to 30 seconds.

## Draft types

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=9&veaction=edit "Edit section: Draft types") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=9 "Edit section's source code: Draft types")\]

The main differentiator between draft types is their pick paradigms.

Bans may either be blind or shown. Blind bans are only revealed after a ban phase ends and allow the possibility of duplicate bans across teams.

### All Random

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=10&veaction=edit "Edit section: All Random") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=10 "Edit section's source code: All Random")\]

_

"All Random" redirects here. For the Normal game queue on [Howling Abyss](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Howling_Abyss "Howling Abyss"), see [ARAM](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/ARAM "ARAM").

_

"All random pick" ("AR") refers to players not being able to choose a champion for themselves, but being randomly assigned one from the selection pool. Each champion may or may not exist more than once per team, depending on the game mode.

Although the point of this draft type is randomness, truly random drafting cannot always work without severe limitations, and the amount of available champions give a very low chance to play each one—a major share of players enjoy playing a handful of champions, not the entire roster. For this reason, there is a middle-ground solution where a number of champions are offered for players to select and form their team with.

The most common game mode that uses All random drafting is [ARAM](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/ARAM "ARAM") (= All Random All Mid). Refer to [this section of its article](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/ARAM#Champion_Select "ARAM") for its rules around offerings. Other modes that utilize it include [All Random Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/All_Random_Summoner%27s_Rift "All Random Summoner's Rift") and [All Random U.R.F.](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ultra_Rapid_Fire "Ultra Rapid Fire").

### Blind

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=11&veaction=edit "Edit section: Blind") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=11 "Edit section's source code: Blind")\]

"Blind pick" refers to simultaneous ally and enemy selections which are revealed after a pick phase ends. Each champion may exist once per team at most and may not be picked again in any subsequent pick phase.

The Blind draft type is used in [Swiftplay](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Swiftplay "Swiftplay"), [Co-op vs. AI](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Co-op_vs._AI "Co-op vs. AI"), and multiple [Featured game modes](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Featured_game_mode "Featured game mode").

#### Standard Blind

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=12&veaction=edit "Edit section: Standard Blind") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=12 "Edit section's source code: Standard Blind")\]

_(est. 2009)_

This is the simplest form of drafting, and it has existed since the game's original launch.

_The pick phase's time may vary depending on the game mode, is typically anywhere from 30s to 90s._

**Pick Phase**: All players simultaneously pick. Allied pick intent selections cannot be selected by more than one team member.

**Finalization Phase** \[10s\]: Preparatory grace period, before the game begins.

#### Standard Blind with Bans

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=13&veaction=edit "Edit section: Standard Blind with Bans") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=13 "Edit section's source code: Standard Blind with Bans")\]

_The pick phase's time may vary depending on the game mode, is typically anywhere from 30s to 90s._

**Ban Phase** \[25s\]: All players simultaneously issue a ban. Enemy bans are revealed at the end of this phase.

**Pick Phase**: All players simultaneously pick. Allied pick intent selections cannot be selected by more than one team member.

**Finalization Phase** \[10s\]: Preparatory grace period, before the game begins.

#### Blind-in-Series

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=14&veaction=edit "Edit section: Blind-in-Series") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=14 "Edit section's source code: Blind-in-Series")\]

_(est. [V13.24](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V13.24 "V13.24"))_

**Declaration Phase** \[10s\]: Preparatory grace period. Players can declare their pick intent to their team.

**Ban Phase** \[25s\]: All players simultaneously issue a ban. Ban intent selections and locks are shown immediately.

**Pick Phase 1** \[30s\]: The first row of players in each team simultaneously pick.

**Pick Phase 2 onward** \[30s\]: The next in order row of players in each team simultaneously pick. Pick phases ensue until all players in a team have picked a champion.

**Finalization Phase** \[10s\]: Preparatory grace period, before the game begins.

### Draft

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=15&veaction=edit "Edit section: Draft") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=15 "Edit section's source code: Draft")\]

_

"Draft pick" redirects here. For the Normal game queue on [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift "Summoner's Rift"), see [Draft Pick](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Draft_Pick "Draft Pick").

_

"Draft pick" refers to alternating picks which are locked in turns from top-to-bottom. Each champion can only exist once in every match.

Draft is the premier drafting type for  [League of Legends](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_of_Legends) games. As such there exist multiple different styles of it.

#### Blind-Ban Draft

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=16&veaction=edit "Edit section: Blind-Ban Draft") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=16 "Edit section's source code: Blind-Ban Draft")\]

_(est. [V7.11](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V7.11 "V7.11"))_

Introduced by popular demand in [Season 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Season_2017 "Season 2017"). This is used in permanent queues for  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift).[\[1\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-10_ban_launch-1)

Pick order is determined on both teams and one team is randomly designated to have the first pick. During a player's turn to pick, their selections are visible to the enemy team as well.

**Declaration Phase** \[15s\]: Preparatory grace period. Players can declare their pick intent to their team.

**Ban Phase** \[30s\]: All players simultaneously issue a ban. Allied ban intent selections and locks are shown immediately, opponents' bans are not revealed.

**Ban Reveal** \[5s\]: Bans are revealed to all teams. _It is possible for teams to ban the same champion._

**Pick Phase** \[30s for 6 turns\]: Blue team is allowed to make the first pick, then teams alternate and pick two at a time until every player has locked in their pick.

**Finalization Phase** \[30s\]: Preparatory grace period, before the game begins. There is an opportunity to initiate champion swaps.

#### Tournament Draft

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=17&veaction=edit "Edit section: Tournament Draft") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=17 "Edit section's source code: Tournament Draft")\]

Also known as "Split Draft". This is the tournament iteration of [Blind-Ban Draft](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#Blind-ban_Draft), used in [Clash](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Clash "Clash") and professional play.[\[2\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-2)

Blue vs Red

Ban intent selections and locks are visible to both teams. During a player's turn to pick, their selections are visible to the enemy team as well.

**Ban Phase 1** \[30s for 6 turns\]: Blue Team issue the first ban; the team captains alternate turns banning a champion, up to three per team.

**Pick Phase 1** \[30s for 6 turns\]: Blue Team pick one champion, then Red Team pick two champions, then Blue Team pick two champions, then Red Team pick one champion.

**Ban Phase 2** \[30s for 4 turns\]: Red Team issue the first ban; the team captains alternate turns banning a champion, up to two per team.

**Pick Phase 2** \[30s for 4 turns\]: Red Team pick one champion, then Blue Team pick two champions, then Red Team pick one champion.

**Finalization Phase** \[60s\]: Preparatory grace period, before the game begins. There is an opportunity to initiate champion swaps.

#### Team Builder Draft

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=18&veaction=edit "Edit section: Team Builder Draft") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=18 "Edit section's source code: Team Builder Draft")\]

_([V6.1](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V6.1 "V6.1")–[V7.10](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V7.10 "V7.10"))_

This style was designed to distribute draft pick power across the team.[\[3\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-3)

**Declaration Phase** \[15s\]: Preparatory grace period. Players can declare their pick intent to their team.

**Ban Phase** \[30s for 6 turns\]: Blue Team issue the first ban, starting with the third in order player, then each team alternate the next player in order banning a champion, up to 3 per team.

**Pick Phase** \[30s for 6 turns\]: Blue Team pick one champion, then teams alternate and pick two at a time until every player has locked in their pick.

**Finalization Phase** \[30s\]: Preparatory grace period, before the game begins. There is an opportunity to initiate champion swaps.

#### Captain's Draft

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=19&veaction=edit "Edit section: Captain's Draft") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=19 "Edit section's source code: Captain's Draft")\]

_([V1.0.0.96](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.96 "V1.0.0.96")\-[V5.24](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V5.24 "V5.24"))_

This style was also the sole and original iteration of Draft pick, used from [Season One](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Season_One "Season One") until the end of [Season 2015](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Season_2015 "Season 2015") in all premier queues including [ranked](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ranked_game "Ranked game")  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift) and  [Twisted Treeline](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Twisted_Treeline_\(League_of_Legends\)) as well as in professional play.

Ban intent selections and locks are visible to both teams.

**Ban Phase** \[30s for 6 turns\]: Blue Team issue the first ban; the team captains alternate turns banning a champion, up to three per team.

**Pick Phase** \[27s for 6 turns\]: Blue Team pick one champion, then teams alternate and pick two at a time until every player has locked in their pick.

**Finalization Phase** \[30s\]: Preparatory grace period, before the game begins. There is an opportunity to initiate champion swaps.

## Fearless Draft

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=22&veaction=edit "Edit section: Fearless Draft") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=22 "Edit section's source code: Fearless Draft")\]

In tournaments, **fearless draft** or **global ban-pick** is a drafting mode where champions are permanently removed from play after having been picked in the best-of series. There are currently two standard styles of **fearless**, Full (or 'Hard') and Soft.[\[4\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-4)

-   The Full Fearless style disallows teams to pick any champion picked by either team for the remainder of the series.
-   The Soft Fearless style disallows teams to pick any champion picked by their team only for the remainder of the series. Teams are thus allowed to pick already-selected champions by the enemy team for 1 game at most, as any other champion.

## Strategy

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=23&veaction=edit "Edit section: Strategy") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=23 "Edit section's source code: Strategy")\]

-   In Draft pick, the **Blue team** always has "the right to pick first", or more simply "first-pick". In champion select this is displayed at the corner, above the first in order Blue team player.
-   In organized- and professional-level play, where teams are familiar with each other, champions that someone on the other team plays exceptionally well are usually banned. More frequently however, bans are issued to champions that are widely considered to be overpowered/strong in the current meta, or are very popular among the playerbase, and are therefore most likely to be picked by the opposite team.
    -   Teams may also pick an enemy's potential champion away from them, to deter them from the pick.
-   Team champion compositions play a heavy part in drafting successfully. The typical strategy is to follow the standards of [champion classes](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_classes "Champion classes") and the interactions between them, and adapt to both your own team's and the enemy team's composition (if the enemy's picks are visible).
-   [Swaps](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#Swapping) are an effective way of realizing the best possible draft order among players.
    -   In the regular Draft and Tournament Draft styles, swaps are often planned in order to either:
        -   A) Secure high-demand champions early on, which removes the risk of the other team picking the champion away.
        -   B) Delay the pick for a certain role, so that the enemy matchup may be picked and shown first. This increases the odds of [counter-picking](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Terminology#Counter_pick "Terminology"), or conversely lowers the risk of the other team doing so.
-   During Tournament Draft's champion select, it is integral to keep track of the picks and bans that have taken place before each pick phase. For example, in the second ban phase, teams usually ban depending on what roles are left, based on the role that already-picked enemy champions are [commonly](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Terminology#Meta "Terminology") started.

## Trivia

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?section=24&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting?action=edit&section=24 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\]

Draft types

-   "To draft" normally means "to recruit," as in the player being recruited for a certain role in the team in League of Legends.
-   All queues that used Draft pick initially saw three bans per team, for a total of six. In both matchmade and non-matchmade competition, the first-in-order player (the assigned "captain") in the lobby would issue all three for each team. On January 24, 2016, during patch [V6.1](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V6.1 "V6.1"), elements of [Team Builder](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_Builder "Team Builder") were implemented in queues that used draft pick, and picks and bans were distributed in priority across players' pick order: the lower three players in each team in pick order were the ones issuing bans, while the first two players on each team still had the right to pick before most players to secure strong champions. The Ranked 3v3 queue for  [Twisted Treeline](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Twisted_Treeline_\(League_of_Legends\))—and the respective Flex queue in a select few regions for a limited time—that were introduced alongside the removal of Dynamic queue in [V6.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V6.23 "V6.23") also used Draft pick (until the map and all its modes were eventually removed in patch [V9.23](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V9.23 "V9.23")). With the launch of the [Season 2017](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Season_2017 "Season 2017") mid-season of competitive, during patch [V7.11](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V7.11 "V7.11"), Riot found it more compelling and fair to move to a full-ban system: in matchmade queues, each player would simultaneously ban one champion of their preference instead, for a total of 10 bans per game on [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift "Summoner's Rift").[\[1\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-10_ban_launch-1) The 10-ban system of competitive Summoner's Rift games has been in place since then.
    -   Parts of the Draft pick process have been used in many other game modes since its introduction, as the process is preferable and enables a basic level of competitiveness and teamwork for players.
-   Along with the game's rising popularity, it became somewhat necessary for Riot to come up with and present assigned roles themselves, based on a perceived optimal gameplay loop for a mode. The earliest case of this was the alpha test of [Nexus Blitz](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Nexus_Blitz "Nexus Blitz") in patch [V8.16](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V8.16 "V8.16"). Regardless of this enforcement, players are ultimately free to form a strategic composition outside of any presented roles so long as the team is aware and accepting of such changes (_still, this is practically not possible in matchmade games_).
    -   The five presently available roles for  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift) queues had once been decided by players sustaining a meta-game in the earliest versions of the game. More specifically, the team formation of 1/1/1/2 (1 Top laner – 1 Jungler – 1 Mid laner – 2 Bot laners, one being a carry and one a non-carry Support) was assembled during the [Season One](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Season_One "Season One") World Championship[\[5\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-5) and took at least one more season after the event to flourish and become popular for the general playerbase, while professional players adopted it almost immediately following its massive success.
-   The current "[Draft Pick](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Draft_Pick "Draft Pick")" ([Normal game](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Normal_game "Normal game")) queue is named after the Draft pick drafting type. The long-removed "[Blind Pick](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blind_Pick "Blind Pick")" ([Normal game](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Normal_game "Normal game")) queue was named after the Blind pick drafting type.
-   The "fearless" in the [Fearless draft](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#Fearless) format likely stems from an ironic juxtaposition of players' comfort in using familiar champions, with the draft format removing part of that comfort each game. The origin of the term itself is currently unknown; the format was innovated and first developed for Honor of King's KPL Fall 2018, named _Global Ban-Pick_, and it would proceed to spread to other mobile-game (non-PC) tournaments in the years following its inception (and in variations).[\[6\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-6) "Fearless" was popularized through China's 2nd-tier LDL, where it would be first ever implemented in a League of Legends (PC) tournament for their 2022 Summer Split.[\[7\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-7)[\[8\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-8) The format was later also adopted by North America's 2nd-tier NACL for their 2024 tournament year,[\[9\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-9) bringing the format into the West, and Korea's 2nd-tier LCKCL also adopted it within the same year starting with their Summer Season. [Riot Games](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Riot_Games "Riot Games") inaugurated the Fearless format for Split 1 of the 2025 competitive season across all tier 1 leagues.[\[10\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-10) Immediately after the final day of the First Stand tournament, on March 16th 2025, Riot announced the incorporation of Fearless for the rest of the 2025 season due to greatly positive reactions to it from viewers and professionals alike.[\[11\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_drafting#cite_note-11)

Champion selection

-   The "None" selection for no ban is actually a champion labeled "None", which does not appear in any other circumstance. Within the champion catalog, as all other champions do, it has an assigned champion ID of `-1`.
-   A "Random" selection exists in the champion drawer, which gives the player a random champion from their available pool.
-   It is possible for multiple players to be assigned the same role in a full pre-made party, unless selections are restricted before queueing.
-   Champion select quotes will play upon locking in your own ban and pick, and for you only. If your declared champion is banned by the opposite team, the champion's ban quote also plays for you.
-   After locking in a pick, you may select champion [skins](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Skins "Skins"), [chromas](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Chroma "Chroma") and [ward skins](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ward_skins "Ward skins"). The selected skin's [splash art](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Splash_art "Splash art") is shown on screen.
