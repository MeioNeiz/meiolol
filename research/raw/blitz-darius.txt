Darius Top Build Guide

Darius Top is B Tier with a 49.6% win rate, 3.4% pick rate, and 6.9% ban rate across 146,058 Emerald+ matches in the World region on Patch 26.20.

The recommended core item build is Plated Steelcaps → Youmuu's Ghostblade → Dead Man's Plate. Conqueror is the recommended primary rune keystone. Zilean is one of the more difficult matchups.

Review Darius counters, synergies, and abilities for more matchup and champion details.
