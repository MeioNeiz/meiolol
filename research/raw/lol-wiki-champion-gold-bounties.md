# Champion gold bounties

[Jump to navigation](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties#searchInput)

The **champion gold bounty** (often simply **champion bounty**) is the [gold](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gold "Gold") reward that a [champion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion "Champion") dispenses upon being slain by an enemy champion. Unlike other [units](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Units "Units") in League of Legends which have a set gold bounty, the worth of a champion is governed by a multitude of rules.

The champion bounty system is the most obvious negative feedback (_comeback_) system that players interact with, though it is one of many others in the game. The purpose of a negative feedback system can be summarized into two ideals: one, it should adjust according to players' advantage ([snowball](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Snowball "Snowball")) or disadvantage, and two, it should grant opportunities for comebacks to take place without severely punishing the leading players.

## General

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: General") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: General")\]

The slain champion's full gold bounty is granted to the champion who is [credited](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties#Champion_kill_credit) the kill. Being [executed](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#Execution "Kill") by a non-champion does not cause the slain champion's bounty to be dispensed, nor affects the champion's worth in any way.

The default, or **base** gold bounty that all champions dispense is based on their [level](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)"):

-    300 / 300 / 300 / 300 / 300 / 300 / 310 / 320 / 330 / 340 / 350 / 360 / 370 / 380 / 390 / 400 / 410 / 420 gold.
-   First Blood grants  100 **bonus** gold.

This base amount varies based on the champion's performance. A champion who is worth more than the base amount is said to be in a _positive_ state. A champion who is worth less than the base amount is said to be in a _negative_ state.

If a kill has been [assisted](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Assist "Assist"), an **assist bounty** is additionally generated and shared equally among every assistor. Assist bounties are always half of the kill bounty that the slain champion has dispensed, capped at 50% of the **base** amount.

There exists a **buffer** of  100 bounty gold provided upon entering a positive state that will not be counted toward accruing a bounty. This exists to reduce variance in bounties near the base value. Excluding this buffer, any amount equal to \[the **base** bounty +  100 bounty gold \] that a champion has accrued is considered "Shut Down gold" and they have a "Shut Down bounty", which is relevant for various mechanics related to champion bounties. Champions who have accrued a Shut Down bounty display their extra worth on the scoreboard to both allies and enemies (rounded to the nearest multiple of  50). If slain, they will be announced as "**Shut Down**".

## Accrual and Depreciation

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Accrual and Depreciation") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Accrual and Depreciation")\]

Champions' gold bounties accrue and depreciate granularly: in increments of  1.

A champion accrues a bounty via the following rules.

1.  By earning  gold from killing champions or assisting in their killing:
    -   at a rate of  1 bounty gold :  3 gold earned.
2.  By earning  gold from killing  [minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion) and  [monsters](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster), as well as [Support](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Item_group#Support "Item group") item [generation](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gold_income "Gold income")—collectively called "_Gold Value sources_" or "_GV sources_":
    -   at a rate of  1 bounty gold :  20 GV-source gold, while in a neutral or positive state.
    -   at a rate of  1 bounty gold :  7 GV-source gold, while in a negative state.
3.  Any Shut Down gold that a killer earns while in a positive state themselves is disregarded for the purposes of accruing a bounty.

A champion's bounty depreciates at a rate of  1 bounty gold :  3.5 gold distributed to the killer and assistor(s), when they are slain. However, if a champion has a Shut Down bounty, all of it is removed, instead of this depreciation (up to [the maximum](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties#Extended_bounties) per death). The minimum kill bounty a champion can dispense is  50; any depreciation of their gold bounty beyond that point is discarded.

### Extended bounties

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Extended bounties") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Extended bounties")\]

When a champion has accrued a positive bounty that exceeds \[ the **base** bounty +  700 \], they are placed in an _extended bounty_ state. A single kill **cannot** award more than this amount to the killer.

If a champion with an extended bounty is slain, any excess bounty after the depreciation applies (due to their death) is added onto their next respawn instead. This repeats as needed until the champion no longer has an extended bounty.

### Other rules

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?section=4&veaction=edit "Edit section: Other rules") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section's source code: Other rules")\]

-   Assist bounties are reduced to 50% – 100% (based on Game Time) of their normal value early in the game.
-   After **6:00** game time, if a team is not convincingly winning then Shut Down gold is instantly and dynamically reduced by 30% / 60% / 90% / 100% (based on team advantage) near the equilibrium.
    -   The advantage is calculated by considering a combination of _team stats_:  [gold](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gold "Gold"),  [experience](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)"), and map objectives ( [turrets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret) and  [monsters](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster)). Team Gold has the highest weight/importance.
        -   The same calculations are used in determining [Objective bounties](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Objective_bounties "Objective bounties").
    -   Positive bounties will still continue to accrue as normal in the background.
    -   When the equilibrium is broken by one of the teams and they are then considered to have gained an advantage, Shut Down gold is once again instantly restored for that team.
-   While [Objective bounties](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Objective_bounties "Objective bounties") are active, Shut Down gold is fully suppressed for the losing team.
-   On  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift), changes to a champion's gold bounty are deferred until 5 seconds after exiting [combat](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Combat_status "Combat status") with enemy champions.
    -   On the  [Howling Abyss](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Howling_Abyss), bounties can increase at the same time as kills and assists are earned, and will be dispensed even if the champion dies immediately after scoring a multi-kill.
-   Gold-generating [champion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion "Champion"), [item](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Item "Item") and [rune](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rune "Rune") effects **never** count toward champion bounties.

[V4.11](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.11 "V4.11")

-   Early kill bounty modifier now starts at 75% from 60%.
    -   _Note: The 100 gold bonus from First Blood is unaffected by this modifier, so its minimum has been increased to 325 gold from 280._
-   Kill streak multiplier per tier reduced to 16.66% from 20%.
    -   _The maximum bounty of 500 gold is now reached at 5 kills from 4._

[V3.15](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.15 "V3.15")

-   **Removed:** After 20 minutes, assist bonuses earned are no longer increased to 50%–80% of the base kill amount, scaling linearly from 20:00 to 35:00 game time.

[V3.14](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.14 "V3.14")

-   **New:** Before 4 minutes, champion bounties earned are now reduced to 60%—100% of the base kill amount. The value scales linearly from 2:00 to 4:00 game time.
-   **New:** After 20 minutes, assist bonuses earned are now increased to 50%–80% of the base kill amount. The value scales linearly from 20:00 to 35:00 game time.
-   **New - Extra Assist Rewards:** If players have at least 2 more assists than kills, an additional 15 gold for each higher assist than kill they have upon scoring any assist is generated, up to 60 gold for each player.
    -   The amount of bonus gold generated to the team is capped at the half the amount dispensed to the killer.
-   **New:** For every 1000 gold earned from minions and monsters while in a death spree, a champion's tier is now increased by 1.

[V3.9](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.9 "V3.9")

-   Death streak multiplier per tier reduced to 20% from 30%.
-   Minimum champion bounty increased to 16.66% (50 gold) from 5% (15 gold).

[V1.0.0.152](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.152 "V1.0.0.152")

-   Maximum champion bounty reduced to 166.66% (500 gold) from 200% (600 gold).
-   Assist gold bonus reduced to 50% from 58.3% of the base amount.
-   Kill streak multiplier per tier increased to 20% from 10%.
-   Death streak multiplier per tier increased to 30% from 15%.

[V1.0.0.138](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.138 "V1.0.0.138")

-   Maximum champion bounty multiplier increased to 200% (600 gold) from 166.66% (500 gold).

[V1.0.0.123](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.123 "V1.0.0.123")

-   Dying to non-champions no longer resets a kill streak and no longer increases a death streak.
-   Scoring an assist no longer resets a champion's death streak.
    -   Now increases the assistor's tier by 1 instead while in a death streak.

[V1.0.0.120](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.120 "V1.0.0.120")

-   Maximum champion bounty multiplier reduced to 166.66% (500 gold) from 333.33% (1000 gold).
-   Minimum champion bounty reduced to 5% (15 gold) from 30% (90 gold).
-   Kill streak multiplier per tier reduced to 10% from 16.66%.
-   Death streak multiplier per tier increased to 20% from 15%.

[V1.0.0.52](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V1.0.0.52 "V1.0.0.52")

-   Base champion bounty increased to 300 from 250.
    -   Assist gold bonus decreased to 58.3% from 70% of the base gold (no effective change).
    -   Kill streak multiplier per tier decreased to 16.66% from 20% (no effective change).
    -   Minimum champion bounty decreased to 30% from 35% (no effective change).
    -   Maximum champion bounty multiplier decreased to 3.333 from 4 (effectively 999.9 from 1000 gold).

[V0.9.22.16](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V0.9.22.16 "V0.9.22.16")

-   Base champion bounty increased to 250 from 200.
-   Assist gold bonus reduced to 70% from 75%.
-   Maximum champion bounty reduced to 1000 from 1400.
-   Death streak penalty per tier reduced to 15% from 20%.
-   Minimum champion bounty reduced to 35% from 50% of the base gold.
-   **Bug Fix:** Players now equally share assist gold.
-   **Bug Fix:** Towers/Minions no-longer clear the player's bounty.

[V0.9.22.7](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V0.9.22.7 "V0.9.22.7")

-   Kill streak multiplier per tier decreased to 20% from 25%.
-   **Undocumented:** Death streak penalty per death decreased to 20% from 25%.

[June 12, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/June_12,_2009_Patch "June 12, 2009 Patch")

-   Minimum champion bounty increased to 50% from 40% of the base gold.
-   Maximum champion bounty decreased by 12.5%.

[May 15, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_15,_2009_Patch "May 15, 2009 Patch")

-   Gold bounty for assists increased to 75% from 50% of the base gold.

[May 9, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_9,_2009_Patch "May 9, 2009 Patch")

-   **Bug Fix:** Champion bounties are no longer worth less than intended.

[May 1, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/May_1,_2009_Patch "May 1, 2009 Patch")

-   **New:** First Blood is now worth 100 bonus gold.
-   Bounty bonus on killing a champion on a kill streak decreased to 25% from 30% per rank of the kill streak.
-   Bounty cap is now reached at 8 kills from 10 kills.
-   Bounty reduction on killing a champion on a death streak decreased to 25% from 30% per rank of the death streak.
-   Penalty floor increased to 40% from 30% of a champion kill.

[April 25, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/April_25,_2009_Patch "April 25, 2009 Patch")

-   Base champion bounty increased to 200 from 100.
-   Added bounty modifiers:
    -   Champions on a kill streak are worth more.
    -   Champions on a death streak are worth less.

[April 18, 2009 Patch](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/April_18,_2009_Patch "April 18, 2009 Patch")

-   **New:** Added Assists (gold and experience bonuses when assisting in a kill).
    -   If a kill is assisted, an extra 50% of the killed enemy's gold worth is split to all assisting players (excluding the killer).
-   You now receive gold for a unit you get the last champion hit on even if a tower or minion gets the last last hit.
-   New bounty system for champion kills.

[Alpha Week 5](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_5 "Alpha Week 5")

-   Champion kills now give 100 gold at all levels.

[Alpha Week 2](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Alpha_Week_2 "Alpha Week 2")

-   Increased the gold gained from champion kills.
