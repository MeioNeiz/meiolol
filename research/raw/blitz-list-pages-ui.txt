Ornn Top Build Guide

Ornn Top is A Tier with a 51.3% win rate, 2.0% pick rate, and 0.3% ban rate across 7,645 Emerald+ matches in the World region on Patch 26.22.

The recommended core item build is Sunfire Aegis → Plated Steelcaps → Thornmail. Grasp of the Undying is the recommended primary rune keystone. Trundle is one of the more difficult matchups.

Review Ornn counters, synergies, and abilities for more matchup and champion details.

#
Key Insights
During 
W
's cast, Ornn cannot be displaced.
If Ornn collides with terrain during 
E
, he knocks up all enemies around him.
Ornn can buy any non-consumable item outside of his base with 
P
.
E
 breaks player-made terrain.
W
 and both instances of 
R
 apply Brittle.
#
Strengths
A well-positioned 
R
 can singlehandedly win teamfights.
Excels with items that enhance his tankiness, crowd control, and durability.
His gank assist and teamfighting become even stronger after unlocking 
R
.
P
's item upgrades give his team "free gold" and better scaling.
#
Weaknesses
Has obvious and telegraphed animations.
R
 has a longer cooldown of 140 seconds at rank 1. Once used, his strength diminishes in fights.
Very reliant on gold and levels.
While Ornn is Unstoppable during 
W
, he will still be affected by CC afterwards.

Bugfixes & QoL Changes
TFT Standalone Client

Teamfight Tactics moves to its own standalone client starting November 4th, 2026, so this patch is a larger download than usual. Swapping between League of Legends and Teamfight Tactics will take a single click, and all existing Teamfight Tactics cosmetics remain usable in League. The first TFT-only Little Legends arrive on December 1st, with full separation of TFT cosmetics, including emotes and icons, on December 15th. After this patch, TFT downloads can be fully opted out of in League patches. For more information, read more here.

Champions
Aurelion Sol

Most Aurelion Sol players prefer maxing Singularity (E) second over Astral Flight (W) despite it being the weaker second max, so Riot is buffing it to make the intuitive choice the right one.

E - Singularity

Total Magic Damage: 50 / 75 / 100 / 125 / 150 (+60% AP) over 5s ⇒ 50 / 80 / 110 / 140 / 170 (+60 / 65 / 70 / 75 / 80% AP) over 5s

Corki

Corki's power was kept in check going into Worlds, and like 
Azir
 and 
Orianna
, he now has room to be stronger for everyone.

R - Missile Barrage

Damage: 90 / 170 / 250 (+85% bonus AD) ⇒ 100 / 190 / 280 (+90% bonus AD)

Big One Damage: 180 / 340 / 500 (+170% bonus AD) ⇒ 200 / 380 / 560 (+180% bonus AD)

Kayle

Kayle's relevance has steadily declined since the top lane shake up from role quests, so she gets a small early laning buff to add a scaling option to top lane.

Q - Radiant Blast

Cooldown: 12 / 11 / 10 / 9 / 8s ⇒ 10 / 9.5 / 9 / 8.5 / 8s

R - Divine Judgment

Cooldown: 160 / 120 / 80s ⇒ 140 / 110 / 80s

Locke

Locke has proven deeper than expected, and skilled players have shown him to still be too strong, especially in the early game.

Q - Ritual Nails

Stack Bonus Damage: 18 / 26 / 34 / 42 / 50 ⇒ 15 / 22 / 29 / 36 / 43

Maximum Stack Damage: 75.6 / 109.2 / 142.8 / 176.4 / 210 ⇒ 63 / 92.4 / 121.8 / 151.2 / 180.6

W - Soul Ignition

Duration: 6s ⇒ 5s

Maokai

Maokai has been almost entirely stuck in support this year, so his buff targets top lane and jungle.

Q - Bramble Smash

Damage: 75 / 120 / 165 / 210 / 255 (+2 / 2.5 / 3 / 3.5 / 4% Target Maximum Health) (+50% AP) ⇒ 75 / 120 / 165 / 210 / 255 (+2.5 / 3 / 3.5 / 4 / 4.5% Target Maximum Health) (+50% AP)

Mel

Mel gets a midscope update: power moves from early game to scaling, her W is reworked into an area-of-effect missile destruction that retaliates rather than reflects, and her Q and R cooldowns drop later in the game so she can stack her passive higher.

Base Stats

Health: 640 + 99/Level ⇒ 590 + 107/Level

Mana: 480 + 28/Level ⇒ 455 + 38/Level

AR: +5.2/Level ⇒ +5.4/Level

AD: 54 + 3.3/Level ⇒ 52 + 3.3/Level

AS: 2.5%/Level ⇒ 2.75%/Level

Passive - Searing Brilliance

Bonus Damage Per Bolt: 8 - 30 (+4% AP) ⇒ 4 - 30 (+4% AP)

First Stack Damage Against Minions: 50% Less ⇒ 45% Less

Q - Radiant Volley

First Hit Damage: 60 / 85 / 110 / 135 / 160 (+55% AP) ⇒ 60 (+25% AP)

Multi-Hit Damage: 5 / 7 / 9 / 11 / 13 (+5% AP) ⇒ 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 (+8% AP)

Total Damage: 85 - 277 (+80 - 100% AP) ⇒ 85 - 285 (+65 - 97% AP)

New - Now Refunds CD it hits an enemy champion at least once: 0.5s - 1.0s Refund (scaling with Champion Level)

Mana Cost: 70 / 75 / 80 / 85 / 90 ⇒ 60 / 65 / 70 / 75 / 80

Missile Speed: 3800 ⇒ 3200 (takes .047s longer to travel to max range)

W - Rebuttal - Reworked

Unleashes a nova that expands to 400u Radius over 0.75 seconds. The nova destroys enemy projectiles and shields and grants move speed to
