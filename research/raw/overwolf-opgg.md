## About the App

**Note: This is a standalone app. It does not require installing the Overwolf client.**

**Your best play with OP.GG for Desktop** From champion select to post-game review, OP.GG for Desktop supports every step of your match. League of Legends, TFT and VALORANT, all in one app.

**League of Legends**

-   **Ban/Pick Assistant**: switches automatically as the draft goes on. Lane tier list during bans, safe blind picks, counter picks once your lane opponent is locked in, and team comp analysis after picks
-   **Auto Runes, Spells & Items**: lock in your champion and your setup is applied to the client
-   **In-game Overlay**: jungle and inhibitor timers, real-time stats vs. your lane and tier average, item builds, skill order, ARAM: Mayhem augment tiers
-   **AI Agent (Beta)**: a voice game coach with laning tips, real-time danger alerts, fight reviews and build coaching. Just push to talk

**Teamfight Tactics**

-   Meta comps ranked by average placement, 1st place rate and top 4 rate
-   **Copy Team Comp**: send any comp straight to the in-game Team Planner
-   Comp details with positioning, core items and recommended augments
-   **Team Builder**: build with drag and drop, share by code, show it in your overlay
-   **Overlay**: shop highlights, synergy recommendations, augment stats and season reward odds

**VALORANT**

-   Callouts for all 13 maps and agent pick/win rates by map
-   Your profile and today's store in one view
-   Agent lineup videos and crosshair collection
-   **Aim Tracker & Aim Review**: hits, headshot accuracy and counter-strafe mistakes for every shot, reviewed after the match
-   Sensitivity Finder in the Range, agent stats on the loading screen, round reports

**And more**

-   Palworld, Diablo IV and more games
-   Available in 24 languages
-   Toggle the overlay with Shift+Tab and turn each widget on or off
