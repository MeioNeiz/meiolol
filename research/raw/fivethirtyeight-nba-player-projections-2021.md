Wins above replacement projections are based on a combination of regular-season and playoff performances and are scaled to an 82 game regular season. The similarity score is an index measuring how comparable one player is to another, scaled such that a score of zero is average similarity and 100 is the highest possible degree of similarity. Plus-minus is derived from our [Robust Algorithm using Player Tracking and On-off Ratings](https://fivethirtyeight.com/features/introducing-raptor-our-new-metric-for-the-modern-nba/) (RAPTOR), a measure of the number of points per 100 possessions that a player contributed to his team, relative to an average NBA player.
