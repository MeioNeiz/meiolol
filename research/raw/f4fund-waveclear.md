01Company Overview

WaveClear develops real-time AI technology that measures and trains focus, unlocking the mindset behind performance. Starting with gamers, WaveClear analyzes how focus, stress, and emotions affect in-game performance, helping players recognize when their focus drops and guiding them to recover it in real time. Built on academic research and validated in live studies, WaveClear has shown measurable impact: gamers improved skills by 36% in three matches and achieved 20% higher win rates.
