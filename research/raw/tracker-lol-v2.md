## Our new League of Legends site! A tonne of new stats and features!

Trn

League of Legends

All

By Tracker Staff

Published Jul 14, 2022, 05:29 PM GMT+1

Share

**We've completely revamped our League of Legends stats site!**

We heard you! We know our League of Legends site needed a lot of love, so we completely rebuilt it! We've added a tonne more stats and features, all under a beautiful new design.

[Check the site out for yourself](https://tracker.gg/lol), however, here are some previews of what's on offer!

Profile Overview: See everything at a glance

See your matches for each game mode, check your performance via the "activity" heat-map, and compare your win rates and KDA with your different teammates. 

Each match can be expanded to get a more advanced report!

We're also offering advanced stats for [your champions](https://tracker.gg/lol), and providing [global champion insights](https://tracker.gg/lol/insights)!

We've also got you covered for live match stats! When you enter a match, simply [go to your stats page](https://tracker.gg/lol), and a "live match" page will appear.

Live match preview

Last but not least, we've just added a [multi-search](https://tracker.gg/lol/multisearch) feature too! Compare your friends or search players in your lobby!

Multi-search/ Lobby search preview

Want access to all this on a mobile app? You're in luck, download for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=network.tracker.app) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tracker-network/id1287696482) here!

We're serious about making this site perfect, we'll continue to make improvements, if you have any suggestions or issues, please let us know.

That's all for now! Don't forget to [follow us on Twitter](https://twitter.com/trackernetwork) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/trackernetwork/) to get all the latest TRN news, announcements, and data insights.
