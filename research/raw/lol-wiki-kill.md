A **kill** is the event of inflicting the final blow on an enemy unit, reducing their [health](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Health "Health") to zero. This is usually accompanied by the slain unit dispensing an  [experience](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)") and  [gold](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gold "Gold") **bounty** to the killer, their [assistors](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Assist "Assist"), and/or nearby allies, depending on the unit. [Champion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion "Champion") kills increase a team's and a player's [Kill score](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill_to_Death_Ratio "Kill to Death Ratio"), while [minion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion "Minion"), [monster](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster "Monster") and [summon](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_summoned_units "Champion summoned units") kills increase a player's [Creep score](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Creep_score "Creep score"). Turret and epic monster kills are tracked in the scoreboard for each team.

When a champion is slain, they enter the [death state](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death "Death"), where they will _respawn_ at their [fountain](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Fountain "Fountain") after a certain amount of time. If the champion is protected by certain effects (e.g.  [resurrection](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Ways_to_prevent_death_when_reaching_zero_health)), reducing their health to zero does not count as a kill. The first champion kill to happen by a champion in the game is called **First Blood**.

Kills and assists together are called _[takedowns](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Takedown "Takedown")_, which may be scored to trigger various beneficial effects.

## Champion kill credit

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Champion kill credit") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Champion kill credit")\]

Champions uniquely do not need to score the final blow on enemy champions in order to take credit for killing them.

A champion is credited a kill on an enemy champion so long as they have affected them with any of the following valid effects within **15** seconds in  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift) or **20** seconds in the  [Howling Abyss](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Howling_Abyss) – even if a non-champion has actually reduced the enemy's health to zero in the end:

-   Any [damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage "Damage") ([attacks](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Basic_attack "Basic attack"), [abilities](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_ability "Champion ability"), [summoner spells](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner_spell "Summoner spell"), [item effects](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Item#Effects "Item") or [debuffs](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Debuff "Debuff")). [Zero-damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Damage#0_Damage_Instances "Damage") spells such as marking the enemy with  [Zilean's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zilean)  [Time Bomb](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Zilean#Time_Bomb) all count as well.
-   Any form of [crowd control](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crowd_control "Crowd control"), excluding  [blind](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blind) and  [cripple](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Cripple) (none of which need to be accompanied by damage).
    -   The  [slow](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Slow) of  [Mordekaiser's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mordekaiser)  [Realm of Death](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Mordekaiser#Realm_of_Death) does not count as [crowd control](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crowd_control "Crowd control"), but entering the Death Realm does.(bug)
-   The summoner spell  [Exhaust](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Exhaust) (which does not deal damage).
-   A damaging  [turret](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret) basic attack after the turret has been affected by an allied  [Bard's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Bard)  [Tempered Fate](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Bard#Tempered_Fate).
-   Enclosing the enemy within  [Veigar's](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Veigar)  [Event Horizon](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Veigar#Event_Horizon) from the start of the cast time (which does not deal damage).

The timer refreshes with each new application of a valid effect, and kill credit is granted to the last player to have affected the enemy within that timer before the enemy's death. Any other players that have affected the slain enemy with a valid kill-crediting effect, or with a few other [indirect effects](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Assist#Indirect_triggers_of_assist_credit "Assist") are credited an **assist** instead. Assists on champion kills are subject to the same timer from the moment of the enemy's death: if a player has contributed longer ago than the timer, they will not be credited an assist, regardless of how long the combat between the killer and the slain enemy lasted.

### Execution

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Execution") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Execution")\]

If a [minion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion "Minion"), [monster](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster "Monster") or [turret](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret "Turret") inflicts the final blow on a champion who has not been affected by an enemy champion within the timer, the slain enemy is **executed** instead.

An executed champion's [gold bounty](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties "Champion gold bounties") is not dispensed nor affected, and their [kill streak](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#Kill_streak_announcements) is not interrupted.

## Rewards

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?section=3&veaction=edit "Edit section: Rewards") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Rewards")\]

### Experience and gold bounties

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?section=4&veaction=edit "Edit section: Experience and gold bounties") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section's source code: Experience and gold bounties")\]

The primary rewards for killing a unit are  experience and  gold bounties. These are dispensed in different ways by different units and in different modes. For example:

-   In  [Classic](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Classic)  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift), being within a certain range of a minion death will split its experience bounty among nearby allies, but will only grant a share of its experience and its full gold bounty to the player that [last-hits](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Farming "Farming") it regardless of range and death status. In [ARAM](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/ARAM "ARAM"), nearby minion deaths always generate some gold.
-   [Turrets](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Turret "Turret") grant no experience,[\[1\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#cite_note-1) but split a gold bounty to the killer and any recent assistors or nearby allies who are alive, and also grant a smaller amount of gold to the team globally. The  [Rift Herald Mercenary](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rift_Herald "Rift Herald") can be summoned to gain the total amount of gold regardless of range instead.
-   Most jungle [monsters](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monsters "Monsters") grant both their experience and gold bounty to the killer, but epic monsters may be different.
-   [Ward](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ward "Ward") kills grant no experience and only dispense gold to the killer.
-   [Jungle plant](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Jungle_plant "Jungle plant") kills grant no experience nor gold.
-   _And so on._

In terms of killing champions, solo and assisted kills grant different amounts of experience and gold bounties. Both the experience and gold bounties for champions may be increased or decreased based on the advantage or disadvantage between the participant(s) and the slain champion. The experience bounty is dispensed and shared equally among the killer, assistors as well as nearby allies. The killer receives the slain champion's full gold bounty, up to a cap, whereas additional gold is generated if the kill was assisted and is split equally among all assistors.

For further reading on experience bounties from any source, see the [Experience (champion)](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Experience_\(champion\) "Experience (champion)") article. For champion and objective gold bounties, see [Champion gold bounties](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_gold_bounties "Champion gold bounties") and [Objective bounties](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Objective_bounties "Objective bounties").

### Buffs

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?section=5&veaction=edit "Edit section: Buffs") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section's source code: Buffs")\]

All epic monsters and certain large monsters grant powerful [buffs](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Buff "Buff") to the killer and/or the ally team, which may be temporary or permanent. Certain map elements, such as  [Blood Roses](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blood_Rose "Blood Rose"), can also reward permanent buffs for their destruction.

If the slain champion was holding  [Crest of Cinders](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Cinders) and/or  [Crest of Insight](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Crest_of_Insight), they are transferred to the killer and additionally have their duration reset. The transferring will not take place if the buff was earned from a _[Corrupted](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_pit#Corrupted_Rift "Baron pit")_ monster, however.

Execution indicator

An **execute** (sometimes informally "certain death") is the process of killing a unit by dealing  100% of their **current** health, though all relevant executes during regular gameplay only occur if the unit is below a specific amount of health and not at any health amount. **Executes** deal  [true damage](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/True_damage) and usually destroy all  [shields](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Shield) before applying their damage. Most have no effect if the unit is  [invulnerable](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Invulnerability). Units under the effect of a  [minimum health threshold](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Death#Ways_to_prevent_dying_by_not_losing_health) will have their  health set to 1 if they are affected by a regular execute and the effect respects invulnerability.

## Multi-kill

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?section=7&veaction=edit "Edit section: Multi-kill") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section's source code: Multi-kill")\]

A multi-kill is the act of killing several champions within 10 seconds[\[2\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#cite_note-2) of each other, with this window refreshing on scoring a kill.[\[3\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#cite_note-3) After scoring a Quadra Kill, the window for scoring a Pentakill increases to 30 seconds; if an enemy respawns before this time, the window ends prematurely.

Special announcements play for champions who score multi-kills: "`<Player1> has slain <Player2> for a <Multi-kill>!`"

-   Double Kill (2)
-   Triple Kill (3)
-   Quadra Kill (4)
-   Pentakill (5)
-   Hexakill (6), only on the  [Hexakill](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hexakill) mode

Notes

-   The window for a pentakill also has a minimum of 10 seconds, just like any other kill in a multi-kill sequence. This is not currently applicable in most game modes however.
-   A multi-kill of six or more champions outside of  [Hexakill](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hexakill) mode is not announced. Even if a champion manages to kill seven champions within the time window, the seventh kill is not announced as a Double Kill. It would be accurately recorded in the "Largest Multi Kill" in the post-match stats, however. The kill sound effect of a Pentakill is played for every additional kill in the multi-kill after 5.

History

Some footage from before the time  [Hexakill](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hexakill) was introduced showcases the possibility of achieving a _Legendary Kill_, which can occur after killing another enemy after a Pentakill, on any gamemode other than **Hexakill**.[\[4\]](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill#cite_note-4) However, this feature seems to have been removed from the game, and the time at which it occurred is uncertain.

## Ace

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?section=8&veaction=edit "Edit section: Ace") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Kill?action=edit&section=8 "Edit section's source code: Ace")\]

An **ace** is scored by killing the last living champion of the enemy team. Scoring an ace is far easier than scoring a Pentakill, since the only requisite is for the entire allied team to kill the enemy team, rather than one player to kill the entire enemy team. There is no direct bonus associated with an ace, but it is accompanied by a unique announcement that credits the player who scored it.

Both teams can be aced at the same time, leaving no champions alive on the map.
