# 롤도사 (Loldosa) — the League bot for your Discord server

**Every server feature is free. No member cap.** Invite it and it runs from day one.

## What your server gets — free

**Scrim queue board** (`/scrim`) — lives in a channel. Tap your lane; at 10 it splits fair teams by everyone's real rank, with lane matchups.

**Draft / ban-pick** (`/draft`) — inside Discord, no third-party site.

**Server leaderboard** (`/rankchannel`) — sits in a channel and refreshes itself every 12 hours.

**Automatic rank roles** — members get their League rank as a Discord role.

**Match feed** (`/livechannel`) — when a member finishes a game, a card drops in the channel. Cheer, vote on outcomes, leave coaching tips.

**Improvement ladder** (`/improveleague`) — ranked by **who improved most**, not who's highest tier.

**Nightly MVP, win streaks, achievements, daily challenges** — reasons to open the server every day.

**No link required** — any member can drop a summoner name and get a read (`/scout`, `/diagnose`).

> Server admins: run `/setup` once to configure rank roles and the feed channel.

## When someone links their Riot ID

**The moment a game is found** — all 10 players scouted in DM: who's on a smurf, who's on a loss streak, how many games they've queued today, your lane matchup, win probability. Before minions spawn.

**The moment it ends** — a report card. Was the pre-game read right? What actually decided the game?

**Within 20 seconds, a full review** — your key moments on the map, how much gold swung when it broke, and one concrete thing to fix next game. Built from your actual match data, not generic advice.

**Ask anything, mid-game** — it reads your live game and answers for _this_ match:

> "How do I play Zed vs Yasuo?" "Should I force baron or take drake?" "What do I build into 3 tanks?"

## Quick start (30 seconds)

1.  **Invite the bot.**
2.  **DM it your Riot ID** — e.g. `YourName#KR1`. (Or use `/link`.)
3.  **Play a game.** The scouting card arrives on its own.

No dashboard, no setup, no commands to memorize.
