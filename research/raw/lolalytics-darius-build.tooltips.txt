[?] => Champion win rate and win rate delta. The win rate delta is the difference between the win rate using individual player tiers (LoLalytics chosen method) vs the win rate derived from using a game averaged tier (simpler method used by some other websites). LoLalytics data is based upon individual player tiers which includes all games by all players of a given bracket and doesn't include games from players outside the chosen bracket. LoLalytics chosen method provides the most accurate picture of how people of a given tier bracket are performing.
