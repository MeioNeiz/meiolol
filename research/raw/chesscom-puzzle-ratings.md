# How do Puzzle ratings work?

Learn how Puzzle ratings work on Chess.com, how rating points are awarded, and how Puzzle Difficulty affects your rating changes.

August 14, 2026

## Puzzle ratings on Chess.com

Just like in chess, your Puzzle rating adjusts based on the difficulty of each puzzle compared to your current Puzzle rating.

**For example:**

-   If your Puzzle rating is 2000 and you solve a 1000-rated puzzle, you’ll gain only a few points. If you miss it, you may lose more.
    
-   If you solve a puzzle rated 2500, you’ll gain more points. If you miss it, you usually won’t lose as much.
    

Your selected Puzzle Difficulty also affects rating changes. Higher difficulty settings generally let you gain more rating points for correct solutions and lose fewer points for incorrect attempts.

 Using a Hint counts as a failed attempt, just like making an incorrect move. This means you’ll lose puzzle rating if you use a Hint.

## How does Puzzle Difficulty affect rating gains and losses?

Puzzle Difficulty controls how challenging your rated puzzles feel compared to your current Puzzle rating. It also affects the balance between rating gains and losses.

-   **Standard** is the default setting. Puzzles are designed to be challenging but solvable, and most players should be able to solve the majority of them. Standard puzzles are generally easier than those in the previous Puzzle system while still providing a meaningful challenge. Since these puzzles are more manageable, correct solutions usually give smaller rating gains, while incorrect attempts cause comparatively larger losses.
    
-   **Hard** gives you more difficult puzzles than Standard. You’ll generally gain more for correct solutions and lose less for incorrect attempts than you would on Standard.
    
-   **Extra Hard** offers a challenge similar to the previous Puzzle system and is intended for players who want the highest level of difficulty. Rating gains and losses are usually about the same on Extra Hard.
    

If you want tougher puzzles and a more balanced rating experience, try Hard or Extra Hard. If you prefer puzzles that are challenging but more consistently solvable, Standard is a good choice.

You’ll earn [Progression Points](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/9681952-what-are-puzzle-points-on-chess-com) when you use a Hint after making one or more correct moves, but you will lose some Puzzle Rating, just like an incorrect move.

## How are puzzle ratings determined?

When a puzzle is first added, its rating is determined by who is able to solve it. **After a set period, the puzzle’s rating becomes locked and does not change further.** In some cases, if a puzzle’s rating is found to be inaccurate, Chess.com may adjust it to better reflect its difficulty.

**You can make practicing rated puzzles more dynamic and fun by enabling [Puzzle Points](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/9681952-what-are-puzzle-points-on-chess-com).**
