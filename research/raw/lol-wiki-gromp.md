The **Gromp** is a neutral  [monster](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster) camp on  [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift). **Gromp's** [basic attacks](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Basic_attack "Basic attack") uniquely deal  100% AD magic damage.

Killing this camp awards  gold and  experience.

## Notes

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gromp?section=1&veaction=edit "Edit section: Notes") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gromp?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Notes")\]

-   Without slows, the **Gromp** has a movement speed of 330. When its  [patience](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Monster#Patience) runs out its movement speed is increased by 20%, to 396.
-   **Gromp** deals 5% of the target's **current** health **bonus** magic damage with its attacks.
-   **Gromp's** attacks are parried by  [dodge](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Attack_effects#Parrying),  [block](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Attack_effects#Parrying), and  [blind](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blind).

## Trivia

\[[edit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gromp?section=2&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia") | [edit source](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Gromp?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\]

-   **Gromp** is a replacement for the  [Wight](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Wight "Wight") on [Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift "Summoner's Rift") since patch [V4.20](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V4.20 "V4.20").
-   **Gromp's** full name is **Lord Grompulus Kevin Ribbiton of Croaksworth**.
-   Its animations were recycled for the  [Dozermaw](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Odyssey:_Extraction#Map_elements "Odyssey: Extraction") and  [Hogtongue](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Odyssey:_Extraction#Map_elements "Odyssey: Extraction") monsters from  [Odyssey: Extraction](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Odyssey:_Extraction), a [rotating game mode](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Featured_game_mode "Featured game mode"), and  [Nomsy](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Nomsy#Set_7.5) from  [Trainer](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Trainer#Set_7)'s pet trait and [mid-set expansion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dragonlands:_Uncharted_Realms_\(Teamfight_Tactics\) "Dragonlands: Uncharted Realms (Teamfight Tactics)")'s  [Dragonlands](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/TFT:Dragonlands) [champ unit](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Champion_\(Teamfight_Tactics\) "Champion (Teamfight Tactics)") of the  [Teamfight Tactics](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Teamfight_Tactics) game.
