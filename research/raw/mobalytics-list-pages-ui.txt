Top Tier List

Darius takes our Top Lane spot after a buff to his max-rank Q cooldown, giving him more opportunities to deal damage and heal during extended fights. Punish enemies who step too far forward, using W to slow them and landing the outer edge of Q for its heal. In teamfights, build your passive stacks on enemies you can reach, then look for an ultimate kill to start chaining resets.

Jungle Tier List

Cho’Gath keeps our Jungle spot, bringing plenty of crowd control and a frontline that gets harder to kill as he builds Feast stacks. Keep your farm up and look to follow your teammates’ crowd control with Q, then use W to silence enemies as your team closes in. You can also hold these abilities to protect your carries when enemies dive them. Around Dragon and Baron, having Feast available alongside Smite gives you a strong way to secure the objective.

Mid Tier List

Ahri takes our Mid Lane spot, bringing a useful mix of mobility, wave clear, and pick potential. Push your wave with Q before looking to move with your jungler, and use Charm to punish enemies who stray too far from their team. Your ultimate helps you find a better angle, dodge an important spell, or escape when a play goes wrong. Once you get a takedown, use the extra dash to chase another target or reposition safely as the fight continues.

Bot Tier List

Kai’Sa takes our ADC spot thanks to her scaling damage and ability to follow up when teammates catch an enemy. Focus on farming towards your item spikes and ability evolutions, then look to punish isolated targets so more of your Q missiles hit the same enemy. In teamfights, attack whoever you can safely reach and use E to reposition.

Support Tier List

Lulu takes our Support spot because she makes it so difficult to finish off the teammate she’s protecting. Her E shield helps your ADC trade, while her W can boost an ally’s attack speed or polymorph an enemy who gets too close. Keep an eye on whoever poses the biggest threat to your carry, and use your ultimate’s bonus health and knock-up to turn their engage against them.

Laning Against Ornn

Constantly adapt your positioning in lane so Ornn is unable to knock you up with his 
E
. Stand away from walls and his Pillar 
Q
 so he is unable to CC.

Refrain from using any major CC abilities on Ornn when his 
W
 is off cooldown as he can use it to dodge or escape CC.

Once Ornn hits level 6, his kill pressure in lane will increase and he may start getting ganks from his Jungler. Get vision in the river so you can spot the Jungler early.

Strategy VS Ornn

In team fights, avoid grouping too closely as Ornn can use his Ultimate 
R
 to get a multi-person knock up and start the team fight.

Ornn has the ability to knock up enemies that are close to his 
Q
, terrain and walls. Avoid fighting inside the jungle as it will allow him to land an easy knockup.

Ornn will tend to stick in a side lane when no team fights are occurring and when there are no major objectives up. Use the numbers advantage to start a team fight while he is away from his team.

Ornn Power Spikes

Once Ornn unlocks his Ultimate 
R
, he will have an easier time in lane. He will be able to look for kills and even set his Jungler up once he has it available to him. Expect him to play more aggressive post 6.

Every time Ornn gets an item upgrade, he will be much stronger as each unique upgrade offers him extra stats.

Ornn is good in team fights. Try to fight when he’s not there as it will make team fighting much easier. Also, setting him behind in the early game will make him less strong in team fights.

League of Legends Patch Notes Breakdown

Welcome to our patch notes breakdown for League of Legends Season 26.22 (Split 3 for LoL)!

Let’s break down all the new changes in LoL coming this week.

We recommend following along with the official patch notes since we won’t be listing all the specific stat changes, but rather focusing on the impact of the changes and the champions updated place in the current meta.

If you’re wondering how these changes will affect the meta, check out our LoL champion tier list!

Buffs, Nerfs, and Changes




Champion Changes

Aurelion Sol (Buff)

Aurelion Sol’s E damage is increased.
This will help him while trading and pushing the minion wave into the enemy tower.
On patch 26.21, they had a 53.5% win rate.




Corki (Buff)

His Ultimate damage is increased, and his empowered Ult is increased as well.
It is going to make Corki’s damage post-6 stronger, but I doubt it is going to increase his popularity in the Mid Lane.
On patch 26.21, they had a 48.1% win rate.




Kayle (Buff)

Her Q cooldown is reduced. This will help Kayle poke the enemy down or last-hit minions from afar more easily. However, the mana cost isn’t changed, so this buff isn’t ideal.
Her Ultimate cooldown is also reduced, which is good because it allows her to go for more frequent all-ins, especially in the early game.
On patch 26.21, they had a 52.7% win rate.




Locke (Nerf)

Locke’s Q stack bonus damage is reduced, which is going to negatively impact his trading and all-in power.
His W duration is reduced as well.
These changes will reduce Locke's all-in power and make him weaker in team fights and skirmishes throughout the game.
On patch 26.21, they had a 48.1% win rate.




Maokai (Buff)

Maokai’s Q damage is increased.
I think that this change will make him a better Top and Jungler, and we might see him being played in the Support role again soon.
On patch 26.21, they had a 51% win rate.




Mel (Adjustment)

Mel is receiving a mid-scope update.
She is getting a variety of base stat changes, most notably her health and mana are lower early, which is not ideal for Mel players.
Her Passive damage is changed.
Mel's Q is slower, and the damage has changed, making it weaker overall for poking. However, she can use this spell more often with the cooldown and mana cost changes.
Her W is the main ability that is reworked. It now destroys projectiles and shields her and her allies, gives her movement speed and will be her main defensive tool.
For Mel's E, the root duration is changed, the costs are changed, and so is the slow.
Her Ultimate cooldown is shorter at levels 11 and 16.
Let’s face it, Mel was in a bad place in the current LoL meta. Let’s see how these changes pan out- they look to be good overall.
On patch 26.21, they had a 47.6% win rate.




Naafiri (Buff)

Naafiri’s Q healing is increased.
Her W bonus AD ratio is increased as well.
I think these changes will help Naafiri during skirmishes and all-ins in both the Mid and Jungle roles.
On patch 26.21, they had a 50.4% win rate.




Rumble (Buff)

Rumble’s R damage per second AP ratio is increased from 35% to 40%.
This will increase his ultimate’s damage and make Rumble a bigger threat in team fights.
I wouldn’t be surprised if Rumble is picked more often in the Top and Jungle roles in this patch.
On patch 26.21, they had a 46.3% win rate.




Shen (Buff)

Shen’s Q bonus damage against monsters is increased.
This will improve his jungle clear and help him clear camps more efficiently.
On patch 26.21, they had a 50.6% win rate.




Smolder (Buff)

Smolder’s W explosion damage is increased.
This is going to help Smolder poke during the laning phase and also help him push lanes quicker, which will help with early and mid-game wave clears.
On patch 26.21, they had a 52.2% win rate.




Thresh (Nerf)

Thresh’s base AD is reduced, which will negatively impact his lane trading.
His Ultimate damage is reduced as well.
While these changes aren't great for Thresh, they won’t affect his pick or play rate.
On patch 26.21, they had a 51.1% win rate.




Viktor (Buff)

Viktor’s R AP ratio is increased.
His health growth is also increased, which will help him throughout the game, especially against strong AP or AD champions in the mid lane.
On patch 26.21, they had a 49.3% win rate.




Yasuo (Nerf)

Yasuo’s Q total AD ratio is reduced.
This will negatively impact Yasuo in the Top, Mid and ADC roles.
On patch 26.21, they had a 52.1% win rate.




Zaahen (Buff)

Zaahen’s Q cooldown is reduced.
This improves his trading and damage output in the laning phase.
On patch 26.21, they had a 52.1% win rate.




System Changes

Overlord’s Bloodmail

Overlord’s Bloodmail’s AD is increased from 30 to 40, while its Retribution bonus AD scaling is adjusted based on missing health.




Doran’s Bow

Doran’s Bow AD is reduced from 8 to 7.
