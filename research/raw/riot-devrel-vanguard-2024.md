# Vanguard Updates

-   **Feb 7, 2024**

-   [Share this on Reddit](https://reddit.com/submit?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.riotgames.com%2Fen%2FDevRel%2Fvanguard)

Hi folks - We wanted to take a moment to address the growing concerns over Vanguard and how it may impact third-party applications. We’re still figuring out all the details, but here’s where we’re at: 

### WHEN

-   While we don’t have an exact date for Vanguard yet, we expect this to be launched in the first quarter
    
-   It will hit PBE first, and you will have at least two weeks to test things out before Vanguard is live
    
-   We will update this post and announce on our Twitter/X account as soon as we have additional info to share
    

### WHAT TO EXPECT

-   We expect this will only impact around 1% of third-party applications
    
-   If you develop for VALORANT, you can use similar tools and methods for LoL and TFT
    
-   External tools reading memory will not be allowed
    

### QUESTIONS?

-   Reach out to us at [our support site](https://support-developer.riotgames.com/)
    

We’ll be in touch with more updates soon!
