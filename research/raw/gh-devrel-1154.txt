# #1154 Release ARAM Mayhem match data on API without win/loss stats
state: open created: 2026-04-22T06:18:13Z closed: null
author: AMDphreak

It is impossible to review any of my match data via websites like op.gg.

I read that this information is removed to keep people trying new things in Mayhem and to prevent aggregator sites from publishing win/loss statistics to "solve" the meta. I do not care about the win/loss statistics. I just want to see the match history as record for people like me who play ARAM Mayhem almost exclusively, as well as sharing item builds with friends. With my solution, you have zero excuse not to publish match data. Even if aggregator sites publish popularity rankings, this doesn't correlate to win-rate.
