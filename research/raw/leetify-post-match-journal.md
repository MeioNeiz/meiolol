# Post-Match Journal

Do you win more when you warm up before your match? Do you win less when you have toxic teammates? Our new feature does the math on how these out-of-game factors influence your win rate.

Leetify Pro users can track what impacts your win rate the most with the Post-Match Journal. This journal prompts you to answer a few yes/no questions after every match, and then does the math to see which of these have the strongest correlation with you winning and losing. Here's how to use it:

# 1\. Create prompts you want to track

Each journal comes with three default prompts. Feel free to archive them and add your own yes/no prompts using the pen icon.

I 5-stack most of my matches, so I chose to archive the "toxic teammates" default prompt. However, I'm really curious if I win more when I frag out or when I focus on calling strats for my team.

You can add as many prompts as you want to your journal! **Leetify will automatically compute correlations for stats we track, so focus your prompts on out-of-game factors that aren't shown on the Match Report.**

Some more prompt inspiration:

1.  I had caffeine before/during the match
2.  I slept well last night
3.  I was tired from work
4.  I had fun this match
5.  I was listening to music during the match

# 2\. Fill out your journal

You can click yes/no for each journal prompt right from your Match Report. Click the "Journal" icon next to the match score, select an answer for each question, and hit "submit."

If you want an extra nudge to complete the journal, there's the option to have it open right away when you visit the match report.

From there, the "View Results" button will show you what we've figured out about your win rate.

If you want to quickly respond to all of your games from the day, you can do that on your [matches list](https://leetify.com/app/matches/list?ref=leetify.com).

# 3\. See your journal results

We calculate the strength of the correlation for each factor you journal on. You can see those [here](https://leetify.com/app/post-match-journal/auto-prompts-correlations?ref=leetify.com).

For manual responses, it can take dozens or even hundreds of entries to get a strongly resolved correlation. So it's normal to see wide ranges when you're early in the process.

In the example above, for the question "I was hungry," this result says that the correlation algorithm is 93% confident that the true answer lies somewhere between -40% and +1% impact on your win rate. **This means that saying "Yes, I was hungry" about a match means I'm historically more likely to lose the match than win.**

As you continue to answer your journal, this confidence interval will converge closer to a "true" answer. For example, "had a toxic teammate" converged really quickly for me to -46%, **meaning I'm 46% less likely to win when I have a toxic teammate than when I do not.** (wow, who would have thought!)

We also have an [automatic correlations page](https://leetify.com/app/post-match-journal/auto-prompts-correlations?ref=leetify.com) which will show you how stats from your matches impact your win rate. No need to journal on things that Leetify already tracks!

Leetify will automatically calculate correlations from stats we already track

Let us know on [Discord](https://discord.gg/UNygC8BAVg?ref=leetify.com) if there are more stats you'd like to see included on the auto-correlations page.

# Get creative with your prompts

This system can track ANY yes/no question. It doesn't just have to relate to your health or focus:

-   Was playing on WIFI
-   I pet the dog each time I lost a round
-   Had my AK Fire Serpent equipped

If there's no correlation, then it'll eventually converge to a number near 0%, indicating that this factor doesn't impact your win rate.
