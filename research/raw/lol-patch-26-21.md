# League of Legends Patch 26.21 Notes

True Damage has arrived with style! Plus tons to look forward to across SR, Mayhem, Classic, and Arena.

[Game Updates](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/)

Riot wroadie, Riot Cashmiir, sternest

10/20/2026

### 

> Worlds has begun, but time still passes for the rest of us non-professional mortals, so here we’ve got 26.21. This is a pretty chunky patch, with changes to some chunks like Rammus, Sion, Nunu (& Willump), as well as to a bunch of other champions across roles who’ve been on top of the game, either in presence in their role or in power. Others can have a bit more breathing room with the Worlds patch locked in, like Azir, Orianna, and Ryze. Finally, we’ve been seeing Gluttonous Greaves & Hubris overperform on ranged characters in particular, so those are getting nerfed a bit, and maybe Dawncore can work its way into some more builds with buffs.

## Mid-Patch Updates

### October 22, 2026 Updates

#### Varus

> This patch we did work to adjust Varus' top and bottom power skew, which involved shifting the balance between his Q and W for laning. While the resulting changes have seemed promising, we unfortunately added a major bug to his Q during this work, causing it to no longer deal reduced damage to subsequent targets. We are fixing that bug, and compensating with a buff.

-   Passive Bonus AD and AP on Kill/Takedown: (1.1 per 10% AS) as bonus AD and AP ⇒ 1.4 per 10% AS
-   Q Minimum Damage: 50 - 230 ⇒ 50 - 250

## Patch Highlights

Prestige True Damage Know My Name Ekko, True Damage Know My Name Akali, True Damage Know My Name Qiyana, True Damage Know My Name Senna, and True Damage Know My Name Yasuo will be available October 21, 2026.

## Replay Download Limits

> Starting during Patch 26.21, replay downloads will be limited to 10 per hour. You will still be able to download your own replays, as well as replays from your friends and other players.  
>   
> This change is necessary to prevent large-scale abuse of downloaded replays by bad actors using vibecoded apps to scrape thousands upon thousands of replays. We opted for a time-based limit to still allow creators to make content, while preventing mass exploitative downloads. We’ll continue to monitor how many replays are downloaded to ensure players have access to this important feature while preventing clear misuse.

## Champions

### [Azir](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/azir/)

> We've kept the Emperor of the Sands’ mana pool pretty dry to make sure he needs to use soldier attacks properly for lane control early instead of just spamming \[Q\] Conquering Sands, but this is overly restrictive now that there are strong rank up incentives on his \[W\] Arise! We’re buffing his Mana Growth so he can more freely use his abilities to contest lane.

* * *

#### Base Stats

-   **Mana Growth**: 40/Level ⇒ **50/Level**

### [Caitlyn](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/caitlyn/)

> Caitlyn’s been at a very high pick and ban rate for most players for months now despite adjustments to other marksmen and the bot meta, so it's time to nerf her. We have felt that her late-game scaling has been too strong for a while now, so we’re moving some of her power from her eyepopping full build headshots into her early game Q damage to rebalance her power curve.

* * *

#### Passive - Headshot

-   **Damage**: 60 - 100% AD (+1% AD per 1% per Crit Chance) (+1% AD per 1% Bonus Crit Damage) ⇒ **60 - 80% AD (+0.8% AD per 1% per Crit Chance) (+0.8% AD per 1% Bonus Crit Damage)**

* * *

#### Q - Piltover Peacemaker

-   **Base Damage**: 50 / 90 / 130 / 170 / 210 ⇒ **50 / 95 / 140 / 185 / 230**

### [Darius](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/darius/)

> Darius has moved a bit too far away from being a persistently durable damage threat that needs to be kited to more of a one shot combo playstyle. Since he has some room for power, we’re giving him a slight boost so he can swing that axe more often in sustained fights.

* * *

#### Q - Decimate

-   **Cooldown**: 9 / 8 / 7 / 6 / 5s ⇒ **9 / 8 / 7 / 6 / 4s**

### [Jinx](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/jinx/)

> Like her best friend Caitlyn, Jinx has been similarly dominant in the meta, even with the Hurricane nerf last patch. We’re slowing her roll to make it a tad harder to get her snowballing.

* * *

#### Base Stats

### [Lee Sin](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/leesin/)

> Lee Sin has continued to be extremely popular since his adjustments to how his \[W\] Safeguard cooldown works with allies. While we like the extra flexibility that has given him, it's a bit too much power, so we’re nerfing the cooldown a bit along with a minor bug fix to how his E reveal works against invisible enemies and another small nerf to his \[R\] Dragon's Rage damage.

* * *

#### W - Safeguard

-   **Cooldown**: 7s ⇒ **8s**

* * *

#### E - Tempest

-   **Bugfix**: No longer reveals invisible enemies if they stealth after being hit by Tempest.

* * *

#### R - Dragon's Rage

-   **Kick Damage**: 175 / 400 / 625 (+200% bonus AD) ⇒ **150 / 350 / 550 (+200% bonus AD)**
-   **Collision Damage**: 175 / 400 / 625 (+200% bonus AD) (+12 / 15 / 18% Kicked Champion's Health) ⇒ **150 / 350 / 550 (+200% bonus AD) (+12 / 15 / 18% Kicked Champion's Health)**

#### Q - Ritual Nails

### [Taric](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/taric/)

> We're smoothing out some of the rough edges in Taric’s kit to allow him to shine a bit more. Way back in the day, Taric’s W was adjusted to combat the popular Master Yi + Taric funnel strats. A consequence of this was that his spells would fizzle if they were cast at the same time that his bound ally became untargetable. We believe this harsh restriction to how his abilities function is no longer necessary given the existence of Role Quests, so we're partially reverting this change so that spells now work on untargetable allies, though we are still keeping an existing exemption on \[E\] Dazzle for champions that disappear on screen because the gameplay is too unclear (sorry, Master Yi).

* * *

#### Q - Starlight’s Touch

-   **Cast Behavior**: Q casts when allies are untargetable will now successfully fire.

* * *

#### E - Dazzle

-   **Cast Behavior**: E casts when allies are untargetable will now successfully fire.
-   **Tooltip**: Updated to include “Dazzle will not cast from Vanished allies”.

* * *

#### R - Cosmic Radiance

-   **Cast Behavior**: R casts when allies are untargetable will now successfully fire.

As a maxed ability, it also offers more gameplay to Varus and his opponents to interact with than some more passive damage on W.

## Items

### [Bastion Breaker](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/)

> Bastion Breaker was buffed into being a bit of a generic stat stick in order to give it a proper swing in the meta. Now, we’re nerfing that generic power with a QoL improvement to keep it a satisfying item in its niche.

-   **Lethality**: 22 ⇒ **18**
-   **Shaped Charge Damage**: 50 Melee + 1.5/Lethality / 25 Ranged + 0.75/Lethality ⇒ **40 Melee + 1.5/Lethality / 20 Ranged + 0.75/Lethality**
-   **Sabotage Duration**: 90s ⇒ **Infinite**
-   **Sabotage Duration Context**: This is how long you have to use the bonus damage against objectives.

* * *

### [Dawncore](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/)

> Dawncore is the enchanter capstone that isn’t, and currently only Soraka picks it up. Even when other supports get to full build, they often prefer other purchases. We’re buffing its AP value to make it less purely bound to Healing and Shielding.

-   **Ability Power per 100% Base Mana Regeneration**: 10 ⇒ **15**

* * *

### [Gluttonous Greaves](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/)

> The greaves are overperforming for marksmen, even when getting very few stacks, so we’re hitting it with a nerf to its cost to delay early acquisitions from getting too much value.

-   **Total Cost**: 1000g ⇒ **1100g**

* * *

### [Hubris](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/)

> While Hubris has been a bit weak for melee champions, it’s clearly been overperforming for ranged users. We’re delineating between the two now to encourage melees and disincentivize ranged users from building it.

-   **Base Bonus Attack Damage**: 12 ⇒ **15 Melee / 10 Ranged**
-   **Bonus Attack Damage per Stack**: 3 ⇒ **3 Melee / 2 Ranged**

-   Gain 50 Ultimate Ability Haste.

## Classic

> While we announced that Classic will be rotating out in January, that doesn’t mean we’re done bringing back the good old days this year. This patch we’re bringing in four more ghosts from the past (or more, if you count their ultimates). We’ll also have multiple champions dropping in .22 and .23, so we’ve got a few more historic additions before everything’s all said and done.   
>   
> We’re also reverting all of Kassadin’s nerfs this patch, so he’s back to full 90% ban rate power. And Atma’s Impaler is back to 2%, so for all you Metagolem enjoyers, now’s your time!

* * *

#### Champions

  **NEW**   **- Mordekaiser**

> Mordekaiser is back with his kit from 2010-2015! Would you like to eat every single possible minion wave, plus both your and your enemy’s Wraith camps? Mordekaiser is the champion for you. All his abilities cost health, but damaging enemies generates a shield, so you can keep fighting no matter how low you go. And when it’s time to win a fight, your ultimate can turn it into a 6v4. Beat them with their own AD Carry!

#### Passive - Iron Man

-   Mordekaiser converts damage dealt by his abilities into a decaying shield.

#### Q - Mace of Spades

-   Mordekaiser’s next attack damages multiple nearby enemies. If his target is isolated, he deals extra damage to them.

#### W - Creeping Death

-   Mordekaiser buffs an ally with a swarm of shrapnel that grants bonus armor and MR and damages nearby enemies over time.

#### E - Siphon of Destruction

-   Mordekaiser deals magic damage to enemies in a cone. That’s it. That’s the tooltip.

#### R - Children of the Grave

-   Mordekaiser curses an enemy champion, dealing damage over time and stealing their stats. If the target dies while cursed, Mordekaiser raises their spirit as a controllable ghost.

* * *

  **NEW**   **- Yorick**

> Ghostman yannik cannot compete to mojo style master yu, but he can do the babushka trick to make him double the champion and unleash omen of smell and omen of battle. If that didn’t make any sense to you, you missed out on an all-timer of a forum post.   
>   
> Yorick is back to his original version from 2011-2016, summoning ghouls with every ability he casts and cloning or temporarily reviving his teammates. If you want a quick jumpstart to playing Yorick to his frustratingly immovable best, consider maxing Omen of Famine and getting an early Chalice of Harmony so that you’ll never run out of mana as you constantly drain your opponents and heal while doing so.

#### Passive - Unholy Covenant

-   Yorick takes reduced damage and his attacks deal bonus damage for every summon he has active. This counts his Q, W, E, and R.

#### Q - Omen of War

-   Yorick’s next attack deals bonus damage and summons a Spectral Ghoul. Spectral Ghouls have bonus attack damage and movement speed, and give Yorick bonus movement speed while they live.

#### W - Omen of Pestilence

-   Yorick summons a Decaying Ghoul in an explosion that damages and slows all nearby enemies. Decaying Ghouls continue to slow enemies around them.

#### E - Omen of Famine

-   Yorick summons a Ravenous Ghoul, damaging an enemy and healing himself in the process. Ravenous Ghouls heal Yorick every time they attack an enemy.

#### R - Omen of Death

-   Yorick conjured the revenant of an allied champion, who can attack for a portion of their total attack damage. If the ally dies while the revenant is active, they are temporarily revived to fight at full power.

  **NEW**   **- Hecarim**

> Hecarim was released in 2012 and hasn’t changed that much over the years, only undergoing some minor mechanical updates to his Q and W. Overall, he goes fast, dives in, and has strong sustained damage while galloping alongside his foes.

#### Passive - Warpath

-   Hecarim is ghosted and gains Attack Damage based on his bonus Move Speed.

#### Q - Rampage

-   Hecarim cleaves nearby enemies. If he hits an enemy with it, the cooldown of the next cast will be reduced.

#### W - Spirit of Dread

#### E - Devastating Charge

-   Hecarim gains Move Speed and causes his next attack to deal bonus damage and knock the target back.

#### R - Onslaught of Shadows

-   Hecarim charges forward, summoning spectral riders that damage enemies they hit. Hecarim inflicts fear on all nearby enemies when ending his charge.

#### Passive - Lightslinger

## Arena

> Welcome back to another Arena patch! This time around we're making a larger set of changes with a couple of goals.  
>   
> We're looking to lower the power level of some of the strongest augments and increase the power of some of the weakest, as well as reduce the sharpness of some of the "one augment combos" so that the champions who're able to high roll with them require a little bit of extra juice to reach their potential.  
>   
> We're also looking to bring some of the champion outliers in power (both high and low) closer to the center. One of the ways we're doing that is through more non-standard changes that allow players the opportunity to use their champions in different ways, and to take advantage of different augments and items.  
>   
> Overall, we've shifted power in ways that we hope will change up the meta a bit and provide some fresh new build options and experiences.
