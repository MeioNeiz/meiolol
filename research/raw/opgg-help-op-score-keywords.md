# What do the timeline OP Score keywords mean?

Bushy

1 year ago Updated

The timeline OP Score is OP.GG’s scoring system that analyzes in-game performance from multiple perspectives.  
The graph allows tracking of in-game activity over time, and users can select individual champions to compare and analyze performance at a glance.

For Summoner's Rift, the timeline OP Score is calculated every 5 minutes; for ARAM, every 3 minutes. The final score at the end of the game is recorded as the final OP Score.  
Based on the shape of the recorded graph, a total of 14 keywords and their interpretations can be reviewed as follows.

| Keyword | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Unstoppable | Displayed top-level performance and excellent strategy that no one could stop, leading to victory. |
| Leader | Took initiative in victory by making appropriate decisions and strategies according to the situation. |
| Victorious | Achieved victory through strong performance during the early to mid game. |
| Dedication | Efforts and dedication toward victory led to positive results. |
| Late bloomer | Improved gameplay over time and was able to secure a win. |
| Resilience | Demonstrated strong resolve by overcoming difficult situations. |
| Average | Played steadily according to the flow of the game. |
| Rollercoaster | Performance fluctuated up and down like a rollercoaster. |
| Decline | Influence weakened over time. |
| Struggling | Faced difficult situations and was unable to perform as desired. |
| Innocent | Showcased the best possible play from the player. |
| Unlucky | Performed well but was hindered by bad luck. |
| Slow starter | Showed steady growth but lacked enough time to make a significant impact. |
| Unyielding will | Demonstrated perseverance by not giving up despite challenges. |
