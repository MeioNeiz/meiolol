# League of Legends Patch 26.20 Notes

Bee woo woop - the Worlds patch is here! Plenty of pro tuning, more champs return to Classic, and HEARTSTEEL are living their lives.

[Game Updates](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/)10/6/2026

### 

> Bee wee woop - it's time for WORLDS! For Summoner's Rift, this is the patch we'll be seeing our year-defining competitive play on. Lock in, because we've got some adjustments to some pro staples like Ambessa & K'Sante, and buffs to weaker champions who might be on the edge of viability like Kennen & Kindred. Rocketbelt is getting another tap to make it a bit worse at just being a purchasable Flash for immobile characters, with some immediate compensation for some core users because this is the patch and it's the only shot we've got. There's quite a bit more, including fun stuff for Mayhem & Classic, so read on for details!

## Private Replays

> Starting with this patch, you will only be able to access replays from matches you played in. Other players will no longer be able to download your replays, and third-party apps & sites will lose access to replay downloads entirely.  
>   
> We are making this change, alongside some restrictions on third party access to game memory, to close some security vulnerabilities that have recently been exploited by cheat developers and other bad actors to build unauthorized tools around League game data. You can read more about the full context in our [Game Memory Removed for Third Party Apps Player Support article.](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/riot/performance/game-memory-access-removed-for-third-party-apps)  
>   
> Rest assured that this change will not impact your ability to VOD review your own matches by downloading and watching your replays directly in the League client!

## Classic

> Are you ready for MORE new old champions?! Because we are! This time around we've got an eclectic crew who have no business forming a band together. Everyone please welcome Aatrox, Caitlyn, Irelia, Karma, and Quinn to the Fields of Justice.  
>   
> As a note, the Act II Battle Pass will be arriving with Patch 26.21

* * *

#### Champions

  **NEW**   **Aatrox**

> Aatrox was just a baby, 3 patches old, when we chose to snapshot him for League Classic. He only ever had four balance changes before his rework in 2017 and subsequent rework in 2018, and we're excited to see how he'll perform again. He's an attack-focused fighter, damaging himself to gain attack speed and revive when he would be killed

-   **Passive - Blood Well**: Aatrox's basic abilities damage himself to fill his Blood Well, which gives him bonus attack speed. If he's killed, he empties his Blood Well to revive on the spot.
-   **Q - Dark Flight**: Aatrox flies up and crashes down, damaging and knocking up enemies in the area.
-   **W - Blood Thirst / Blood Price**: Toggle between Blood Thirst and Blood Price, each of which have bonus effects every three attacks. Blood Thirst heals Aatrox, increased when he's low on health. Blood Price deals bonus damage and self-damages.
-   **E - Blades of Torment**: Aatrox fires out two projectiles that damage and slow all enemies they touch.
-   **R - Massacre**: Aatrox damages all nearby enemy champions and gains bonus attack speed and attack range for several seconds.

* * *

  **NEW**   **Caitlyn**

> Classic's version of Caitlyn was live from 2011-2015. Did you know when she first came out, some players were upset that she was too similar to Miss Fortune?

-   **Passive - Headshot**: Every few attacks, Caitlyn fires a headshot, dealing bonus damage. Headshot charges up faster while attacking from brush.
-   **Q - Piltover Peacemaker**: Catilyn fires a piercing shot that deals less damage to each subsequent target.
-   **W - Yordle Snap Trap**: Caitlyn puts a trap on the ground that roots and damages the first enemy champion to walk over it. She can have up to 2 traps down at a time.
-   **E - 90 Caliber Net**: Caitlyn fires out a massive net, knocking herself back in the process. The net damages and slows the first enemy it hits.
-   **R - Ace in the Hole**: Caitlyn channels then fires a single bullet at an enemy champion, which deals tons of damage, but can be intercepted by other enemy champions.

* * *

  **NEW**   **Irelia**

> Welcome back the 2010-2018 version of Irelia! The original Trinity Force poster child is here to even the odds no matter how bad they look. She's been Q-max, W-max, and E-max at various points in time, so see what build order works for you! She's a fast but deceptively durable fighter who loves to be the underdog.

-   **Passive - Ionian Fervor**: Irelia gains bonus Tenacity based on how many visible enemy champions are nearby.
-   **Q - Bladesurge**: Irelia dashes to an enemy, dealing damage and applying on-hit effects. If it kills the target, Bladesurge's full cooldown and some of its mana cost are refunded.
-   **W - Hiten Style**: Passively, Irelia restores some health with every attack. While active, her attacks deal bonus true damage and restore even more health.
-   **E - Equilibrium Strike**: Irelia damages and slows a nearby enemy. If they have higher %HP than her, she stuns them instead..
-   **R - Transcendent Blades**: Irelia sequentially fires out four blades in a line, each damaging all enemies they hit and restoring some health to Irelia. This is definitely not just a wave clear tool.

* * *

  **NEW**   **Karma**

> Karma came out just two patches after Caitlyn and was only live for just over two years before being reworked in early 2013. Karma is arguably League's first battlemage, rewarding her for getting in the thick of it and making her stronger the more health she's missing.

-   **Passive - Inner Flame**: Karma gains bonus AP based on her missing health.
-   **Q - Heavenly Wave**: Karma fires out blades in a fan, err, cone in front of her, damaging all enemies it hits. If empowered with Mantra, it also heals all allies (herself included), scaling with their missing health.
-   **W - Spirit Bond**: Karma bonds with another target, giving allies (herself included) move speed, slowing enemies, and creating a tether between the two. Any champion caught in the clothesline is hasted or slowed as if they were the initial target. Enemies who get caught in the clothesline are damaged. If empowered with Mantra, all move speed effects are doubled.
-   **E - Soul Shield**: Karma shields a target. If empowered with Mantra, it also damages all enemies around her target.
-   **R - Mantra**: Mantra is available at level 1 and can hold up to two charges. She can cast it to empower her next basic ability.

* * *

  **NEW**   **Quinn and Valor**

> Quinn and Valor have returned, having only existed in this form for just over two years, from 2013-2015. Quinn is a ranged and evasive auto-attacker, while her ultimate calls in Valor to fight as a blistering-fast melee skirmisher instead.

-   **Passive - Harrier**: Valor periodically marks nearby enemies, whom Quinn can attack to deal bonus damage.
-   **Q - Blinding Assault**: Valor flies out to damage and blind enemies in an area.
-   **W - Heightened Senses**: Passively gives Quinn bonus attack and move speed whenever she triggers Harrier. Activate to briefly reveal a large area around her.
-   **E - Vault**: Quinn dashes to and then vaults off an enemy, damaging, knocking back, and slowing them. Valor then marks the target with Harrier.
-   **R - Tag Team**: Quinn retreats from the battlefield, sending Valor in her place. Valor has substantial bonus move speed, permanently has an empowered version of Heightened Senses' attack speed buff, and casts modified versions of Blinding Assault and Vault. Re-casting (R) casts Skystrike, damaging all nearby enemies and returning Quinn to the battlefield.

* * *

**Fiora**

> Fiora is still the game's most dominant top laner even after her last set of nerfs, so we're back for one more round. We don't want to take down her explosive ultimate any further, so we're just going for a generic damage nerf.

#### W - Riposte

-   **Bonus Attack Damage**: 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 ⇒ **10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30**

* * *

**Graves**

> Graves came out swinging, being both wildly popular and arguably the game's strongest champion. We've got some feel-good changes to his animations, especially his critical strike one, and some less-feel-good nerfs as he desperately needs them. We're looking to preserve his bursty shotgunning and unique aspects as a tanky ADC, so that means we simply have to reduce his sustained damage.

#### Base Stats

-   **Basic Attack and Critical Attack Animations**: Sped up
-   **Base Attack Damage**: 54.1 ⇒ **50**
-   **Attack Damage per Level**: 3.1 ⇒ **2.5**
-   **Attack Speed per Level**: 2.9% ⇒ **2.5%**

#### R - Collateral Damage

-   **Cone Damage**: 140 / 250 / 360 + 120% bonus AD ⇒ **140 / 230 / 320 + 100% bonus AD**
-   **Projectile Damage**: Unchanged

* * *

**Fizz**

> Fizz came out right behind Graves as a super strong performer, but mostly as an AP assassin in mid lane. We want to preserve both his assassin and fighter playstyles, so we're skewing our nerfs toward his higher-AP builds. We're pulling in lighter versions of a couple of nerfs from 2013 and 2015, aimed at bringing his guaranteed burst down to size, since he has so much reliable damage already with Deathfire Grasp and 75% AP ratio Lich Bane.

#### Q - Urchin Strike

-   **Ability Power Ratio**: 60% ⇒ **40%**

#### W - Seastone Trident

-   **Active Ability Power Ratio**: 35% ⇒ **20%**

* * *

**Kennen**

> Kennen hasn't quite reached the same heights as his other ninja compatriots and we're confident he isn't performing as well as he should. AD Kennen needs substantially more help than his other builds, so we're skewing his buffs in ways to help those players out most of all

#### W - Electrical Surge

-   **Passive Damage**: 40 / 50 / 60 / 70 / 80% total AD ⇒ **25 / 40 / 55 / 70 / 85 + 100% bonus AD**

* * *

**Nautilus**

> Unlike his other water-themed friends, Nautilus didn't come out swinging. Sometimes that anchor just weighs him down… Ultimately, he's weak in all three of his main roles (Support, Jungle, Top) and we'd like to help those latter two most of all. So we're fixing one major bug while pulling in buffs and reverting nerfs from early in his lifetime, giving him more reason to soak up experience and wade into the thick of the fight.

#### Passive - Staggering Blow

-   **Bug Fix**: Fixed a bug where the empowered attack was critting for too much damage.

#### W - Titan's Wrath

-   **Bug Fix**: Fixed a bug where the shield wasn't factoring in armor or magic resistance.

-   **Shield Duration**: 9.75 ⇒ **10**
