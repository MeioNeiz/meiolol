Ahri Mid Build Guide

Ahri Mid is A Tier with a 51.5% win rate, 5.3% pick rate, and 1.7% ban rate across 36,335 Emerald+ matches in the World region on Patch 26.21.

The recommended core item build is Malignance → Sorcerer's Shoes → Shadowflame. Electrocute is the recommended primary rune keystone. Gwen is one of the more difficult matchups.

Review Ahri counters, synergies, and abilities for more matchup and champion details.
