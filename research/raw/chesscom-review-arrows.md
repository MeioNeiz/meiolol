# What do the colored arrows in Game Review mean?

Learn what Coach's colored arrows and squares mean in Game Review and how to change or turn them off in settings.

Updated over 2 weeks ago

## Coach arrows in Game Review

During Game Review, your Coach may display colored arrows and highlighted squares on the board to help illustrate the feedback shown in the speech bubble. These visuals are generated automatically by Game Review, rather than drawn by you during a live game.

**Each color helps explain a different idea your Coach is highlighting.** You may see multiple arrows or highlighted squares on the board for a single move.

 Colored arrows in [Move Trainer® lessons in Courses](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/10319151-what-do-the-colored-arrows-and-circles-mean-in-movetrainer) follow a different color guide. This article is only for arrows that appear during [Game Review](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8584089-how-does-game-review-work).

## What the colors mean

Your Coach uses different colors depending on the chess idea being explained. While the arrows and highlighted squares may change from move to move, each color generally represents the following:

Highlighted squares follow the same color system as arrows and mark important squares mentioned in your Coach's explanation.

**Best Move arrows:** If your 'Show Arrows' setting is set to Best Move or Coach's Arrows + Best Move, you may also see a separate green arrow indicating the engine's best move when your played move wasn't the best. This arrow is separate from the Coach color system described above.

## How to change or turn off Coach arrows

You can control which arrows appear on the board during Game Review using the Show Arrows setting. Here are your options:

-   **Coach’s Arrows** — colored Coach arrows and squares only
    
-   **Best Move** — engine best-move arrow only
    
-   **Coach’s Arrows + Best Move** — both
    
-   **Off** — no review arrows on the board
    

## Web

1.  Open Game Review for a finished game.
    
2.  Click the gear icon (Settings) in the top-right corner, just outside the board.
    
3.  In the pop-up, open the Interface tab, then the 'Review' sub-tab.
    
4.  Use the 'Show Arrows' drop-down menu to select your preferred option.
    

## Mobile

1.  Open Game Review for a finished game in the Chess.com app.
    
2.  Tap the gear icon (Settings) on the review screen.
    
3.  Find 'Show Arrows' and choose your preferred option.
