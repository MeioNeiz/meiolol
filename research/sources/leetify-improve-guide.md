# Leetify Guide: How to start improving in CSGO

Why: Leetify's own loop from match data to focus areas to practice.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor guide, Nov 2020 (CS:GO era); the page names may have changed in
  CS2, the loop is the point.
- Reasoning: fair; explains the intended loop and sample size caution.
- Interests: vendor; sells Pro for team features.
- Weigh: describes intent, not proof it works.

## Findings

- [learning] [ux/postgame] The product's core is finding the player's weaknesses from
  their own matches and turning them into a practice routine. ^f1
  > Leetify analyses the actual CSGO matches you play, and uses data from those matches to help identify your weaknesses.
- [ux/postgame] Dashboard: a radar chart of areas (Aim, Utility), then a list of weak and
  strong areas; the advice is to ignore strengths and fix weaknesses. ^f2
  > which includes a radar chart that gives you a high level overview of your weaknesses
  > Pat yourself on the shoulder for the strong areas, but for now focus entirely on the weak areas
- [learning] Training recommendations are picked from the most urgent weaknesses. ^f3
  > These are based on your most urgent weaknesses and aim to help you improve as efficiently as possible.
- [ux/postgame] Each stat has a ? explaining how it is calculated and a How to improve
  section. ^f4
  > don’t miss the “?” that explains what each stat is and how it’s calculated. Also don’t miss the “How to improve” and “View skill details” part for more information.
- [ux/postgame] Map zone tool: where on the map you do well or badly, filterable by side
  and round phase; flash tool clusters your flashes by lineup with effectiveness. ^f5
  > will give you an overview of which positions you’re performing well or poorly in.
- [learning] Progress is checked by comparing a current with a previous period; sample
  size advice: last 30 days. ^f6
  > For the “Aim” and “Utility” pages, the “Current Period” and “Previous Period” statistics make this comparison a breeze
  > **Sample size is key.** The more matches you include in your data, the more accurate it becomes.
- [learning] The loop is meant to repeat: weaknesses become strengths, move down the
  list, re-check every couple of weeks. ^f7
  > **Practice routines need to be constantly updated.** So make sure to check back to Leetify regularly to see what now needs improvement.
- [tools/paid] Pro (2020) added team features: filter by friends, team map zones,
  win rate with each player. ^f8
  > **The “Pro” plan** has additional features that will help you improve your team
