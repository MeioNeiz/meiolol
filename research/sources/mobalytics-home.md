# Mobalytics homepage

Why: the tool that sells itself on a returning improvement loop.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. The live page through real Chrome.
- Reasoning: weak. Marketing; the 27% figure links to their own research.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: the climb claim is self-selected users; not verified.

## Findings

- [ux/layout] Main call to action is downloading the desktop app (an Overwolf installer). ^f1
  > Download desktop app for Windows
- [learning] Pitch is a loop across the whole session: before, during, after and between games. ^f2
  > Our tools work together to guide you every step of the way with features that help you before, during, after, and even between your matches.
- [learning] Between games: analyse your matches to level up strengths and weaknesses (GPI). ^f3
  > Analyze your matches to identify and level up your strengths and weaknesses.
- [ux/trust] (claim) 27% more players climb with Mobalytics, from its own ladder research. ^f4
  > 27%more players climb with Mobalytics relative to players who don’t use Mobalytics.
- [ux/trust] Testimonials name rank gains (Silver IV to Platinum IV) and streamers. ^f5
  > I was Silver IV when I started using Mobalytics, now I’m Platinum IV.
- [market] Claims over 10 million players; multi-game (LoL, TFT, PoE2, Diablo 4 and more). ^f6
  > JOIN OVER 10 MILLION GAMERS RANKING UP EVERYDAY
- [ux/features] Personal match history and GPI pages under LoL, plus a Plus subscription. ^f7
  > Personal Match History
  > Mobalytics Plus
- [ux/trust] (inferred) "Users win more and climb faster" is the uplift wording meiolol must not use; Mobalytics uses it on its homepage. ^f8
  > Mobalytics users win more and climb faster than non-Mobalytics users.
