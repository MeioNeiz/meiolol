# Valorant is going after instalock tools (Player.One, 2026-09-04)

Why: Riot's latest action on automated pick tools, the precedent for auto pick and ban in
League champ select.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Gaming news analysis dated 4 Sep 2026, citing Valorant patch notes 13.05
  and Riot's developer policy. Valorant, not League.
- Reasoning: fair. Separates human instalocking from automation; cites Riot texts.
- Interests: none known.
- Weigh: the penalty fact rests on Riot's patch notes (not read); the "help versus
  decide" test is the writer's framing of Riot's policy, matching
  [[riot-policies-general#^f6]].

## Findings

- [tools/removed] [policy] Valorant 13.05 (Sept 2026): Riot penalises players detected
  using instalock tools; temporary bans first, escalating to permanent. ^f1
  > Riot says it has begun issuing penalties against players detected using instalock tools, with first offenses resulting in temporary bans and repeated violations escalating toward permanent ones.
- [policy] Target is automation of selection, not quick human picks. ^f2
  > An instalock tool automates or accelerates Agent selection beyond normal manual input, removing some or all of the human interaction from the process.
- [policy] [draft] Riot's policy cited: tools should increase decision diversity and not
  remove decisions from players. ^f3
  > Riot's third-party policy specifically states that approved products should increase rather than decrease the diversity of in-game decisions, and shouldn't remove decisions from players entirely.
- [policy] [draft] Riot is moving its anti-cheat boundary into the selection screen. ^f4
  > Patch 13.05 shows Riot is willing to move that boundary earlier, into the menu screen before a match even begins.
- [policy] [draft] Test for selection tools: help the player decide, or decide for them. ^f5
  > does the app help a player make a decision, or does the app make the decision for them?
- [tools/removed] [policy] (inferred) League auto pick, auto ban and auto lock tools
  (Team Advisor still advertises auto pick and ban, [[overwolf-team-advisor]]) sit on
  the "decide for them" side; after Valorant's player bans, an "op" tool should not
  automate picks or bans even for friends, as the penalty falls on the player's account.
  ^f6
