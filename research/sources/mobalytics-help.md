# Mobalytics Help Center: desktop app, account and Plus articles

Why: Mobalytics' account model, supported queues and paid plans in support's own words.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor support articles, updated 2023-06 to 2026-10.
- Reasoning: fair. Short procedural answers.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: some articles are dated (2023 to 2024) but still served.

## Findings

- [tools/accounts] Own Mobalytics account (sign up per game); login in the desktop app runs
  through the default browser (Chrome advised). ^f1
  > To sign up for Mobalytics all you need to do is to choose a game and click on "Sign Up" at the bottom of the login screen.
  > If you are using the Desktop app, make sure Google Chrome is set as your default browser
- [tools/accounts] The game account is detected from the League client login, or typed in
  as summoner name and region; no Riot sign-in link step is described. ^f2
  > Our app automatically detects your account when you log in to League client.
- [tools/phase] Supported LoL queues: Normal Draft, Ranked Solo/Duo, Ranked Flex, ARAM,
  Blind Pick (role stats inaccurate), Arena. ^f3
  > For Normal Blind Pick you will be able to check champion information and import builds, but role stats won’t be accurate.
- [tools] Two builds: an Overwolf app and a standalone app. ^f4
  > If you are using our standalone application (not Overwolf), please try the following:
- [tools/paid] Plus: auto-renewing monthly, 3-month or yearly plans by PayPal or card,
  bought from the web or the desktop app banner; 7-day free trial for all users. ^f5
  > You can choose between three auto-renewing subscriptions: monthly, 3 months, or a year and you can make a purchase using Paypal or a credit card.
  > Yes, there is a 7-day free trial available for all users.
- [tools/paid] New-user promo (Oct 2026): 3 days of free premium, then a discounted year
  with a skin shard or Little Legend egg code; no refund. ^f6
  > you get 3 days of free premium which will expire automatically with no charges afterward unless you decide to buy a discounted 1 year subscription on the third day.
- [policy] (claim) Running the app is not a ToS risk; Mobalytics says it talks to Riot
  regularly. ^f7
  > We talk to Riot regularly to make sure that our platform is compliant with their TOS.
- [tools/accounts] Checked, not there: no article on friends, groups, Discord login or Riot
  Sign On in the search results for those words. ^f8
