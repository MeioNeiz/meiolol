- [learning] Pros script their first 5 to 10 minutes before the game. @0:00 ^f1
  > every pro player pre-plans the details of their first five to 10 minutes of the game before it begins
- [learning] Wave 2 crash plus a ward that lasts to 3:20 covers the crab contest; a wave 3
  crash (cannon) bounces the wave back, fine only when junglers path to opposite
  sides. @3:05 @3:55 ^f2
  > this ward that you place lasts until 3 minutes and 20 on the game clock
- [learning] 2:55 marks the end of the jungler's first clear, the scuttle spawn and the
  best early recall (the next wave is a cannon). @5:30 ^f3
  > 2 minutes and 55, which marks three distinct things, the end of the jungler's first clear, the spawn of the scuttle crabs, and the best early recall timer
- [learning] After the first clear a jungler has 25 to 30 s with no camps up, the most
  likely time to be ganked. Holds in our games: jungler kills peak at 3:00-3:30,
  about twice the 2:30 or 4:30 count ([[first-8-minutes-measured#^f2]]). @7:03 ^f4
  > it's the single most likely and most punishing timers that you can get hit by an early gank
- [learning] 4 to 5 minutes is calm (second clear, no cannons). Holds: the kill rate is at
  its lowest since 2:00 then ([[first-8-minutes-measured#^f1]]). @7:48 ^f5
  > From 4 to 5 minutes, junglers are just doing their second clear.
- [learning] At 5:00 top and mid hit level 6 on the cannon wave, dragon spawns and the
  jungler has a second free window, so mid base-and-TP plus the jungler take dragon.

  by about 8:00 ([[first-8-minutes-measured#^f4]]). @7:48 @9:20 ^f6
  > A jungler's second period of free time between clears overlaps with the spawn of dragon and when a solo laner hits level six at 5 minutes.
