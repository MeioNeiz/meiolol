# Google for Publishers: How Blitz.gg took the gaming industry by storm (2023)

Why: how a big companion app makes money and how big it is.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Self-reported figures in an ad-vendor case study; no audit.
- Reasoning: weak. Promotional for both parties.
- Interests: Google sells Ad Manager; Blitz promotes itself.
- Weigh: trust the monetisation mix (ads matter more than subscriptions); treat user
  counts as order-of-magnitude claims.

## Findings

- [market] (claim) Approaching 10M monthly visitors in 2023; bio claims growth to 3 million daily active users in 3 years. ^f1
  > Adil and his team are well on their way to hitting 10M monthly visitors.
  > Adil helped grow the business from a smaller niche to 3 million daily active users in 3 years.
- [market] Monetisation is ads plus subscriptions (Blitz Pro, Blitz Enterprise); ads drove most revenue growth and fund the free tier. ^f2
  > Their subscription tiers, Blitz Pro and Blitz Enterprise, offer power users and enterprise teams premium features
  > Blitz's revenue has significantly grown with the introduction of digital ads on our site.
  > Ads increase our revenue, which funds our free tier and increases accessibility of Blitz to people who can't afford a subscription.
- [data] [policy] Early Blitz was taken down for nearly three months by API limits, server failures and obfuscated data; it used a waitlist to recover. ^f3
  > After facing API limits, server failures, obfuscated data, and other obstacles Blitz finally went down completely for nearly three months.
- [market] Origin: TSM's Solomid.net, rebranded Blitz.gg; Swift Media (TSM's parent) acquired the Blitz App, an "AI-powered gaming coach". ^f4
  > This new web app, later rebranded to Blitz.gg, aimed to redefine competitive gaming with a revolutionary desktop app.
  > played a pivotal role in helping TSM's parent company Swift Media acquire and run the Blitz App, an AI-powered gaming coach platform.
- [market] (inferred) The incumbents' model is scale plus ads: a newcomer cannot compete on generic stats traffic, so it needs a feature they don't have. ^f5
