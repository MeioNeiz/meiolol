## Findings

- [learning] Kill and XP rewards for champions depend on the advantage or disadvantage
  between the participants and the victim. ^f1
  > Both the experience and gold bounties for champions may be increased or decreased based on the advantage or disadvantage between the participant(s) and the slain champion.
- [learning] An executed champion (killed by minion, turret or monster) gives no gold
  bounty and keeps its streak. ^f2
  > An executed champion's gold bounty is not dispensed nor affected
