# GitHub Terms of Service (2026)

Why: can meiolol use GitHub to store backups or data, and what are the limits?

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. GitHub's own docs, current on 2026-10-11.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: vendor (platform terms).
- Weigh: binding limits and policy wording.

## Findings

- [ops] [privacy] Licence lets GitHub store, archive, parse and copy Your Content,
  including for training AI features and Affiliates' models. ^f1
  > You grant GitHub and our Affiliates the right to store, host, archive, parse, display, and make copies of Your Content as necessary to provide, develop, and improve the Service, including by training AI Features
- [ops] [privacy] Private repositories are treated as confidential with listed exceptions. ^f2
  > We treat the content of private repositories as confidential
- [ops] [privacy] After termination content is deleted within 90 days, though some may
  remain in encrypted backups. ^f3
  > we will delete your full profile and the Content of your repositories within 90 days of cancellation or termination (though some information may remain in encrypted backups)
- [ops] [privacy] (inferred) Personal data pushed to GitHub, even encrypted, sits under a
  broad licence and GitHub's own backups; client-side encryption is the only safe form. ^f4
