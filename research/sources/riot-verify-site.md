Why: exact verification process.

## Findings

- [policy] Text string issued after submitting the production application. ^f1
  > After submitting your production application, you’ll receive a text string to upload to your URL that proves you’re the website owner.
- [policy] Plain text file named riot.txt with nothing but the string, at the site root. ^f2
  > Do not leave any space or additional text before or after the text string.
  > Upload the **riot.txt** file to the root directory of your website.
- [policy] Submit domain in the application window or under your apps; remove file after verification. ^f3
  > Submit your domain for verification. You can do this either in the Application Submission window, or by viewing your apps under your username.
  > Remember to remove or hide the verification file from public access after the verification process is completed
