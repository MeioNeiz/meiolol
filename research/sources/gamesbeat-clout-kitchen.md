# GamesBeat: Clout Kitchen raises $4.45M for Backseat AI (7 Oct 2024)

Why: the first creator-voice live AI coach for League; what it sold, and is it still
alive in 2025-2026?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Trade press with a CEO interview and investor statements; funding and price
  are first-hand. Predates the window; its fate is the 2025-2026 part.
- Reasoning: fair. States the business model plainly.
- Interests: company and investors.
- Weigh: trust funding, price and model. Status in 2026: www.backseat.gg returned HTTP 404
  on 6 Nov 2026 (skip logged); no shutdown notice found in two searches, so "gone or
  moved" is inferred, not confirmed.

## Findings

- [tools] [tools/phase] Backseat AI: a live in-game League buddy, speaking tips in an AI
  voice twin of a creator, built on Riot's in-game API. ^f1
  > Backseat AI is a guide, built to be compliant with Riot Games' in-game API, that gives players helpful tips on how to better play the game, all spoken by an AI voice twin of a content creator.
- [tools/paid] Early access June 2024; full release $4.99 a month per creator "buddy"
  (Tyler1, Emiru, Jankos), aimed at entry to mid-level players. ^f2
  > the full release costs players $4.99 a month for each creator-specific "buddy" they choose
- [market] $4.45M seed led by Peak XV's Surge and a16z Speedrun. ^f3
  > Peak XV's Surge and a16z Speedrun led the round with AppWorks, Antler, Hustle Fund, Founders Launchpad, Orvel Ventures and several creators participating.
- [tools] Besides the voice it had visual overlays with tips and stats. ^f4
  > We also have visual overlays that provide tips, tricks and stats.
- [market] The pitch is creator scale (sell a creator's voice to every fan), with plans
  for World of Warcraft and fitness, i.e. League was a first market, not the end goal. ^f5
  > We're exploring games like World of Warcraft, as well as non-gaming use cases like fitness.
- [market] (inferred) The same creator-voice model is now inside OP.GG (voice store,
  [[opgg-help-desktop#^f14]]) while the standalone pioneer's site is gone, so the
  feature survived and the single-feature startup did not. ^f6
