# OP.GG patch notes 29 Dec 2022: Riot account linking features

Why: when OP.GG adopted RSO linking and what it sold it on.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor release note, Wayback copy (Jan 2025) of the retired helpscoutdocs centre (404 live).
- Reasoning: fair. Explains the use of each feature.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: dates the launch; current list in [[opgg-help-rso]] is the same minus the badge wording.

## Findings

- [tools/accounts] RSO linking launched on OP.GG on 29 Dec 2022, about six weeks after Riot's 12.22 champ select anonymity (Nov 2022). ^f1
  > December 29, 2022 Patch Notes
  > Link your OP.GG account with your Riot account to access the exclusive features.
- [tools/accounts] [ux/trust] A verification badge on linked accounts' profile portrait: the public page shows who has proven ownership. ^f2
  > A verification badge will be featured on the profile portrait of linked accounts.
- [tools/phase] [tools/accounts] Champ select: the verified summoner URL is pitched for sharing your own page with teammates in the lobby. ^f3
  > Sharing your URL is an effective and easy way to share your information with your teammates in the lobby.
- [tools/phase] [learning] Post game: private match memos framed as self-feedback. ^f4
  > Giving yourself feedback could help you improve your skills.
  > Don’t worry, only you can see your memo so try being as honest and straightforward as you can!
- [fun] Vanity: tagline, favourite team logo, champion or skin cover image. ^f5
  > You can customize your profile page with champion and champion skin images.
- [policy] (inferred) The timing suggests OP.GG answered 12.22 with an opt-in: since teammates can no longer be looked up in ranked champ select, a player who wants to be seen pastes a verified link. ^f6
