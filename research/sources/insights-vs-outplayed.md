# Insights.gg blog: Insights Capture vs Outplayed (Oct 2026)

Why: what the two biggest League auto-recorders offer, what is paid, and how Insights left Overwolf.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Vendor blog dated 8 Oct 2026, one side of the comparison; Outplayed facts are checkable against [[overwolf-outplayed]].
- Reasoning: fair. Concedes Outplayed's editor is its standout.
- Interests: vendor (Insights), SEO page for "Outplayed alternative".
- Weigh: trust the feature outline; discount performance claims. The download buttons still point at download.overwolf.com, so "standalone" may still ship through Overwolf's installer.

## Findings

- [tools/paid] Outplayed is free with ads; Outplayed Premium removes them. ^f1
  > Free with ads; Outplayed Premium removes them.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Post game (recorders): Outplayed auto-detects kills, deaths and objectives and saves clips; capture modes are highlights, full match or full session; a built-in editor stitches highlights (Supercut). ^f2
  > Highlights, full match, or full session, with capture settings for resolution, FPS and encoder in the advanced options.
- [tools] Insights Capture V3 runs without Overwolf (claim); free, no ads, no watermark. ^f3
  > one app, no third-party client, fewer background processes competing with your game.
- [tools/phase] [learning] Post game: Insights adds a team VOD review workspace (timestamped comments, drawing on frames, merged team POVs) used by coaches and teams (claim). ^f4
  > Insights.gg includes a VOD review workspace: upload the match, drop timestamped comments, draw on frames, review as a team.
- [tools/accounts] [fun] Shard program pays users in gift card credit while the recorder runs; site has a "Log in". ^f5
  > earn Shards for gift cards while the recorder runs.
- [needs] Recurring Outplayed complaints listed by a competitor: needs Overwolf, ads on free, recording breaking with driver or client updates, no review layer. ^f6
  > driver conflicts, the client needing reinstalls, capture breaking after updates.
- [tools] (inferred) The page's download links still go to download.overwolf.com with an Overwolf extension id, so the standalone build is likely distributed via Overwolf's installer. ^f7
