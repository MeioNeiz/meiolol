does it touch match data?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. The product itself, live since 2022 (per press), ~2M visits a month ([[similarweb-loldle#^f4]]).

## Findings

- [fun] Five daily modes, all about champion identity (attributes, quotes, abilities, emoji, splash crops). ^f1
  > Guess League of Legends champions

- [fun] [draft] A temporary event mode tied to Worlds: build a dream roster and win the cup. ^f2
  > Build your dream roster, win the cup
- [market] Funded by personalised advertising via a consent platform with hundreds of ad partners. ^f3
  > shared with 664 partners or used specifically by this site
- [market] [fun] The same team runs a family of -dle games for other franchises (Pokédle, Dotadle, Smashdle, OnePiecedle, Narutodle): the format scales across fandoms. ^f4
  > Play our other games:
