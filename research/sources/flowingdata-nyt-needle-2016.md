# FlowingData: Showing uncertainty during the live election forecast

Why: an outside visualisation expert's critique of the NYT needle.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Nathan Yau, long-running visualisation blog and books; short opinion.
- Reasoning: fair. Names a cause (the metaphor) but hedges.
- Interests: none known.
- Weigh: opinion; the 25-75 band fact is Aisch's ([[aisch-jittery-gauges-2016#^f1]]).

## Findings

- [ux/uncertainty] [ux/trust] The gauge metaphor misleads: everyday gauges do not
  wobble, so a jittering one reads as broken, not as uncertain. ^f1
  > "If any of those jittered, you’d be confused or assume something broke."
- [ux/trust] (claim) Readers lack a grasp of uncertainty; Yau calls it a data literacy
  problem more than a design one, and wonders whether a short explanation would have
  calmed people. ^f2
  > "I’m curious if a brief explanation of the needle movement would’ve made people less upset."
  > "this is more of a data literacy challenge than it is a visualization one"
