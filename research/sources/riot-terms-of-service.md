# Riot Games Terms of Service (last modified 2024-12-01)

Why: does the player-facing ToS (incorporated into the API Terms as the "Master TOU") say
anything about AI, scraping or third-party programs that affects meiolol or its users?

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Riot's own ToS, last modified 1 Dec 2024.
- Reasoning: weak. Legal list of prohibitions, no rationale.
- Interests: Riot.
- Weigh: binds meiolol's users; "unauthorised" programs is the hinge, and registration
  in the Developer Portal is how an app becomes authorised.

## Findings

- [policy] ToS last modified 1 Dec 2024. ^f1
  > Last Modified: December 1, 2024
- [policy] Banned: unauthorised third-party programs that interact with Riot Services,
  intercept communications, or read game memory. ^f2
  > Using any unauthorized third party programs, including mods, hacks, cheats, scripts, bots, trainers and automation programs that interact with the Riot Services in any way, for any purpose, including any unauthorized third party programs that intercept, emulate, or redirect any communication relating to the Riot Services and any unauthorized third party programs that collect info about the Riot Services by reading areas of memory used by the Riot Services to store info;
- [policy] Banned: content or routines that mine, scrape or expropriate data. ^f3
  > surreptitiously intercept or mine, scrape or expropriate any system, data or personal info
- [policy] Banned: business use of Riot Services without express written consent. ^f4
  > Using the Riot Services for any business purpose without our express written consent or after we’ve asked you to stop using the Riot Services.
- [policy] [data] Checked, not there: no clause on AI or training models on Riot data; the
  only machine-learning mention is Riot's own moderation tooling. ^f5
  > We use both human moderators and technological means, such as automated systems and machine learning tools, to uphold our User Rules.
