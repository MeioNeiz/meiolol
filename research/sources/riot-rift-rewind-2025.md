# Rift Rewind Hackathon (Riot + AWS, 2025)

Why: the most recent Riot-run developer contest for League; shows what Riot's League
product and publishing people want third-party tools to be in 2025.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Official hackathon page on Devpost, run with AWS; 2,631 participants;
  five of ten judges are Riot League staff (product lead, tech lead, insights analyst,
  publishing PM, marketing creative director).
- Reasoning: fair. Clear brief and criteria, no explanation of Riot's motives.
- Interests: AWS co-sponsor (Bedrock push); Riot (marketing, Worlds tickets).
- Weigh: the AWS/genAI requirement is sponsor-driven; the player-facing goals (reflect,
  learn, celebrate, share, beyond op.gg) read as Riot's. Most current first-party signal
  of appreciated tool types.

## Findings

- [learning] [fun] Brief: personalised agent from end-of-game match history to help
  players reflect, learn and celebrate; insights actionable, fun and shareable. ^f1
  > Using the League API’s end-of-game match history data, your mission is to help players reflect, learn, and celebrate through insights that are actionable, fun, and shareable.
- [market] Riot and AWS ran it Sep 15 to Nov 10 2025, $26,000 prizes, 2,631
  participants; third annual Riot/AWS hackathon. ^f2
  > **Sep 15 – Nov 10, 2025**
- [learning] [fun] Wanted tools: persistent strengths/weaknesses, progress over time,
  shareable year-end summaries, social comparisons with friends. ^f3
  > Insights into persistent strengths and weaknesses
  > Visualizations of player progress over time
  > Social comparisons (e.g., how you stack up against friends or which playstyles complement yours)
- [market] [tools] Explicit bar: go beyond op.gg and typical stat tools. ^f4
  > These tools should go beyond what’s available on op.gg
  > Does it bring something new or memorable to the player experience that stands out from typical stat tools?
- [learning] Judging: insight quality for the average player (improve or reflect),
  technical execution, creativity and UX, AWS integration, uniqueness. ^f5
  > Are the takeaways clear, helpful, and relevant for the average League player? Do they actually make it easier to improve or reflect?
- [policy] Judges include League product lead, tech lead, insights analyst, publishing
  PM and marketing creative director from Riot. ^f6
  > _League - Product Lead | Riot Games_
  > _League Publishing - Principal Product Manager | Riot Games_
- [method] Submission requires public open-source repo (MIT or Apache 2.0), public URL,
  demo video and a methodology write-up of the coaching agent. ^f7
  > The repository must be public and open source by including one of the following open source license files:
  > **Methodology Write Up:** Include a brief explanation of how your coaching agent works
- [fun] Side prizes include a "Roast Master 3000" and "Hidden Gem Detector": humour and
  discovery are rewarded. ^f8
  > Roast Master 3000 Prize
  > Hidden Gem Detector Prize
- [data] Data scope is post-game: end-of-game match history over a full year; nothing
  live, in champ select or in game. ^f9
  > **Full-Year Match History** – use this to identify growth areas, uncover persistent habits, and generate an end-of-year recap experience.
- [policy] (inferred) The theme matches the policy line "play first, then analyse and
  reflect later" ([[riot-support-third-party-apps#^f3]]): Riot's 2025 showcase for
  third-party work is post-game reflection plus social sharing, not in-game or draft
  advice. ^f10
