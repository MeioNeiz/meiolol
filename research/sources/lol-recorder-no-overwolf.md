# I made an automatic recorder that doesn't use Overwolf (Ascent, r/leagueoflegends, Mar 2025)

Why: complaints about existing tools (Overwolf, ads, bloat) and demand for recording for
VOD review and sharing.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. 773 upvotes, 98% upvoted, 207 comments; an Mar 2026 update post scored
  872/219 (not read). Recorder library open source.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: the developer's launch; commenters mostly enthusiastic.
- Weigh: the most upvoted comments are about Overwolf, not recording, which makes this a
  good gauge of Overwolf distaste. Not a random sample.

## Findings

- [tools] [needs] Players feel recorders (Outplayed, Medal, Insights) have declined; the
  pitch is lightweight, no ads, no Overwolf, free, with cloud sharing to coaches and
  friends. ^f1
  > If you're like me, you've been frustrated by how apps like Outplayed, Medal, and Insights have steadily gone downhill.
  > No ads, no Overwolf, completely free
- [market] Reception: 773 upvotes, 98% upvoted; top comment upvotes it for leaving
  Overwolf. ^f2
  > upvoted for separating from overwolf.
- [needs] [ux] Overwolf-free is the deciding factor for several commenters; some uninstall
  Overwolf on the spot. ^f3
  > Having something like this not linked to overwolf is what I've always wished for.
  > Not being tied to Overwolf is definitely the deciding factor for me
  > Looks good, just installed and deleted Overwolf
- [tools] [market] Players narrate a cycle of good free tools dying or being
  "enshittified" with ads. ^f4
  > medal.tv (enshittified with ads)
  > outplayed (slowly being enshittified)
- [ux] Bloat and ads named as the norm; a clean simple app stands out. ^f5
  > this is a great reminder of the kind of software we could be having if everything didnt want to be bloatware
  > Ascent is incredible, no bloat, no ads, clean UI
- [learning] [fun] Uses: clips of good plays, VOD review, sharing games with coaches and
  friends; a commenter asks for coaching integration. ^f6
  > automatically records your games, allowing you to make clips, VOD review, and even share your games online.
  > Maybe a coaching integration?!
- [market] Business model asked about repeatedly; answer: optional paid storage tier,
  costs about a cent per user per month. ^f7
  > we have optimized the cloud storage to the point that it is nearly free even in the worst case (~a cent per user per month).
