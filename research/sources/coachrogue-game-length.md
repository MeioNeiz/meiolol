# Coach Rogue: WHY do games ALWAYS last (about) 30 mins in low elo?

Why: does game length fall with rank in our games, and is the post-baron explanation
visible in the data?

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. The rank and length link comes from an on-screen "game time stats"
  chart whose source, sample and patch aren't named; the explanation rests on two
  hand-picked replays (one Grandmaster, one Gold) and a student's game. Season 13 (Sep
  2023), three years and many patches before our data. 20.9k views.
- Reasoning: weak on the statistics (one pair of replays for a population claim), fair
  on the mechanism (death timers near 50 s at 30 minutes do allow a one-lane push).
- Interests: Patreon and coaching links; nothing sold in the video.
- Weigh: the direction holds in our data ([[game-length-by-rank-measured#^f1]]); the
  sizes in the title fit our range; the macro story is untested by the numbers we have.

## Findings

- [data] Outside the top ranks most games last about 30 minutes or a bit longer;
  Challenger games end closer to 25; game time correlates with rank. @0:00 ^f1
  > most games they play last about 30 minutes or a little longer whilst when you look at the match history of a Challenger player they're finishing games much closer to 25 minutes
  > there's a direct correlation between someone's Rank and their game time
- [learning] Why 30: by then death timers are about 50 s, long enough to end the game
  down one lane after winning a fight; high-rank teams use several lanes instead. @0:00 ^f2
  > at around 30 minutes in the game death timers will start to be about 50 seconds long
- [learning] (inferred) The 50 s holds by the wiki: level 15-16 timers are 45 to 47.5 s
  before the game-time factor, about 12% more at 30 minutes
  ([[lol-wiki-death#^f1]], [[lol-wiki-death#^f2]]). ^f3
- [learning] Contrast: a Grandmaster team spreads out after a 20-minute baron and has
  all three lanes pushed a minute later; a Gold team recalls and fights in the river with
  no wave past it. @1:30 ^f4
  > blue team has all three lanes pushed up until their enemy's base
  > not a single Minion wave is even past the river
- [learning] Advice: "wave before everything", skip recalls that buy nothing (Shyvana
  with 700 gold), fill the lane a teammate left, then take the tower or bounce to the
  next lane. @3:50 @5:20 ^f5
  > wave before everything
  > this is the massive benefit that skipping unnecessary recalls gives you
- [data] Checked, not there: no sample size, no source for the chart, no patch; the
  replays are chosen to show the point. ^f6
- [needs] Reception: comments agree from experience that low-rank teams fight instead of
  pushing after baron; none checks the numbers. ^f7
  > Convincing teammate to setup the waves after a Baron is impossible
