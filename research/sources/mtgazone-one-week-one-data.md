# MTG Arena Zone (Sierkovitz): Phyrexia All Will Be One limited data, too fast or just an unsolved format? (Feb 2023)

Why: a measured case of first-days numbers misleading in a known direction, from
another game with public per-game data.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. One set, one metric, figures as images; the author is a known 17Lands
  analyst and checks the claim against the source data himself.
- Reasoning: fair. States two rival explanations and what later data would show for
  each; admits small samples.
- Interests: writes for a guides site.
- Weigh: the direction and the one number; not a general settle time.

## Findings

- [data] [method/dynamics] The first published numbers for the new set came from a
  different population (pre-release early access, free play, streamers), not from
  normal games. ^f1
  > "Comparing normal games with EA is not a fair comparison."
- [method/dynamics] One day of normal games moved the headline number about half way
  back: first-player win rate 56% to under 53%, game length 8.1 to 8.4 turns. ^f2
  > "the colossal on play advantage shrunk by a half, from 56-44 to just under 53-47"
- [method/dynamics] His working explanation for the rest is unrefined play, which
  predicts a drift toward older sets' pattern as decks improve; the rival (a true
  feature of the set) predicts none. ^f3
  > "over time we should see the points linked to ONE on the graph moving up, closer to the trend line, as decks become more streamlined and competitive"
- [method/dynamics] Comparing a new period's first day with old periods' full-life
  averages is itself a bias he flags. ^f4
  > "data for all other formats on those graphs are taken from the end of the format, and here we have ONE data after just one day"
- [method/dynamics] Checked, not there: per-card win rates by day, how long the drift
  took, a rule for when early data is safe. ^f5
- [method/dynamics] (inferred) For us: the analogue of early access is the patch's
  first hours, when 75-89% of players have not yet played it
  ([[patch-day-discovery-measured]]); a day-0 number should be compared with the same
  players' or the same hours' numbers on the old patch, not the old patch's whole-life
  rate. Our TS4 check found no such curve for win rates on 16.21. ^f6
