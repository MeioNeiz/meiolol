## Findings

- [learning] Behind, you can't contest mid waves on arrival, so catch them only at your tower and spend the time between hiding in the jungle to test the enemy. @3:04 ^f1
  > You either catch a wave as soon as it arrives so that you can utilize wave towave macro or you catch it when it goes into your tower at the last possible second
- [learning] A pick on a support puts the enemy out of action for almost two minutes with death timer and walk back. @6:20 ^f2
  > a kill like this puts an enemy out of action for almost two minutes
- [learning] (claim) A lead's relative value shrinks as the game goes on, even before the leading team has six items. @6:20 ^f3
  > the relative value of a lead diminishes as the game goes on, even before a team maxes out on six items
- [learning] XP to level up: 280 at level 1, 1,880 at level 17, so a constant XP gap is fewer levels later. @6:20 @7:06 ^f4
  > At level one, you only need 280 XP for a level up
  > at level 17, you need 1,880 XP
- [learning] (claim) If the leading team doesn't extend its lead, the behind team slowly catches up; don't force fights. @8:43 ^f5
  > You will slowly catch back up to your opponent if they don't extend their lead over you
- [learning] Ashe gains 78 armour from levels, over 50% physical damage reduction late, so armour penetration items matter for catching up. @7:06 @7:56 ^f6
  > a generic ADC champion like Ash gains 78 armor through level ups alone
- [learning] Behind: ward off the usual sweeper and control ward spots; buy blue trinkets when losing, sweepers when winning, as one pro team did. @10:36 ^f7
  > when you're winning, you want more sweeping lens trinkets and when you're losing, you want more blue trinkets
- [learning] (claim) Players surrender too quickly. @0:00 ^f8
  > people just give up and surrender games often way too quickly
