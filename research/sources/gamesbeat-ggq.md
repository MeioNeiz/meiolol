# GamesBeat: GGQ, a personalised AI game assistant for League (Jul 2025)

Why: which AI coach startups entered League in 2025-2026, what they do, and at what scale.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. A founder interview; every number (users, latency, survey figures) is the
  CEO's. The date is taken from a date line on the page (14 Jul 2025) and fits the post
  id sitting just before Xbox's 6 Aug 2025 Copilot story.
- Reasoning: weak. Product pitch; no word on how in-game feedback is produced or on Riot
  rules.
- Interests: vendor and its marketing partner Intel.
- Weigh: take the existence, positioning, funding and markets as reported; treat user
  counts as claims.

## Findings

- [tools] [learning] GGQ (Seoul, founded 2019) sells an "AI Mate" for League: an always-on
  AI companion that coaches strategy and focus. ^f1
  > GGQ said its gaming-informed AI can help you understand strategy, pivot when you need to, and tell you what your focus should be.
- [market] (claim) 700,000 to 800,000 monthly active players and 2.5 million total users
  since announcing in June. ^f2
  > the company now has 700,000 to 800,000 monthly active players (and 2.5 million total users)
- [tools/phase] It works before, during and after the game: pre-game strategy, real-time
  guidance in game, feedback and highlight videos after. ^f3
  > Our AI companion goes inside in the game and give real-time guidance or real-time feedback during gameplay and after the game.
- [tools/paid] [market] Paid version launched in South Korea in 2025; NA and Europe launch
  that week with Intel as marketing partner; 17 staff, $10 million raised. ^f4
  > The company has 17 people and it has raised $10 million to date. The company launched a paid version in South Korea this year and is launching now in North America and Europe
- [tools] Captures gameplay events on the device (CPU/NPU) rather than only from the
  cloud. ^f5
  > GGQ can capture more than 180,000 gameplay events in a match. It uses real-time processing directly on CPU/NPU
- [tools] [fun] Pitch goes past coaching to emotional support and companionship, even "an
  AI girlfriend who knows my game". ^f6
  > If someone wants an AI girlfriend who knows my game, you can choose that.
- [market] The CEO dates the AI companion boom to Microsoft and Razer showing plans at
  GDC (Mar 2025). ^f7
  > The AI companions for gamers are booming right now because Microsoft and Razer showed up at GDC saying they had plans for AI companions.
- [policy] Checked, not there: no price, no word on Riot approval, API key, Vanguard or
  the in-game help rules (Riot's Aug 2025 cut on decision prompts came weeks later,
  [[overwolf-aug-2025-riot-guidelines#^f3]]). ^f8
- [market] Expansion planned to TFT and Valorant in 2025, with LG monitor software
  pre-install talks for Valorant. ^f9
  > GGQ plans to expand to Teamfight Tactics (TFT) and Valorant later this year.
