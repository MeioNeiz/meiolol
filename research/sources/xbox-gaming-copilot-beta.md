# Xbox Wire: Gaming Copilot (Beta) rolls out on Game Bar (6 Aug 2025)

Why: the platform owners' AI coaches are the biggest new entrants; does Windows itself
now ship a live in-game assistant that works in League?

## Assessment

- Evidence: A for what Microsoft shipped and when (its own announcement); nothing here on
  quality or League specifically.
- Reasoning: fair. Clear on scope and limits (Insiders, 18+, English, regions).
- Interests: vendor launch post.
- Weigh: trust dates and scope. Coverage in search snippets says a public beta followed on
  19 Sep 2025 (not read). Riot's view on it: none found.

## Findings

- [tools] [learning] 6 Aug 2025: Gaming Copilot (Beta) came to Game Bar on Windows PC for
  Xbox Insiders in the PC Gaming Preview, after an earlier test in the Xbox mobile app. ^f1
  > Gaming Copilot (Beta), the ultimate gaming sidekick, is available on Game Bar for Windows PC for Xbox Insiders enrolled in the PC Gaming Preview beginning today.
- [tools/phase] Voice Mode works while playing any PC game, so it is a live in-game
  assistant, not tied to a game's API. ^f2
  > use Copilot in Voice Mode while you're actively playing any game on PC
- [tools] [privacy] It reads screenshots of the gameplay to answer, a screen-reading route
  that needs no game data access (and so no Riot API key or memory read). ^f3
  > it can now use screenshots of your gameplay to provide you with more helpful responses
- [tools] Proactive coaching is promised, not shipped, at this stage. ^f4
  > richer game assistance such as proactive coaching, and many more features will be coming to Gaming Copilot in the future
- [tools] Scope at launch: English, Insiders aged 18 and over, the US, Australia, New
  Zealand, Japan, Singapore and other listed regions. ^f5
  > available in English for Xbox Insiders aged 18 and older in the U.S., Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, and other regions
- [policy] Checked, not there: no mention of League, Riot, anti-cheat or competitive
  integrity. ^f6
- [policy] (inferred) A general screen-reading assistant built into Windows sits outside
  Riot's developer policies (no key to revoke), so Riot's levers against it would be its
  game rules or Vanguard, not DevRel. ^f7
