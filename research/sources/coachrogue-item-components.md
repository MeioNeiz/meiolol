# Coach Rogue: What Item Components Should You Buy First?

Why: what a coach says about first-back purchases and early boots, the part of a build
our timelines see (purchase order, lead at purchase) and stats pages mostly don't show.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Component stat arithmetic (Long Swords vs Tunneler vs Caulfield's for
  1,150 gold), the LoL Wiki gold efficiency table (linked) and two replay clips (a
  ranged vs melee top lane with and without boots, a bot lane two kills up rushing
  boots). No game data or win rates. 68k views, 960 likes, three weeks old.
- Reasoning: fair. The rules are explained, but a top comment (106 likes) says the
  three Long Swords advice is wrong for jungle clear (the pet scales off health and
  armour, so Tunneler clears faster), and the Tiamat dismissal is disputed (waveclear,
  proxy farming). Gold efficiency ignores passives, which he admits.
- Interests: two minutes selling Coach Rogue Academy (paid Discord, about 60 members)
  with a coaching giveaway; Waveclear code in the description.
- Weigh: trust the arithmetic; the boots rules are testable in our timelines (boots
  before first item, by lane range gap and gold lead at purchase). Captions: "Varys"
  is a ranged top laner (likely Vayne or Varus), "T-MAT" Tiamat, "Cfield's" Caulfield's,
  "Kindle gem" Kindlegem, "Jyn" Jhin.

## Findings

- [learning] [ux/features] Says the shop's auto-buy (right click a completed item) mostly buys the wrong components; it always buys Kindlegem first on the three main support items, which in lane gives a third of a Ruby Crystal's health per gold. @1:51 @2:37 ^f1
  > it more often than not buys the wrong thing
  > You spend the same amount of gold, but only receive a third of the health for it
- [learning] Ability haste is a weak stat in lane (spells aren't spammed off cooldown) and a better one in teamfights, so Kindlegem early on a third item, Ruby Crystal and Cloth Armor on a first. @2:37 ^f2
  > in the early laning phase, you don't want ability haste as a stat because you're never going to be spamming your spells off cool down in lane
- [learning] "Core pillar of stats": first buy what your abilities scale off; for AD junglers (Lee Sin, Jarvan, Vi) 1,150 gold as three Long Swords gives 30 AD against Tunneler's 15, which auto-buy picks. @3:22 @4:57 ^f3
  > So TLDDR in the early game first by the stats that your champion's abilities scale off since they have the biggest overall effect
  > The auto buy feature buys you tunneler, giving you plus 15 attack damage instead of my suggested plus 30
- [learning] Complementary stats: a kit with lots of health makes armour and MR components worth more; built-in attack speed makes AD components worth more. @6:34 ^f4
  > If your champion already gains a huge influx of health from its kit, consider armor and magic resist components to be more valuable than health
- [learning] Rush boots in matchups with a range gap: a ranged top laner with tier 2 boots kites a melee one who bought stats, and the reverse when the melee champion rushes boots; melee base stats act like a gold lead. @7:10 ^f5
  > rushing boots early becomes significantly more important in matchups with a range disparity
- [learning] Rush boots when already ahead (a bot lane three kills up both buy tier 2 boots to force fights they win); never when behind, and not between two melee supports with even base stats, as boots delay the first item spike. @7:58 @8:53 @9:39 ^f6
  > So rushing boots early when ahead is good since it allows you to gap close and create fights that you already know you win
  > the worst time to rush early boots is when both champions in the lane have very similar ranges
  > don't light money on fire by rushing tier 2 boots when you're losing
- [learning] Gold efficiency from the wiki: Tiamat is 73% efficient, so a top laner building Stridebreaker might buy three Long Swords instead; Refillable Potion is over 355% against Ruby Crystal. @11:13 ^f7
  > T-MAT, an item component for a lot of Bruiser items, has an incredibly low gold efficiency of just 73%
  > an absolutely absurd stat efficiency of over 355%
- [learning] Top comment (106 likes) disputes the jungle advice: Tunneler clears faster than three Long Swords as the jungle pet scales with health and armour; resistances usually beat health in lane. ^f8
  > Most junglers, including the ones you mentioned, clear faster with tunneler compared to 3 long swords
- [needs] Comments: guides say which item to build but not component order; tank players itemise against the wrong damage type; anti-heal components are worth buying early. ^f9
  > I see a lot of Guedes telling you which item to build, but never specify the order of components and how much that affects the build
  > building only armor into mixed damage champs like Ornn or Yorick
