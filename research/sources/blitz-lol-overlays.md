## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor's own catalogue, live Oct 2026.
- Reasoning: fair. One line per overlay.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: trust as what Blitz advertises; not checked in the app.

## Findings

- [tools/phase] Loading screen: scouting of allies, enemies and matchups (listed as an
  overlay). ^f1
  > Get key insights into allies, enemies, champion matchups and more.
- [tools/phase] In game: benchmarking of key metrics in real time, item value difference
  between players, recommended skill order from the chosen Blitz build. ^f2
  > Track key performance metrics to better guage performance in real time.
  > Easily evaluate and compare the value of players item inventories.
  > Show which skill to level up in game based on your selected Blitz build.
- [tools/phase] In game: minimap timers for jungle camps, inhibitors and dragon; jungle
  pathing route; trinket ready highlight. ^f3
  > Know exactly why Jungle Camps, Inhibitors, and Dragon respawn times are in real time.
  > Minimize trinket ward downtime by highlighting your trinket when it's ready to be used.
- [tools/phase] [tools/removed] In game: ultimate timers only for teammates, on their
  portraits; no enemy ultimate overlay listed (Riot's line, [[pcgamesn-blitz-plugins#^f2]]). ^f4
  > Display teammate ultimate timers on their portrait allowing you to always know when they're available.
- [tools/phase] In game, other modes: ARAM Mayhem augment tier list, ARAM health relic
  timers, Arena augment stats as you pick. ^f5
  > Get our hand-crafted ARAM Mayhem Augments Tier List right in your game as your pick augments.
- [tools/phase] Checked, not there: no champ select, post-game or between-games item
  on this page (those are on [[blitz-lol]]); no login named. ^f6
