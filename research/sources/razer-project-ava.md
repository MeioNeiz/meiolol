# Razer AVA (formerly Project AVA) product page, Nov 2026

Why: Razer showed Project AVA at CES 2025 as a live esports AI coach; did it ship, and
what became of it?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. The maker's own page, current in Nov 2026; the FAQ states the pivot and
  the launch slip in its own words.
- Reasoning: fair. Says what it is and when; nothing on how coaching works.
- Interests: vendor marketing a product still in reservation.
- Weigh: trust dates and positioning; capabilities are promises. The CES 2025 coaching
  pitch is known only from search snippets (not read).

## Findings

- [tools] [market] Razer itself says AVA started as the 2025 esports AI coach concept and
  became a general AI companion: the hardware coach pivoted before launch. ^f1
  > Razer AVA has evolved from Project AVA, the 2025 esports AI coach concept, into a fully integrated AI companion.
- [tools] Not shipped: early access is planned for early 2027, after a limited
  invitation-only beta. ^f2
  > Razer AVA is expected to launch under Early Access in early 2027.
- [tools/paid] Price not set; a $20 refundable deposit reserves one and counts towards
  the purchase. ^f3
  > If you've reserved Razer AVA with a $20 deposit, you're all set.
- [tools] [privacy] It reads the PC screen ("PC Vision Mode") over a wired USB-C link,
  Windows only, like Microsoft's screenshot route
  ([[xbox-gaming-copilot-beta#^f3]]) rather than game APIs. ^f4
  > This wired connection ensures the high-bandwidth data transfer required for "PC Vision Mode" to analyze screen content with minimal latency
- [tools] Gaming is now one use among many (planning, chat, translation, games). ^f5
  > From planning your day and talking through everyday decisions to brainstorming ideas, translating conversations, and helping you explore games and other interests
- [policy] Checked, not there: no game names, no League, no word on anti-cheat or
  competitive rules on the page. ^f6
