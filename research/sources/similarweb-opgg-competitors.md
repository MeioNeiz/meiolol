# Similarweb: op.gg competitors (Sept 2026)

Why: how big are the established LoL stats sites, relative to each other?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Similarweb is the standard third-party traffic estimator; free view shows
  ranks and engagement only, no visit counts.
- Reasoning: weak. Panel-based estimates, no method on the page.
- Interests: none in LoL; sells analytics.
- Weigh: trust the ordering, not absolute numbers. op.gg, mobalytics and tracker.gg are
  multi-game sites, so their rank overstates LoL-only traffic. Ranks are global, not EUW.
