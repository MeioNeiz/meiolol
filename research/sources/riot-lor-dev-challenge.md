# Legends of Runeterra Developer Challenge Announcement (Riot DevRel, 2019)

Why: Riot DevRel's own judging standard, stated by its director, plus evidence of
Riot co-building tools with a third-party company.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Riot DevRel post by Paul Sullivan, Director of Developer Relations,
  Nov 4 2019.
- Reasoning: strong. Criteria defined with reasons; "values" spelt out.
- Interests: Riot (launching LoR's developer ecosystem).
- Weigh: LoR not League, but the same DevRel team reviews League apps; the "Values"
  criterion restates the game integrity policy in DevRel's own words.

## Findings

- [market] [tools] Riot worked with Mobalytics for months before LoR's preview to build
  its card gallery, deck builder and Twitch extension. ^f1
  > We worked with them over the past several months to make those tools, and we’re specifically looking for something new, something that scratches a different itch.
- [policy] Riot asked for pitches of something not yet made, explicitly not another
  Mobalytics-style gallery or deck builder. ^f2
  > We’re asking for you to pitch us something you want to make that hasn’t been made yet
- [policy] Criterion Value(s): daily usefulness to players plus Riot's values: don't
  exploit players, give unfair advantages or break the game. ^f3
  > how valuable the app would be to Legends of Runeterra players to use daily and how it shares our values as Rioters (i.e. don’t exploit players, don’t give players unfair advantages, don’t break the game).
- [policy] Criterion Novelty: surprise and delight. ^f4
  > **Novelty**: We want you to surprise and delight us, be creative, and think outside the box.
- [policy] Criterion Polish: something that feels shippable. ^f5
  > have something that feels shippable by the deadline
- [policy] The game's dev team weighs heavily in picking the winner. ^f6
  > we’ll actively involve the Legends of Runeterra dev team to take a look at the finalists. Their judgment will weigh heavily on our choice of winner
- [policy] Applicants were vetted from a detailed pitch of what they would make and
  which developer tools enable it (echoes the production key application). ^f7
  > please give as detailed an explanation as you can of what you, and your potential teammates (up to 5 TOTAL), intend to make and how the developer tools for Legends of Runeterra will enable your features
- [policy] (inferred) DevRel's three tests (daily value within Riot's values, novelty,
  polish) are a usable rubric for a League production key pitch: show daily use, show
  it is not a clone of existing sites, and show a working, polished build. ^f8
