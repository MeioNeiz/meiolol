# I am making a list of the best streamers to watch by champion (r/summonerschool, Sep 2020)

Why: how do players learn a new champion, and what do they look for in a source?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. 4.5k upvotes, 97% upvoted, 1,203 comments (one of the most commented posts
  in r/summonerschool top-of-all-time results). 2020, so names are stale.
- Reasoning: weak to fair. Mostly name suggestions, some reasoning on why a streamer
  teaches well.
- Interests: none; some streamers self-promote in comments.
- Weigh: the volume shows how much players lean on one-trick creators to learn a
  champion, and that finding the right one is manual work.

## Findings

- [learning] [needs] Learning a new champion means watching a better player of that
  champion; finding one per champion was manual and took the author ~15 hours. ^f1
  > this is for anyone who wants to learn new champs and needs to watch someone better play them etc.
  > thank you to everyone of you who helped me through this incredible task which took approximately 15 hours (So far)
- [market] Reception: 4.5k upvotes, 97% upvoted, ~1.2k comments. ^f2
  > Edit: Almost finished adding Twitch links to every single name possible
- [learning] Recommended sources are mostly high-elo one-tricks (OTPs) of that champion. ^f3
  > Saiyans for fizz, he’s an NA challenger fizz otp and educational streams 6 days a week!
  > Garen otp that peaked chall euw this season and knows all the mus and builds extremely well.
- [learning] [needs] Players separate educational from entertaining creators and want
  explanation, not just high rank or mechanics. ^f4
  > He's more for entertainment than education
  > Nidhogg has too specific a style to try and learn from so I dont like him as a learning resource
- [learning] Time-poor players want content that gets straight to the point. ^f5
  > I don't have much time to play so learning champs I have even less, this guy gets straight into it and doesn't have any clickbait/filler.
- [learning] What viewers say they learn: gank timings, matchups, what to do with a lead,
  macro. ^f6
  > Specific times to expect ganks from jungle. Champion match ups. What to do with leads.
