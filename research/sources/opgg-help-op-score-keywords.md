# OP.GG Help: What do the timeline OP Score keywords mean?

Why: how OP.GG turns a per-game score curve into a named verdict.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor help centre, "1 year ago Updated"; 29 of 58 readers found it helpful.
- Reasoning: weak. Lists the labels, not the rules for picking them.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: the label set is the fact; whether the shapes behind them are meaningful is unknown.

## Findings

- [ux/postgame] [ux/charts] Each game gets one of 14 keywords chosen from the shape of the player's timeline OP Score graph. ^f1
  > Based on the shape of the recorded graph, a total of 14 keywords and their interpretations can be reviewed as follows.
- [ux/trust] The keywords are character verdicts, mostly flattering; even losing shapes are framed kindly (Unlucky, Unyielding will, Innocent). ^f2
  > | Unlucky | Performed well but was hindered by bad luck. |
  > | Innocent | Showcased the best possible play from the player. |
- [ux/trust] Win shapes credit decisions the score cannot see. ^f3
  > | Leader | Took initiative in victory by making appropriate decisions and strategies according to the situation. |
- [ux/postgame] Negative shapes get mild words: Decline, Struggling, Rollercoaster. ^f4
  > | Decline | Influence weakened over time. |
  > | Rollercoaster | Performance fluctuated up and down like a rollercoaster. |
- [ux/trust] Reception signal: 29 of 58 readers marked it helpful. ^f5
  > 29 out of 58 found this helpful
- [ux/trust] (inferred) OP.GG names a game's arc as a story about the player, not a mistake; the opposite pole from Mobalytics' blunt badges ([[mobalytics-match-ui#^f9]]). Neither ties the word to a moment in the game. ^f6
