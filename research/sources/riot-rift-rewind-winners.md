# Rift Rewind Hackathon project gallery and winners (2025)

Why: which concrete tools Riot and AWS judges picked in 2025.

## Findings

- [learning] [fun] Winner Heimer Academy: recommends new champions from playstyle and
  mastery data and teaches their abilities by comparison with champions you know. ^f1
  > Heimer provides personalized coaching - it recommends new champions based on your playstyle using mastery data and teaches you their abilities by comparing them to champions you already know.
- [learning] Winners include AI coaches built on match data (Rift Analyzer, League
  Wrapped, Jinx's Magical Rewind Machine). ^f2
  > Your AI coach that turns match data into mastery.
  > An AI-powered coaching agent that analyzes League of Legends player performance data to provide personalized insights, trend analysis, and actionable feedback.
- [fun] Winners include "year wrapped" recaps with personas and friend head-to-heads
  (Rift Rewind Chronicles, Rift Wrapped, riftrewind.lol). ^f3
  > Your League year wrapped — Uncover your persona on the Rift.
  > Your League of Legends year rift-wrapped with AI : epic highlights, player head to heads, and an interactive chatbot to explore your stats.
- [fun] Non-winning entries include social party games built on stats (awards show,
  game show of memory and regret, region persona compared with friends). ^f4
  > The Rift Remembers turns your League stats into a hilarious game show of memory, ego, and regret, our social party game!
- [tools] (inferred) Seven winners on page one; none is a champ select, draft or
  in-game tool. Champion discovery (Heimer Academy) is the closest to picks, framed as
  broadening the pool, which fits the diversity rule
  ([[riot-policies-general#^f6]]). ^f5
