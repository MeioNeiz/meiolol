# UK GDPR Article 13, information when collected from the data subject (legislation.gov.uk, revised)

Why: privacy-rules.md said "Quote of Art 13/14 not yet in research/". Art 13 covers data
people give us themselves: a Riot ID typed into Your games, an account login, an email.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. The law, revised text with dated amendments (DUAA 2025: complaint to the
  controller from 19 Jun 2026, "Commission" from 30 Sep 2026)
- Reasoning: fair. Statute
- Interests: none
- Weigh: the list is the checklist for the privacy page

## Findings

- [privacy] Must give at collection: controller identity and contact, purposes and legal basis, the legitimate interests when relying on 6(1)(f), recipients. Art 13(1) ^f1
  > the identity and the contact details of the controller and, where applicable, of the controller's representative
  > the purposes of the processing for which the personal data are intended as well as the legal basis for the processing
  > where the processing is based on point (f) of Article 6(1), the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party
  > the recipients or categories of recipients of the personal data, if any
- [privacy] Must also give the storage period, or the criteria for it. Art 13(2)(a) ^f2
  > the period for which the personal data will be stored, or if that is not possible, the criteria used to determine that period
- [privacy] Must name the rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability. Art 13(2)(b) ^f3
  > the existence of the right to request from the controller access to and rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing concerning the data subject or to object to processing as well as the right to data portability
- [privacy] New since 19 Jun 2026: the right to complain to the controller itself (DPA 2018 s 164A), besides the regulator. Art 13(2)(ca) ^f4
  > the right to make a complaint to the controller under section 164A of the 2018 Act
- [privacy] The regulator is now "the Commission" (Information Commission, 30 Sep 2026), complaint under s 165. Art 13(2)(d) ^f5
  > under section 165 of the 2018 Act
- [privacy] Automated decision-making with Article 22C safeguards must be disclosed, with the logic. Art 13(2)(f) ^f6
  > the existence of automated decision-making, including profiling
- [privacy] Transfers outside the UK must be stated (where applicable). Art 13(1)(f) ^f7
  > the fact that the controller intends to transfer personal data to a third country or international organisation
