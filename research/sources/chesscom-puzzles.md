# Chess.com Help: How do Puzzles work on Chess.com?

Why: how the biggest chess site splits puzzles into modes for learning, speed and play.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. The product's own help (September 2026), used by millions.
- Reasoning: fair. Describes, does not justify.
- Interests: vendor; membership limits shape the modes.
- Weigh: trust the structure; nothing on what works for learning.

## Findings

- [learning] [ux/features] Rated puzzles move a puzzle rating up or down by the
  puzzle's rating against yours. ^f1
  > Your rating increases when you solve a puzzle correctly and decreases when you don't.
- [learning] Hints exist but cost the reward: a hint after a correct move still earns
  progression points, a hint first earns nothing. ^f2
  > If you make one or more correct moves before asking for a hint, you’ll earn progression points for that puzzle (no rating points).
- [ux/features] After a puzzle a panel shows its rating, target time, how often others
  solve it, attempts and its themes. ^f3
  > Pass Rate: This shows how often chess.com users successfully solve the puzzle.
  > Themes: These are the categories the puzzle is tagged with.
- [fun] Puzzle Rush is a separate unrated speed mode: as many as you can in 3 or 5
  minutes, three misses ends it, with leaderboards and friends. ^f4
  > you have either 3 or 5 minutes to solve as many puzzles as you can
  > Your puzzle rating won’t be affected by your performance in Puzzle Rush, so aim for that high score without worrying!
- [fun] Missed puzzles from a Rush can be reopened and retried. ^f5
  > You can click on any puzzle to open it in a separate window for a closer look or to try solving it again if you got it wrong.
- [fun] Puzzle Battle: a 3 minute rush against another player. ^f6
  > Puzzle battle is a 3-minute puzzle rush, but played against another player!
- [fun] [learning] The Daily Puzzle has the Coach reacting to each move and Hearts so a
  mistake does not end the attempt; results can be shared and discussed. ^f7
  > As you solve the puzzle, Coach reacts to your moves, and the Hearts system gives you room to recover from mistakes instead of ending your attempt immediately.
  > Once you've finished, you can share your result and read or join the discussion in the comments.
- [learning] Custom Puzzles is a no-pressure drill: pick themes and rating range, see your
  solve rate per theme, optionally only puzzles you failed before. ^f8
  > There’s no time pressure and no impact on your rating
  > you can opt to include all puzzles or only those you have previously failed and set a specific rating range
- [learning] (inferred) Pattern across modes: rated and drill modes teach, timed and
  versus modes are for fun and are kept unrated so play never punishes learning. ^f9
