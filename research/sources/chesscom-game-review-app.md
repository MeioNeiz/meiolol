# Chess.com Help: How do I use Game Review on the app?

## Findings

- [ux/postgame] [tools/phase] After the game a pop-up offers rematch, new game or Game
  Review: review sits beside the replay buttons. ^f1
  > After finishing a game, you'll get a pop-up window with the option to rematch your opponent, start a new game, or run the Game Review and see how you did on the game!
- [ux/postgame] Phone navigation: swipe the move list or step with arrows; a green Next
  button bottom right jumps to the next key move. ^f2
  > Tap the green 'Next' button in the bottom-right corner to jump to the next **key move** in the game.
- [ux/postgame] The Coach bubble can cover the eval bar and settings on a phone; the
  player drags it down. ^f3
  > If the coach's speech bubble is too large and blocks options like the evaluation bar, settings, or self-analysis, simply tap and drag the bubble down to uncover these features.
- [ux/postgame] [learning] Show plays out the engine's expected reply line to your move,
  so you see why it was a mistake; Hide returns. ^f4
  > Tap 'Show' to see the computer's predicted moves after yours, helping you understand why a move was a mistake or blunder.
- [ux/postgame] [learning] Best shows the best move with a reason, and the score you
  would have had sits in the bubble's corner. ^f5
  > Shows the best possible move and explains why it’s optimal. The top-right corner of the coach's speech bubble displays your positional score if you had played the suggested move.
- [ux/postgame] [learning] Retry lets you replay the position, with a hint on request;
  a blundered retry gets Coach feedback. ^f6
  > After tapping 'Retry', you can request a hint if needed. If your retry move is a blunder, the coach will provide feedback to guide you toward improvement.
- [ux/postgame] Settings: Coach voice, Coach avatar, show best moves on board or hide
  them until Best is tapped, eval bar position, engine and its strength. ^f7
  > Show the best move on the board as you review. When turned off, you can reveal the engine's suggested move yourself by tapping 'Best.'
  > Choose where the evaluation bar appears on the Game Review screen.
- [tools/paid] Some higher engine strengths are Diamond only. ^f8
  > Some higher strength levels are available only to Diamond members.
