## Findings

- [draft] Calls mixed damage and a safe blind pick common knowledge, then adds: neutral picks, synergies, champion function into enemy comps. @0:00 ^f1
  > You also probably want a safe blind pick, so you don't get counter picked and lane and end up going 0 10
- [draft] A neutral pick protects the draft from your own teammates: self-sufficient junglers (Jarvan, Xin Zhao, Pantheon) have damage and CC; Nidalee needs setup and halves the support's options. @0:54 @1:41 ^f2
  > Having a champion pick like this will ensure that you win draft against the biggest opponent of all, your own teammates
  > by picking Nidalee, you've instantly restricted your supports playable characters in half
- [draft] Rule of thumb for self-sufficient: can go in, stun and kill alone. @2:31 ^f3
  > if a champion can go in, stun, and kill an enemy on their own, they're self-sufficient and they fit this category
- [draft] Distinguishes neutral (fits any ally) from blind-safe (also fine into any enemy comp): Lux is neutral but loses to divers; Ahri is both; Viktor neither; Syndra in between. @3:17 @4:07 @4:54 ^f4
  > Lux is a neutral champion pick that works with anything your teammates might play, but it doesn't mean that she can play against anything that your enemies might play
  > Ahri, the ultimate blind pick, functioning with anything your team can play and functioning against anything your enemy will play
- [draft] Blind support: fast, reliable crowd control (Leona, Nautilus, Rakan), not Braum's delayed stun. @4:54 @5:46 ^f5
  > for supports, think to add a champion into your pool to blind pick that has reliable and quick-to-land crowd control
- [draft] "Payoff picks": countering the enemy comp type is called the most important thing when finalising a pool; avoid clones with the same strengths and weaknesses (Lux, Vel'Koz, Xerath; Trundle and Udyr). @5:46 @6:38 ^f6
  > effectively countering these types of comps is the most important thing to consider when finalizing your champion pool
  > Udyr is a clone of Trundle in the sense that he has the same strengths and weaknesses
- [draft] Example pools: mid Ahri blind, Malzahar into assassins, Swain into tanks, Neeko into squishy no-CC teams; support Leona, Braum, Rell, Bard; ADC Corki blind, Nami-backed payoff pick, Kai'Sa or Ezreal when unsafe. @7:33 ^f7
  > as a mid laner, you might play Ahri as your blind pick and Malzahar to counter assassins with his ults, and Swain to counter tanks
- [draft] [learning] Pool size: about three to four champions, each good against the opposite thing to the others. @7:33 ^f8
  > The core idea here is to have about three to four champions in your pool that you can play at any time
