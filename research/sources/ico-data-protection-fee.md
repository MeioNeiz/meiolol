# ICO Guide to the data protection fee

Why: does a UK solo developer running a player lookup site need to register with the ICO,
and what does it cost?

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. The regulator's own page, current figures (fetched 2026-10-11).
- Reasoning: fair. Rules plus rationale (fees set by Parliament by risk).
- Interests: regulator.
- Weigh: exemptions are in [[ico-fee-exemptions]]; meiolol's processing (storing and
  showing players' match data) is not on that list, so the fee applies.

## Findings

- [privacy] [policy] Controllers pay a fee unless an exemption applies; the fee landing
  page says this includes sole traders (Data Protection (Charges and Information)
  Regulations 2018). ^f1
  > Not all controllers must pay a fee. Many can rely on an exemption.
- [privacy] Three tiers; tier 1 (micro: turnover up to £632,000 or at most 10 staff) is
  £52 a year; £5 off by direct debit. ^f2
  > You have a maximum turnover of £632,000 for your financial year **or** no more than 10 members of staff. The fee for tier 1 is £52.
  > If you choose to pay your fee by [direct debit](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/renew/ "Renew"), you will receive an automatic discount of £5 at the point of payment.
- [privacy] Default is tier 3 (£3,763) until you tell the ICO otherwise. ^f3
  > We regard all controllers as eligible to pay a fee in tier 3 unless and until they tell us otherwise.
