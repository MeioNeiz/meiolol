# HSReplay: New Filter: Player Experience (Oct 2018)

Why: what does a Hearthstone stat site do about a deck's (or patch's) first games being
played by people still learning it?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. The biggest Hearthstone stat site describing its own shipped feature; the
  one chart is an image (not read), no numbers in the text.
- Reasoning: weak. Asserts the learning effect, no measurement in the post.
- Interests: vendor; the filter is a Premium upsell.
- Weigh: trust that the filter exists and what it does; the size of the effect is not
  given.

## Findings

- [tools] [method/dynamics] The site's answer to inexperienced pilots is a filter, not a
  model: stats restricted to games by players with at least 25 or 50 games on that deck. ^f1
  > users can only see stats from games recorded by players with _a minimum of 25 or 50 games played with a given deck_
- [method/dynamics] Their stated reason: a deck's win rate among new pilots understates
  it, more so for combo and control decks than aggro. ^f2
  > players who are just starting to play with a new archetype might not know the deck's intricacies
  > combo and control decks can have multiple win conditions that require more experience to properly master
- [method/dynamics] Checked, not there: nothing on what the filter shows in a deck's or
  patch's first days, when nobody has 25 games on it yet; no blend with older data. ^f3
- [method/dynamics] (inferred) The League analogue is a "games on champion" cut (League
  of Graphs shows the curve, [[leagueofgraphs-darius-stats#^f4]]); on a patch's first
  days it would thin the sample further, so it is a reading aid, not a day-0 estimate. ^f4
