# U.GG Privacy Policy (Outplayed Inc., last updated 2026-09-10)

Why: second large approved lookup site; how it frames looked-up players' data.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A for what U.GG states (current policy of a top-5 League site,
  [[similarweb-opgg-competitors#^f3]]); US company under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- Reasoning: weak. Generic US SaaS template; game data barely mentioned.
- Interests: Outplayed (U.GG).
- Weigh: like OP.GG, it does not address profiles of non-users directly; the only hint is
  that summoner names may be "entered by you or others".

## Findings

- [privacy] Service data includes summoner names entered by the user or by others. ^f1
  > either entered by you or others who use the Services with you (for example, League of Legends summoner name, favourite champions, etc.)
- [privacy] EEA, UK and Swiss residents: Outplayed is controller; bases are consent,
  contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests. ^f2
  > Under applicable law, Outplayed is the controller of the Personal Information of EEA Residents.
  > Legitimate interests: we may process your Personal Information when we have a legitimate interest do so and this does not override your rights and freedoms.
- [privacy] Retention is open-ended: as long as the service is used or as needed for
  stated purposes. ^f3
  > We retain the Personal Information we receive as described in this Privacy Policy for as long as you use our Services or as necessary to fulfil the purpose(s) for which it was collected
- [privacy] Deletion and access requests by email to privacy@u.gg. ^f4
  > You have the right to access, modify, correct or delete any personal data which we may have collected. To exercise this right please contact privacy
- [policy] Policy page carries the Riot "isn't endorsed" boilerplate. ^f5
  > U.GG Stats isn't endorsed by Riot Games and doesn't reflect the views or opinions of Riot Games or anyone officially involved in producing or managing League of Legends.
- [privacy] Checked, not there: no section on Riot API match data, no removal process
  for looked-up profiles, no ownership check for requests. ^f6
