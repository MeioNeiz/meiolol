# OP.GG Desktop app patch notes (2024 to 2026)

Why: dates for when OP.GG's desktop features arrived (and anything removed), which the
feature pages ([[opgg-desktop-lol]], [[overwolf-opgg]]) don't give.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor's own changelog, 53 updates, latest 3.1.4_B on 2026-09-30.
- Reasoning: fair. Dated, itemised, with occasional reasons ("based on user feedback").
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: trust dates and what was added; removals are only stated when OP.GG chose to.
  Raw is the index plus the seven entries, concatenated.

## Findings

- [tools] 53 updates; latest 3.1.4_B on 2026-09-30; notes are for Windows, Mac may lack
  features. ^f1
  > This patch note is based on the Windows version. Some features may not be available on the Mac version.
- [tools/paid] Feb 2024: an OP.GG Premium guide added to the desktop app, headed "Come
  and subscribe to OP.GG!". ^f2
  > Added OP.GG Premium Guide
- [tools/phase] May 2024: spell and skill tracker overlay, loading screen information and
  control ward counter added; the headline says spell overlays can be shared with team
  members. ^f3
  > New overlays have been added Spell & skill tracker
  > Can share spell check overlays with team members, and you’re not using this?
- [tools/removed] (inferred) The spell tracker (May 2024, still on op.gg/desktop in Oct
  2026, [[opgg-desktop#^f1]]) is missing from the 13 overlays on the current LoL page
  ([[opgg-desktop-lol#^f4]]); no patch note in the index says it was removed. ^f4
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] May 2025: beta AI chatbot for League questions (tier lists,
  runes, items), North America only at first. ^f5
  > Currently in beta and only available in North America.
  > What are the best assassin runes for jungle Naafiri?
- [tools/phase] Post game, Oct 2025: a My Performance page appears after each match in the
  In-Game Info tab; LoL badges for achievements on My Page. ^f6
  > After a match ends, a My Performance page will now appear on the In-Game Info tab, allowing you to review your detailed gameplay data.
  > You can now view your badges on My Page and review the achievements you've earned in your matches.
- [fun] Engagement events: daily check-in prizes in the app (Apr to Dec 2025; Oct 2025
  draw for an iPhone 17 Pro). ^f7
  > Check in every day for a chance to win an iPhone 17 Pro!
- [tools/phase] In game, Apr 2026: AI Voice launched with more voices. ^f8
  > AI Voice is now available!
- [tools/removed] [needs] Jun 2026: removed the post-game recommended video popup after
  user feedback; added a lighter Enhanced Overlay (windowed or borderless only). ^f9
  > Based on user feedback, we removed the recommended video popup that appeared after game sessions.
  > The Enhanced Overlay only works in Windowed or Borderless Windowed mode.
- [tools] Aug 2026 "Spectate is back" was a bug fix, not a policy return. ^f10
  > Fixed an issue that prevented LoL spectating from working.
- [tools/accounts] Mar 2024: the app recognises the account you log into LoL with (it
  follows the client login, no separate Riot sign-in named). ^f11
  > From now on, your account will be recognized better when you log into LoL.
- [tools/removed] Checked, not there: no index title mentions a Riot-requested removal
  (ultimate timers, champ select names, auto-accept). ^f12
