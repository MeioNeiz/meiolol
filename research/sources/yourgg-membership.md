# YOUR.GG (Gen.G) membership page, EUW, Nov 2026

Why: what an esports-team-owned AI coach sells in 2026, at what price, and whether in-game
"decision" help survived Riot's Aug 2025 rules.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. The vendor's live pricing page (EUW, GBP incl. VAT); what is on sale is
  first-hand. Gen.G ownership (May 2024) is from search snippets, but the support email is
  at geng.gg.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: vendor; testimonials are selected.
- Weigh: trust prices and tiers; feature quality unknown.

## Findings

- [tools/paid] Three paid tiers, monthly, in GBP incl. VAT: PLUS £3.49, PRO £5.49,
  PRO+ £12.99. ^f1
  > £3.49 VAT incl. / per month
  > £5.49 VAT incl. / per month
  > £12.99 VAT incl. / per month
- [tools/paid] [learning] AI coaching is metered: pre-game AI voice coaching costs 100
  credits a play, 2,000 credits on PLUS, 5,000 on PRO, unlimited on PRO+; unused allowance
  lapses monthly. ^f2
  > Pre-game AI voice coaching (100 credits per play) 2,000 credits 5,000 credits Unlimited
  > It resets monthly.
- [tools/paid] LLM cost passes to the user: tokens spent count even if the answer is
  never read. ^f3
  > AI tokens already consumed for analysis and response generation remain deducted, even if you leave or don't read the response.
- [tools] [learning] AI video coaching is the newest paid feature, sized by plan
  (storage 2 GB to 10 GB). ^f4
  > AI video coaching NEW
- [tools/phase] [policy] Paid tiers list an in-game decision support overlay, and the
  overlay shows enemy critical item detection and lane opponent return, in Nov 2026,
  after Riot's Aug 2025 ban on decision prompts
  ([[overwolf-aug-2025-riot-guidelines#^f3]]). ^f5
  > In-game decision support overlay
  > Our event timeline shows objective fight advantage, core item purchase timing, lane opponent return, and enemy critical item detection.
- [tools/phase] Champ select help (live ban and pick guidance) sits in every paid tier. ^f6
  > Live ban and pick guidance
- [tools/paid] Free tier keeps the overlay, pre-game guide and post-game history; reports
  are one champion free, unlimited paid; past reports lock when you cancel. ^f7
  > Reports generated during your subscription are only viewable while your Membership is active.
- [market] Gen.G link: support goes to a geng.gg address, and a testimonial found it
  through "Gen.G's manager". ^f8
  > email us at tech@geng.gg
- [policy] Riot "isn't endorsed" boilerplate; no compliance or approval claim on the page.
  ^f9
  > YOURGG isn't endorsed by Riot Games
- [policy] (inferred) An esports-team-owned app still sells in-game "decision support"
  15 months after Riot's cut; either the overlay was narrowed to allowed facts or
