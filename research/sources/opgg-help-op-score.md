# OP.GG Help: OP Score explained

Why: what OP.GG's per-game score is, how it is named and what OP.GG admits about it.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor's own help centre for the largest LoL stats site; "1 year ago Updated".
- Reasoning: weak. No inputs or formula; says only "summoner stats".
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: trust the scale, awards and timing; the helpful vote (37 of 121) hints readers wanted more than this.

## Findings

- [learning] [ux/postgame] OP Score is a 0 to 10 rating of each player's game from their stats. ^f1
  > The OP Score is an evaluation system that estimates in-game performance based on summoner stats. Scores range from 0 to 10, depending on performance in the match.
- [ux/postgame] Awards: MVP to the top scorer of all ten on the winning team, ACE to the top scorer on the losing team. ^f2
  > **MVP**: The summoner on the winning team with the highest OP Score among all 10 players
  > **ACE**: The summoner on the losing team with the highest OP Score
- [ux/charts] A timeline OP Score is computed every 5 minutes on Summoner's Rift (3 in ARAM) and drawn as a graph that can be compared champion by champion; the last value is the official score. ^f3
  > The timeline OP Score is calculated every 5 minutes on Summoner’s Rift and every 3 minutes in ARAM, with the final value at the end of the match recorded as the official OP Score.
- [ux/trust] OP.GG calls the score beta and says it can be wrong for some games; players report bad scores by 1:1 inquiry. ^f4
  > The OP Score is currently in beta and may occasionally produce inaccurate results depending on the characteristics of each game.
- [ux/trust] Reception signal: 37 of 121 readers marked the article helpful. ^f5
  > 37 out of 121 found this helpful
- [ux/trust] Checked, not there: no list of inputs, weights or comparison basis (rank, role or champion average) for the score. ^f6
- [policy] (inferred) A 1st-to-10th rating of the other nine players is a verdict on named strangers; meiolol's review should rate the player's own game against facts, not rank teammates ([[opgg-game-detail-ui]]). ^f7
