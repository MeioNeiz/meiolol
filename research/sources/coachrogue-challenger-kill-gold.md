# Coach Rogue: Why do CHALLENGER PLAYERS KILLS GIVE MORE than yours?

Why: do kills pay more, or do leads come earlier, at higher rank in our games?

## Assessment

- Evidence: D for the numbers: the starting observation is a student's read of Rogue's
  own Grandmaster match history; the rest is replays. Season 13 (Sep 2023). 45k views.
- Reasoning: fair as coaching (why chaining a kill is worth more than recalling), weak
  as a rank comparison: no count of chained kills or lead timing by rank.
- Interests: Patreon and coaching links; nothing sold in the video.
- Weigh: the title is about what a kill leads to, not kill gold. Kill gold per kill is
  the same at every tier we have, chained kills too, and leads at 10 are only a little
  bigger at Master+ ([[kill-gold-by-rank-measured#^f1]],
  [[kill-gold-by-rank-measured#^f6]], [[kill-gold-by-rank-measured#^f7]]).

## Findings

- [data] A student saw in Rogue's Grandmaster games the winning team building a big lead
  by about 10 minutes; in his own lower-ranked games the lead came only after laning,
  from a teamfight. @0:00 ^f1
  > in my grand master ELO games the winning team was generally gaining a very substantial lead at around 10 minutes or earlier
  > this generally wasn't really happening until after laning phase ended
- [learning] By about 8 minutes everyone has level 6 and most lanes are decided; from
  then, play through winning lanes rather than helping losing ones. @1:25 ^f2
  > by around 8 minutes the timer in which everyone in the game will have hit Level 6 and get their ultimates most Lanes have already been decided
  > people trying to help their losing Lanes too much it is losing you a ton of games
- [learning] "Multi-part plays": after a kill, if still stronger, don't recall; chain
  into the next kill, dive or plates, including off a teammate's solo kill. @1:25 @2:49
  @5:20 ^f3
  > you can keep going and you can start chaining your kills together
- [learning] Recall often and spend: holding over 1,000 gold raises the risk and stops a
  won fight from chaining. @5:20 ^f4
  > if you're running around with more than a thousand gold
- [learning] The title means kills lead to more (chained kills, plates, a laner denied
  XP), not that they pay more gold; the closing Kled example is a missed three-person
  proxy kill. @6:51 ^f5
  > had this been a challenger game this is exactly what would have happened
  > now that's why Challenger players kills mean more than yours
- [data] Checked, not there: no numbers at all (no lead size, kill counts or rank
  split); bounty gold is not mentioned. ^f6
