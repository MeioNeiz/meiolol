# iTero: best League of Legends companion app in 2026 (2026-03-31)

Why: one source surveying every major companion app's drafting tool and stating how Riot approval works in practice.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Written by a competitor (iTero ranks itself first); dated March 31, 2026; first-hand testing of each app.
- Reasoning: fair. Explains ratings with examples.
- Interests: vendor, one side of a comparison.
- Weigh: trust feature descriptions and the industry-insider account of how approval works; discount rankings.

## Findings

- [tools] [method] iTero's drafting coach uses custom-trained models to recommend the best-win-rate pick for the comp, with explanations. ^f1
  > It’s flagship tool, an AI Drafting Coach, uses custom trained models to recommend Champions that give you the best win rate for your team composition, and explains why.
- [tools] All "big three" (Mobalytics, Porofessor, Blitz) plus Team Advisor and ProDraft ship champ select drafting recommenders; Team Advisor shows app-assigned scores for every possible pick. ^f2
  > The big three apps, Mobalytics, Porofessor and Blitz remain the most popular of the applications
  > You can see all the possible picks and their app-assigned scores throughout the draft.
- [policy] Riot approves API applications based on what is described but never publicly "approves" apps, since it cannot control future features. ^f3
  > Most apps need to go through an application process in order to get an API key. Riot will “approve” these applications, but they are basing that on the details in the application. Therefore, Riot are careful never to officially and publicly “approve” apps, since they can’t control the features these apps launch in future.
- [policy] Overwolf polices compliance with Riot; non-compliant apps risk delisting and losing their API key. ^f4
  > Overwolf work closely with Riot to ensure all apps available on their app store (all the apps above, except Blitz) are compliant, and any issues Riot have are usually resolved quickly, otherwise the app risks being de-listed and losing their API key.
- [policy] Feature removed for policy: Riot clarified queue auto-accept is not permitted and it was removed from all apps (Team Advisor lost its main draw). ^f5
  > a feature Riot recently clarified shouldn’t be permitted and was removed from all apps
- [policy] Riot's Streamer Mode broke Porofessor's core champ select scouting for players who enable it. ^f6
  > Porofessor was hit the hardest by Riot’s recent addition of Streamer mode , which, when enabled, kills their core feature (Scouting).
- [policy] Article answers "Are they approved by Riot?" with "compliant with Riot's Terms", not "approved". ^f7
  > At the time of writing this article, all the apps I’ve referenced in this article are compliant with Riot’s Terms & Conditions
- [policy] Checked, not there: no mention of any drafting or win-rate recommendation feature being removed or restricted by Riot. ^f8
