# League of Legends Patch 26.1 Notes (Riot)

Why: primary source and date for the season 2026 changes to minion waves, minion gold
and XP, kill gold and role quests that the wiki pages describe.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Riot's own patch notes for the season start (Jan 2026).
- Reasoning: fair. Short designer rationale per section.
- Interests: none beyond Riot's framing of its own changes.
- Weigh: canonical for what changed in 26.1; later patches (26.2 to 26.20) may tune
  further, so check the wiki history for anything after.

## Findings

- [learning] [data] Waves every 25 s from 14:00 and 20 s from 30:00, with a melee
  pruned from cannon waves and a caster from 30:00; Riot says it kept total gold and XP
  from ballooning this way. ^f1
  > At 14 minutes, minion waves now spawn every 25 seconds. When a cannon minion spawns on these waves, one fewer melee minion spawns.
  > At 30 minutes, minion waves now spawn every 20 seconds. One fewer caster minion spawns on these waves.
- [learning] Riot's stated intent: more frequent waves mid and late, with less value
  per wave so totals don't balloon. ^f2
  > we're increasing the rate of minion wave spawns in the mid and late game. In order to not balloon the amount of gold and experience given by these more frequent waves, we're pruning the occasional melee minion out of the mid game
- [data] New minion XP: melee 62, ranged 31, cannon 75; melee gold 20. ^f3
  > Melee minion XP: 65 (61.75 / 40.3) ⇒ 62 (62 / 40.3)
  > Ranged minion XP: 32 (30.4 / 19.84) ⇒ 31 (31 / 20.15)
- [data] [learning] Cannon gold now grows 1 per 90 s from 50 (was 60 + 3 per 90 s), so
  the cannon's late value was cut sharply. ^f4
  > Cannon/Super minion gold: 60 + 3 every 90 seconds⇒ 50 + 1 every 90 seconds
- [learning] First Blood gives +100 gold again (Feats of Strength removed). ^f5
  > First Blood once again grants +100g to the killer.
- [learning] Kill gold devalues faster for champions who die often and recovers faster
  as they earn gold; Riot wants repeated ganks on one lane to pay less. ^f6
  > Base kill gold devaluation per 1 gold given on death: 0.2 ⇒ 0.4.
  > By reducing the value of repeated kills, players are nudged toward ganking other lan
- [learning] [data] Role quests change farm value by role: laners get 25% less minion
  gold and XP outside their lane until level 3; after quest completion the bot laner
  gets +2 gold per minion and +50 per takedown, the top laner 12.5% more XP and level
  cap 20, the jungler +10 gold and +10 XP per large monster. ^f7
  > Gold and experience from minions reduced by 25% outside of top lane until level 3
  > +2g per minion kill for the rest of the game
  > +50g per champion takedown for the rest of the game
  > All future experience gains increased by 12.5%.
  > After quest completion, Junglers gain +10g and +10 XP per large monster killed
- [learning] Minions now spawn at 0:30 instead of 1:05. ^f8
  > Minion spawn time: 1:05 ⇒ 30 seconds
- [learning] Checked, not there: no change to champion kill XP or to Summoner's Rift
  death timers in 26.1 (death timers shortened only in Swiftplay). ^f9
  > death timers are significantly shorter through most of the game
