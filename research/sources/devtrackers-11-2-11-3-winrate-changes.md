# Patch 11.2 -> 11.3 Champion Win Rate Changes (r/leagueoflegends, Feb 2021), Riot Phlox replies via devtrackers

Why: Riot's own practice for reading a patch's effect, and a designer's list of why a
buff's measured effect misses its intended one.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. First-hand remarks by a Riot champion balance designer (Riot Phlox) on
  his team's routine; reddit comments, no numbers.
- Reasoning: weak. Asserted in passing, though the list of causes matches Riot's
  longer posts.
- Interests: Riot.
- Weigh: trust as a description of practice in 2021 (the before/after table is the
  tool, inside Riot too); the spreadsheet itself was not read.

## Findings

- [method/dynamics] Riot's balance team circulates the same thing internally: a
  per-patch table of changed champions' win rate before and after. ^f1
  > "I actually send out an internal email that's pretty darn similar to this every patch."
- [method/dynamics] [data] Riot would not publish it every patch because early
  patch-to-patch data misleads without context. ^f2
  > "there's lots of weirdness with patch to patch data, especially early on in a patch"
- [method/dynamics] [method/causal] Why a buffed champion often does not improve, in
  his words: the rest of the game moved, pick rate rose and depressed the win rate for
  a while, a new build or skill order became best and players had not found it, or
  the power landed in one rank band only. ^f3
  > "Sometimes its playrate going way up depressing winrate for a while."
  > "Sometimes its weird stuff like a new build/max becoming optimal and people messing up."
- [method/dynamics] Checked, not there: any expected size per change, how many days
  Riot waits before reading the table, or a score of intended against measured moves. ^f4
- [method/dynamics] (inferred) Two of his four causes are temporary by his own account
  ("for a while", players "messing up"): a buffed champion's first-days rate would then
  understate where it settles. Our 16.21 check found no such curve (docs/learned.md:
  late minus early -0.2 ± 0.3 pp), so on our data this is a designer's belief, not a
  measured effect. ^f5
