# Microsoft Writing Style Guide: Headings

Why: rules for section headings, the most repeated text element on meio.lol pages.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Microsoft's public writing standard.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: written for documents; most rules carry over to UI section titles.

## Findings

- [ux] Headings as short as possible, most important idea first. Writing headings ^f1
  > Keep headings as short as possible, and put the most important idea at the beginning.
- [ux] Unread headings mean unread text below. Writing headings ^f2
  > If readers don't read the headings, they probably won't read the text that follows, either.
- [ux/layout] Subheadings only when there are at least two distinct topics. Writing headings ^f3
  > If you can't find at least two distinct topics, skip the second-level headings.
- [ux/layout] One heading level is usually enough for a page or two. Writing headings ^f4
  > One heading level is usually plenty for a page or two of content.
- [ux] Same grammatical form for headings at one level. Writing headings ^f5
  > Use parallel sentence structure for all headings at the same level.
- [ux] No filler text added just to separate two headings. Writing headings ^f6
  > But don't insert filler text just to separate the headings.
