# Mobalytics: OPGG and Mobalytics, make sense of your LoL stats

Why: how the leading "coaching" tool positions itself against the stats sites, and what
learning features exist already.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Mobalytics is the second most visited LoL tool site globally
  ([[similarweb-opgg-competitors#^f3]], though multi-game); page dated by nav (tier list
  15.24, i.e. late 2025), (c) 2016-2026 Gamers Net, Inc.
- Reasoning: fair. States the gap it built for, but it is a marketing page.
- Interests: vendor, one side of a comparison.
- Weigh: trust its feature list and its framing of the op.gg gap; not its claims of
  effectiveness.

## Findings

- [market] op.gg: Seoul-based, launched January 2013, "the most popular statistics website for League", and the template for stats sites. ^f1
  > OPGG is the most popular statistics website for League of Legends. The Seoul-based company combines a robust database with a simple UI
  > Their website was released in January 2013 and quickly became the default place to go for stats for millions of Summoners around the world.
- [needs] [learning] Mobalytics' founding gap: stats pages show what happened but not how to improve beyond "die less" or "farm better". ^f2
  > it can be difficult to understand how to really improve your performance outside of “die less” or “farm better”.
- [learning] GPI: profiles a player's strengths and weaknesses as a graph, then gives personal advice; tracks skills over time. ^f3
  > The Gamer Performance Index (GPI) makes it easy for anyone to understand their strengths and weaknesses since they are displayed with a simple graph.
  > Once they receive their GPI analysis, players are given specific advice for how they can individually improve.
- [tools] [learning] Live Companion gives pre-game info on every player plus matchup advice and builds. ^f4
  > Our Live Companion helps players get ready for their very next game by providing information on every summoner in their match as well as matchup advice and build recommendations.
- [tools] Tier list is expert-curated per patch, split low and high elo, and tagged by difficulty (not purely statistical). ^f5
  > Every patch, Mobalytics experts analyze every buff and nerf to champions and items to rank the best champions for both low ELO and high ELO.
- [learning] Champion pages include combos and advice beyond builds. ^f6
  > We include everything from rune and item builds to combos and advice.
- [market] Multi-game: LoL, TFT, Destiny 2, Genshin Impact; Twitch extension. ^f7
  > Copyright © 2016-2026 All rights reserved by Gamers Net, Inc.
- [learning] Checked, not there: no mention of lane-phase timelines, gold or XP diffs, or matchup power curves; the improvement angle is about the player's own aggregate skills (GPI), not a specific matchup over time. ^f8
