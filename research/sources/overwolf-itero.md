# The iTero AI Coach on the Overwolf app store (v3.11.7, Oct 2026)

Why: iTero's features per phase, its paid tier and reach, beyond [[itero-home]].

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Store listing: 1.1M downloads, 4.4 from 117 ratings, version 3.11.7 dated 13 Oct 2026; Electron app.
- Reasoning: fair. Feature list; "PROVEN" win claims without evidence.
- Interests: vendor copy; reviews are user-written but picked by recency.
- Weigh: the feature set and the free plus subscription shape are facts; win claims are marketing. Price not on the page.

## Findings

- [tools/paid] Free with in-app ads, with a subscription available ("Premium": removes ads, unlocks premium features); no price on the listing. ^f1
  > Subscription available
  > Support the dev with The iTero AI Coach Premium,
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Champ select: AI drafting coach recommends champions for any draft (claim: proven to win more games). ^f2
  > AI-Powered Drafting Coach which provides you with Champion recommendations to suit any draft, proven to win you more games.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Loading screen: lobby scouting profiles every player loading in, down to laning aggression and farm or fight preference, so other players' play style is shown. ^f3
  > Advanced Lobby Scouting that provides you all the essential information about every player loading into the game, from how aggressive they play the laning phase to whether they prefer to farm or fight in the mid game.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Between games: an account analyser with over 500 statistics, each adjusted by champion pool, compared to the player's elo and tracked over time; an AI macro coach picks the areas needing most work. ^f4
  > Each one is adjusted by your Champion pool, compared against your elo and tracked over time.
  > AI-Powered Macro Coach that analyses these statistics to determine which areas of your gameplay requires the most improvement.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Champ select: rune and summoner spell recommendations based on team composition; in-game overlay and second monitor support. ^f5
  > Composition-based Rune and Summoner recommendations.
- [market] 1.1M downloads, 4.4 from 117 ratings; Electron, 152.3 MB; v3.11.7 dated 13 Oct 2026 (short page lines, unquotable). ^f6
- [needs] [draft] A Feb 2026 review credits the drafting tool with explaining why counters work (lane pressure, scaling, team comp) and personalising to play style, rather than just naming a pick. ^f7
  > It does not just tell you what to pick, it helps you think about lane pressure, scaling, and overall team composition.
  > It adjusted suggestions based on how I play, which made it feel personalised rather than generic.
- [needs] Reviewers praise situational rune pages and item builds over standard builds, and fast Discord support for a subscription problem. ^f8
  > lots of updated rune pages/item build for the specific situation instead of the standard builds u get with other apps

  > You’re not required to sign in to use apps, and you could opt out from data collection.
