# Coach Rogue: An Itemization Guide Like You've Never Seen Before!

Why: which itemisation choices a coach says depend on the game state (gold lead, enemy
resists, objectives), and what a build page could show for them from our data.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Gold value arithmetic on boot stats, base stat growth (armour 3 to 4 a
  level, MR about 1) and his own shield "testing"; no game data or win rates. Says he
  swaps the support item "in about 50% of games I play" (own habit, not data). 254k
  views, 10k likes. Patch 25.x (Feats of Strength, Atakhan); boot numbers will have
  moved.
- Reasoning: fair. Opportunity cost is stated and used (boots vs core item before a
  fight), and the ahead/behind rule is clear; but gold value is a crude yardstick, as a
  long comment argues (Glowing Mote prices haste low, percent magic pen is
  multiplicative), and he forgot Symbiotic Soles (pinned reply).
- Interests: sells coaching (Metafy) and Patreon recordings, Waveclear in the
  description.
- Weigh: trust the mechanics after a wiki check; the state-dependent rules (behind:
  don't buy defensive; ahead: tankier; pen boots only into MR) are the testable part
  and match what learned.md says our data can speak to (lead at purchase). Captions:
  "Zia"/"Zire" at 5:21 to 6:39 is an ADC name misheard, "Asante or Remis" likely
  Malphite or Rammus, "ZAS" is Zhonya's, "Renardo" likely Renata, "Leandre" Liandry's,
  "Adakan/Attacon" Atakhan.

## Findings

- [needs] Opens with the question he says he gets more than any other: when to upgrade to tier 3 boots. @1:18 ^f1
  > When should you upgrade to tier 3 boots?
- [learning] Groups tier 3 boots: swiftness and haste upgrades worth buying at once; attack speed, armour and MR upgrades situational, as a short-term spike before a big fight; magic pen upgrade only once the enemy has MR ("tank killing"). @4:35 ^f2
  > Swiftness boots and cool down reduction boots are just OP
  > magic penetration shoes are your ultimate tank killing upgrade
- [learning] Opportunity cost: completed items spike more than tier 3 boots, which spike more than any one component; treat the boot upgrade like an elixir for a fight that's about to happen. @3:49 @4:35 ^f3
  > Completed items will always give you a bigger power spike than what completing your tier three shoes will give you
  > If a gamedefining dragon spawns in 30 seconds, and you've only got 500 gold to spare, upgrade your boots instead of buying item components
- [learning] Component order: buy the stat your abilities scale off first (AD, AP or health; armour on a few), which also makes each mana point do more damage. @5:21 ^f4
  > The simple answer is prioritize the stats that your abilities scale off of
- [learning] Multiplicative scaling: crit ADCs stack four multiplying stats (AD, attack speed, crit, percent armour pen) and scale late; assassins have one or two and spike early. @7:30 ^f5
  > Crit ADCs scale well into the late game because they have four different types of multiplicative scalings
- [learning] Jinx with Lethal Tempo, her Q and an attack speed support (Lulu) can skip the attack speed item and rush Infinity Edge and armour pen, coming online "one item faster". @7:30 @8:21 ^f6
  > we can entirely skip the need to build a dedicated attack speed item and instead rush buying items like infinity edge and armor penetration item first
- [learning] Armour pen timing from base stats: champions gain about 80 armour but only about 20 MR by level 18, so physical damage needs percent armour pen even without enemy armour items; magic damage can delay Void Staff until the enemy buys MR. @9:12 @10:02 ^f7
  > most champions only gain about 20 extra magic resistance, but will gain about 80 armor
  > if you're a magic damage dealer, you can hold off on buying a void staff until later when the enemy actually buys defensive items
- [learning] By lead: a fed ADC can delay armour pen to the fourth item, an ADC behind in gold needs it third. @10:02 ^f8
  > in games where the ADC player is behind in gold, you'd likely need to build that armor penetration as your third item
- [learning] After Elder Dragon buy a damage over time item (Liandry's) to keep reapplying the burn; with Baron or Atakhan's AP, buy Deathcap at once. @10:59 @11:28 ^f9
  > If you get Elder Dragon, it's a really good idea to buy a damage overtime item
- [learning] Rule: when behind you can't afford to itemise defensively (a 0/3 mid buying Zhonya's second becomes useless); when ahead, itemise a little tankier. @11:51 @12:44 ^f10
  > When you are behind, you cannot afford to itemize defensively
  > when you're ahead in gold, itemizing to be slightly tankier is a really good idea
- [learning] The upgraded support item can be sold and rebought as another upgrade for 120 gold; he swaps in about half his games: offensive (Bloodsong) when behind and needing to dive a fed carry, defensive (Celestial Opposition, Solstice Sleigh) when ahead and fighting front to back. @11:51 @13:34 @14:22 ^f11
  > You only lose 120 gold in the process, so it's not costly at all
  > I honestly swap support item midame in about 50% of games I play
- [needs] Top comment (1,800 likes) shows the swap was unknown to many viewers; another says many master+ players think the opposite of the ahead/behind rule. ^f12
  > YOU CAN SELL THE SUPPORT ITEM AND BUY THE OTHER VARIATIONS OF IT MID GAME?? FOR 120 GOLD
  > in master+ elo I am convinced 90% of people think the exact opposite
- [learning] Commenters push back: spending all your gold each recall can beat stat priority (a rank 1 Evelynn changes builds by what components she can afford); gold value oversimplifies tier 3 boots. ^f13
  > the most important thing when leaving the base is spending all of your gold
  > Bottom line is gold value as a single argument is oversimplifying
- [ux/features] (inferred) Nearly every item rule here is conditional on game state (gold lead or deficit, enemy MR or armour, an objective just taken), which a build page of most played items by champion and role can't show; our timelines have the lead at each purchase, so "what players buy second when behind vs ahead" is showable. ^f14
- [learning] (inferred) Testable in our data: defensive second item when behind vs ahead (win rate given the lead at purchase), tier 3 boot upgrade timing, support item swaps (sell and rebuy events) and their rate, armour pen as third item when behind. ^f15
