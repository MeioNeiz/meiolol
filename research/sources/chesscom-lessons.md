# Chess.com Help: How do Lessons work on Chess.com?

Why: the teach-then-practise structure of a big chess learning product.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Vendor help, July 2026.
- Reasoning: weak. Navigation help only.
- Interests: vendor; paywall shapes access (Basic gets one lesson a day).
- Weigh: structure only; the lesson content itself was not read.

## Findings

- [learning] Each lesson pairs explanation from a Coach with interactive challenges to
  practise it straight away. ^f1
  > Each lesson includes guidance and explanations from your Coach, along with interactive challenges to help you practice and reinforce what you've learned.
- [learning] [ux/layout] A path orders courses through four levels; tiles show locked,
  available and completed; a course ends in a graduation cap. ^f2
  > The path is divided into four main skill levels
  > You can tell whether a lesson is locked, available, or completed based on how its tile appears
- [ux/layout] The path is optional: a library lets players pick any lesson, filtered by
  level, theme or instructor. ^f3
  > You can also filter lessons by skill level, theme, or instructor using the dropdown menus under the search bar
- [fun] The path is framed as celebrating progress. ^f4
  > The Learn Path celebrates your progress and makes learning more engaging.
- [learning] Checked, not there: the article does not say whether challenges come before
  or after the explanation, nor how a wrong answer is explained. ^f5
