# OCI Cloud Free Tier FAQ (Oracle, 2026)

Why: does upgrading to Pay As You Go cost anything while we stay within Always Free?

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Oracle's own FAQ.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: vendor (wants upgrades).
- Weigh: binding on what Oracle charges; silent on whether PAYG gets A1 capacity sooner.

## Findings

- [ops] After upgrading, Always Free resources stay free; you pay only for standard
  resources used; no minimums or prepayment. ^f1
  > with a paid account, you'll continue to have access to Always Free resources, and you'll only pay for the standard resources you use. No minimums and no prepayment are required
- [ops] No downgrade back to a free account once upgraded. ^f2
  > There is no option to downgrade your account.
- [ops] Charges only for usage beyond Always Free. ^f3
  > You will only be charged for services that you use that exceeds Always Free.
- [ops] PAYG accounts have different capacity limits than Always Free ones (not said:
  better A1 availability). ^f4
  > Pay As You Go accounts are subject to different capacity limits than Always Free accounts.
- [ops] Oracle may place temporary card authorisation holds, released in 3-5 days. ^f5
  > Oracle may periodically check the validity of your card, resulting in a temporary “authorization” hold.
- [ops] Upgrade is done from Account Management in the Console, Pay As You Go or
  Monthly Flex. ^f6
  > You may upgrade an account to paid status at any time from the Account Management area in Oracle Cloud Console.
- [ops] Free accounts idle 30+ days may be suspended. ^f7
  > Accounts left idle for 30 days or more may be deemed abandoned
