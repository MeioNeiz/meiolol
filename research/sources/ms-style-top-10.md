# Microsoft Writing Style Guide: Top 10 tips for style and voice

Why: Microsoft's house rules for UI copy.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Microsoft's public writing standard for products and docs.
- Reasoning: fair. Before and after examples, no measurements.
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: qualitative; no numeric limits on this page.

## Findings

- [ux] Shorter is always better: bigger ideas, fewer words. Use bigger ideas, fewer words ^f1
  > Our modern design hinges on crisp minimalism. Shorter is always better.
- [ux] Example of the cut: a 16 word sentence becomes "Ready to buy? Contact us." Use bigger ideas, fewer words ^f2
  > With this: Ready to buy? Contact us.
- [ux] Most important first, keywords at the start for scanning. Get to the point fast ^f3
  > Lead with what's most important. Front-load keywords for scanning.
- [ux] Just enough to decide; prune every excess word. Be brief ^f4
  > Give customers just enough information to make decisions confidently. Prune every excess word.
- [ux] Sentence case by default, never title case. When in doubt, don't capitalize ^f5
  > Default to sentence-style capitalization
- [web/qa] No full stop or colon at the end of titles, headings and UI titles: a
  mechanical rule. Use end punctuation in the right places ^f6
  > Don't use a period or a colon at the end of titles, headings, subheadings, and UI titles.
