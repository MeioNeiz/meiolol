# How to Use the Mobalytics App for League of Legends (beginner guide, Sep 2026)

Why: what Mobalytics' champ select advice is based on now (the desktop guide we hold is
from 2020: [[mobalytics-desktop-guide]]).

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor's own guide, updated Sep 2026; a large, long-running app.
- Reasoning: fair. Says what each recommendation draws on in a phrase, no method.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: inputs are named loosely ("factors like"); no sample or patch-day statement.

## Findings

- [tools/phase] [draft] Pre Game opens in champ select and recommends picks and bans from the meta and the player's champion pool. ^f1
  > it’ll recommend which champions you should pick and ban based on factors like the meta and your champion pool.
- [tools/phase] [draft] Counter-picks appear as enemies lock, for you and allies still to pick. ^f2
  > as your enemies select their champions, you’ll see counter-pick recommendations for you and your remaining allies to consider.
- [tools/phase] Builds after lock-in are chosen on win rate and popularity, then imported. ^f3
  > explore different build options (based on win rate and popularity) and then import your runes and item builds directly into your client.
- [method] [ux/features] The tier list is curated by experts, two lists (low and high elo), not computed; win rate, pick rate, ban rate and matches sit below it. ^f4
  > There are two tier lists curated by our experts, one for low ELO and one for high ELO.
- [ux/controls] Low elo list advised until about high Diamond 2. ^f5
  > we generally recommend using the low ELO tier list until around high Diamond 2.
- [ux/features] Arrows show tier moves against the last patch. ^f6
  > Keep an eye on the up and down arrows to track how champions are changing in placement in comparison to the last patch.
- [ux/uncertainty] Checked, not there: no game counts, minimum games, ranges or patch-day
  rule for the champ select recommendations. ^f7
- [method] (inferred) An expert-curated tier list is Mobalytics' patch-day answer in effect: it can move on patch notes before games exist, where a computed list cannot. ^f8
