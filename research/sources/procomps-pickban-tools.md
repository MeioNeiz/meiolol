# ProComps Hub: LoL pick ban tool, which one is best (Aug 2022)

Why: what the draft simulators (ProDraft, Draftlol) do, for the mock-draft side of an "op" tool.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. 2022, a vendor blog that ends on its own tool.
- Reasoning: fair; gives concrete pros and cons for each.
- Interests: vendor (ProComps).
- Weigh: old; the simulators' basics have not changed but check current state.

## Findings

- [draft] [ux/features] ProDraft (prodraft.leagueoflegends.com) gives separate blue and red links so each team sees the draft from its side; used by many tournaments; all champions open at every stage; dated interface with few settings. ^f1
  > In the tool, you can create a game and different links for blue and red teams.
  > the interface and the features are outdated
- [draft] [ux/features] Draftlol offers the same with settings: disable champions, change timers, disable bans or picks per team; the "crowd favorite" for tournaments. ^f2
  > You can disable champions, change the ban/pick times, disable bans or picks for either team, etc.
- [draft] PickBan is a single-user brainstorming board with a drawing tool for screen sharing, no opponent mode. ^f3
  > PickBan is a single-user LoL Pick Ban tool that you can use when you want to brainstorm about drafting.
- [draft] [ux/features] ProComps can be pointed at a live ProDraft or Draftlol link (copy and paste) to advise during a scrim draft. ^f4
  > ProComps provides the option where you can just copy/paste the link.
- [needs] The stated need: teammates who do not own a champion cannot pick it early, so teams mock draft outside the client. ^f5
  > if your teammate doesn’t have it, you can’t pick that champion before it’s your turn.
