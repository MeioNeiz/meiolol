# Coach Rogue: Rune Customization Secrets ONLY PRO Players Know

Why: what a coach tells players to look at when picking runes, which of it a build page
can show from our data, and which rules our data could test.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. A former pro's (self-stated: 7 years) rules of thumb, shown on his own
  replays and rune pages; the only numbers are pick shares he read off a stats site
  (Jarvan Conqueror 100%, Karma Aery/Comet 50/50), base stats and rune tooltips. No win
  rate is given for any rule. 93.9k subscribers, 150k views, 6k likes.
- Reasoning: fair. Each rule names its mechanism (Glacial helps allies not you, Aery's
  short cooldown needs autos, Bone Plating only blocks one enemy), but the costs of the
  "payoff" choice are asserted, not measured, and Hail of Blades and several runes are
  left out (top comment, 480 likes).
- Interests: sells guides and coaching (coachrogue.com, Patreon) and promotes his
  Waveclear app in the description; the video itself is not about the app.
- Weigh: the frame is the useful part: rune choice depends on the lane opponent, which
  our per-matchup builds can show. Each "take X against Y" is a testable split; our
  learned.md (Items) says runes in a row differ by about 0.6 points of true win rate,
  so expect small effects. Captions: "ChutaBunda" at 3:36 is a player name.
