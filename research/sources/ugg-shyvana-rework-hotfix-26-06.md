# U.GG News: Shyvana rework given emergency hotfix after 57% win rate (18 Mar 2026)

Why: how Riot itself reads a changed champion's first-day win rate, and which way it
expects the number to move next.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B for the quote (Riot's lead designer, first-hand, relayed by a stat site);
  one champion, one number, no sample size. The slug's "26-06" is a guess at the patch;
  the date is the article's.
- Reasoning: weak. A statement of expectation, no data on how much such champions rise.
- Interests: Riot explaining a nerf; U.GG driving traffic to its build page.
- Weigh: shows the practice (act on under a day of data with a direction prior for
  reworks), not a size. A rework is a new kit; a number tweak has no such learning.

## Findings

- [method/dynamics] Riot acted on under a day of data: a micropatch nerf the day the
  rework went live. ^f1
  > "she came out the oven with a 57% win rate in less than 12 hours on the Rift"
- [method/dynamics] Riot's stated prior for a rework's first-day number is a rise from
  there as players learn it, so a high day-one rate is read as an underestimate. ^f2
  > "We expect her to only get stronger as players optimize so we put together a micro patch nerf."
- [method/dynamics] The stat site's own reading of early numbers on new kits: strength
  and unfamiliarity are mixed and cannot be told apart yet. ^f3
  > "Often, early release champions/reworks can be caught up in a mix of being overpowered and simply not being understood."
- [data] A hotfix inside the patch means a patch's first day can be a different game
  from its second: the nerf list covers five abilities, live within the day. ^f4
  > "It should be live now."
- [method/dynamics] Checked, not there: sample size, rank filter, how much Riot expected
  her to rise, any such expectation for plain number changes. ^f5
- [method/dynamics] (inferred) For us: a patch's day 0 for a reworked or hotfixed
  champion is not the same champion as days 1+, so a micropatch needs its own boundary
  in the patch offset (or the champion's day-0 games dropped), and reworks alone carry
  a learning trend; our 16.21 "no curve" finding covers number changes only. ^f6
