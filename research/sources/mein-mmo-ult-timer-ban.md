# Riot bans enemy ultimate timer apps from 13 March 2025 (Mein-MMO)

Why: date and scope of the 2025 enemy ultimate timer removal.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Gaming news site, dated 13 Mar 2025, links the @RiotGamesDevRel X post
  (status 1899532362637250955) and the Reddit thread; editor's note says a passage was
  corrected for which apps are banned.
- Reasoning: fair. Reports the rule, then community debate on jungle timers.
- Interests: none known (ad-funded news).
- Weigh: date and scope credible; the Riot wording itself is in the X post, not quoted
  here. The rule text is also in [[overwolf-riot-compliance#^f4]] and
  [[riot-developer-api-policy#^f4]].

## Findings

- [tools/removed] [policy] In game: from 13 March 2025 Riot acts against apps offering
  enemy ultimate timers. ^f1
  > Seit dem 13.03.2025 will Riot gegen Apps, die eine Enemy-Ultimate-Timer-Funktion bieten, vorgehen.
- [tools/removed] [policy] The ban covers manual timers the player starts as well as
  automatic ones. ^f2
  > Dabei ist es egal, ob es manuelle Timer sind, die ihr selbst startet, oder solche, die automatisch gestartet werden.
- [tools/removed] [policy] Announced by Riot Games Developer Relations on X as a change to
  its terms: apps must remove enemy ultimate timers or be banned. ^f3
  > postete der offizielle Account Riot Games Developer Relations, dass man die Nutzungsbedingungen geändert hat, und alle Third-Party-Apps, die Enemy Ultimate Timer entfernen müssen, oder sie werden verboten.
- [tools] [ux/features] Before the ban many companion apps showed enemy ult cooldowns
  alongside runes, builds, counters and skill order. ^f4
  > Viele davon zeigen auch die akteulle Abklingzeit der Ultimates an, doch das verbietet Riot jetzt.
- [needs] Community view in the linked thread: tracking jungle timers is a skill that
  separates junglers; others want official timers. ^f5
  > dass das Tracken der Timer und die Route des Jungles seit dem Start des Spiels ein Skill sei, der Jungler voneinander unterscheidet.
- [policy] (inferred) Riot's pattern: a feature common across apps (ult timers, like
  auto-accept and champ select names) is removed by a DevRel announcement with no
  grandfathering; anything an "op" tool adds in game is exposed to the same treatment,
  even behind a login. ^f6
