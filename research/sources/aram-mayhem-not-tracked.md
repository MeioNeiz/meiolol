# ARAM Mayhem not being tracked in websites is the best thing ever (r/ARAM, Jan 2026)

Why: what casual-mode players want from stats tools, and the meta-homogenisation worry.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. 244 upvotes but only 79% upvoted, 114 comments; the top reply (178)
  disagrees with the post. Jan 2026, current mode.
- Reasoning: fair; both sides argue (discovery vs access to information for time-poor
  players).
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: a direct test of the "tier lists homogenise play" worry in a casual mode: the
  community splits, and personal stats and interaction data win more upvotes than the
  anti-tracking post's premise.

## Findings

- [needs] [fun] Post: no tracking means players can't blindly follow metas and must
  think for themselves; cites TFT guide-following. ^f1
  > Makes it harder for people to blindly follow metas, allowing people to think for themselves.
- [market] Reception: 244 upvotes at 79% upvoted, so contested. ^f2
  > ARAM Mayhem not being tracked in websites is the best thing ever.
- [needs] Top reply (178) disagrees: unclear augment tooltips mean players want damage
  and healing stats to see what affects what. ^f3
  > Disagree, actually. There are a lot of niche builds I'd like to do but since the tooltips for augments are kind of unclear, I can't tell if the interaction would work out like I think it would.
- [fun] [needs] Wanted: a forum-style site listing every augment with user-suggested
  builds and combos, no win rates, "I had fun with this". ^f4
  > What I want is a forum style website that lists every augment with user suggested builds/champions/augment interactions/discussion/etc.
  > No winrate tracking, just “hey I had fun with this combo”.
- [fun] [needs] Players want their own Mayhem stats to analyse for fun and to show
  friends; one is missing 900-1000 untracked games. ^f5
  > I really like to see my own statistics and even analyse them for fun.
  > I hate it, I wanna show my friends cool match stats.
- [data] A hobby developer says Riot's API does not give special modes such as Mayhem,
  so their app shows no recent matches (claim, not verified here). ^f6
  > I haven't been able to track any of the special gamemodes I play on my testing app because Riot doesn't give the API for those.
- [needs] [tools] Casual ARAM players use OP.GG to skip reading patch notes and maximise
  play time with little time to study. ^f7
  > As ARAM only player, I have used OP.GG for years so that I don’t have to catch up with patch notes to play the few games I play on a given week.
  > Not having the ability to use the tools mainly affects those that have limited time to invest in the game.
- [tools] [market] Commenters mention Blitz ranking Mayhem augments and a paid Your.gg
  overlay recommending augments. ^f8
  > Your.gg does have an overlay that tells you which augment is best for your champion but it’s a paid membership
