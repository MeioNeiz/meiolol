# Chess.com Help: What are Skills on Chess.com?

Why: a progression layer inside Game Review; a reason to open the next review.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor help page for a new feature (2026); no data on use.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: shows the direction (rewarding good moves, not only flagging bad ones); not
  proven to retain.

## Findings

- [ux/postgame] [learning] Skills reward moves that show a good habit (develop early,
  spot a fork, castle in time) with a point each, found in Game Review. ^f1
  > Skills is a feature in Game Review that rewards you for making moves that show good chess habits, like developing your pieces early, spotting a fork, or castling at the right time.
- [ux/postgame] Skill moves are marked in the move list; reaching one plays a short
  animation and adds the point. ^f2
  > As you step through the game and reach one of those moves, you'll see a short animation play and your point get added.
- [ux/postgame] Highlights shows the points waiting in this game (e.g. +2) before you
  start, a pull to step through. ^f3
  > The Highlights screen also shows how many Skill points are up for grabs in that game (for example, "+2") before you've even started reviewing.
- [learning] Enough points masters a skill and unlocks the next; five categories
  (Fundamentals, Openings, Tactics, Strategy, Endgame). ^f4
  > **Mastering a Skill unlocks the next one in that category.**
- [ux/postgame] Mastery gets a celebration animation and Coach congratulations. ^f5
  > When you collect enough points to master a Skill, you'll see a celebration animation and your Coach will congratulate you.
- [ux/postgame] On mobile Skills takes the action row; otherwise it shows Retry, Show and
  Best. ^f6
  > If Skills is off, or the move can’t earn a point, you’ll see Retry, Show, and Best instead.
