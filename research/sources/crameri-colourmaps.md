# Fabio Crameri: Scientific colour maps

Why: a source of ready-made diverging maps, including dark-centred ones for dark
backgrounds.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Version 8.0.1, archived on Zenodo, used across geoscience and in
  matplotlib via cmcrameri ([[cmcrameri-berlin]]).
- Reasoning: fair on this page; the reasoning is in [[crameri-misuse-colour-2020]].
- Interests: none (free).
- Weigh: page lists properties; the map data comes from cmcrameri.

## Findings

- [ux/colour] The maps are built to be perceptually uniform and ordered. ^f1
  > The colour gradients are perceptually uniform and ordered to represent data both fairly – without visual distortion – and intuitively
- [ux/colour] They stay readable for colour-vision deficient readers and in black and
  white. ^f2
  > The colour combinations are readable both by colour-vision deficient and colour-blind people, and even when printed in black&white
- [ux/colour] Versioned and archived, so a chosen map can be cited and reproduced. ^f3
  > The colour maps and their diagnostics are permanently archived and versioned
