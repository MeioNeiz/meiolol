# Chess.com News: Game Review Now Available For All Chess.com Members

Why: the vendor's own statement of what Game Review is for and why each piece exists.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Official launch post (updated 9 Nov 2022; 372 comments).
- Reasoning: fair: gives the why for accuracy, retry, Brilliant and Great.
- Interests: vendor marketing; names the team.
- Weigh: the stated aims (enjoy, celebrate, understand) are the design intent; the
  2026 help pages ([[chesscom-game-review-help]]) show what survived.

## Findings

- [ux/postgame] [learning] Stated purpose: relive your best moves, find where it went
  wrong, and get coach explanations; enjoyment is named next to learning. ^f1
  > Game Review is a revolutionary new tool to help you better understand and enjoy your games. You'll be able to relive your greatest moves, pinpoint the moments where things went wrong, and get explanations from your Virtual Coach to help you learn from your mistakes.
- [learning] The coach exists to translate engine verdicts into why. ^f2
  > Game Review helps you understand computer evaluations by having your virtual coach explain WHY moves are good and bad
- [ux/postgame] [fun] Accuracy is one of the most popular features; players celebrate
  records, and the rescale aimed to make it more engaging and rewarding. ^f3
  > Since we introduced Accuracy Scores, they quickly became one of the most popular features on Chess.com.
  > This encouraged us to work hard to improve our Accuracy Scores and make them even more engaging and rewarding.
- [ux/postgame] [learning] Retry key moves gives a new adjusted accuracy if you find the
  better move: the fix is rewarded with a number. ^f4
  > You'll have the chance to retry mistakes that you made during the game, and you'll then get a new adjusted accuracy score based on your improvements!
- [ux/postgame] Key moves cover good moves as well as missed chances; Start Review walks
  them in order. ^f5
  > you'll be escorted through critical positions throughout the game—both good moves and missed opportunities.
- [ux/trust] Brilliant was reworked after feedback that it was too rare and not always
  brilliant: now a sacrifice that is best or nearly best, softer for new players. ^f6
  > we've heard your feedback that Brilliant Moves can be too rare and sometimes not that brilliant.
  > Brilliant Moves will also be a bit more generously awarded for newer players
- [ux/postgame] Great was added so more good moves get praise: winning move, saving
  resource, or only good move; blue outline and single !. ^f7
  > Brilliant Moves may be rare, but there are many other special moves that deserve to be celebrated and recognized in a chess game.
  > If you found a winning move, spotted a resource to save a losing position, or found the only good move in a position, you surely deserve some praise.
- [tools/paid] At launch Platinum had unlimited reviews and Diamond unlimited Coach
  explanations; Gold lost unlimited review on 1 Sep (plan change). ^f8
  > Platinum members have unlimited access to game reviews, while Diamond members also receive unlimited access to Coach explanations!
  > Gold members no longer have unlimited access to game reviews.
