# OP.GG Help: How to link a Riot account

Why: OP.GG's account model: own login plus RSO as a connected app.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor help page.
- Reasoning: fair. Steps only.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: short, plain; fits [[opgg-help-rso]].

## Findings

- [tools/accounts] Two layers: an OP.GG account (own login) first, then Riot linked under Connected Apps through Riot's own sign-in page (RSO). ^f1
  > In the [Connected Apps] tab, click the Riot Games connect button.
  > You will be redirected to the Riot Games login page.
- [tools/accounts] A Riot account belongs to one OP.GG account at a time: linking it elsewhere unlinks the old one, so a verified claim follows whoever proves Riot ownership last. ^f2
  > If a Riot account is already linked to an OP.GG account and it is linked again to a new OP.GG account, the link with the previous OP.GG account will be automatically disconnected.
