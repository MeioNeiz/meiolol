# Phreak: Patch 26.07 Preview (YouTube, 26 Mar 2026)

Why: do Riot's designers publish an expected win-rate change per buff or nerf that we
could use as the prior mean of a champion's patch offset, and test on 16.23?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. First-hand, by the designer in charge of live balance at the time;
  25k views. Auto captions (names and numbers misheard: "10.7" for 26.07, "N steps"
  for Endstep).
- Reasoning: fair. Explains why each change was made and what could go wrong; no
  numbers on expected effect.
- Interests: Riot explaining its own patch.
- Weigh: trust what it shows about the form of Riot's public forecasts (direction, role
  skew, hedged); the absence of numbers is for this one video, older rundowns not
  checked.

## Findings

- [method/dynamics] Checked, not there: no expected win-rate change in points for any
  champion in the patch (Shyvana, Cassiopeia, Kalista, Rell, Karma, Nami, Ornn, Singed,
  Veigar); each change gets a reason and a direction only. Whole transcript grepped for
  "win rate", "percent", "%", "worth", "expect". ^f1
- [method/dynamics] The forecasts he does give are about where the change lands (role
  or rank skew), hedged, and based on what the same change did before. @17:07 ^f2
  > "we think from historical data this is going to be mid skewed. There's a chance that we are wrong."
- [method/dynamics] [method/causal] He names why one patch's win rate move is a poor
  measure of a change: other champions changed in the same patch, and a pick-rate rise
  in one role brought lower-skill players. @17:07 ^f3
  > "there can always be weird meta game factors with uh measuring win rate on an individual uh patch"
- [method/dynamics] Play tests don't give Riot a win-rate forecast for a systems change.
  @10:56 ^f4
  > "it's hard to like prove out win rate from play tests"
- [data] This was his last patch preview: he left live design after 26.07, so no Phreak
  rundown exists for 26.22 or 26.23 (his channel's later videos are League Classic
  previews; checked 14 Nov 2026). He points to another Riot designer's channel
  (captions: "N steps", Riot Endstep) for design content. @0:00, @1:37 ^f5
  > "This will be my last patch covering as a live designer."
- [method/dynamics] Bugs are fixed between patches by micropatch within days of
  release (Sylas with Shyvana Q, patched on the 24th for a video recorded on the 25th).
  @13:13 ^f6
  > "These bugs got micro patched."
- [method/dynamics] (inferred) A designer's public preview is not a usable numeric
  prior for 16.23: no numbers, and the series has ended. What it could give is a sign
  and a role skew per change, which the patch notes' buff or nerf label already gives. ^f7
