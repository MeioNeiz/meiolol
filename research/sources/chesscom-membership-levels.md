# Chess.com Help: What does each level of premium membership get me?

Why: what of Game Review is free and what each paid tier adds.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Vendor help page; plans effective since 1 Sep 2022, page updated Jul 2026.
- Reasoning: weak (a list).
- Interests: vendor sales page in help form.
- Weigh: the comparison table is an image grid (ticks not in the raw); the per-plan
  lists are text. Prices are not on this page.

## Findings

- [tools/paid] Free accounts get one full Game Review a day. ^f1
  > 1 full game review analysis per day
- [tools/paid] Platinum gets unlimited Game Review and analysis. ^f2
  > UNLIMITED game review and analysis
- [tools/paid] Diamond adds Coach explanations on all moves (so lower tiers get them on
  fewer moves), plus Insights across all games. ^f3
  > Unlimited Game Review PLUS get coach explanations on all of your moves!
  > Access the Insights feature to see stats and data on all your games!
- [tools/paid] Diamond also lists Advanced Stats and Brilliant Moves, and custom analysis
  settings. ^f4
  > Access to [Advanced Stats](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/15451190-how-do-i-use-advanced-stats) and Brilliant Moves
- [tools/paid] Free accounts also get 3 puzzles a day and one extra lesson a day: daily
  allowances across the product, not only review. ^f5
  > Practice with puzzles, 3 per day
- [tools/paid] Family plan: six accounts share one Diamond yearly. ^f6
  > allows up to six Chess.com members to share a Diamond yearly membership
