# League of Graphs Privacy policy (WARGRAPHS)

Why: how a French (EU, CNIL-supervised) lookup site states its basis and retention for
player data it shows without sign-up.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Long-running site (League of Graphs, Porofessor; part of the M.O.B.A.
  Network, [[porofessor-faq#^f6]]); EU company, so GDPR applies directly. Live page is
  behind Cloudflare; Wayback copy of 1 Apr 2026, policy dated 16 Nov 2022.
- Reasoning: weak. Generic template text (mentions Valofessor contact for League site).
- Interests: WARGRAPHS.
- Weigh: shows the practical floor an EU lookup site accepts: legitimate interest, long
  retention, rights handled by email. Not evidence the regulator agrees.

## Findings

- [privacy] Personal data processed: IP addresses and browsing, pseudonyms and player
  IDs. ^f1
  > \- IP addresses and browsing details;
  > \- Pseudonyms and ID players.
- [privacy] Player data reaches them from third parties (the Riot API) without the
  player's involvement. ^f2
  > \- the transmission of information from third parties;
- [privacy] Lawful bases: consent for cookies, legitimate interest, legal obligation;
  legitimate interest is to provide and improve the service. ^f3
  > \- when the person has specifically given consent to it (regarding cookies);
  > WARGRAPHS’ legitimate interest is, for instance, to provide a better and personalized service or to improve its services.
- [privacy] Retention: browsing data 7 days; pseudonyms and player IDs 10 years from the
  player's last connection on Riot Games. ^f4
  > IP adresses: Browsing data 7 days from last activity
  > Pseudonyms and players: ID 10 years from last connection on RIOT GAMES
- [privacy] Lists GDPR rights including deletion and objection; complaint to the CNIL. ^f5
  > \- right to deletion (right to request the deletion of its data from the processing) ;
  > \- the right to appeal to a data protection authority (CNIL).
- [privacy] Rights exercised by email or form with proof of identity (e.g. signed ID
  card copy); request records kept 5 years. ^f6
  > The exercise of these rights by a person is done by proving his identity (e.g. copy of your identity card signed) by mail to the following address: support@porofessor.gg
  > Requests to exercise rights are processed for tracking and proof purposes and are kept for 5 years.
- [privacy] (inferred) An ID card does not prove ownership of a Riot ID; the policy
  names no game-account ownership check for removal requests. ^f7
