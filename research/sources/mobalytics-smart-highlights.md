# Mobalytics Smart Highlights landing page

Why: what Mobalytics' current post-game puts first (moments as clips), and its sharing hook.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor's live landing page.
- Reasoning: weak. Feature list only.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: "AI studies every second" is marketing; the free or paid status conflicts with [[mobalytics-plus#^f4]] (top 5 plays listed as a Plus feature).

## Findings

- [learning] [tools/phase] Post game: clips generate automatically after every game, no replay digging or manual clipping. ^f1
  > Tired of going through replays or forgetting to clip? Smart Highlights instantly generate after every game.
- [learning] [ux/postgame] The review is moments: the AI picks the player's best and worst plays. ^f2
  > Our AI Identifies Your Best and Worst Plays
- [ux/postgame] Each moment carries facts: damage stats and every champion's items at that time. ^f3
  > Each highlight includes damage stats and even what items champions had at the time so you can understand them even better.
- [fun] Framed for fun as well as learning: relive a clutch play or laugh at a disaster. ^f4
  > Whether you want to relive a clutch play or laugh at a disaster, save clips in your highlight manager to watch any time.
- [fun] Return and share hook: one-click links to share highlights. ^f5
  > Link directly to your highlights and share them to different places with one click.
- [tools/paid] The page sells it as free; Windows only. ^f6
  > Get the Smartest Highlights for League of Legends FREE now
  > Currently only available for Windows
- [ux/postgame] (inferred) Mobalytics has moved its post game from a score (GPI) towards moments with context, the shape its 2017 letter said it wanted ([[mobalytics-letter-to-users#^f10]]). ^f7
