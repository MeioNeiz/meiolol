# Overwolf developers newsletter Aug 2025: Riot's LoL Season 3 feature removals

Why: the 2025 wave of in-game removals with a date, beyond enemy ult timers.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Overwolf's own developer newsletter (Aug 2025), platform relaying Riot's
  rules to the apps it hosts (Porofessor, Mobalytics, Blitz-style overlays).
- Reasoning: fair. List plus a one-line reason.
- Interests: Overwolf (needs Riot's goodwill; asks devs to comply).
- Weigh: matches [[overwolf-riot-compliance#^f4]], which is the current text. The list
  widens March 2025's enemy ult timer ban ([[mein-mmo-ult-timer-ban#^f1]]) to all
  enemy ability and summoner spell cooldowns.

## Findings

- [tools/removed] [policy] Riot's Season 3 guidelines, compliance deadline 27 Aug 2025. ^f1
  > With Season 3 of League of Legends right around the corner, Riot has introduced new guidelines that will affect the functionality of many third-party apps. Please make sure to review and act on these changes before August 27, 2025.
- [tools/removed] [policy] In game: power spike alerts must go. ^f2
  > Notifications that alert power spikes (e.g., "X champion has hit level 6")
- [tools/removed] [policy] In game: notifications suggesting game decisions must go. ^f3
  > Notifications that suggest game decisions (e.g., "Gank top lane now!")
- [tools/removed] [policy] In game: tracking enemy ability or summoner spell cooldowns
  must go (wider than the March 2025 ult-only rule). ^f4
  > Tracking enemy ability or summoner spell cooldowns
- [tools/removed] [policy] Displaying minor rune data must go (scope not explained). ^f5
  > Displaying minor rune data
- [policy] Stated aim: competitive integrity and balance. ^f6
  > These changes aim to preserve competitive integrity and gameplay balance.
- [tools/removed] Checked, not there: nothing on champ select, pick suggestions, auto
  accept, auto pick or ban, or AI coaching in this list. ^f7
- [market] Same newsletter promotes in-app house ads and brand campaigns: Overwolf apps
  are ad-funded. ^f8
  > These partnerships are driving higher advertiser demand, stronger monetization opportunities, and sustainable revenue growth across the Overwolf ecosystem.
