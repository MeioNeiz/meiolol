# ProComps on the Overwolf app store (v10.1.1, Oct 2026)

Why: the draft assistant that ties champ select to the pick-ban simulators, and the only draft app seen with teams and shared champion pools.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. 563K downloads but only 22 ratings (4.2), newest visible review Dec 2024; v10.1.1 dated 15 Oct 2026, so still shipped.
- Reasoning: weak. Feature list.
- Interests: vendor copy; a coach reviewer is positive.
- Weigh: the friends and teams model is the useful part for the "op" tool; reach per rating is thin.

## Findings

- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Champ select: real-time draft assistant with team gamestyle and scaling insights, strategic alerts, and suggestions from counters, synergies, gamestyles and scaling. ^f1
  > Live insights into team gamestyles, scaling, and strategic alerts.
  > Champion suggestions based on counters, synergies, gamestyles, scaling and more.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Loading screen or pre-game: a guide for planning tactics for each match. ^f2
  > Pre-game guide for planning tactics tailored to each match.
- [draft] Reads drafts from the League client and from the ProDraft and Draftlol simulators (scrims and tournaments). ^f3
  > Integrates with League Client, Prodraft, & Draftlol
- [tools/accounts] [ux/features] Friends and teams: generate a champion pool, invite friends and share pools; create teams whose pools merge across devices. ^f4
  > Invite friends and share champion pools
  > Create teams, invite friends, and integrate champion pools seamlessly across devices.
- [ux/features] Between games: custom tier lists per lane that feed live draft insights; a comp builder and a comp generator from a champion pool. ^f5
  > Create custom champion tier lists for each lane
  > Input your champion pool and preferences
- [tools/paid] Free with ads; no subscription listed (short page lines, unquotable). ^f6
- [needs] A coach of amateur teams calls it the go-to drafting app; another user values making their own tier list when they disagree with the default. ^f7
  > As a fellow team coach when teaching amateur teams this is by far my go-to app.
  > If you do not agree with the tier-list (for example, you have high skills with a certain champion), there is the option to create your own tier-list.
- [market] 563K downloads, 4.2 from 22 ratings, Discord of 7000+ members (short lines). ^f8
  > Join more than 7000 community members on our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/3BTGqnpQyf)
