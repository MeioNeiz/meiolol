## Findings

- [draft] The blind first pick should be a flex pick, a champion with no direct counter, or the comp's anchor. ^f1
  > Uncontested champions with no direct counter
- [draft] Last pick is for the counter; patterns are anti-hook, anti-engage, anti-assassin, lane matchup. ^f2
  > The last pick (red side 10th pick) is for the counter.
- [draft] Flex picks hide the role so the enemy commits its counter first. ^f3
  > By flex-picking, you force the enemy to commit their counter before you reveal the role.
- [draft] Comp first: choose a strategy, then champions that fit it. ^f4
  > A team composition is the strategy the team executes. Choose one before drafting, then pick champions that fit.
- [draft] Five archetypes, each with what it needs and what beats it: engage, poke, scaling, protect the carry, split push. ^f5
  > Counters: early-game snowball comps that win before your team scales
- [draft] Engage loses to poke that wears it down first; poke loses to engage that closes the gap. ^f6
  > Counters: teams that poke you down before the engage lands
- [draft] [learning] Listed mistakes: no plan, forced comps, ignoring patch meta. ^f7
  > Ignoring patch meta: picking champions the patch just nerfed
- [learning] It suggests drafting practice by simulation and reviewing drafts that lost. ^f8
  > Any loss that happened in draft, review. Would a different pick have worked?
- [draft] Checked, not there: nothing on lane priority, power spikes by minute, or solo queue specifics (it says blind pick is a different context). ^f9
