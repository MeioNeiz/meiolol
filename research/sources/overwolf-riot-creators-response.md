# Supporting Creators in Riot's Ecosystem: Overwolf's response to Riot's third-party ad ban (2025)

Why: the 2025 ad ban changes how League companion apps can make money, so what is paid
versus free.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Overwolf's blog, quoting the @RiotGamesDevRel post (status
  1928141776415568329, 29 May 2025 by id and context). The post is undated on the page.
- Reasoning: fair. Defensive; no figures.
- Interests: Overwolf (ad platform; its revenue is at stake).
- Weigh: Riot's quoted sentence is the fact; Overwolf's claims about its ads are vendor
  claims.

## Findings

- [tools/removed] [tools/paid] [policy] 29 May (2025): Riot banned third-party in-game
  ads, in overlays, loading screens and the Riot Client. ^f1
  > On May 29th, Riot [announced a policy change](https://x.com/RiotGamesDevRel/status/1928141776415568329?ref=blog.overwolf.com): _"Third party in-game advertisements are no longer allowed, this includes ads in overlays, during loading screens, and in the Riot Client"_.
- [market] Overwolf frames app makers' livelihoods as dependent on this revenue. ^f2
  > we also recognize the concern this raises for the many creators whose livelihoods depend on building useful tools and overlays.
- [tools/paid] (claim) Overwolf says its ads are embedded in app windows, never floating. ^f3
  > All ads served through Overwolf are embedded in app windows and require user engagement.
- [tools/paid] Overwolf's plan: clarify with Riot and look for other monetisation. ^f4
  > We'll explore alternative monetization methods, always keeping user safety and game integrity in mind.
- [policy] Overwolf hints that other actors caused the rule. ^f5
  > Unfortunately, not every actor in this space shares the same commitment, and we hope the actions of a few won't diminish the opportunities for many.
- [tools/paid] (inferred) Since mid-2025 ad money cannot come from anything shown in the
  Riot Client, champ select overlay, loading screen or in-game overlay; ads survive only
  in desktop app windows and on websites, which pushes tools toward subscriptions. For
  meiolol: no ads in any overlay or client surface. ^f6
