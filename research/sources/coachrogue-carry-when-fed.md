# Coach Rogue: THIS Is Why You Can't Carry Games When You're Fed

Why: coach-stat-claims: do fed players lose as often as the video implies, and is the
14-minute mid tower the turning point he says?

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Coaching sessions and single replays (Vex, Miss Fortune, Ezreal, Zeri,
  his own pro game); the numbers are his own worked sums for one moment (602 gold, 605
  XP), not measured over games. October 2024, about patch 14.20. 325k views, 9.8k likes.
- Reasoning: fair. Each mistake comes with a mechanism (wave timing, tower armour after
  plates, mid priority) and a cost, but nothing tests how often the mistakes decide games,
  and the "convert half of these losses" figure is his coaching experience.
- Interests: Patreon and coaching plugs.
- Weigh: two parts are checkable in our data: how often a fed player loses when the rest
  of the team is behind ([[fed-player-win-rate-measured]]) and whether a mid outer tower
  counts for more than a side one, around 14 minutes or not
  ([[coach-objectives-measured]]). Auto captions: "nefy", "nefir" are Naafiri, "ezil"
  Ezreal, "02" 0/2.

## Findings

- [learning] (claim) His students with a "fed but lost" match history usually turn at least half of those losses into wins. @0:00 ^f1
  > my students who have a match history with this kind of problem are usually able to convert at least 50% of these losses into wins
- [learning] Mistake 1: chasing kills after a won fight instead of pushing the nearest wave; his sum for one Miss Fortune chase puts the swing at 602 gold and 605 XP, twice the kill's gold. @2:16 @5:16 ^f2
  > when you're losing games with score lines like this your problem is not a shortage of kills and gold it's that the kills you do get don't actually mean anything
  > the total go Val of all of this is a swing of 602 gold and 605 XP
- [learning] (claim) A level up's stats are worth about 600 gold on average. @6:46 ^f3
  > the value stats given to a champion on average for a level up is 600 gold worth
- [learning] Mistake 2: once about 1,000 gold or a level ahead in lane, stop freezing; push, take plates or move to other lanes. @10:26 ^f4
  > once you've made a significant enough lead in your own leane usually around 1,000 gold or a full level lead you should basically never freeze
- [learning] Mistake 3: by 14 minutes the team's strongest player should hold mid; 14 minutes is when plates fall and macro begins. @12:13 @13:43 ^f5
  > 14 minutes is your absolute cut off for when you have to swap into mid
  > 14 minutes is the beginning of macro
- [learning] One-replay evidence for the mid tower as a turning point: 16 kills each before it fell, one-sided after. @13:43 @15:13 ^f6
  > before this mid Tower was taken the game was 50/50 both teams are trading back kills evenly and the score is 16 to 16
- [learning] Mistake 4: lost time (needless recalls, stopping for jungle camps) costs most when you are the fed one. @17:20 ^f7
  > stopping to take jungle camps when you are fed especially if your teammates are behind
- [market] Reception: 325k views; a coach in the comments agrees tempo beats kills and notes the mistake gives no feedback. ^f8
  > I value tempo > kills in almost every scenario
- [data] Checked, not there: no data set; no rate of fed players losing, no measure of how much a mid tower or a chased kill moves win chance. ^f9
