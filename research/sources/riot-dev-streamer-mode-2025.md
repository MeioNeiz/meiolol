# /dev: Account linking and streamer mode (Riot Phroxzon, 2025-10-06)

Why: the 2025 direction on player identity in lobbies and in third-party apps, the
data lookup and scouting tools depend on.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Official League dev blog by Riot Phroxzon, dated 06/10/2025 (en-gb).
- Reasoning: fair. Gives the harm (inting and griefing streamers) for streamer mode.
- Interests: Riot.
- Weigh: binding direction for product; the third-party effect is one clause, not a
  developer policy text.

## Findings

- [policy] Streamer mode (25.20) offers "Hide My Name": Riot ID hidden from others in
  champ select, in game and in third-party apps. ^f1
  > This will hide your RiotID and Game Name to all other players in champ select, in game, and in third party apps
- [policy] "Hide My Everything" also hides account progression (mastery, challenges,
  rank border) in third-party apps. ^f2
  > This will hide your RiotID, Game Name, **and** all of your account progression to all other players in champ select, in game, and in third party apps.
- [policy] Rationale: protect pros and creators from players who int or camp them once
  identified. ^f3
  > what isn’t cool are the players that run it down in these players’ games to get a cheap laugh, camping these player’s lanes
- [policy] Built with creators and pros. ^f4
  > we’ve built an upgraded streamer mode in partnership with our creators and pros to protect against negative actions like these.
- [policy] Same post: alt account linking (25.23) applies penalties across accounts
  using hardware and behavioural data, enabled by Vanguard. ^f5
  > we will use a variety of hardware and behavioral data points to link a player’s identity across their accounts
- [policy] Framing: fair games where both teams have a fair shot. ^f6
  > we believe League is at its best when both teams have a fair shot at winning
- [policy] (inferred) Riot keeps narrowing what third-party tools can learn about other
  players in a live lobby (2022 champ select anonymity, [[riot-champ-select-anon-apps-1222#^f1]];
  2025 opt-in hiding in third-party apps; [[itero-companion-apps#^f6]] reports Porofessor
  scouting broken). Tools built on scouting named opponents are moving against Riot;
  tools about the player themselves are not. ^f7
- [fun] Related articles show a Riot "Team Voice" dev update (08/09/2026): Riot is
  building first-party voice (not read). ^f8
  > TL;DW: Team Voice, Classic & More Dev Update
