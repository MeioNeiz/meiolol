# Fandom: Licensing

Why: under what licence may we reuse Leaguepedia data, and how do we credit it?

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Fandom's legal page.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: Fandom is the host.
- Weigh: governs wiki text; facts (who picked what) are arguably not copyrightable, but credit costs nothing.

## Findings

- [policy] Wiki text is CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported unless a wiki states otherwise. ^f1
  > the text on Fandom communities (known as “wikis”) is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike License 3.0 (Unported) (CC BY-SA)
- [policy] Reuse, commercial included, is allowed with attribution and share-alike on derivatives. ^f2
  > including commercial use, in accordance with the CC BY-SA license. Such use is allowed where attribution is given and the same freedom to re-use and re-distribute applies to any derivative works
- [policy] Attribution by a hyperlink to the article(s) is enough. ^f3
  > a) attribution through a hyperlink (where possible) or URL to the article or articles you contributed to
- [policy] Example credit line for reuse on your own site. ^f4
  > This article uses material from the [“Endor”](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Endor) article on the [Star Wars wiki](https://starwars.fandom.com/) at [Fandom](https://www.fandom.com/) and is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- [policy] Images are not covered by the text licence (we use our own champion art anyway). ^f5
  > Non-text media on Fandom should not be assumed to be available under the same license as the text.
