# WaveClear on the Overwolf app store (v1.16.3.47, Oct 2026)

Why: what is the practice app Coach Rogue promotes in [[coachrogue-moneyball-stats]], how is
it built and paid for, and how far has it got? The video's bit.ly link redirects here (with
`utm_campaign=beta`).

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Store listing: 30.7K downloads, 25 ratings averaging 4.3; earliest review April
  2025; version 1.16.3.47 dated 13 Oct 2026, so actively shipped for 18 months or more.
- Reasoning: fair; a feature list with no evidence that "focus" or mental training improves
  play.
- Interests: the developer's own store copy, promoted by a coach who calls it "my app".
- Weigh: platform, price and reach are facts; the "mental performance" framing is marketing.
  Reviews are mostly short Portuguese ones, one naming a Brazilian creator partnership, so
  reach looks like influencer-driven bursts rather than organic use.

## Findings

- [tools] [learning] WaveClear pitches itself as a real-time mental performance and VOD
  debrief coach for League, not a stats tool. ^f1
  > Real-time mental performance and VOD debrief coach for League - improve skills and rank up, for free
- [ux/features] [learning] Features: VOD and mental debrief synced to key moments, a live
  focus tracker with skill challenges, an experimental AI coach ("Coach Aimes"), one-skill-at-a-time
  training tracked over time, and a draft companion for setting a goal before the match. ^f2
  > VOD & Mental Debrief - review gameplay with mental and game-skill insights synced to key moments.
  > Focus Tracker - train live challenges while tracking focus, consistency, and performance.
  > Coach Aimes - experimental AI mental performance coach for reflection, debrief, and training.
  > Draft Companion - set your goal and prepare before the match.
- [ux/features] Supports draft, ranked solo/duo, ranked flex and ARAM queues. ^f3
  > Supports Draft, Ranked Solo/Duo, Ranked Flex, and ARAM.
- [market] Free with in-app ads (Overwolf pays the creator 70% of ad revenue); no
  subscription is listed. ^f4
  > WaveClear App contains ads.App creators get 70% of the ad revenue.
- [market] 30.7K downloads and 25 ratings averaging 4.3 out of 5 (short page lines,
  unquotable). ^f5
- [market] Overwolf client app (not Electron), 8.8 MB; version 1.16.3.47 dated 13 Oct 2026. ^f6
  > Overwolf Client (8.8MB)
- [market] Most visible reviews are short Portuguese ones from April 2025 to July 2026; one
  says the user found it through a partnership with the Brazilian creator League of HU3 and
  praises its real-time stats. ^f7
  > conheci o WaveClear pela parceria junto do League of HU3, nunca tinha visto um aplicativo com estatisticas em tempo real
- [policy] [tools] The maker (WaveClear LTD, "gamers and behavioral researchers") says the
  app is hardware-free and publisher-rule friendly: no cheating, automation or gameplay
  control. ^f8
  > Our systems are designed to be safe, hardware-free, and publisher-rule friendly: no cheating, no automation, no gameplay control.
- [needs] (claim) The maker's thesis: players already know what to do, and the hard part is
  staying focused and consistent, so more data is not enough. ^f9
  > learned that more data is not enough. Most players already know what to do.
- [privacy] No registration needed to use it. ^f10
  > free, no registration, no hardware.
- [policy] Checked, not there: no mention of Riot API use, a production key, Riot
  registration, or what input data the focus tracker reads. ^f11
