# League of Legends' New Stats Feature Is Now Live (ComicBook.com, 2018)

Why: a case of Riot building in-client what third-party stats sites offered, and Riot's
stated reason (improvement over achievement).

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Mainstream outlet, Tanner Dedmon, 7 Aug 2018, quoting Riot's
  announcement and FAQ (na.leagueoflegends.com link, not read).
- Reasoning: fair. The Riot FAQ quote gives a reason for the design choice.
- Interests: none known.
- Weigh: trust the quoted Riot text; whether the tab still exists in 2026 is not
  covered here (believed removed later, unverified).

## Findings

- [learning] [tools] Riot added a client Stats tab so players need not go to
  third-party sites for detailed post-game breakdowns. ^f1
  > if you want a more detailed breakdown, you’d have to go elsewhere and visit third-party sites with more information.
  > That’s now changed with the client’s new stats section built into players’ profiles.
- [learning] Scores by position or champion in three categories, showing where you are
  above or below other players. ^f2
  > Scores break down into three categories: Combat, Income, and Map Control
- [fun] [learning] Comparison with friends or players of similar rank. ^f3
  > Players can also compare their stats to others who are in various positions be it on their friends list or those in similar ranks.
- [learning] Riot's FAQ: performance-improvement features are more useful and used over
  time than achievement or investment stats, so Riot chose improvement. ^f4
  > performance-improvement features tend to be more useful (and used) over time, so we steered today’s feature in that direction.
- [fun] Riot left achievement/investment stats as possible future work. ^f5
  > We still think there’s room to improve on the achievement and investment side of the coin
- [market] (inferred) Riot has form in absorbing third-party stats features into the
  client; tools whose core is a per-player stats page or rank comparison carry
  replacement risk, though third-party stats sites have outlived this tab. ^f6
