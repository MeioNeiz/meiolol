# OP.GG Premium Guide (Web), May 2024

Why: what OP.GG charges for and how Premium ties to RSO.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor help page, Wayback copy (live helpscoutdocs is 404); updated 28 May 2024.
- Reasoning: fair. Detailed steps and limits.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: 2024; the Oct 2026 membership page ([[opgg-membership]]) adds the AI Agent and keeps the 10/30 favourites.

## Findings

- [tools/paid] [market] At launch Premium was Korean cards only. ^f1
  > Currently, we only accept cards issued in Korea as payments.
- [tools/paid] [tools/accounts] Several Premium features need a "primary account" picked from the RSO-linked Riot accounts; changeable once per 24 hours, across all OP.GG services. ^f2
  > Some of OP.GG Premium features require setting up a primary account.
  > Once the primary account is set, it cannot be changed for 24 hours, and it applies to all OP.GG services.
- [tools/paid] [ux/features] Between games: a personal dashboard (My Page) with more match history and data for the primary account. ^f3
  > Setting up a primary account lets you access your personalized dashboard on My Page.
- [tools/paid] [tools/accounts] Favourites limits: 5 logged out (not synced), 10 logged in, 30 with Premium; game-start notifications on mobile. ^f4
  > In the mobile app, you'll receive game start notifications for summoners added to your favorites list.
- [tools/paid] Unlimited match history refresh for the primary account (free users wait between updates). ^f5
  > Now you can update your latest match history more frequently without time limits.
- [tools/paid] Ad removal across web, mobile and desktop when logged in. ^f6
  > ads are removed from all OP.GG services, including mobile and desktop apps when logged in with the same account.
- [tools/accounts] (inferred) The primary account lock (24 hours, one per subscription) stops one subscription serving a group of friends' accounts. ^f7
