# League of Legends Wiki: Gustwalker Hatchling (jungle item)

Why: the video adds the jungle item's XP bonus to camp XP; how big is it and does it
scale?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Official community wiki; notes come from a template shared by the three
  jungle pets.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: trust; jungle role quest bonus (+10 gold, +10 XP per large monster after
  completion) comes from [[lol-patch-26-1#^f7]].

## Findings

- [data] [learning] Jungle item: every large monster gives a flat 80 bonus XP (150 more
  for the first one), so it adds a constant, not scaling, amount. ^f1
  > Awards 80 **bonus** experience at all times, plus an additional 150 **bonus** experience from the first one slain.
- [learning] Catch-up XP: more than 1.1 levels behind the game's average, +50 XP per
  level behind on each large monster. ^f2
  > While you are more than 1.1 levels behind the game's average champion level, awards an additional 50 **bonus** experience for every integer level of disadvantage.
- [data] Bonus treats: one stored every 60 s from 1:30; killing a large monster with one
  stored gives 20 extra gold. ^f3
  > If you have a _bonus_ treat stored, killing a large monster consumes 1 stack to grant you 20 when you feed your pet.
- [learning] Minion penalties for junglers: 70% to 0% less minion XP over 20 minutes,
  and 13 less gold plus 50% less XP per minion while minion gold exceeds 40% of monster
  gold, until 14:00. ^f4
  > killing a lane minion will grant you 13 less gold and 50% reduced experience. This penalty is indefinitely removed at 14 minutes.
- [learning] (inferred) Gromp for a jungler: 200 XP at camp level 1 to 2, 230 at 3, 260
  at 8 and after (plus 10 after the role quest); gold 80 flat (100 with a bonus treat,
  90 after quest). A solo kill passes Gromp XP at victim level 7 (308 vs 254 to 260),
  so the video's "level 8 breakpoint" for junglers matches in XP, but kill gold (300+)
  is above Gromp gold (80) at every level. ^f5
