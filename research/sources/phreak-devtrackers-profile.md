# Riot Phreak's Reddit replies on PBE changes (Jul-Aug 2025), via devtrackers

Why: does Riot attach an expected win-rate size to a change before it ships, and in
what form, so that a stated size could be a prior mean?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. First-hand, by the designer responsible for live balance at the time;
  reddit replies, two changes, no follow-up on whether the sizes held.
- Reasoning: fair. Gives the logic (size the buff, ship a nerf of similar size) and
  says it can be wrong.
- Interests: Riot explaining its own changes.
- Weigh: shows Riot does size single changes in win-rate points and how small the
  intended sizes are; such numbers appear in scattered replies, not per patch, so they
  are not a usable list for 16.23.

## Findings

- [method/dynamics] Riot sizes a single change in win-rate points before shipping it,
  with a range: one extra second on an Illaoi effect read as worth 0.5-0.75 points.
  Aug 2025 reply ^f1
  > "Our read is that the 1 second duration is a measurable increase in power (somewhere around 0.5-0.75% win rate), so we're delivering a small compensation nerf that should be of similar size."
- [method/dynamics] The estimate is held loosely and corrected after the fact. ^f2
  > "We can of course be wrong and need to correct the mistake, but that's the logic."
- [method/dynamics] The stated target for a buff is a sign and "small", and a miss in
  either direction is corrected the next patch (Rek'Sai, Jul 2025). ^f3
  > "Overall goal is lightly win rate positive. If we over or under-shoot, we'll adjust in the following patch."
- [method/dynamics] Champions are balanced at mid-to-high experience on the champion;
  he puts a usable personal win rate at about 25 games on it. ^f4
  > "She is in fact balanced relative to other champions at mid-high levels of experience (where we tend to peg all champions)"
- [method/dynamics] Checked, not there: a list of expected sizes for a whole patch, any
  comparison of a stated size with the measured move, how many days of data Riot
  waits for before correcting. ^f5
- [method/dynamics] (inferred) Intended sizes are well under a point for an ordinary
  change, the same order as our measured 0.5 pp RMS move of unchanged champions on
  16.21's first day and far under one day's noise for most champion-roles; and since a
  miss is corrected the following patch, a champion changed in patch N is more likely
  than others to be changed again in N+1, in the direction that undoes the error. ^f6
