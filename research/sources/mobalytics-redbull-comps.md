# This Is the Team Comp Winning in High-Level League (Mobalytics via Red Bull, 2017)

Why: are archetype comps (engage, poke, pick, AoE) measured against win in solo queue?

## Assessment

- Evidence: D. Raw archetype win rates by rank; numbers only in chart images; no sample
  sizes, intervals or control for champion strength. Archetype labelling method is in
  a previous article, not here.
- Reasoning: fair on mechanism (AoE punishes low-elo grouping), none on confounding.
- Interests: Mobalytics content marketing.
- Weigh: shows comp archetype effects may depend on rank; not usable as an effect size.

## Findings

- [draft] Seven hand-curated comp archetypes; split, kite and pick are about 85% of
  Bronze-Gold comps. ^f1
  > those three compositions combined accounted for around 85 percent of all team comp in these rankings
- [draft] (claim) AoE comps have the highest win rate in Bronze, Silver and Gold despite
  low pick rate; numbers only in images. ^f2
  > it was actually Area of Effect (AoE) compositions which took the cake in terms of win rate
- [draft] (claim) At higher ranks AoE win rate tapers and pick comps rise. ^f3
  > as players climbed, AoE started to taper off while Pick began to rise
- [draft] Split push share falls from 49% in Bronze to 34.1% in Masters. ^f4
  > as low as 34.1 percent in Masters — quite a decrease from 49 percent, the rate at which Bronze players built Split comps
- [method] Checked, not there: win rate numbers in text, sample sizes, how a team is
  assigned an archetype, any held-out prediction. ^f5
