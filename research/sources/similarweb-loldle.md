# loldle.net on Similarweb (September 2026)

Why: how much traffic does the best-known League daily guessing game get, and how crowded
is the genre?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Third-party panel estimate, not first-party; ranks and engagement are reasonably comparable between sites, absolute visits less so.
- Reasoning: n/a (data).
- Interests: Similarweb sells the full data.
- Weigh: rank and the competitor list are solid; the monthly visits figure is hidden on the free page (only "2M" beside loldle.net in a compare widget), so treat ~2M/month as rough.

## Findings

- [market] [fun] loldle.net ranks about #22,000 globally in Sep 2026, falling from #17,682 over three months. ^f1
  > Over the last three months, loldle.net's global ranking has increased from 17,682 to 22,040.
- [market] Traffic fell 24% month on month into Sep 2026. ^f2
  > loldle.net's web traffic has decreased by 24.11% compared to last month.
- [market] [fun] Strong engagement for a daily game: 5.22 pages and 4m32s per visit, 18.72% bounce (page shows these as separate short lines, so unquotable). ^f3
- [market] (inferred) The compare widget shows "2M" next to loldle.net, suggesting on the order of 2 million visits a month; another estimate (Semrush, Nov 2025, via search snippet only) put it at about 2.65 million. ^f4
- [market] Audience is young and international: top country US at only 14%, then Germany, Turkey, France, Spain (others 59%); 53% aged 18-24. ^f5
  > The largest age group of visitors are 18 - 24 year olds.
- [market] [fun] The genre is crowded with League clones and variants: champdle, guessr.gg, lolchallenge, buildle, superloldle, infiniteloldle, guesschamp. ^f6
  > champdle.com is the website with the highest similarity score to loldle.net.
- [market] [fun] The same audience plays the creator's other -dle games (narutodle, pokedle, onepiecedle) and op.gg. ^f7
- [fun] Site describes itself as daily champion guessing with clues, quotes, emojis and spell icons: all static champion data, no match data. ^f8
  > daily guess league of legends champions with clues, random quotes, emojis, spell icons
- [data] (inferred) LoLdle's modes need only Data Dragon style champion assets, not a Riot API key; it shows a huge audience exists for League trivia without match data. ^f9
