# OP.GG Privacy Policy (last updated 2026-09-14)

Why: what an approved, large lookup site writes in its privacy policy, and how it frames
data about players who never signed up.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Biggest League stats site ([[similarweb-opgg-competitors#^f1]]), a Korean
  company with GDPR representatives; policy current (Sep 2026).
- Reasoning: fair. Per-purpose legal bases and retention periods, written by lawyers.
- Interests: OP.GG (self-description).
- Weigh: good template for structure; but it is silent on the core question (lawful
  basis for profiles of people who never registered), so not evidence that silence is safe.

## Findings

- [privacy] [policy] Per-purpose GDPR legal bases: contract for accounts, legitimate
  interest for recommendations and analytics, consent for ads in the EEA and UK. ^f1
  > Legal basis: performance of a contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  > Legal basis: our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in providing a useful product, balanced against your rights via the objection right.
- [privacy] Describes publisher game data as received when a user links a game account,
  and grants access, rectification and erasure of it. ^f2
  > We receive in-game data (e.g., kills, deaths, gold earned, experience points, damage dealt, character usage) from game publishers, including Riot Games, PUBG Corp., Nimble Neuron, and Blizzard, when you link your OP.GG account to a game account with those publishers.
  > You have the right to request access, rectification, and erasure of this data.
- [privacy] Checked, not there: no statement of the lawful basis for showing match
  history of players who never registered (the summoner pages anyone can search), and no
  removal process for them. ^f3
- [privacy] Retention table: account data for the account's life, analytics and logs 1
  year, payment 5 years, data subject request records 3 years. ^f4
  > **Automatically Collected Information.** Retained for 1 year.
  > **Data subject request records.** Retained for 3 years from resolution.
- [privacy] Lists GDPR rights including objection to legitimate-interest processing and
  complaint to a supervisory authority. ^f5
  > Right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, including profiling.
  > Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
- [privacy] [policy] Non-EU controller names an EU representative (GDPR Art. 27) and a UK
  representative (UK GDPR Art. 27), both Bird & Bird services. ^f6
  > ## EU Representative. (GDPR Article 27)
  > ## UK Representative. (UK GDPR Article 27)
- [privacy] Adults only (18+, 19 in Korea); deletes data of children found. ^f7
  > We require all users to be of the age of majority in their country of residence at the time of registration
- [privacy] States 72-hour breach notification for GDPR-covered breaches. ^f8
  > within 72 hours for GDPR-covered breaches
