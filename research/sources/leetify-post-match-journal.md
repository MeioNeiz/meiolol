# Leetify blog: Post-Match Journal (Mar 2025)

Why: a reflective, between-games return hook from a CS2 (and League) stat site.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Vendor post describing its own paid feature; no usage numbers.
- Reasoning: fair. Explains the wide early ranges honestly.
- Interests: sells Leetify Pro.
- Weigh: a design to note, not evidence it retains.

## Findings

- [retention] [tools/paid] A paid feature asks a few yes/no questions after every match
  and computes which go with winning or losing. ^f1
  > This journal prompts you to answer a few yes/no questions after every match, and then does the math to see which of these have the strongest correlation with you winning and losing.
- [retention] Prompts are about out-of-game factors (sleep, caffeine, tilt), because stats
  the site tracks are correlated automatically. ^f2
  > focus your prompts on out-of-game factors that aren't shown on the Match Report.
- [ux/uncertainty] Early results show wide ranges that narrow with entries; it can take
  dozens or hundreds. ^f3
  > it can take dozens or even hundreds of entries to get a strongly resolved correlation
- [retention] An automatic page shows how the player's own tracked stats go with their
  win rate, no journaling needed. ^f4
  > We also have an automatic correlations page
- [retention] (inferred) For meio.lol: "what goes with your wins" from the player's own
  games (time of day, games in a row, champion) needs no input and gives a reason to
  come back as the range narrows; rate wording must avoid "+X% win rate". ^f5
