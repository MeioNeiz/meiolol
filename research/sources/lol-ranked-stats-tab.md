# Timewinder: old client Ranked Stats tab recreated (r/leagueoflegends, Sep 2019)

Why: do players want long-run personal stats and nostalgia, not just recent match lists?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. 12.4k upvotes, 96% upvoted, ~470 comments; by molenzwiebel (Mimic,
  Deceive), a known community developer. Old (2019).
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: none commercial; site was free and open source.
- Weigh: strong appetite signal for personal history and nostalgia; the endpoint it used
  was unsupported then and is likely gone now. Many comments are about load failures.

## Findings

- [needs] [fun] Players wanted lifetime per-season ranked stats the new client had
  dropped; the site showed them back to season 1, styled as the old client. ^f1
  > Maybe you just want to see your current ranked stats in a detail that hasn't been possible since the old client got retired three seasons ago?
  > All of the stats from the old client are there and go back all the way to season 1.
- [market] Reception: 12.4k upvotes, 96% upvoted. ^f2
  > Thanks, now I know I'm shit but in **_Legacy_**
- [needs] Commenters resent the client showing less than a hobby site; one blames Riot for
  not spending a dev day on it. ^f3
  > it **is** a fact that the stats you can see in the client are far, far, _far_ less than on the website provided here.
  > They could have tasked a dev for 1 or 2 days to do this, just like you did
- [fun] Uses are personal trivia and nostalgia: lifetime game counts, pentakill
  droughts, largest crit, champions won with. ^f4
  > Ive been desperatly needing to comfrim that i havent gotten a pentakill(ranked) since Zac was reworked in 2017
  > Since season 3 I've played 4,884 games of ranked
- [needs] Normals players wanted the same; only ranked was tracked. ^f5
  > I wish this existed for normals...most of my play time are normals in Season I-III
- [data] [policy] Riot told the developer at registration that the data was unsupported
  and could disappear. ^f6
  > the data you're using is unsupported and could disappear at any time
- [data] Viral launch overloaded the site; the developer cached to limit Riot requests. ^f7
  > I was caching data to prevent requesting a lot of info from Riot
