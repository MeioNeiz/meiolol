# U.GG: Riot Moved Thousands of LoL Players Out of Their Ranks (2026)

Why: how many ranked lobbies are dodged (games we never see, selected on champ select),
and how often an autofilled player faces a main in the same role.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Secondary report by a stats site; the dodge figure is attributed to Riot
  but the original post was not found; the autofill figure quotes Riot Phroxzon on X.
- Reasoning: fair (relays numbers, no method).
- Interests: U.GG is a stats site; no stake in these numbers.
- Weigh: order of magnitude only; check against a Riot post if a decision depends on it.

## Findings

- [method/causal] [data] Riot reported dodged ranked lobbies falling from 19% to 4%
  after its 2026 ranked changes. ^f1
  > Dodged lobbies dropping from 19% to 4%
- [method/causal] [data] Role-parity matching (EUW trial live in 26.4) cut autofilled
  junglers facing a main jungler at Master+ from 35% to 5%; below Master it was already
  rare. ^f2
  > Autofill JG vs Main JG in high MMR (Master+) go from 35% >>> 5%
  > Outside of high MMR, this was already happening, so not much is changing.
- [method/causal] (inferred) Dodges select which drafts become games: before 2026 about
  one lobby in five never produced a match, now about one in 25. If players dodge
  drafts they read as lost, observed drafts are truncated at the bad end and draft
  effects measured on played games are shrunk; at 4% the truncation is small for our
  data (patch 16.x), larger for any older data. Role-parity matching also means
  autofill now mostly cancels within a lane, so autofill confounds matchups less. ^f3
