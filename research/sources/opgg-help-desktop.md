# OP.GG Help Center: desktop app, Ad-free and account articles

Why: how OP.GG's desktop app handles accounts, friends, paid tiers and removals, in
OP.GG's own support words (category page is JS-rendered; text taken from the help
centre's public article API, one snapshot with each article's URL and update date).

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor support articles, updated Dec 2025 to Oct 2026; 52 articles in the
  desktop category.
- Reasoning: strong for facts. Short procedural answers; one states a Riot-policy removal
  outright.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: trust procedures and the removal statement; the "safe" answer is a vendor claim.

## Findings

- [tools/removed] [policy] The spell and skill tracker overlay is gone "due to policy
  changes by Riot Games" (article updated 2026-05-27; the overlay arrived May 2024,
  [[opgg-desktop-patch-notes#^f3]]). ^f1
  > The spell & skill tracker overlay feature is no longer offered due to policy changes by Riot Games.
- [tools/accounts] OP.GG has its own accounts; the app works logged out with some features
  restricted; login unlocks personalised features and events. ^f2
  > The app can be used without logging in, but some features may be restricted.
- [tools/accounts] A Riot account is linked from OP.GG account settings through the Riot
  Games login page (RSO); one Riot account per OP.GG account, relinking moves it. ^f3
  > After being redirected to the Riot Games login page and completing the link, return to the app and click the refresh button to finalize the linking process.
  > If a Riot account already linked to an OP.GG account is linked to a new OP.GG account, it will be automatically unlinked from the previous OP.GG account.
- [tools/phase] Lobby: the OP.GG Pick page opens when a lobby is created: averages for
  your tier, your season's most played champions with counters and synergies, patch
  notes. ^f4
  > When a game lobby is created in the League client, the OP.GG Pick page is automatically activated.
  > OP.GG Pick is a feature that assists in selecting optimal champions based on user data and OP.GG’s statistical data to help secure game wins.
- [tools/phase] Champ select: spells chosen automatically by pick rate when the game
  starts. ^f5
  > the OP.GG app will automatically select spells based on high pick rates from champion analysis.
- [tools/phase] Between games: real-time recommended champions in solo and flex,
  refreshed hourly, with synergy on hover. ^f6
  > OP.GG's real-time recommended champions data is updated every hour
- [tools/accounts] Between games: duo finder in the app (tier, main role, recent stats,
  preferred champions) with Add Friend and direct lobby invite, only between users of the
  app; notes need a linked Riot account. ^f7
  > click the 'Add Friend' button to send a friend request instantly or click the 'Invite' button to invite them directly to the lobby.
  > This feature works only if the other person also has the OP.GG Desktop App installed.
- [tools/accounts] [fun] A rival feature compares stats, placements and tier graph with a
  chosen friend (TFT placements 1st to 8th). ^f8
  > The rival feature allows for a visual comparison of key stats, rankings, and win rates with friends selected for comparison.
- [tools/paid] Ad-free subscription removes ads in all OP.GG services and apps when
  logged in; LoL adds a personal stats dashboard for a main account and upgraded
  favourites; duo posts get a highlight badge. ^f9
  > With an OP.GG Ad-free subscription, ads are removed from all OP.GG services and apps.
  > Access various additional features, including a personalized stats dashboard and upgraded favorites.
- [tools/paid] The main account for paid dashboards can change only once per 24 hours;
  payment by card on the PC web only. ^f10
  > Once a main account is set, it cannot be changed for 24 hours.
  > OP.GG Ad-free currently supports credit/debit card payment only and payment can only be completed through the PC web browser.
- [policy] (claim) Using the app is not grounds for suspension; OP.GG says it complies with
  Riot's policies and talks with Riot during development. ^f11
  > Using the OP.GG Desktop App is not grounds for account suspension.
- [tools/phase] The overlay is described as giving builds, opponent data and spell timers
  (article updated 2026-09-29, after the spell tracker removal). ^f12
  > The overlay feature provides real-time information such as champion builds, opponent data, and spell timers during gameplay.
- [tools/removed] (inferred) "Spell timers" in the Sep 2026 getting-started text
  contradicts the May 2026 removal article; likely stale copy. Which spell timers remain
  is unverified. ^f13
- [tools/paid] AI Voice is sold per voice in an AI voice store (streamers, pro players); a
  voice subscription includes ad-free OP.GG; it runs only in the desktop app, logged in. ^f14
  > Select and purchase a preferred voice from the AI voice store.
  > Subscribing to OP.GG AI Voice includes an ad-free experience on OP.GG.
- [tools/phase] In game: AI Voice answers typed or spoken questions and alerts on objective
  spawns, enemy respawns and enemy item completions; gives fight and build advice. ^f15
  > Highlights easily missed timings such as objective spawns, enemy respawns, and core item completions.
  > Should we stay together or split?
- [policy] (inferred) Teamfight calls ("staying grouped ... is best") are notifications that
  dictate action from game state, the category Riot prohibits via Overwolf
  ([[overwolf-riot-compliance#^f4]]); OP.GG still ships them in Oct 2026. ^f16
