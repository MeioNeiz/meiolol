# European Gaming: GIANTX launches iTero Standalone (Nov 2025)

Why: iTero's per-phase features as of its move off Overwolf, and the GIANTX ownership.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Dated 18 Nov 2025; reads as a GIANTX press release (bold marketing lines, no independent testing).
- Reasoning: weak.
- Interests: vendor-sourced.
- Weigh: feature list and timeline are useful; "highest chance of winning" wording is marketing, and notably the kind Riot's production key rules forbid in champ select.

## Findings

- [tools] iTero Standalone launched Nov 2025 as a direct download independent of Overwolf; iTero Gaming founded 2022, bought by GIANTX in 2024; 500,000+ downloads. ^f1
  > The new version is fully independent and **available for direct download**
  > Founded by **Jack Joseph Williams** in 2022 and **acquired by GIANTX in 2024**
- [tools/phase] [draft] Champ select: recommends the champion with the highest chance of winning from both comps, the player's stats and the meta. ^f2
  > The AI analyzes your team’s and the enemy’s composition to recommend the champion that has the highest chance of winning that game, based on your stats and the current meta.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Champ select: items, runes and summoners chosen against the enemy comp, including whether anti-heal or anti-shield pays off. ^f3
  > That even includes checking whether anti-heal or anti-shield items are efficient enough to buy!
- [tools/phase] [learning] Post game and between games: macro feedback after each session; after ten games an AI Macro Coach compares stats with same-rank players. ^f4
  > After ten games, you unlock the AI Macro Coach, which compares your stats with players of your same rank and shows where you’re consistently falling behind.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] Loading screen: scouting of other players' habits (enemy support invades, jungler power-farms, lane opponent aggressive). ^f5
  > whether that’s learning that the enemy support loves to invade, your Jungler likes to spend their early game power-farming, or that your lane opponent plays aggressively.
- [tools/phase] [ux/features] In game: overlays for gold difference, inhibitor respawn timers and Baron buff holder. ^f6
  > From tracking the gold difference, timing your inhibitor respawns, or quickly checking who has the Baron buff
- [tools/paid] Checked, not there: no price, paid tier or account requirement mentioned. ^f7
