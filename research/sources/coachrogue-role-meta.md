## Findings

- [learning] Mid is solo early because the lane is shorter (safer for immobile mages, quicker from base); top suits fighters for long trades and tanks; bot's long lane gets a support to protect the ADC. @1:23 @2:11 ^f1
  > the lane is shorter mid than what it is in either of the side lanes
  > our vulnerable ADC player gets to be babysit by a support
- [learning] History: bot became the duo lane because dragon was the only objective, spawning at 2:30. @2:59 ^f2
  > it was only logical to put two members bot so that you'd have more people in proximity to the only objective on the map
- [learning] Mages need levels for ability ranks, ADCs need gold and items, so the ADC shares XP with the support; ADC-mid picks (Tristana, old Corki) were ones whose power scaled with level. @4:23 @5:15 @6:05 ^f3
  > Mages scale better with champion levels than ADCs do
  > we play ADC's bot lane because someone is going to have to share the XP with the support
- [learning] Mid game: the duo moves mid because mid priority controls side waves and the shorter lane protects an ADC who is 2-3 levels behind the solo laners. @7:05 @7:56 ^f4
  > solo laners would have laned by themselves for a while and will generally outlevel the ADC by about two to three levels
- [draft] Draft tip: pair an immobile mid with an ADC that is safe in a side lane (Lucian, Kai'Sa, Vayne), or an immobile ADC with a mobile or melee mid (Caitlyn with Ahri or Akali); never Xerath with Caitlyn. @8:28 @9:16 ^f5
  > You'll never see something like Zerath Caitlyn
  > So if you want to play something immobile like Caitlyn, pair it with a mid laner with mobility like Ahri
- [draft] Example: Froggen's 2014 Anivia with Twitch, kept on different lanes at 20 minutes. @10:02 ^f6
  > pair immobile midlaners with mobile ADCs or mobile ADCs with mobile midlaners
