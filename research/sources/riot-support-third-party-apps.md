# Third Party Applications: Riot's Stance (Riot Support, 2025-02-10)

Why: the player-facing rules for third-party apps, beyond the developer portal.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Riot's own support article; page dated 10 Feb 2025 (likely last edit).
  The old League article (support-leagueoflegends.riotgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/225266848)
  redirects here.
- Reasoning: fair. Principles with examples; defers detail to per-game dev policies.
- Interests: Riot.
- Weigh: player-facing summary; developer policies are the operative text.

## Findings

- [policy] Stance: no software should compromise integrity from pressing Play until log
  off (so champ select, which comes after Play, is in scope). ^f1
  > No software should compromise game integrity or negatively impact the in-game player experience, from the moment you press “Play” until you log off.
- [policy] Principle: competitive games rely on equal information. ^f2
  > Competitive games work because each player is afforded the same amount of information about the game and other players.
- [policy] Problem apps give measurable advantage: exposing obfuscated info, acting for
  you, drawing conclusions during gameplay, altering field of intelligence. ^f3
  > Exposing information that's intentionally obfuscated
  > Drawing conclusions for you during gameplay (ie we want to see you play the game first, then analyze and reflect later!)
  > Altering your field of intelligence (zoomhacks or global ult alerts)
- [policy] Positive uses named: onboarding newer players, stat tracking, finding teammates. ^f4
  > Third party applications can be great for onboarding newer players, tracking your stats, finding like-minded teammates, hosting/playing tournaments, and more.
- [policy] Approved apps on the official API should rarely cause player issues. ^f5
  > If a third party application is approved and built using the official Riot API, players should rarely have an issue.
- [policy] Riot gives no list of safe apps. ^f6
  > we can't provide a full list of which ones are safe to use.
- [policy] Violating apps can be taken down after review. ^f7
  > If a third party application is found to be intentionally violating our policies, we will conduct a thorough review which, if verified, could result in the takedown of the application.
- [policy] (inferred) "Drawing conclusions for you during gameplay" is the line a pick
  recommender sits nearest; champ select is not "gameplay" in the narrow sense but is
  after "Play", so presenting options rather than a single answer matters. ^f8
- [policy] Checked, not there: no explicit mention of champion select, pick
  recommendations, win rates or dodging. ^f9
