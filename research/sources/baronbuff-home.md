# Baron Buff: League of Legends AI Coach and LoL Tracker (homepage)

Why: what a new LLM-era League tool puts in its post-game review, and what it charges for.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Small newcomer; claims "6+ million new matches analysed per week" and an app on the Microsoft Store; no user numbers. Listed in awesome-league ([[awesome-league#^f6]]).
- Reasoning: weak. Marketing copy with an FAQ; its competitor comparison is its own.
- Interests: vendor; sells Premium.
- Weigh: trust the feature split and the paywall; the coaching quality and "real science" are claims.

## Findings

- [learning] [tools/phase] Post game: review the decisions that mattered with evidence from the match; reports add comparisons with players at your rank. ^f1
  > Review the decisions that mattered, with evidence from your match. Track your match history and progress in Tracker; reports add coaching comparisons with players at your rank.
- [learning] Its pitch against Mobalytics: feedback tied to evidence, why the decision mattered, and one improvement for next game. ^f2
  > It connects feedback to evidence from your match, explains why your decisions mattered and identifies one specific improvement to focus on next game.
- [learning] (claim) Reviews are generated by an AI system tuned with high-profile coaches' insights; the method blends data mining, machine learning and large language models. ^f3
  > Our pre-game tips and post-game analyses are both generated by a proprietary AI-powered system, trained on insights from real high-profile coaches.
- [tools/paid] Free: champ select analysis, builds, spells and runes. Paid: live tips, game reviews, more champion pools, auto-import. ^f4
  > The paid plan unlocks live coaching tips, game reviews, multiple champion pools per role for the draft analysis and auto-import for runes and builds.
- [tools/paid] Premium bundles unlimited game plans, in-game coaching and post-game reviews, with regional pricing (no price on the page). ^f5
  > Baron Buff Premium brings unlimited game plans, in-game coaching and post-game reviews together in one subscription, with regional pricing designed to make regular coaching more accessible.
- [learning] Retention: Tracker gathers recorded match history, progress, and rank and LP history from recorded ranked games. ^f6
  > It also shows rank and LP history from your recorded ranked matches.
- [ux/features] Keeps advice short by design: one win condition and up to three tips per phase, detail adjustable. ^f7
  > one main win condition and up to three tips per game phase. You can adjust the level of detail and choose what appears in the overlay
- [policy] (inferred) Its homepage advertises auto-accept, which Riot said apps may not do ([[itero-companion-apps#^f5]]). ^f8
  > When your queue pops, auto-accept can accept the match for you.
- [learning] (inferred) LLM reviewers converge on OP.GG's shape ([[opgg-membership#^f2]]): a short verdict and one thing to fix next game; the paid line is the review itself. ^f9
