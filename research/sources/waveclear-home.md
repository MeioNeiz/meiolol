# WaveClear home page (waveclear.gg)

Why: what WaveClear does, who makes it, how it is distributed.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. The maker's own page; copyright 2025, so partly older than the October 2026
  store listing ([[overwolf-waveclear]]).
- Reasoning: weak; headline numbers (58.5% win rate, +36% skills) with testimonials, method on
  [[waveclear-research]].
- Interests: vendor marketing.
- Weigh: trust the product description and distribution; discount the numbers.

## Findings

- [tools] [learning] Core feature is a Focus Tracker that claims to measure focus while you play,
  with no extra hardware, to keep you away from tilt and distractions. ^f1
  > The Focus Tracker (no hardware required) measures your FOCUS while playing to train mindset, game skills, and keep you away from toxicity and distractions
- [needs] (claim) Thesis: climbing needs dying less, not new champions or roles, and that is a
  matter of focus. ^f2
  > ranking up does NOT require a new role or a new champ, but just dying twice less in lane.
- [ux/features] [learning] Pre-match preparation and post-match review sold as replacing stats:
  awareness, mistake detection and progress in 60 seconds. ^f3
  > Prepare for your next match by improving your awareness, detecting mistakes, and seeing your progress in 60 seconds
- [market] Every download button goes to the Overwolf store (utm_campaign=beta); free. ^f4
  > Download WaveClear for LoL
- [market] Team and partners listed: Jake (STRMX) Camilleri as head coach, founder YuvGel (EUW),
  partners Lundqvist Lightside (ERL), RiftQ and Revealense Behavioral Science. Coach Rogue
  is not named on the page, though he calls it "my app" in [[coachrogue-moneyball-stats]]. ^f5
  > Jake (STRMX) Camilleri
  > Revealense Behavioral Science
- [policy] Carries Riot's standard not-endorsed legal boilerplate. ^f6
  > WaveClear isn't endorsed by Riot Games and doesn't reflect the views or opinions of Riot Games
- [learning] (claim) Headline results: 58.5% win rate and +36% game skills. ^f7
