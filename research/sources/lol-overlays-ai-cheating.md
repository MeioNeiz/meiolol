# League Overlays and AI: When Data Analysis becomes borderline Cheating? (r/leagueoflegends, Jun 2025)

Why: what players think is fair for a data tool to do, and where draft tools sit.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. 1.0k upvotes, 93% upvoted, 285 comments, Jun 2025. The iTero founder
  replies in thread.
- Reasoning: fair to strong. Long argued post; commenters give reasons and benchmarks.
- Interests: the OP built proof-of-concept AI overlays; iTero's founder is a draft-tool
  vendor and says so.
- Weigh: a self-selected, competitive-minded audience; casual players are under-
  represented. Useful as the community's line on fairness, which matches Riot's.

## Findings

- [needs] [policy] Top comment (1.0k): ban all third-party live in-game assistance; if a
  tool is not allowed in pro play it should not be on the ladder. ^f1
  > Frankly I think Riot should draw a hard line and outright ban the use of all third-party tools and overlays for live assistance in-game.
  > A good benchmark should be if a tool isn't accessible or allowed in a professional match then it shouldn't be allowed on the competitive ladder.
- [market] Reception: post 1.0k upvotes, 93% upvoted. ^f2
  > Yes, this is a long read, but it's important in my opinion.
- [learning] [needs] Rough consensus: in-game help is bad, post-game review is fine,
  draft is the grey area. ^f3
  > TLDR: In-game bad, pre-game/post-game good
  > I agree that post-game is definite OK, in-game definitely Bad, then draft is the grey area.
- [learning] [market] iTero's founder: review tools (stats of your own games, AI analysis,
  auto VOD review) make coaching accessible to players who cannot pay a coach. ^f4
  > It will never replace human coaches, but it makes it more accessible for the 99% of players who can't justify paying for one.
- [draft] [market] iTero's founder: draft-tool users lack time to follow the meta and
  learn every matchup; many use it to try new champions. ^f5
  > Our main userbase tend to be people who just don't have the time to keep up with meta and don't play enough to know EVERY match-up and counter.
- [draft] [needs] A long-time Masters player objects to draft help: the game starts in
  champ select, and reliance on apps that "tell them" has eroded player knowledge. ^f6
  > Pre-game (i.e. draft) is not okay either in my opinion - the game starts in champ select.
  > so many people rely on apps like these to just "tell them" what's good.
- [draft] [method] Sceptics say draft AI is useless outside pro play because it cannot
  see players' skill on their champions. ^f7
  > AI can't know your or your enemies' level of knowledge on those champs. Winning draft outside of pro-play means almost nothing.
- [tools] [ux/trust] Some avoid Porofessor, Mobalytics and Trophy Hunter because of distrust of
  Overwolf and its resource use. ^f8
  > I avoid overlay apps like Porofessor, Mobalytics or Trophy Hunter simply because I do not trust Overwolf as a business
- [tools] [needs] [ux/trust] Stats sites like OP.GG are accepted when they show history and leave
  conclusions to the player. ^f9
  > they should only be allowed to present historical data and all conclusions reached should come from the player.
- [learning] [needs] Arms-race worry: unrestricted tools force non-users to adopt them to
  stay competitive. ^f10
  > by not limiting these tools for other players they essentially force people into using these tools in order to stay competitive because they are powerful.
