# UK GDPR Article 14, information when not obtained from the data subject (legislation.gov.uk, revised)

Why: crawled players' data comes from Riot's API, not from them: Art 14, not 13. Which
items the privacy page owes them, and whether a public page is enough.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. The law, revised text; DUAA 2025 rewrote the exemptions as 14(5)(e)-(f)
  and added 14(6)-(7) on 5 Feb 2026
- Reasoning: fair. Statute
- Interests: none
- Weigh: the EU text (eugdpr-art14) has the same exemption as 14(5)(b)

## Findings

- [privacy] Art 14 adds two items to Art 13's list: the categories of data, and where the data came from (and whether public). Art 14(1)(d), 14(2)(f) ^f1
  > the categories of personal data concerned
  > from which source the personal data originate, and if applicable, whether it came from publicly accessible sources
- [privacy] Same core items as Art 13: identity, purposes and legal basis, legitimate interests, storage period, rights, complaints to the controller and the Commission. Art 14(1)-(2) ^f2
  > the period for which the personal data will be stored, or if that is not possible, the criteria used to determine that period
  > the right to make a complaint to the controller (see section 164A of the 2018 Act)
- [privacy] Timing: within a reasonable period, at the latest one month after obtaining the data. Art 14(3)(a) ^f3
  > within a reasonable period after obtaining the personal data, but at the latest within one month
- [privacy] Exemption: no individual notice where it is impossible or a disproportionate effort, judged by number of people, age of data and safeguards. Art 14(5)(e), 14(6) ^f4
  > providing the information is impossible or would involve a disproportionate effort
  > whether providing the information would involve a disproportionate effort depends on, among other things, the number of data subjects, the age of the personal data and any appropriate safeguards applied to the processing
- [privacy] Relying on the exemption requires protective measures, including making the information public: for crawled players the public privacy page is that measure. Art 14(7) ^f5
  > must take appropriate measures to protect the data subject’s rights, freedoms and legitimate interests, including by making the information available publicly
- [privacy] (inferred) We have no contact with crawled players (Riot gives no email) and they number in the hundreds of thousands, so 14(5)(e) applies; the privacy page must then carry every Art 14 item, including categories and source. ^f6
