# Google: To slash or not to slash (2010)

Why: /champions or /champions/ for pages that are also directories (/champions/karthus).

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Google's own blog; old (2010) but still linked from current docs.
- Reasoning: fair.
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: the rule is simple and unchanged; nothing newer contradicts it.

## Findings

- [seo] With and without a trailing slash are two separate URLs to Google. ^f1
  > Google treats each URL above separately (and equally) regardless of whether it's a file or a directory, or it contains a trailing slash or it doesn't contain a trailing slash.
- [seo] Serving one version and redirecting the other is best; the slash is conventional for directories but either is fine. ^f2
  > If only one version can be returned (that is, the other redirects to it), that's great!
  > but you can choose whichever you like.
