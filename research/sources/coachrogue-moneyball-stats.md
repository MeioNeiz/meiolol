# Coach Rogue: The Stats You NEED To Know To Win More (Moneyball In League)

Why: Jacob shared it; does its "farm early, fight later" stats argument hold up, and is
it something meio.lol's Learn or data pages could show better?

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. A coach's own charts; kill timing from a dataset he says he "manually
  calculated", averages from League of Graphs, no sample size, patch or rank given.
  93.9k subscribers, 90k views and 1.2k likes in three days.
- Reasoning: fair. The mechanism (flat farm reward vs rising kill reward times rising
  kill rate) is clear and testable, but costs are left out (risk of the gank failing,
  wave lost, tempo) and kill gold and XP are drawn as one curve.
- Interests: the closing two minutes sell his app Waveclear, said to weight CS early
  and damage per minute late; the argument justifies that design.
- Weigh: the kill rate shape is checkable in our timelines (done:
  [[kill-rate-by-minute-measured]]); the mechanics numbers need the wiki (reader out).
  Auto captions: "gang" at 6:19 is "gank".

## Findings

- [learning] Claims minion wave gold and XP stay flat all game, with a small dip after 14 minutes from one fewer melee minion on cannon waves. @1:20 ^f1
  > the gold and XP reward for killing a minion wave doesn't change over the course of the game
  > after 14 minutes, cannon waves spawn with two melee minions instead of three
- [learning] Claims a champion kill gives more XP than a full cannon wave from about level 7, and kill XP grows little early, a lot mid game, little late. @1:41 ^f2
  > You get more XP from a champion kill than you get from an entire cannon wave starting from around level seven
- [learning] Claims jungle camp XP scales up to level 8 then flattens, so level 8 is the jungler's breakpoint to gank more than farm. @2:20 @3:29 ^f3
  > making level eight for junglers a sort of breakpoint for when to transition into a more gank heavy play style
- [learning] Claims minion and monster rewards are flat while champion kill rewards, gold included, scale with level. @2:30 ^f4
  > the reward you get for taking minions and monsters is relatively flat, whilst the reward for killing champions is entirely dependent on scaling
- [data] Averages from League of Graphs: about 67 kills a game and 2.32 kills a minute, a death every 26 seconds. @3:54 ^f5
  > there are 2.32 kills per minute. That means that someone dies on average every 26 seconds
- [data] Kill rate by game time rises into the mid game, peaks, then falls in the 25 to 30 minute bracket, put down to games ending before 30 minutes and longer death timers. @4:39 ^f6
  > They continue to scale up until we get to that 25 to 30-minute bracket, where we actually see a decline
- [data] Mid game has three times the early game's kills per minute. @5:31 ^f7
  > By the mid game, though, there are three times more kills happening per minute
- [learning] Rule of thumb: a wave is worth three kills at level 2, about one at level 8, half a kill at level 12; a kill happens about every 85 s at level 2, 32 s at level 8, 23 s at level 12. @5:31 ^f8
  > At level two, a minion wave is worth three times more than a champion kill. At level eight, they're worth roughly the same. And at level 12, the kill is now worth double.
- [learning] Advice: split push off a 15-minute top tower for plates and waves; after about 20 minutes push the side wave and group mid even without an objective. The mid game has an early split-push part and a later grouping part. @6:19 @7:09 ^f9
  > But once you get more than 20 minutes into the game or so, the reward for kills is scaled up considerably
- [tools] Waveclear practice goals weight CS more early and champion damage per minute more late. @7:09 ^f10
  > It values CS more in the early stages when the relative value of minions and monsters is higher
- [tools] Waveclear's post-game debrief scores focus from inputs (champion combos, idle time, APM) plus core metrics on a timeline; flagging 5-6 moments for VOD review is announced. @8:01 ^f11
  > By analyzing inputs as you play such as champion combos, idle time, APM, and how you perform in core game metrics
- [market] Reception: top comments are praise ("Blowing my mind every time dude", 28 likes); none in the top 20 checks the numbers. ^f12
  > Blowing my mind every time dude
