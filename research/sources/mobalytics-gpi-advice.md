# Mobalytics: How GPI advice is created and given to you (2017)

Why: how a review tool turns stats into a named weakness and words of advice.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Vendor's own first-hand account of its pipeline, by Agilio Macabasco, August 25, 2017, during the open beta; still served in 2026.
- Reasoning: fair. Walks through the pipeline with one worked example; admits what can't be measured and that specific advice is hard to make reliable.
- Interests: vendor.
- Weigh: old; the sensei voices and the 20-game minimum may have changed. The shape (rule triggers into pre-written advice) is the durable part.

## Findings

- [learning] Advice is rule based: conditions and triggers on stats pick an advice concept, which is then written up. ^f1
  > These triggers send signals to the GPI and the GPI recognizes the signs to send advice types which tell the player that they probably need to ward better and position better.
- [learning] Each skill is built from metrics, and each metric from smaller attributes; Vision is wards placed, cleared and bought. ^f2
  > Vision is defined by the metric combination of Wards Placed, Wards Cleared, and Wards Bought.
- [learning] Advice text is either linked creator content (streamers, YouTubers, pros) or written with the site's Challenger coaches. ^f3
  > External advice is drawn from the League of Legends community – these are streamers, Youtubers, and pro players.
- [learning] [ux/trust] A weakness is named as a pattern in the player's own games plus a fix: the base example. ^f4
  > When you get ganked, you often die. Improve your survivability by focusing on having better positioning and revisiting your Vision advice to improve your awareness.
- [learning] [ux/trust] The same advice comes in three voices the player chooses at onboarding: nurturing, neutral, tough love. ^f5
  > We have the nice and nurturing, the neutral middle-path, and the tough-love styles.
- [ux/trust] The tough-love voice insults on purpose, and the vendor says some users want that. ^f6
  > Unfortunately, it seems like you just never learn.
  > It’s a little harsh (and pretty funny), but some users simply like that sort of delivery to make the message hit home.
- [learning] Admits communication and shot calling can't be measured from game data. ^f7
  > communication/shot-calling for example are big parts of the game, but cannot be measured by current technologies
- [learning] [ux/trust] Admits reliable specific advice (e.g. a tank top laner misusing Teleport) is hard. ^f8
  > The problem is telling you that reliably and accurately is not easy, and we don’t like being inaccurate and unreliable.
- [learning] [stats] GPI needs at least 20 games in one role before it reads a play style, and used up to 100. ^f9
  > Our GPI needs at least 20 games in one of your roles (and for now, up to 100 games) to make a read on your play style.
- [learning] Sold as a graph that morphs as you play: the coming-back hook is your scores moving. ^f10
  > As you play, your graph morphs, your scores go up and down, and you’re delivered (to an almost spooky level) incredibly relevant advice to how you can improve.
- [ux/trust] (inferred) Mobalytics' blunt badge names on the match page ([[mobalytics-match-ui#^f9]]) descend from this tough-love voice, now applied to everyone rather than chosen. ^f11
