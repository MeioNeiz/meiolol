# Coach Rogue: Comparing All Of League's Neutral Objectives (Ranked By Win %)

Why: coach-stat-claims: do the objective win chance numbers hold in our ranked data?

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Numbers are second hand: a data scientist working with the Broken by
  Concept podcast, 30,000 games, which a commenter claiming to be that data scientist
  puts at evenly spread Platinum IV to Diamond I and calls win probability added (WPA).
  2025 season (Atakhan still in the game), patch not named, no intervals. 45k views.
- Reasoning: fair. He separates win chance added from raw win rate (soul teams win
  about 90% but were already ahead) and explains the ranking (permanent buffs survive
  throws); the grubs figure is multiplied by three by guesswork, which the data
  scientist corrects in the comments (+1.97% for all three).
- Interests: Black Friday sale on his guides and coaching; credits and links Broken by
  Concept.
- Weigh: the ranking is checkable in our timelines ([[coach-objectives-measured]]);
  Atakhan can't be (removed in 2026). The side-lane advice (fan out after a won fight) is
  coaching, untested here. Auto captions: "Adakan", "Adacon", "Attacon" are Atakhan.

## Findings

- [data] Source of the numbers: a data scientist for the Broken by Concept podcast sampled 30,000 games for the win chance added by each neutral objective. @2:55 ^f1
  > They had a data scientist sample 30,000 games in order to find win rate increases for neutral objectives taken
- [data] A commenter who says he is that data scientist: 30k ranked games evenly spread from Platinum IV to Diamond I; all three grubs add 1.97%, with split pushers 2.5%. ^f2
  > this data was calculated using 30k ranked games, evenly spread out between P4 and D1
  > the result is you only get +1.97% when taking all three grubs
- [data] First dragon adds 6% to the taking team's win chance. @2:06 ^f3
  > taking the first dragon, for example, increases your team's chance to win the game by a sizable 6%
- [data] Grubs add 1.37% for one or more; he triples it for all three and flags it as unsure. @2:55 ^f4
  > void grubs is a 1.37% win rate increase if you get one or more
- [data] Herald adds 1.5%; he has been giving it up for cross-map plays for a year. @3:43 ^f5
  > Getting Rift Herald is a win rate increase of only 1 and a half%
- [data] Atakhan adds 8.82%, the most of any objective. @3:43 ^f6
  > giving you an 8.82% higher chance to win the game if you manage to get it
- [data] Dragon soul adds 6.15%, though soul teams win about 90%, since they were already ahead. @4:29 @5:22 ^f7
  > you're looking at a 6.15% win rate chance increase
  > a team who gets a dragon soul is expected to win around 90% of the games if you look at those stats
- [data] Baron adds 6.2%; elder 8.29%. @7:06 @7:52 ^f8
  > 6.2% is the magic number
  > Elder Dragon sitting at a very comfortable 8.29%
- [learning] (inferred by him) Objectives with permanent buffs rank higher because solo queue teams throw leads and lose temporary buffs on death. @4:29 ^f9
  > objectives like dragon and that give permanent buffs to your team generally correlate to a higher win rate increase
- [learning] (claim) Untested prediction: by rank, the impact of grubs, herald, baron and elder would rise in high ranks. @8:38 ^f10
  > In high rank games, the impact of objectives like void grubs, herald, baron, and elder dragon would go up
- [learning] (claim) Baron's buff is about 1,500 team gold plus AD and AP worth 6,500 gold at its weakest; nine in ten players he coaches below Master take an inhibitor over baron. @6:09 @7:06 ^f11
  > it also gives your team just over 1,500 gold
  > Nine times out of 10 when I coach people below the Apex tiers, they are choosing inhib over Baron
- [learning] (claim) An undefended mid-game wave does about 2,000 damage to a tower; after a won fight with no objective up, fan out to push side waves rather than group mid. @9:17 @14:59 ^f12
  > the answer is about 2,000 tower damage
  > you should pretty much always fan out as much as you can across the map
