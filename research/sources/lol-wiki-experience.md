# League of Legends Wiki: Experience (champion)

Why: how champion kill XP depends on victim level and level difference, how minion XP
is shared, and jungle XP rules, to check the video's kill vs wave XP ratios.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Official community wiki; kill XP values cite Riot's 13.4 notes.
- Reasoning: fair. Rules with history; the per-level kill XP values live in a template
  (see [[lol-wiki-experience-src]]).
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: one inconsistency: the body says shared kill XP depends on the slain
  champion's level, the V14.10 history line says "your level" (^f8).

## Findings

- [learning] [data] Solo kill XP depends on the slain champion's level: 42 at level 1
  up to 990 at level 18. ^f1
  > When only a single ally is eligible for the champion's experience bounty (solo experience), they earn 42 – 990 (based on slain champion level) experience.
- [learning] Assisted kills give less total XP, split among everyone eligible. ^f2
  > When more than one ally is eligible for the champion's bounty, they share a total of 27.97 – 891 (based on slain champion level) experience.
- [learning] Level difference matters: each benefitting champion gets 20% less or more
  per full level of difference beyond the first (penalty floored at 40% total, from the
  13.4 history line). ^f3
  > 20% less or more, respectively, experience per full decimal level of difference beyond the first.
  > Experience penalty (earned experience from a lower level target) still has a minimum of 40% total experience.
- [learning] Minion XP: one champion gets 100%, two get 65% each (130% total), up to
  six at 21.7% each. ^f4
  > One champion receives 100% of the base experience bounty (_solo experience_).
  > Two champions each receive 65% of the base experience bounty (130% ÷ 2).
- [data] XP needed per level rises by 100 each level (280 to reach 2, 880 to reach 8,
  1280 to reach 12); the wiki's table puts level 8 at 14 solo waves and level 12 at
  30. ^f5
  > | 8 | 880 | 4060 | 14 | 21 |
  > | 12 | 1280 | 8580 | 30 | 43 |
- [learning] Jungle item users lose 70% to 0% of minion XP over the first 20 minutes,
  plus a further 50% if minion gold exceeds 40% of monster gold before 14:00; a
  jungler more than 1.1 levels behind the average gets 50 bonus XP per level behind
  from each large monster (catch-up XP). ^f6
  > They receive 70% – 0% (based on minutes) less experience from minion kills.
  > If they are more than 1.1 levels behind the game's average champion level upon killing a large monster, they gain an additional 50 **bonus** experience for every integer level of disadvantage, rounded to the nearest integer.
- [learning] Minions give catch-up XP too: a champion more than one level below the
  minion gets 20% or more extra, once enemy minions reach level 6. ^f7
  > This rule activates only after the enemy minions have reached level 6.
- [learning] Last change to shared kill XP: V14.10, 66.6% / 82% / 90% of solo by level
  (body text says slain champion's level, this line says yours). Solo kill XP values
  last changed in V13.4. ^f8
  > Shared champion kill experience modifier reduced to 66.6% / 82% / 90% (based on your level) of the solo kill experience from 66.6% / 82% / 100% (based on your level).
- [learning] Since V3.14 minions give the same XP all game; level thresholds were cut
  to match. ^f9
  > Minions are no longer worth more experience based on game time.
