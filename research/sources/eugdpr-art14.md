# EU GDPR Article 14 (gdpr-info.eu)

Why: EEA players are covered by the EU GDPR as well (identity-and-privacy C11); check the
EU text of the exemption matches the UK one.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. gdpr-info.eu, a widely used unofficial copy of the Regulation (the
  official text is EUR-Lex); wording matches the Regulation as adopted
- Reasoning: fair. Statute
- Interests: the site is run by a law firm's publisher (ads for its services)
- Weigh: trust the text; cite EUR-Lex if a regulator ever asks

## Findings

- [privacy] EU exemption is 14(5)(b): impossible or disproportionate effort, with the same duty to make the information public. Art 14(5)(b) ^f1
  > the provision of such information proves impossible or would involve a disproportionate effort
  > In such cases the controller shall take appropriate measures to protect the data subject’s rights and freedoms and legitimate interests, including making the information publicly available
- [privacy] (inferred) EU and UK texts agree on what a public page for crawled players must do; the UK adds the complaint to the controller (14(2)(da)) and names the regulator "the Commission". ^f2

  WP260, which say how to judge disproportionate effort: lead if challenged
