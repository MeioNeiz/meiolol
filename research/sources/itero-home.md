Why: iTero is a live, widely installed AI pick recommender in champ select, the closest commercial analogue to meiolol.

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Claims 1,000,000+ downloads and 4.4 rating; on the Overwolf store and standalone; operating Oct 2026.
