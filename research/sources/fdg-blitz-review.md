# Full Deck Gaming: Blitz.gg review, features, overlays, interface (video, 2022)

Why: Blitz's window structure, phase screens and a player's view of what reads well.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. One small creator, Aug 2022, 18k views; app on screen, ads hidden by the
  creator's own cards.
- Reasoning: fair. Opinions with reasons.
- Interests: none known for Blitz (referral is for Porofessor elsewhere).
- Weigh: structure is first-hand; the app has changed since.

## Findings

- [ux/desktop] Blitz's main window: profile with match history in the middle, ranked
  journey on the left, champion performance below, a table view toggle; tabs for
  statistics, champion pages, pro builds, tier list, overlays. @0:52 ^f1
  > on your profile page it has your match history in the middle
  > on the left is your season 12 ranked journey
- [ux/desktop] [needs] Wide stat tables are judged useless; the reviewer asks for charts
  like Porofessor's. @0:52 ^f2
  > look at this is anyone actually going to go through this and read any of these stats hell no
  > we need a visual representation of this data if we are ever going to get anything from it at all
- [ux/desktop] Overlays are switched on and off in one settings tab listing each overlay
  (benchmarking, jungle timers, skill orders, ult timers, trinket reminders). @6:05 ^f3
  > if we look at the overlays tab you can turn on some overlays and turn off some overlays
- [ux/desktop] Pre-game (after lock-in): the team comp with rank, win rate and games,
  coloured dots marking duos, then the champion page with rune and spell import, then a
  tier list, in one scrolling column. @7:44 ^f4
  > you can see who is on a team indicating the colored dots who's duo queuing
  > you can scroll down to see a lot of information from the champion page and this is where you can import your runes and spells
- [ux/desktop] Post-game: medals for what went well and an LP counter at the top, three
  bars comparing income, vision and combat to every rank, then benchmarking, a timeline,
  both teams' tables, an advanced stats page and a gold graph tab. @8:40 ^f5
  > starting from the top there are medals to award you for what you did well and an lp counter
  > there are three bar graphs that compare you to all of the ranks in terms of income vision and combat
- [needs] The advanced stats page is "too cluttered"; the gold graph is loved because it
  shows where the game turned. @8:40 ^f6
  > there is an advanced stat page that is way too cluttered to be any type of useful
  > there is a gold graph tab that i absolutely love you can see exactly where the game got out of hand
- [needs] Ads covered content the reviewer needed to see. @10:40 ^f7
  > there were times when i was making this video and i couldn't get an ad out of the way to see what i wanted to talk about behind it
