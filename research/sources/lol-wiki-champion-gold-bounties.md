# League of Legends Wiki: Champion gold bounties

Why: does kill gold scale with victim level, as the video says, or is it a flat base
adjusted by bounties?

## Assessment

- Evidence: B. Official community wiki; 26.1 items match Riot's notes
  ([[lol-patch-26-1#^f5]], [[lol-patch-26-1#^f6]]).
- Reasoning: fair. Detailed rules and long history; some rates come from editors'
  testing.
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: base values and history are solid; the suppression thresholds are not public.

## Findings

- [learning] [data] Base kill gold does depend on victim level, but mildly: 300 for
  levels 1 to 6, then +10 a level to 420 at 18 (40% more at most). Since V25.09; before
  that it was 300 at all levels. ^f1
  > 300 / 300 / 300 / 300 / 300 / 300 / 310 / 320 / 330 / 340 / 350 / 360 / 370 / 380 / 390 / 400 / 410 / 420 gold.
  > Base champion bounty increased to 300 – 420 (based on level) from 300 at all levels.
- [learning] First Blood gives 100 bonus gold (back since V26.01). ^f2
  > First Blood grants 100 **bonus** gold.
- [learning] [data] Assist gold: half the kill bounty, shared among assisters, capped at
  half the base (150 to 210), and cut to 50% to 100% early in the game. ^f3
  > Assist bounties are always half of the kill bounty that the slain champion has dispensed, capped at 50% of the **base** amount.
  > Assist bounties are reduced to 50% – 100% (based on Game Time) of their normal value early in the game.
- [learning] Actual kill gold moves far more with performance than with level: it
  rises with the victim's kills, assists and farm (1 bounty gold per 3 kill gold, 1 per
  20 farm gold), falls when they die (1 per 3.5 gold given), never below 50 and never
  above base + 700 per kill. ^f4
  > at a rate of 1 bounty gold : 3 gold earned.
  > The minimum kill bounty a champion can dispense is 50
  > A single kill **cannot** award more than this amount to the killer.
- [learning] After 6:00, shutdown gold is cut 30% to 100% for teams not convincingly
  ahead, so close games pay less for kills on fed players. ^f5
  > After **6:00** game time, if a team is not convincingly winning then Shut Down gold is instantly and dynamically reduced by 30% / 60% / 90% / 100% (based on team advantage) near the equilibrium.
- [learning] Latest change on the live season: V26.03 slowed depreciation to 1:3.5 and
  negative-state recovery to 1:7. ^f6
  > Depreciation rate from deaths reduced to 1 bounty gold : 3.5 gold distributed from 1 bounty gold : 2.5 gold distributed.
- [learning] (inferred) Kill gold vs a cannon wave (152 gold early, 141 after 14:00,
  137 to 148 later): a level 2 kill (300, 400 with First Blood) is already worth about
  2x a cannon wave in gold; a level 12 kill (360) about 2.5x. So in gold the kill never
  falls below a wave, and the ratio grows only modestly with level. ^f7
