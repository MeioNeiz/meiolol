# GOV.UK writing guidelines: Summaries

Why: numeric limit for the one-line description under a title.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. GOV.UK publishing standard.
- Reasoning: fair. Number justified by search snippet length.
- Interests: none.
- Weigh: the closest GOV.UK analogue to a page's one-line lede on meio.lol.

## Findings

- [ux] Summaries at most 160 characters including spaces. Keep your summary short ^f1
  > Keep all summaries to 160 characters, including spaces.
- [ux] If longer, the main point goes in the first 160 characters. Keep your summary short ^f2
  > If your summary is longer, make sure you cover the main point of the page in the first 160 characters.
- [ux] No preamble naming the content type ("this consultation is about"). Avoid redundant introductory words ^f3
  > Do not mention the content type in the summary.
- [ux] The summary adds words the title lacks rather than repeating it. Avoid redundant introductory words ^f4
  > Use as few words from the title as possible, and include keywords that you've not used in the title.
- [ux] (inferred) A page lede on meio.lol: one line, 160 characters at most, no "This
  page shows". ^f5
