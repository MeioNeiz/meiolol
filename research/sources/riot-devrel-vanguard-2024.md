# Vanguard Updates for third-party developers (Riot DevRel, 2024-02-07)

Why: what Vanguard's arrival in League (2024) removed from third-party tools.

## Assessment

- Evidence: A. Riot DevRel, dated 7 Feb 2024; short notice before the April FAQ
  ([[riot-vanguard-faq-devrel]]).
- Reasoning: fair. Plain bullets, no reasons.
- Interests: Riot.
- Weigh: the 1% figure is Riot's estimate before launch.

## Findings

- [tools/removed] [policy] Riot estimated Vanguard would affect about 1% of third-party
  apps. ^f1
  > We expect this will only impact around 1% of third-party applications
- [tools/removed] [policy] External memory-reading tools are not allowed under Vanguard. ^f2
  > External tools reading memory will not be allowed
- [policy] Valorant's allowed tools and methods carry over to LoL and TFT. ^f3
  > If you develop for VALORANT, you can use similar tools and methods for LoL and TFT
- [policy] Launch planned for Q1 2024 via PBE with at least two weeks' testing. ^f4
  > It will hit PBE first, and you will have at least two weeks to test things out before Vanguard is live
- [tools/removed] Checked, not there: no list of affected apps or features; overlays and
  LCU apps are not named (the April FAQ says they keep working). ^f5
