# ONE Esports: LS's new draft website (Mar 2023)

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. News relay of LS's own announcement, March 2023; current state of drafting.gg unchecked.

## Findings

- [draft] [ux/features] Draft simulator, solo or "versus mode" by sending a link; drag and drop champions; planned slider for extra "theoretical drafts". ^f1
  > You can simulate the draft on your own, or click on “versus mode” at the top to send a link to another party to get involved.
- [tools/accounts] Sign-up is optional and only saves tier lists and drafts; sharing works by generated URL. ^f2
  > Signing up with his site allows you to save your lists and drafts.
  > LS’ version has a search function, and a URL generator so that you can share your lists easily.
- [needs] [draft] Target users are Clash teams who scout opponents on OP.GG and mock draft with friends. ^f3
  > They look up their opponents on OP.GG… and do mock drafts with their friends.
- [ux/features] Planned "Rift" map: fog of war and wards toggles, travel time from items. ^f4
  > it will also have the capability to display how much time a champion takes in order to move to a particular location on the map based on the items they have.
