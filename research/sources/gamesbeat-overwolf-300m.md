# GamesBeat: Overwolf paid out $300M to creators in 2025 (Nov 2025)

Why: is Overwolf, the platform most League apps grew up on, shrinking as apps go
standalone, or growing?

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Company figures relayed by a trade reporter; no audit. Payouts are what
  creators received, not Overwolf revenue.
- Reasoning: weak. Milestone release.
- Interests: Overwolf PR ahead of its CEO's GamesBeat Next panel.
- Weigh: direction (growth, mods-led) more than the exact numbers.

## Findings

- [market] (claim) Overwolf paid creators $300M in 2025 and targets $1 billion a year by
  2030. ^f1
  > Our $300 million payout this year is a milestone, but the work isn't over, as we are on a mission to pay $1 billion in annual creator payouts by 2030.
- [market] (claim) Scale: 1,500+ games, 178,000 creators, 113 million monthly active users. ^f2
  > With over 1,500 supported games, 178,000 creators, and 113 million monthly active users
- [market] The growth story is mods (Minecraft, The Sims 4) and Premium Mods, not in-game
  apps for competitive games; League is not mentioned. ^f3
  > supporting titles across genres, including Minecraft, with 240,000 mods and 93 billion downloads, and The Sims 4, with 100,000 mods and 2.8 billion downloads.
- [tools/paid] Revenue split: opt-in display ads pay creators a share; Premium Mod authors
  get 50%. ^f4
  > Creators who opt in to display ads receive a share of every ad dollar, while Premium Mod authors earn 50% of all revenue generated.
- [market] (inferred) Overwolf is healthy but its weight has moved to mods; League apps
  leaving for standalone builds (Porofessor Dec 2025, iTero Nov 2025) after Riot's May 2025
  in-game ad ban ([[overwolf-riot-creators-response#^f1]]) cost it little in its own story. ^f5
