# Coach Rogue: Core Item Build Secrets ONLY PRO Players Know!

Why: how a coach tells players to read an item build (order, synergy, enemy team), and
what that means for build pages that list first items by pick and win rate.

## Assessment

- Evidence: C. Item mechanics read from tooltips (Infinity Edge needs crit, Sterak's
  scales with bonus health, percent pen before flat pen) shown on one Jarvan and one
  Diana example; no game data, win rates or timings. Former pro (self-stated, 7 years).
  235k views, 9.2k likes.
- Reasoning: fair. Explains why each item order works and names the condition (enemy
  damage type, tankiness, unavoidable CC) for switching core, but never weighs the cost
  of a slower spike against a lead, and the "power spike at three items" is asserted.
  A commenter makes that point (by item three most of the outcome is decided).
- Interests: plugs his paid guides at 0:38 (a commenter balks at the ~100 price) and
  Waveclear in the description.
- Weigh: the mechanics are checkable on the wiki; the "Eclipse first on bruiser Jarvan
  is a mistake" kind of claim is testable as a first-item order split in our builds data,
  but learned.md (Items, 13 Oct) found the finished item adds almost nothing once timing
  and lead are known. Captions: "coachtro.com" at 10:35 is coachrogue.com.

## Findings

- [learning] Says champions mostly build the same core two to three items before any variance, and a wrong order "delays your multiplicative scalings". @0:00 ^f1
  > tons of different champions most often end up building the same core two to three items as one another before they're able to show any variance at all
- [learning] Frame: a core build is one or two key items you maximise, and everything else exists to enable them. @1:46 ^f2
  > they boil down to basically one or two key items that you're trying to maximize the effect of, and everything else that you're building exists to simply enable
- [learning] Infinity Edge's passive is only gold efficient at 75% crit, so two crit items come first and a non-crit first or second item delays the spike. @1:46 ^f3
  > this passive only becomes gold efficient when you reach a 75% critical strike chance
- [learning] Picks the core by the enemy team: unavoidable CC (Poppy, Vi) and mostly physical burst (Graves, Vi, Mel) call for a tankier bruiser Jarvan with armour, not lethality. @2:27 ^f4
  > When versing an enemy team composition that has unavoidable crowd control, you generally want to opt for a tankier build
- [learning] Sterak's Gage shield scales with bonus health, so build two health items first (Sundered Sky into Black Cleaver), spike at three items, bigger spike at four with Death's Dance. @3:12 @4:02 ^f5
  > you need to get a good foundation of health items before buying a Sterak's Gage
  > we receive a significant power spike at three items when we get our Sterak's and we receive a massive game-altering power spike at four items
- [learning] Calls a first Eclipse on bruiser Jarvan a mistake: each item is good alone, but they don't build toward the same core, like Youmuu's first on a crit ADC. @4:53 ^f6
  > Individually, all of these items are incredible on Jarvan, but together they don't make any sense because they don't contribute to the same core item build
- [learning] Some champions have two cores chosen by the enemy team: Diana's Riftmaker bruiser build (with Liandry's) into tanky teams, Deathcap burst with Lich Bane into squishy teams. @5:15 @6:06 @6:57 ^f7
  > Some champions even have multiple choices for core item builds that you can change depending on the enemy team composition you're going against that game
  > the full AP burst assassin centered around Deathcap and Magic Pen
- [learning] Percent penetration applies before flat, so burst mages and assassins build flat pen first and percent pen at the core spike; champion kits that shred resists (Aphelios's lethality, Rumble E, Jarvan Q) stack with it. @7:39 @8:46 @9:40 ^f8
  > the game calculates percentage pen before it calculates flat pen
  > You first build a foundation of flat pen, and then you combine that with percentage pen
- [learning] [needs] Closing advice: find the item at the centre of your build, read what it scales off, and buy what complements it. @10:35 ^f9
  > think about your champion, think about which of these items you buy, and then go read up on exactly what the item does, and specifically what stats does the item scale off of
- [needs] [ux/features] Top comments: viewers want the reason to pick one item over another, not "just do this build"; one blames the client for not showing item ratios. ^f10

  > by the time you get your third item, if the game even goes that long, most of the inputs to the game's outcome have already happened by then
