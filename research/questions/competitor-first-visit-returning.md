# What do the big League tools show a first-time visitor, and what brings players back (accounts, progress, habit, notifications)?

Feeds: docs/plan.md (home page, Daily Draft, Squads, the review team's next steps)

## Answer

First visit: stats sites lead with one search box for a Riot ID (OP.GG, Blitz) or with
data and sample size (LoLalytics), under a consent banner; draft tools open straight on
an empty draft board (DraftGap, LoLDraftAI, which adds a 16-step tour); Mobalytics leads
with an app download and an improvement-loop pitch (C1, C2, C3). Nobody leads with a
worked example of a reviewed game.

Returning use is mostly paid and account based: OP.GG favourites with game-start alerts
(10 free, 30 paid, alerts on mobile), U.GG PLUS's Discord bot with post-match summaries
and a squad leaderboard, Mobalytics Plus progress tracking, Challenges and highlights
(C4, C5, C6). Daily content has arrived at the incumbents: Porofessor's new Arcade has
daily puzzles (C7). AI match review is now an OP.GG Premium feature, worded as advice
(C8), and OP.GG and Mobalytics push live in-game coaching (C9). Uplift wording ("users
win more") sits on Mobalytics' homepage (C10).

## Claims

- C1: Stats sites' first screen is a consent banner and a Riot ID search box, or data and sample size, not an example. for: [[opgg-home#^f1]], [[blitz-lol#^f1]], [[lolalytics-home#^f1]], [[leagueofgraphs-home#^f2]]
- C2: Draft tools open on an empty draft board with a sorted list or a Suggest button; LoLDraftAI adds a guided tour. for: [[draftgap-site#^f1]], [[draftgap-site#^f2]], [[loldraftai-draft#^f1]], [[loldraftai-draft#^f2]]
- C3: Mobalytics' first ask is a desktop (Overwolf) download, pitched as a loop before, during, after and between games. for: [[mobalytics-home#^f1]], [[mobalytics-home#^f2]]
- C4: OP.GG brings players back with favourites and game-start alerts, more of both on paid tiers. for: [[opgg-membership#^f4]], [[opgg-membership#^f5]]
- C5: U.GG's return hook is a paid Discord bot: post-match summaries and a squad leaderboard, plus personal counters and tier list. for: [[ugg-home#^f1]], [[ugg-plus#^f2]], [[ugg-plus#^f4]]
- C6: Mobalytics Plus sells progress tracking, Challenges and post-game highlights. for: [[mobalytics-plus#^f2]], [[mobalytics-plus#^f3]], [[mobalytics-plus#^f4]]
- C7: Daily puzzles now exist at an incumbent: Porofessor Arcade (daily puzzles, linked from Porofessor and League of Graphs). for: [[porofessor-home#^f2]], [[leagueofgraphs-home#^f5]]
- C8: OP.GG Premium has an AI Agent that reviews your match as advice ("clear the wave before grouping"). for: [[opgg-membership#^f2]], [[opgg-membership#^f3]]
- C9: OP.GG and Mobalytics sell live in-game coaching (voice calls, overlays). for: [[opgg-ai#^f1]], [[opgg-ai#^f3]], [[mobalytics-home#^f2]], [[blitz-lol#^f5]]
- C10: Mobalytics claims users win more and climb faster (27%), uplift wording on its homepage. for: [[mobalytics-home#^f4]], [[mobalytics-home#^f8]]
- C11 (inferred): Free, account-free return hooks (daily puzzle with local streak, shareable result) and a worked example on first visit are where meiolol differs from the paid account loops. from: C1, C4, C5, C6
