# What kinds of third-party League tools does Riot encourage, feature or partner with, and what is it moving against?

Feeds: docs/concept.md; production key application framing (docs/production-key.md)

## Answer

Riot's own contests consistently reward post-game learning, reflection and improvement
(C1), social and shareable features (C2) and novelty over another stats site (C3), judged on
polish, working demos and a written method (C4). The latest, Rift Rewind 2025 (judged by
League product and publishing staff), asked for exactly this, and a winner recommends new
champions from mastery data (C8). The registration popup's "Please Do" list says the same:
connect players with friends, help them evaluate and improve their own gameplay.

Riot is moving against tools that identify or scout players in live lobbies and against
non-API data (memory, replays) (C6), and its rules reject apps that dictate decisions (see
riot-production-key C3). It builds popular features into the client itself (C7, weak).
There is no current "Riot-approved" or partner list (C5). Pre-2020 winners are weak
templates: some of what Riot praised then is now off-limits (C9).

## Claims

- C1: Riot's contests consistently reward post-game learning, reflection and improvement tools. for: [[riot-hackathon-2018#^f3]], [[riot-rift-rewind-2025#^f1]], [[riot-rift-rewind-2025#^f5]], [[comicbook-lol-stats-tab#^f4]]
- C2: Riot explicitly values social and shareable features (friends, sharing, comparisons). for: [[riot-api-challenge-2018#^f2]], [[riot-rift-rewind-2025#^f3]], [[riot-api-challenge-winter-2019#^f3]]
- C3: DevRel judges novelty over clones of existing stats sites. for: [[riot-rift-rewind-2025#^f4]], [[riot-lor-dev-challenge#^f2]], [[riot-lor-dev-challenge#^f4]], [[riot-api-challenge-2018#^f4]]
- C4: DevRel weighs polish, working demos and documentation or method write-ups. for: [[riot-api-challenge-2018#^f5]], [[riot-lor-dev-challenge#^f5]], [[riot-rift-rewind-2025#^f7]]
- C5: Riot has treated big tool companies as partners (judges, co-builders), but no current League partner or approved list exists. for: [[riot-hackathon-2018#^f4]], [[riot-lor-dev-challenge#^f1]], [[riot-curse-voice-partnership#^f1]], [[riot-support-third-party-apps#^f6]]
- C6: Riot is moving against tools that identify or scout players in live lobbies and against non-API data access. for: [[riot-dev-streamer-mode-2025#^f1]], [[riot-champ-select-anon-apps-1222#^f1]], [[itero-companion-apps#^f6]], [[riot-memory-access-removed#^f1]], [[riot-memory-access-removed#^f2]], [[lol-patch-26-20#^f2]], [[pcgamesn-blitz-plugins#^f2]]
- C7: Riot ships first-party versions of popular third-party features, a replacement risk for generic stats or social utilities. for: [[comicbook-lol-stats-tab#^f1]], [[riot-dev-streamer-mode-2025#^f8]]
- C8: Champion discovery from mastery data won Riot's 2025 hackathon and fits the diversity rule. for: [[riot-rift-rewind-winners#^f1]], [[riot-policies-general#^f6]]
- C9: Some tools Riot praised in 2018 (in-game reactive advice) are now unapproved. for: [[riot-hackathon-2018#^f9]], [[riot-support-third-party-apps#^f3]]
- C10: A draft guess-the-winner game on match data got a production key and RSO quickly once it showed traffic (2023). for: [[reddit-aramguessr-update#^f2]], [[reddit-aramguessr-update#^f3]], [[reddit-aramguessr-update#^f4]]
- C11 (inferred): The most approvable meiolol is post-game and between-games learning plus a social game, with champ select help limited to champion facts. from: C1, C2, C3, C6, C10

## Disagreements

None direct. Contest briefs show what Riot showcases, not what production review approves;
C10 is the only approval data point for a game, and it is 2023.
