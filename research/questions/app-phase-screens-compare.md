# Per phase (champ select, loading, in game, post game), what do Porofessor, Blitz, Mobalytics, OP.GG, U.GG and iTero desktop apps show at a glance, what do players praise or complain about, and what does meiolol's app lack?

Feeds: the app screens rebuild (docs/plan/app.md "Window layout"; meionotes 132-139); ranked proposals in data/team/2026-11-05-meionotes/cmp-app.md. Builds on [[desktop-app-layout]] (layout shapes) with what each phase shows and what players use.

## Answer

On the loading screen players look at their lane opponent: runes, MR or armour, off-role or off-main (C1); Porofessor's cards pack about fifteen figures in fixed zones with meaning-coloured tags and a team stat row (C2), which is why it reads as dense where ours is centred lines. No rival shows the lane's expected result, which our data has (C5, inferred). In game players keep apps for timers and builds and drop stats panels (C3); rivals' in-game build shows alternatives with win rates (desktop-app-layout C8, C11). Post game the at-a-glance layer is award figures and mirrored rows with items (desktop-app-layout C12). Recent independent reviews (2025-26) are thin: three searches found vendor roundups only.

## Claims

- C1: On the loading screen players check the lane opponent (runes, MR or armour, off-role or off-main) more than anything else. for: [[yt-stop-loading-stats#^f2]], [[yt-stop-loading-stats#^f3]], [[reddit-lookup-stats-sentiment#^f3]]
- C2: Porofessor's loading cards hold about fifteen figures in fixed zones plus meaning-coloured tags explained with a number, and a team stat row under the cards. for: [[porofessor-download#^f12]], [[yt-porofessor-tags#^f1]], [[yt-porofessor-tags#^f2]]
- C3: Some players keep companion apps for timers (jungle camps, spells) and ignore the stats. for: [[yt-stop-loading-stats#^f4]]
- C4: A critic view holds that loading screen stats make players play to the numbers. for: [[yt-stop-loading-stats#^f1]]
- C5 (inferred): A lane strip with the expected lane result between our card and theirs is ours alone and answers C1 without player data. from: C1, C2
