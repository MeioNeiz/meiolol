# How do good teaching products and coaches teach a skill like drafting, and what should meio.lol's Learn copy?

Feeds: docs/learn-proposal.md (Learn redesign, docs/plan/site.md "Learn")

## Answer

Teach by letting the player try first, then name the idea against their own attempt
(C1, C3), with hints that point attention rather than give the rule (C2). Predictions
must be committed before the reveal and answered at once; confident wrong guesses teach
most (C4). Puzzles come from real games and keep only ones with a single clearly best
answer (C8): the lichess rule, and the reason near-even matchups felt unfair to Jacob.
Difficulty is measured from attempts on one rating scale with the player (C9). Concepts
are named and drillable alone, but the default round mixes them so the player must
recognise the idea (C10); practice returns to weak and decaying ideas over days (C5,
C11). Fun modes (streaks, rush) sit beside, unrated (C12, C13). Chess explains little
in words because the line explains itself; a draft puzzle has no line, so the reveal
must carry the why, with numbers (C14). Asking for the reason checks understanding
better than the answer alone (C6). Coaches' own vocabulary (comfort first, then
structure, then counters; safe blind picks) comes only from weak sources (C7).

## Claims

- C1: Attempting a problem before the idea is taught beats teaching first, for
  understanding and transfer. for: [[sinha-kapur-2021-ps-i#^f1]], [[sinha-kapur-2021-ps-i#^f2]]
- C2: Guided discovery beats pure discovery; a hint on what to look at, without the
  rule, is enough guidance. for: [[mayer-2004-three-strikes#^f1]], [[mayer-2004-three-strikes#^f2]], [[mayer-2004-three-strikes#^f5]]
- C3: The teaching after the attempt should build on it, contrasting the learner's
  answer with the right one. for: [[sinha-kapur-2021-ps-i#^f5]], [[sinha-kapur-2021-ps-i#^f6]]
- C4: Predict then reveal works when players commit with some confidence and see the
  result at once; stating a belief after the answer does not. for: [[brod-2021-predicting#^f2]], [[brod-2021-predicting#^f3]], [[brod-2021-predicting#^f4]], [[brod-2021-predicting#^f7]]
- C5: Bring ideas back later as fresh questions, not pages to reread. for: [[dunlosky-2013-techniques#^f1]], [[dunlosky-2013-techniques#^f3]], [[brod-2021-predicting#^f9]]
- C6: Asking for the reason checks understanding better than the pick alone. for: [[otieno-2011-fading#^f6]], [[otieno-2011-fading#^f7]]
- C7: Coaches teach draft as win condition, engage vs disengage, lane priority, blind vs
  counter pick, and comfort before counters. for: [[boostroom-draft-comps#^f3]], [[boostroom-draft-comps#^f5]], [[boostroom-draft-comps#^f6]], [[boostroom-draft-comps#^f8]], [[guildorder-draft-theory#^f1]], [[guildorder-draft-theory#^f3]]
- C8: Strong puzzle products make puzzles from real games and keep only positions with
  one clearly best answer. for: [[lichess-puzzle-db#^f1]], [[lichess-puzzle-db#^f3]], [[lichess-new-puzzles-blog#^f1]], [[lichess-new-puzzles-blog#^f2]]
- C9: Difficulty comes from attempts: puzzles and players rated on one scale, used to
  pick the next puzzle. for: [[lichess-new-puzzles-blog#^f6]], [[chesscom-puzzle-ratings#^f3]], [[lichess-puzzle-ui-strings#^f4]], [[duolingo-hlr-paper#^f7]]
- C10: Concepts are named, defined in a line and drillable alone; the default round is
  mixed, and naming the theme is a hint. for: [[lichess-puzzle-themes#^f1]], [[lichess-puzzle-themes#^f2]], [[lichess-puzzle-themes#^f5]], [[chesscom-puzzles#^f8]], [[lichess-new-puzzles-blog#^f7]]
- C11: Practice returns to each learner's weak or decaying items over days; a better
  schedule raised Duolingo's daily engagement. for: [[duolingo-hlr-paper#^f1]], [[duolingo-hlr-paper#^f3]], [[duolingo-hlr-paper#^f4]], [[duolingo-hlr-paper#^f5]]
- C12: Timed, versus and streak modes are kept apart from rated learning. for: [[chesscom-puzzles#^f4]], [[chesscom-puzzles#^f8]], [[lichess-puzzle-ui-strings#^f5]]
- C13: A daily streak brings players back; forgiving breaks help. for: [[duolingo-streaks#^f3]], [[duolingo-streaks#^f4]], [[duolingo-streaks#^f5]], [[chesscom-puzzles#^f7]], [[lichess-puzzle-db#^f7]]
- C14 (inferred): Chess puzzles explain almost nothing in words; a draft puzzle has no
  forced line, so meio.lol's reveal must say why, with numbers. from: C8, C10

## Disagreements

Worked examples first ([[otieno-2011-fading#^f1]], novices) against problem first
([[sinha-kapur-2021-ps-i#^f1]]). The problem-first edge is for understanding and
transfer only ([[sinha-kapur-2021-ps-i#^f3]]), and worked examples help early on
([[otieno-2011-fading#^f2]]). Reading: open each idea with a situation, then the worked
answer; a complete newcomer gets one solved draft first.
