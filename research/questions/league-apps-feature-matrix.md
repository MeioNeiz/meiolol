# Feature by feature, what do existing League apps and sites offer in each game phase, what is paid, how do accounts work, and what did Riot make them remove?

Feeds: two-product plan: the public app and site (production key rules, docs/production-key.md "Product rules") and the private "op" tool for Jacob and friends behind logins. Extends [[league-tools-landscape]], [[competitor-first-visit-returning]], [[riot-production-key]], [[riot-appreciated-tools]], [[player-tool-needs]]. Removals timeline: [[league-tools-riot-removals]].

## Answer

Every big companion (OP.GG, Porofessor, Blitz, Mobalytics, iTero, Facecheck, Team Advisor,
U.GG) ships the same phase loop: pick and ban suggestions plus rune and build import in champ
select, scouting of all ten players on the loading screen, jungle and inhibitor timers and gold
leads in game, a post-game report, and progress pages between games (C1, C2, C3, C4). Draft
tools differ in depth: personal pools and mastery (C5), scores or team win % per candidate
(C6). Lobby and queue help is now just one-click accept and duo finders (C7).

Paid tiers sell little that is "op": ad removal, comfort (more favourites, unlimited refresh,
storage), personal views (a dashboard, a personal tier list, Masters+ builds) and AI review;
the best draft help is mostly free (C8, C9). Accounts: the vendor's own login plus an optional
RSO link for vanity and a verified badge; most Overwolf apps need no sign-in, and a forced login
that only saves settings draws complaints (C10, C11). Group features are thin and vendor-bound:
favourites with game-start alerts, a 12-player Discord leaderboard, rival comparison, shared
champion pools in one small app (C12).

Riot has cut, by DevRel notice and without grandfathering, champ select names (2022), enemy ult
and spell cooldowns, power spike and decision prompts, in-game ads (all 2025), auto-accept and
streamer-mode identities (2025) ([[league-tools-riot-removals]]). Some shipped apps still sit
on the line (OP.GG AI voice calls, Mobalytics power spike copy) (C13).

Two products: a personal key covers "a small private community", but no Riot text exempts it
from the game integrity rules, and RSO needs a production key, so the private tool needs its
own login (C14, C15, C16). What may differ is wording, depth and audience, not the hard lines
(C17, C18).

## Claims

- C1: Champ select pick and ban suggestions with rune and build import are standard in the big apps. for: [[overwolf-opgg#^f2]], [[overwolf-opgg#^f3]], [[porofessor-download#^f2]], [[blitz-lol#^f2]], [[overwolf-facecheck#^f2]], [[overwolf-itero#^f2]], [[mobalytics-desktop-guide#^f3]]
- C2: Loading screen scouting of all ten players (rank, habits, aggression, streaks, tags) is mainstream and valued by players for the lane opponent. for: [[overwolf-itero#^f3]], [[europeangaming-itero-standalone#^f5]], [[porofessor-download#^f4]], [[overwolf-facecheck#^f2]], [[pcgamesn-blitz-plugins#^f4]], [[reddit-lookup-stats-sentiment#^f3]], [[reddit-porofessor-without-overwolf#^f1]]
- C3: In game, apps now show jungle and inhibitor timers, gold leads and benchmarks against your tier; teammate ult timers survive, enemy ones do not. for: [[overwolf-porofessor#^f3]], [[opgg-desktop-lol#^f3]], [[blitz-lol-overlays#^f4]], [[overwolf-mobalytics-desktop#^f4]]
- C4: Post game and between games: AI review or AI score, highlights, recordings, progress tracking against same-rank players, private notes. for: [[opgg-membership#^f2]], [[deeplol-profile#^f1]], [[mobalytics-plus#^f4]], [[overwolf-itero#^f4]], [[opgg-help-rso#^f5]], [[overwolf-outplayed#^f2]], [[insights-lol#^f1]]
- C5: Draft help personalises to the player's pool, mastery and recent losses (Mobalytics bans from your last 20 games, iTero "Consider Mastery"). for: [[mobalytics-desktop-guide#^f2]], [[mobalytics-desktop-guide#^f3]], [[loldraftai-vs-itero#^f4]], [[itero-home#^f2]]
- C6: Leading draft apps show ranked candidates with scores or a team win % and pitch "highest chance of winning", with no visible Riot pushback. for: [[loldraftai-vs-itero#^f2]], [[europeangaming-itero-standalone#^f2]], [[overwolf-team-advisor#^f1]], [[itero-companion-apps#^f2]], [[metasrc-counter-picker#^f3]]
- C7: Lobby and queue help is one-click accept, auto-hover of bans and picks, duo finders and LFG boards; auto-accept is gone. for: [[overwolf-team-advisor#^f2]], [[overwolf-team-advisor#^f3]], [[overwolf-team-advisor#^f4]], [[opgg-help-desktop#^f7]], [[porofessor-download#^f8]]
- C8: Paid tiers sell ad removal, comfort and personal views, not unique stats: Blitz $3.33 to 4.99 (ad-free, Masters+ and OTP builds), Tracker $2.92 to 3.99 (cosmetic), U.GG PLUS $2.49 to 3.99 (personal tier list, Discord bot), OP.GG (dashboard, 30 favourites, unlimited refresh). for: [[blitz-premium#^f3]], [[tracker-premium#^f2]], [[tracker-premium#^f4]], [[ugg-plus#^f1]], [[ugg-plus#^f4]], [[opgg-hs-397-op-gg-premium-guide-web#^f3]], [[opgg-hs-397-op-gg-premium-guide-web#^f5]], [[opgg-help-desktop#^f9]]
- C9: Where depth is paid, it is models, coaching and storage: LoLDraftAI desktop sync and pro model ($3.99, $29.99), iTero model customisation, OP.GG and Ascent AI review, Mobalytics Plus ($5.83 to 9.99). for: [[loldraftai-pricing#^f2]], [[loldraftai-vs-itero#^f3]], [[itero-home#^f3]], [[opgg-membership#^f2]], [[ascent-update-2026#^f2]], [[mobalytics-plus#^f1]]
- C10: The usual account model is the vendor's own login plus an optional RSO link (one Riot account per vendor account) that unlocks a verified badge, vanity and private notes. for: [[opgg-help-rso#^f1]], [[opgg-help-desktop#^f3]], [[opgg-hs-372-december-29-2022-patch-notes#^f2]], [[opgg-help-rso#^f2]], [[tracker-lol-launch#^f1]]
- C11: Most Overwolf apps need no sign-in; Porofessor's forced standalone login drew complaints, and "no account" is praised. for: [[overwolf-itero#^f9]], [[overwolf-facecheck#^f9]], [[porofessor-standalone-reddit#^f6]], [[porofessor-standalone-reddit#^f7]], [[ascent-update-2026#^f11]]
- C12: Friend and group features are thin and limited to one vendor's users: favourites with game-start alerts (5, 10, 30), U.GG's Discord leaderboard for 12, OP.GG rival and duo invites, ProComps' shared pools and teams. for: [[opgg-hs-397-op-gg-premium-guide-web#^f4]], [[ugg-plus#^f2]], [[opgg-help-desktop#^f7]], [[opgg-help-desktop#^f8]], [[overwolf-procomps#^f4]], [[opgg-duo#^f3]]
- C13: Some shipped features still sit on Riot's prohibited line in Oct 2026: OP.GG AI voice fight calls, Mobalytics power spike copy, Facecheck enemy spells. for: [[opgg-help-desktop#^f15]], [[overwolf-mobalytics-desktop#^f4]], [[overwolf-facecheck#^f3]], [[overwolf-aug-2025-riot-guidelines#^f2]], [[overwolf-aug-2025-riot-guidelines#^f3]]
- C14: A personal key is meant for "just the developer or a small private community", at 20/1s and 100/2min, never public, one product per key. for: [[riot-docs-portal#^f13]], [[riot-docs-portal#^f7]], [[riot-docs-portal#^f8]], [[riot-policies-general#^f8]], [[riot-docs-portal#^f11]]
- C15: Riot's policies bind every product owner regardless of key; no text exempts personal-key or LCU products from the game integrity rules. for: [[riot-docs-portal#^f14]], [[riot-policies-general#^f3]], [[riot-devrel-lcu-api-policy#^f4]]
- C16: RSO is only for production keys, so a private tool cannot offer Riot sign-in. for: [[riot-docs-lol#^f12]], [[riot-faqs#^f6]]
- C17: Hard lines for both products: no names or stats of others in ranked solo/duo champ select (obfuscated in the API anyway), no enemy cooldowns, power spike or decision prompts in game, no de-anonymising streamer mode, no augment win rates, no ads in game or loading screens, no automation of selection. for: [[riot-champ-select-anon-apps-1222#^f1]], [[riot-champ-select-anon-apps-1222#^f2]], [[overwolf-aug-2025-riot-guidelines#^f4]], [[riot-docs-lol#^f5]], [[riot-docs-lol#^f11]], [[riot-policies-general#^f12]], [[playerone-valorant-instalock#^f1]]
- C18 (inferred): The private tool may go further on wording (win %, a top pick), depth (models, full pool analysis, opponent scouting from loading screen on), group pooling of friends' own data and Discord, but not past C17; Riot's "need it to be competitive" test argues against it ever going public. from: C6, C14, C15, C17
- C19 (inferred): The public app should match the mainstream phase loop without the line-sitting parts: 3 to 5 explained options with pool and mastery, static pre-game matchup facts, post-game review as facts, progress between games, opt-in sharing of your own stats. from: C1, C4, C5, C13, C17

## Disagreements

C6 vs meiolol's rule against "+X%" wording: Riot's text bans apps that "dictate player
decisions" ([[riot-docs-lol#^f10]]), not win percentages, and iTero, LoLDraftAI and Team
Advisor show them unchallenged. The production-key rule is our cautious reading; a Riot answer
in the application would settle it. Players split on scouting: opponents yes, teammates divides
([[reddit-lookup-stats-sentiment#^f5]], C/fair).
