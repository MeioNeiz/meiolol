# What changed for League and comparable game tools in 2025-2026: new entrants, AI or LLM coaching apps, Vanguard, overlay and policy changes, paywalls, shutdowns?

Feeds: docs/plan/app.md "Policy line" and Steps; docs/production-key.md; what kinds of feature are safe to build next

## Answer

Ownership consolidated: every big League companion now has a corporate or esports owner
(Porofessor's maker to MOBA Network 2023, YOUR.GG to Gen.G 2024, iTero to GIANTX 2024,
Mobalytics to ESL FACEIT Group, Saudi PIF backed, Mar 2025) (C1). Apps moved off
Overwolf to standalone builds (iTero Nov 2025, Porofessor Dec 2025), while Overwolf grows
on mods (C2).

AI coaching is the new feature, and it launches paid and metered: YOUR.GG's voice and
video coaching on credits with LLM costs passed on (£3.49-12.99 a month), OP.GG's
creator voices and Premium match review, Loldosa's one free review a day (C3). The
standalone live-voice pioneer (Backseat AI, Tyler1, $4.45M seed 2024) has a dead site
by Nov 2026 while the feature lives inside incumbents (C4, inferred). Platform owners
entered by reading the screen (Microsoft Gaming Copilot, Aug 2025), out of Riot's policy
reach; Razer's esports coach pivoted to a general companion before launch (C5). OP.GG
serves its stats and anyone's match history to AI agents through an official server
(Apr 2025), close to Riot's no-data-broker rule (C6).

Policy: Riot has no rule on AI coaches as such, but blames AI-built apps for two 2026
cuts, the memory block (6 Oct) and a 10-an-hour replay download cap (26.21, 20 Oct)
(C7). In-game cuts were preceded by public pressure and Riot staff saying so months
ahead (C8). Enforcement looks uneven: vendors still advertise in-game decision support
in late 2026 (C9). No feature moved between free and paid at the big four in two
searches; no shutdown confirmed. Removals before Oct 2026: [[league-tools-riot-removals]].
