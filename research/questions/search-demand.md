# What do League of Legends players search for most, and which of those searches could meiolol answer better than existing sites?

Feeds: repositioning for improving ranked players (Silver to Emerald, learning first):
which pages to build first, and whether "champion or items vs enemy team comp" has
search demand.

## Answer

No absolute volumes: Google Trends refused us (429) and keyword tools paywall volumes, so
the ranking is from Similarweb's top-5 keywords, Google autocomplete and Reddit. The
big sites are mostly reached directly or by brand name (C1); their non-brand head terms
are tier lists and champion builds (C2). Autocomplete on a champion name offers
counters and builds (C3). Below the head sit learning searches: "<champ> vs <champ>",
"how to beat X as Y", "how to play X vs Y", "<champ> matchup guide/spreadsheet" (C4), and
item searches by enemy trait or opponent: "items against tanks/shields/lifesteal",
"<champ> build vs tanks/<opponent>", "lethality vs crit" (C5). Climbing searches seek
Reddit (C6).

Existing answers to the learning searches are weak: the top matchup page shows 15-minute
snapshots and paywalls its tips (C7); op.gg's tips are thin; players stitch stats and
Reddit (C8). Item-vs-enemy searches get stale listicles (C9); the one tool adapting
items to the enemy team is a live in-game overlay (C10), and u.gg filters builds by one
lane opponent only (C11).

Comp demand exists but is phrased as a class against a comp type, mostly tanks: "best adc
against tanks", "mages vs tanky comps", "how to play against melees/juggernauts" (C12).
Generic "counter team comp" phrasing barely appears (C13); "team comp" searches want a
builder or analyzer tool (C14), a niche LoLTheory, DraftGap and iTero already serve with
ranked pick lists (C15). The Reddit threads on comp types get many replies, contradict
each other and cite no data (C16).

So meiolol's best openings are (C17): matchup pages that answer "how to beat X as Y"
with its minute curves (when the lane turns) and free explanations; and "<class or
champion> vs tanks / poke / dive" pages with item advice by enemy comp profile, which
nobody serves with current data. Tier lists, plain builds and counters are crowded and
won by brand habit.

## Disagreements

- Low-elo advice says play what you know and adapt builds, not counter-pick
  ([[ss-adapt-pick-build-matchup#^f4]], [[ss-dont-counterpick-unfamiliar#^f1]]): a
  comp-vs page should lead with items and play for the player's own champion, not a pick.
