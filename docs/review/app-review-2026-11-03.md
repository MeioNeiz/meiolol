# Jacob's app review, 3 Nov 2026

Jacob's site notes 94 to 121 on the app review pages (/private/app, every mock scene in
both editions; arch-app.md "Review pages"), unedited, by screen. Note 96 (sign-in line
off to the side) was a fix, not a wish. Plan from it: docs/plan/app.md "Jacob's app
review".

## Themes (index, not his words)

- A full app, not a champ select card: settings, a menu reachable in every phase, the
  site's features inside it (draft tester, pick planning, patch, champion pool), the
  site's style and quality, links to the site for more
- Fewer words everywhere; loading and download as animations and bars, not text
- Fits full screen as well as small; less scrolling (the comp view went unseen)
- Every phase has something worth seeing: lobby, queue (recent form, what you play
  well), in game (items, levelling, tips), no empty or "boring" screens
- More options and their effects: many more picks with why, each effect as a champion
  and a number, win chance, win rate by game time, who carries, runes, summoners,
  global next to EUW
- Other seats: click a role for its perspective; players (mains, roles, recent picks,
  streaks, funny notes, Porofessor-like comments) where policy allows
- More, shorter and funnier comments (loading cards, comp tips)
- Interaction: move picks around, bans to guess roles
