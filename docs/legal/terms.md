## What meiolol is

A free learning tool for League of Legends. It shows how lane matchups usually unfold,
from ranked games collected through the official Riot Games API, and *(when live)* how
your own games compare. Its statistics are estimates from past games. They describe what
happened, not what you should do, and they can be wrong or out of date.
